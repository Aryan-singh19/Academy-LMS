@ au501 | AU-501 | Automotive Engines | Engine construction, fuel systems, combustion, engine management, performance and testing of automotive engines.
# Engine Construction
- Engine Classification for Vehicles | Automotive engines are classified by layout and cycle. | Inline Engine = cylinders arranged in a single row | V-Engine = two cylinder banks forming a V | Boxer Engine = horizontally opposed cylinders
- Cylinder Block & Head | Main structural parts house the combustion process. | Cylinder Block = casting containing cylinders and coolant passages | Cylinder Head = closes cylinders and holds valves | Cylinder Liner = replaceable wet or dry sleeve
- Piston, Rings & Connecting Rod | Reciprocating parts transmit gas force. | Piston = transmits gas force to connecting rod | Compression Ring = seals combustion gases | Oil Control Ring = scrapes excess oil from cylinder wall
- Crankshaft, Camshaft & Valve Train | Rotating parts convert and time motion. | Crankshaft = converts reciprocating motion into rotation | Overhead Camshaft = camshaft placed in the cylinder head | Hydraulic Lifter = self-adjusting tappet removing valve clearance
# Fuel Systems
- Petrol Fuel Supply | Fuel is stored, pumped and metered. | Fuel Pump = delivers fuel from tank to engine | Fuel Filter = removes contaminants | Fuel Pressure Regulator = keeps injector pressure constant
- Electronic Fuel Injection | Sensors and ECU meter fuel precisely. | MAP Sensor = measures intake manifold pressure | Lambda Sensor = measures exhaust oxygen for closed-loop control | Throttle Body Injection = single injector above throttle
- Diesel Fuel Systems | Diesel engines inject fuel at high pressure. | Inline Pump = separate plunger for each cylinder | Distributor Pump = single plunger distributing to cylinders | CRDI = common rail direct injection with electronic injectors
- Alternative Fuel Systems | Gaseous fuels need special systems. | CNG Kit = cylinder, regulator and mixer or injectors | LPG System = liquefied petroleum gas vaporiser and mixer | Dual Fuel = diesel pilot ignites gaseous fuel
# Combustion & Engine Management
- SI Engine Combustion & Knock | Abnormal combustion limits compression ratio. | Detonation = autoignition of end gas | Pre-ignition = ignition before the spark by hot spots | Knock Sensor = detects knocking to retard timing
- CI Engine Combustion | Diesel combustion has distinct phases. | Ignition Delay Period = time before combustion starts | Rapid Combustion = uncontrolled burning of accumulated fuel | Controlled Combustion = burning as fuel is injected
- Engine Management Systems | ECUs control fuel, ignition and emissions. | Engine Management System = ECU controlling fuel, spark and idle | Closed-loop Control = feedback from oxygen sensor | OBD-II = on-board diagnostics standard for fault codes
- Variable Valve Timing | Valve events adapt to speed and load. | VVT = variable valve timing changing cam phase | VTEC = Honda system switching cam profiles | Cylinder Deactivation = shutting cylinders at light load
# Engine Performance
- Performance Characteristics | Torque and power vary with speed. | Torque Curve = engine torque versus speed | Brake Power = BP = 2πNT/60000 kW | Specific Power = power per litre of displacement
- Engine Testing Methods | Dynamometers measure engine output. | Engine Dynamometer = absorbs and measures engine power | Chassis Dynamometer = tests vehicle on rollers | Heat Balance = accounts for distribution of fuel energy
- Supercharging & Turbocharging | Forced induction increases power density. | Turbo Lag = delay in boost response | Wastegate = limits turbocharger boost pressure | VGT = variable geometry turbocharger
- Engine Downsizing | Smaller turbocharged engines save fuel. | Downsizing = smaller engines with turbocharging | Friction Reduction = lower friction coatings and oils | Start-Stop System = stops engine at idle
# Cooling, Lubrication & Emissions
- Cooling System Components | Coolant circulates through radiator. | Radiator = heat exchanger rejecting coolant heat | Water Pump = circulates coolant | Thermostat = opens at set temperature to allow flow
- Lubrication System | Oil reduces friction and cools parts. | Wet Sump = oil stored in engine sump | Dry Sump = separate oil tank for racing engines | SAE Grade = viscosity rating such as 10W-40
- Exhaust System | Exhaust carries gases out quietly. | Exhaust Manifold = collects gases from cylinders | Muffler = reduces exhaust noise | DPF = diesel particulate filter
- Emission Aftertreatment | Catalysts clean exhaust. | Three-way Catalyst = oxidises CO and HC, reduces NOx | SCR = selective catalytic reduction using urea for NOx | Lean NOx Trap = stores NOx during lean operation
