@ mt-rm | MT-RM | Research Methodology & IPR | Research design, literature review, data analysis, technical writing, ethics and intellectual property rights for M.Tech students.
# Research Fundamentals
- Meaning & Types of Research | Research is a systematic search for new knowledge. | Basic Research = expands fundamental knowledge without immediate application | Applied Research = solves a specific practical problem | Research Problem = clearly stated question the study answers
- Research Process | Research follows a structured cycle. | Hypothesis = testable statement predicting a relationship | Research Design = plan for collecting and analysing data | Research Gap = unanswered question identified from literature
- Literature Review | Reviews position the work among prior studies. | Systematic Review = structured search with inclusion criteria | Citation Database = Scopus, Web of Science, Google Scholar | Annotated Bibliography = sources with summaries and critique
- Problem Formulation | Good problems are specific and feasible. | Objectives = measurable aims of the research | Scope = boundaries of the study | Feasibility = time, data and resources available
# Data Collection & Analysis
- Data Collection Methods | Data comes from experiments, surveys and simulations. | Primary Data = collected first-hand for the study | Secondary Data = collected earlier by others | Sampling = selecting a representative subset
- Statistical Analysis | Statistics test hypotheses. | t-test = compares means of two groups | ANOVA = compares means of several groups | Regression = models relationship between variables
- Design of Experiments | Experiments are planned to isolate effects. | Factorial Design = varies several factors simultaneously | Taguchi Method = orthogonal arrays for robust design | Replication = repeating runs to estimate error
- Modelling & Simulation | Models test ideas cheaply. | Simulation = imitating system behaviour on computer | Validation = checking model against reality | Sensitivity Analysis = effect of parameter changes
# Technical Writing
- Structure of a Research Paper | Papers follow IMRaD. | IMRaD = introduction, methods, results and discussion | Abstract = concise summary of the whole paper | Keywords = terms used for indexing
- Thesis Writing | A dissertation documents the full work. | Thesis Chapters = introduction, review, methodology, results, conclusion | Figures and Tables = present results clearly | Future Scope = directions for further work
- Referencing Styles | Citations credit prior work. | IEEE Style = numbered citations in brackets | APA Style = author-date citations | Reference Manager = Zotero or Mendeley
- Publishing | Journals and conferences disseminate research. | Peer Review = evaluation by experts before publication | Impact Factor = average citations per article | Open Access = freely available publication
# Research Ethics
- Ethics in Research | Researchers must act honestly. | Research Ethics = principles of honesty and integrity | Informed Consent = participants agree knowingly | Conflict of Interest = competing interests affecting judgement
- Plagiarism | Copying without credit is misconduct. | Plagiarism = using others' work without attribution | Similarity Check = software such as Turnitin | Self-plagiarism = reusing own published text without citation
- Research Misconduct | Fabrication and falsification undermine science. | Fabrication = making up data | Falsification = manipulating data or results | Predatory Journal = publishes without genuine peer review
- Reproducibility | Results must be repeatable. | Reproducibility = others obtain same results with same data | Open Data = sharing datasets publicly | Code Availability = publishing analysis code
# Intellectual Property Rights
- Types of IPR | IPR protects creations of the mind. | Patent = exclusive right to an invention for 20 years | Copyright = protects original works of authorship | Trademark = distinctive sign identifying goods
- Patents | Patents require specific criteria. | Novelty = invention must be new | Inventive Step = not obvious to a skilled person | Industrial Applicability = can be made or used in industry
- Patent Process in India | Applications follow defined stages. | Provisional Specification = early filing to secure priority date | Complete Specification = full description and claims | Patent Claims = define scope of protection
- Other IPR & Technology Transfer | Other protections and commercialisation. | Industrial Design = protects appearance of products | Geographical Indication = product linked to origin | Technology Transfer = licensing research to industry

# Probability & Statistics

- Joint Distributions & Bayes | Multiple variables and inference. | Joint Distribution = probability of variables together | Bayes' Rule = posterior ∝ likelihood × prior | Covariance Matrix = pairwise covariances of a random vector
- Estimation | Parameters are estimated from data. | Maximum Likelihood Estimation = parameter maximising likelihood | MAP Estimation = maximises posterior | Unbiased Estimator = expected value equals true parameter
- Limit Theorems & Inequalities | Bounds describe large samples. | Law of Large Numbers = sample mean converges to expected value | Central Limit Theorem = sum of many variables tends to Gaussian | Chernoff Bound = exponential tail bound
# Random Processes & Queueing

- Markov Chains | Next state depends only on current state. | Markov Property = memoryless state transitions | Transition Matrix = probabilities between states | Stationary Distribution = π = πP
- Queueing Models | Queues model networks and servers. | M/M/1 Queue = Poisson arrivals, exponential service, one server | Little's Law = L = λW | Utilisation = ρ = λ/μ

- Gradient Methods | Iterative descent finds minima. | Gradient Descent = x ← x − η∇f(x) | Newton's Method = uses Hessian for quadratic convergence | Stochastic Gradient Descent = uses random mini-batches
- Constrained Optimisation | Constraints are handled with multipliers. | Lagrangian = f(x) + λg(x) | KKT Conditions = optimality conditions with inequality constraints | Duality Gap = difference between primal and dual optima

- Spectral Graph Theory | Matrices reveal graph structure. | Graph Laplacian = L = D − A | Algebraic Connectivity = second smallest Laplacian eigenvalue | Spectral Clustering = clusters using Laplacian eigenvectors
- Combinatorial Counting | Counting supports algorithm analysis. | Generating Function = power series encoding sequences | Catalan Numbers = count binary trees and balanced parentheses | Inclusion-Exclusion = counts union by alternating sums
- Information Theory Basics | Information measures underpin ML. | Entropy = H(X) = −Σ p log p | KL Divergence = difference between distributions | Mutual Information = shared information between variables

- Parameterised Complexity | Fixed-parameter tractability. | FPT = solvable in f(k)·poly(n) time | Kernelisation = reduce instance to size depending on k | Bounded Search Tree = branching with depth k
