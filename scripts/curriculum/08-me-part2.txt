@ me601 | ME-601 | Thermal Engineering & Gas Dynamics | Boilers, steam nozzles and turbines, condensers, compressors and compressible flow.
# Steam Generators & Boilers
- Classification of Boilers | Boilers generate steam for power and process. | Fire-tube Boiler = hot gases pass through tubes surrounded by water | Water-tube Boiler = water flows in tubes heated externally; high pressure | Babcock and Wilcox Boiler = inclined water-tube boiler
- High-pressure Boilers | Modern boilers operate at very high pressures. | La Mont Boiler = forced circulation boiler | Benson Boiler = supercritical once-through boiler | Supercritical Boiler = operates above critical pressure; no boiling
- Boiler Mountings & Accessories | Fittings ensure safety and efficiency. | Safety Valve = releases excess pressure | Economiser = preheats feed water using flue gases | Superheater = raises steam temperature above saturation
- Boiler Performance & Draught | Performance is rated by evaporation and efficiency. | Equivalent Evaporation = water evaporated from and at 100 °C | Boiler Efficiency = heat used in steam / heat supplied by fuel | Natural Draught = chimney creates draught by density difference
# Steam Nozzles & Turbines
- Flow Through Nozzles | Nozzles convert enthalpy into kinetic energy. | Nozzle Exit Velocity = V = 44.72√(Δh) with Δh in kJ/kg | Critical Pressure Ratio = about 0.546 for superheated steam | Supersaturated Flow = steam expands without condensing (metastable)
- Impulse Turbines | Impulse turbines expand steam in nozzles only. | Impulse Turbine = pressure drop occurs only in nozzles | De Laval Turbine = single-stage impulse turbine | Blade Efficiency = maximum when blade speed ratio is cos α/2
- Compounding of Turbines | Compounding reduces rotor speed. | Velocity Compounding = Curtis stage with moving and fixed blade rows | Pressure Compounding = Rateau stages of nozzles and blades | Pressure-Velocity Compounding = combination of both
- Reaction Turbines | Steam expands in both fixed and moving blades. | Reaction Turbine = pressure drop in fixed and moving blades | Degree of Reaction = enthalpy drop in moving blades / stage drop | Parsons Turbine = 50% reaction turbine
# Condensers & Cooling Towers
- Steam Condensers | Condensers lower back pressure and recover water. | Surface Condenser = steam and cooling water do not mix | Jet Condenser = steam mixes with cooling water | Condenser Vacuum = lower pressure increases turbine work
- Condenser Efficiency | Air leakage reduces performance. | Vacuum Efficiency = actual vacuum / ideal vacuum | Dalton's Law = total pressure is the sum of partial pressures of steam and air | Air Pump = removes air from condenser
- Cooling Towers | Cooling towers reject heat to atmosphere. | Natural Draught Cooling Tower = hyperbolic tower using buoyancy | Range = inlet minus outlet water temperature | Approach = outlet water temperature minus wet-bulb temperature
- Rankine Cycle Improvements | Plant efficiency is raised by modifications. | Rankine Cycle = ideal steam power cycle | Reheat = steam reheated after partial expansion | Regeneration = feed water heated by bled steam
# Air Compressors
- Reciprocating Compressors | Pistons compress air in cylinders. | Isothermal Efficiency = isothermal work / actual work | Clearance Volume = reduces volumetric efficiency | Volumetric Efficiency = 1 + C − C(p2/p1)^(1/n)
- Multistage Compression | Staging with intercooling saves work. | Intercooling = cooling air between stages | Perfect Intercooling = air cooled to initial temperature | Optimum Pressure Ratio = equal ratio per stage, √(p3/p1) for two stages
- Rotary Compressors | Rotary machines deliver continuous flow. | Centrifugal Compressor = impeller imparts kinetic energy converted in diffuser | Axial Flow Compressor = rows of rotor and stator blades | Surging = unstable flow reversal at low flow rates
- Compressor Performance | Performance maps show operating limits. | Slip Factor = ratio of actual to ideal whirl velocity | Choking = maximum flow when Mach 1 is reached | Pressure Coefficient = actual / isentropic pressure rise
# Gas Dynamics
- Compressible Flow Basics | Density changes matter at high speeds. | Mach Number = M = V/a | Speed of Sound = a = √(γRT) | Stagnation Properties = properties when flow is brought to rest isentropically
- Isentropic Flow in Nozzles | Area changes accelerate subsonic and supersonic flow differently. | Area-Velocity Relation = dA/A = (M² − 1)dV/V | Convergent-Divergent Nozzle = needed to reach supersonic speeds | Choked Flow = maximum mass flow at M = 1 at throat
- Normal Shock Waves | Shocks are abrupt irreversible changes. | Normal Shock = discontinuity converting supersonic to subsonic flow | Prandtl Relation = V1·V2 = a*² | Entropy Rise = entropy increases across a shock
- Fanno & Rayleigh Flow | Friction and heat transfer affect duct flow. | Fanno Flow = adiabatic flow with friction | Rayleigh Flow = frictionless flow with heat transfer | Choking Length = duct length driving flow to M = 1

- Endurance Limit Modifying Factors | Real components have lower endurance limits. | Surface Finish Factor = rougher surfaces reduce fatigue strength | Size Factor = larger parts have lower endurance | Reliability Factor = adjusts for desired reliability
- Design of Shafts | Shafts carry torque and bending. | ASME Shaft Equation = combines bending and torsion with shock factors | Equivalent Torque = Te = √((KmM)² + (KtT)²) | Critical Speed = shaft speed causing resonance

- Leaf Springs | Laminated springs carry vehicle loads. | Leaf Spring = layered plates with graduated lengths | Nipping = pre-stress by different curvature of leaves | Full-length Leaves = longest leaves including master leaf
- Rolling Contact Bearings | Ball and roller bearings reduce friction. | Dynamic Load Rating = load for L10 life of one million revolutions | Bearing Life Equation = L10 = (C/P)^3 for ball bearings | Equivalent Dynamic Load = P = XFr + YFa

- Bevel Gears | Bevel gears connect intersecting shafts. | Bevel Gear = conical gear for intersecting shafts | Pitch Cone Angle = cone angle of the pitch surface | Miter Gears = equal bevel gears at 90°

- Brakes | Brakes absorb kinetic energy. | Block Brake = shoe pressed against drum | Internal Expanding Shoe Brake = used in vehicles | Disc Brake = calipers clamp a rotating disc
- Engine Components Design | IC engine parts are designed for gas and inertia loads. | Cylinder Design = wall thickness from hoop stress | Piston Design = crown thickness by Grashoff's formula | Connecting Rod = I-section designed against buckling
- Crankshaft & Flywheel | Rotating parts handle torque fluctuation. | Crankshaft = converts reciprocating to rotary motion | Flywheel = stores energy to smooth speed fluctuation | Coefficient of Fluctuation of Speed = (ωmax − ωmin)/ω
