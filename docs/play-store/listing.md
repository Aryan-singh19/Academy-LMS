## Full description (max 4,000 characters)

```
Academy LMS is a free study companion for RGPV students. Pick your branch and semester once, and the app shows only your subjects: no hunting through other semesters, no clutter.

★ EVERY BRANCH, EVERY SEMESTER
• B.Tech semesters 1–8: CSE, IT (CSIT), ECE, Electrical (EX), Mechanical, Civil and Automobile
• M.Tech semesters 1–2: Computer Science, VLSI Design, Thermal, Structural and Power Systems
• 160+ subjects and 3,300+ topics, following the RGPV scheme and paper codes

★ NOTES BUILT FOR EXAMS
Every topic has:
• Core concepts and key points in plain language
• A concept map and a quick revision table
• An in-depth explanation for long answers
• An RGPV exam blueprint: what to write for 2, 4 and 7 mark questions
• Standard textbook references and lecture videos

★ PRACTICE THAT STICKS
• Thousands of RGPV-style MCQs, by topic or as a full mock test
• Track accuracy and see what to revise next

★ DOWNLOAD AND STUDY OFFLINE
• Unit-wise notes PDFs for every subject
• RGPV-pattern model question papers with answer hints
• Question banks with answers
• The study material is bundled in the app, so notes and quizzes still open without internet

★ YOUR OWN STUDY DESK
• Highlights, notes and bookmarks on any topic
• Progress per subject and semester
• Switch branch or semester any time. Your progress is never deleted
• Optional Google sign-in to sync across phone, laptop and the Windows app

Free, no ads, open source.

Note: model papers are practice papers generated from the syllabus, not official RGPV question papers. Always confirm your scheme on the official RGPV portal.
```
