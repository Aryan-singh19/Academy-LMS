Terms of use

Academy LMS is an educational study platform prototype. Students should use the material for revision support and still verify critical academic content with class notes and instructors.

Users should avoid posting abusive, illegal, or copyrighted material they do not have permission to share. Chat, comments, and direct messages are meant for academic collaboration.

The current identity model is lightweight and not yet production-authenticated, so highly sensitive data should not be stored here.
