Terms of Use & Code of Conduct

By using Academy LMS, you agree to participate in an honest, collaborative, and academically rigorous environment.

1. Educational Scope & Purpose

Academy LMS provides revision notes, algorithm diagrams, solved exam proofs, and practice testing modules for university Computer Science students. While content is crafted to match standard university syllabi, students are advised to also cross-verify university-specific exam notices and guidelines.

2. Respectful Community & Lecture Chat

The lecture chat rooms, discussion threads, and student profiles are dedicated purely to academic learning, technical inquiries, and peer collaboration. Harassment, spam, hate speech, or sharing copyrighted exam papers without permission is strictly prohibited.

3. Account & Device Responsibility

You are responsible for the security of your local browser data and Google account access. We recommend regular data exports of your personal notes via the Profile tab.
