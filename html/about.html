Why this exists

A calmer prep space for technical subjects.

Academy LMS brings topic notes, quizzes, resources, lectures, local memory, and early social learning tools together so students do not have to jump across ten tabs and three panic levels.

What students get

Persistent notes, highlights, progress tracking, topic comments, lecture chat, practice history, and profile analytics. The goal is to feel like a study room, not a cold file dump.

The current version uses browser storage plus Neon-backed APIs on Vercel. That makes it easy to keep iterating now and evolve into a fuller hosted product later.
