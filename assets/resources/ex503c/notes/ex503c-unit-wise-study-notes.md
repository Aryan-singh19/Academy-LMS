# EX-503(C) Renewable Power Generation — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Solar thermal and PV, wind energy, small hydro, biomass, fuel cells, storage and grid integration.

## Unit 1: Energy Scenario

### Renewable Energy Overview

Renewables reduce fossil dependence.

- **Renewable Energy:** naturally replenished energy
- **India's RE Target:** 500 GW non-fossil capacity by 2030
- **Capacity Factor:** actual output / rated output

### Solar Radiation

Solar resource estimation.

- **Solar Constant:** 1367 W/m²
- **Global Radiation:** beam plus diffuse radiation
- **Pyranometer:** measures global solar radiation

### Energy Policy

Policies support renewables.

- **MNRE:** Ministry of New and Renewable Energy
- **Renewable Purchase Obligation:** mandatory RE share for utilities
- **Net Metering:** billing for rooftop export

### Environmental Benefits

Renewables lower emissions.

- **Carbon Credit:** tradable unit for reduced emissions
- **Life Cycle Emissions:** emissions over full life
- **Sustainability:** meeting needs without depletion

## Unit 2: Solar Energy

### Solar Thermal Systems

Heat from sun is collected.

- **Flat Plate Collector:** low-temperature water heating
- **Concentrating Collector:** focuses sunlight for high temperature
- **Solar Pond:** salt gradient pond storing heat

### Photovoltaic Cell

PV converts light to electricity.

- **PV Cell:** p-n junction producing current from light
- **Fill Factor:** Pmax/(Voc Isc)
- **Cell Efficiency:** output / incident power

### PV Modules & Arrays

Cells form modules and arrays.

- **Series Connection:** increases voltage
- **Parallel Connection:** increases current
- **Bypass Diode:** protects shaded cells

### MPPT & PV Systems

Electronics extract maximum power.

- **MPPT:** maximum power point tracking
- **Perturb and Observe:** common MPPT algorithm
- **Grid-tied PV:** feeds power to grid via inverter

## Unit 3: Wind Energy

### Wind Power Basics

Wind kinetic energy is converted.

- **Power in Wind:** P = ½ρAV³
- **Betz Limit:** maximum 59.3% extraction
- **Tip Speed Ratio:** blade tip speed / wind speed

### Wind Turbines

Turbines differ in axis orientation.

- **HAWT:** horizontal axis wind turbine
- **VAWT:** vertical axis wind turbine, e.g. Darrieus
- **Pitch Control:** adjusting blade angle

### Wind Generators

Generators convert rotation to electricity.

- **DFIG:** doubly fed induction generator
- **PMSG:** permanent magnet synchronous generator
- **Full Converter:** decouples generator from grid

### Wind Farm Planning

Sites and layouts optimise output.

- **Wind Resource Assessment:** measuring wind speeds
- **Wake Effect:** reduced speed behind turbines
- **Offshore Wind:** turbines at sea

## Unit 4: Other Renewable Sources

### Small Hydro

Small hydro plants up to 25 MW.

- **Small Hydro:** up to 25 MW in India
- **Run-of-river:** no large storage
- **Micro Hydro:** up to 100 kW

### Biomass & Biogas

Organic matter produces energy.

- **Biomass Gasification:** converts biomass to producer gas
- **Biogas Plant:** anaerobic digestion producing methane
- **Cogeneration:** power and heat from biomass

### Geothermal & Ocean

Earth and ocean energy.

- **Geothermal Plant:** uses underground heat
- **Tidal Power:** uses tidal range
- **OTEC:** ocean thermal energy conversion

### Fuel Cells

Electrochemical generation.

- **Fuel Cell:** converts chemical energy directly to electricity
- **PEMFC:** proton exchange membrane fuel cell
- **Green Hydrogen:** hydrogen from renewable electrolysis

## Unit 5: Storage & Grid Integration

### Energy Storage

Storage balances intermittency.

- **Battery Storage:** lithium-ion and flow batteries
- **Pumped Hydro Storage:** largest grid storage
- **Supercapacitor:** high power short duration

### Grid Integration Issues

Variable renewables challenge grids.

- **Intermittency:** variable output of solar and wind
- **Grid Code:** rules for connecting generators
- **Curtailment:** forced reduction of RE output

### Power Electronic Interfaces

Converters link RE to grid.

- **Grid-tie Inverter:** synchronises with grid
- **Islanding Protection:** disconnects during grid outage
- **Harmonics:** distortion from converters

### Hybrid & Microgrids

Combining sources improves reliability.

- **Hybrid System:** solar-wind-battery combination
- **Microgrid:** local grid able to island
- **Energy Management System:** optimises sources and loads
