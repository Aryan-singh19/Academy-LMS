## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Renewable Energy: naturally replenished energy
- **Q1(b):** Solar Constant: 1367 W/m² | Global Radiation: beam plus diffuse radiation | Pyranometer: measures global solar radiation
- **Q2(a):** Fill Factor: Pmax/(Voc Isc)
- **Q2(b):** Series Connection: increases voltage | Parallel Connection: increases current | Bypass Diode: protects shaded cells
- **Q3(a):** Full Converter: decouples generator from grid
- **Q3(b):** Wind Resource Assessment: measuring wind speeds | Wake Effect: reduced speed behind turbines | Offshore Wind: turbines at sea
- **Q4(a):** Fuel Cell: converts chemical energy directly to electricity
- **Q4(b):** Small Hydro: up to 25 MW in India | Run-of-river: no large storage | Micro Hydro: up to 100 kW
- **Q5(a):** Pumped Hydro Storage: largest grid storage
- **Q5(b):** Intermittency: variable output of solar and wind | Grid Code: rules for connecting generators | Curtailment: forced reduction of RE output
- **Q6(a):** Sustainability: meeting needs without depletion
- **Q6(b):** Renewable Energy: naturally replenished energy | India's RE Target: 500 GW non-fossil capacity by 2030 | Capacity Factor: actual output / rated output
- **Q7(a):** Power in Wind: P = ½ρAV³
- **Q7(b):** HAWT: horizontal axis wind turbine | VAWT: vertical axis wind turbine, e.g. Darrieus | Pitch Control: adjusting blade angle
- **Q8(a):** Grid Code: rules for connecting generators
- **Q8(b):** Grid-tie Inverter: synchronises with grid | Islanding Protection: disconnects during grid outage | Harmonics: distortion from converters
