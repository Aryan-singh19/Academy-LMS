## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Optical Fibre: thin glass waveguide carrying light
- **Q1(b):** Total Internal Reflection: reflection when angle exceeds critical angle | Critical Angle: sin θc = n2/n1 | Snell's Law: n1 sin θ1 = n2 sin θ2
- **Q2(a):** Rayleigh Scattering: loss proportional to 1/λ⁴
- **Q2(b):** Intermodal Dispersion: different modes arrive at different times | Chromatic Dispersion: different wavelengths travel at different speeds | Polarisation Mode Dispersion: differential delay of polarisation modes
- **Q3(a):** Fabry-Perot Laser: simple cavity laser with multiple modes
- **Q3(b):** Direct Modulation: varying drive current | External Modulator: e.g. Mach-Zehnder modulator for high speed | Chirp: frequency shift during modulation causing dispersion
- **Q4(a):** Transimpedance Amplifier: converts photocurrent to voltage
- **Q4(b):** Responsivity: R = Ip/P0 A/W | Quantum Efficiency: electrons generated per incident photon | Cut-off Wavelength: λc = 1.24/Eg µm
- **Q5(a):** System Margin: extra power allowed for ageing
- **Q5(b):** Rise Time Budget: system rise time is root-sum-square of components | NRZ Requirement: tsys ≤ 0.7/B | Component Rise Times: transmitter, fibre dispersion and receiver
- **Q6(a):** Number of Modes: approximately V²/2 for step-index fibre
- **Q6(b):** Optical Fibre: thin glass waveguide carrying light | Advantages of OFC: huge bandwidth, low loss, EMI immunity | Optical Windows: low-loss bands near 850, 1310 and 1550 nm
- **Q7(a):** Surface-emitting LED: emits from the surface with broad pattern
- **Q7(b):** Stimulated Emission: photon triggers emission of identical photon | Population Inversion: more electrons in excited state than ground | Threshold Current: current above which lasing begins
- **Q8(a):** NRZ Requirement: tsys ≤ 0.7/B
- **Q8(b):** WDM: multiple wavelengths on one fibre | DWDM: dense WDM with narrow channel spacing | EDFA: erbium-doped fibre amplifier for the 1550 nm band
