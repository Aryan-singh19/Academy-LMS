## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Gradient: vector of maximum rate of increase of a scalar
- **Q1(b):** Cartesian Coordinates: x, y, z | Cylindrical Coordinates: ρ, φ, z | Spherical Coordinates: r, θ, φ
- **Q2(a):** Energy Stored: W = ½CV²
- **Q2(b):** Tangential E: continuous across a dielectric boundary | Normal D: differs by surface charge density | Perfect Conductor Boundary: tangential E is zero
- **Q3(a):** Vector Magnetic Potential: B = ∇ × A
- **Q3(b):** Lorentz Force: F = q(E + v × B) | Inductance: L = NΦ/I | Magnetic Energy: W = ½LI²
- **Q4(a):** Skin Depth: δ = 1/√(πfμσ)
- **Q4(b):** Faraday's Law: emf = −dΦ/dt | Displacement Current Density: Jd = ∂D/∂t | Lenz's Law: induced current opposes the change causing it
- **Q5(a):** Lossless Line: R = G = 0, Z0 = √(L/C)
- **Q5(b):** Reflection Coefficient: Γ = (ZL − Z0)/(ZL + Z0) | VSWR: (1 + magnitude of Γ)/(1 − magnitude of Γ) | Standing Wave: pattern of maxima and minima from reflected waves
- **Q6(a):** E: −∇V = field is the negative gradient of potential
- **Q6(b):** Gradient: vector of maximum rate of increase of a scalar | Divergence: net outward flux per unit volume | Curl: circulation per unit area
- **Q7(a):** Biot-Savart Law: dH = I dl × aR / (4πR²)
- **Q7(b):** Ampere's Law: closed line integral of H·dl = Ienc | Point Form: ∇ × H = J | Solenoid Field: H = nI inside a long solenoid
- **Q8(a):** VSWR: (1 + magnitude of Γ)/(1 − magnitude of Γ)
- **Q8(b):** Input Impedance: depends on load, Z0 and electrical length | Quarter-wave Transformer: Zin = Z0²/ZL | Half-wave Line: repeats the load impedance
