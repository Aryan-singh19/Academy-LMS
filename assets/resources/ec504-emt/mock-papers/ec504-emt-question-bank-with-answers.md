## Unit 2: Dielectrics, Capacitance & Boundary Conditions

- **Q.** Define *Relative Permittivity*.  
  **Ans.** εr = ε/ε0

  **Ans.** C = εA/d
- **Q.** Define *Energy Stored*.  
  **Ans.** W = ½CV²

- **Q.** Define *Tangential E*.

- **Q.** Define *Laplace's Equation*.  
  **Ans.** ∇²V = 0 in charge-free regions
- **Q.** Define *Uniqueness Theorem*.  
  **Ans.** solution satisfying boundary conditions is unique

- **Q.** Explain *Capacitance* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Capacitance stores electric energy. Parallel Plate Capacitance — C = εA/d; Energy Stored — W = ½CV²; Coaxial Capacitance — C = 2πεL / ln(b/a).
- **Q.** Explain *Boundary Conditions* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Fields obey rules at material interfaces. Tangential E — continuous across a dielectric boundary; Normal D — differs by surface charge density; Perfect Conductor Boundary — tangential E is zero.

  **Key points:** Potential satisfies differential equations. Poisson's Equation — ∇²V = −ρv/ε; Laplace's Equation — ∇²V = 0 in charge-free regions; Uniqueness Theorem — solution satisfying boundary conditions is unique.

## Unit 3: Magnetostatics

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Biot-Savart Law*.  
  **Ans.** dH = I dl × aR / (4πR²)
- **Q.** Define *Field of Infinite Wire*.  
  **Ans.** H = I/(2πρ)
- **Q.** Define *Magnetic Field Intensity*.  
  **Ans.** H in amperes per metre
- **Q.** Define *Ampere's Law*.  
  **Ans.** closed line integral of H·dl = Ienc
- **Q.** Define *Point Form*.  
  **Ans.** ∇ × H = J
- **Q.** Define *Solenoid Field*.  
  **Ans.** H = nI inside a long solenoid
- **Q.** Define *Magnetic Flux Density*.  
  **Ans.** B = μH
- **Q.** Define *No Magnetic Monopoles*.  
  **Ans.** ∇·B = 0
- **Q.** Define *Vector Magnetic Potential*.  
  **Ans.** B = ∇ × A
- **Q.** Define *Lorentz Force*.  
  **Ans.** F = q(E + v × B)
- **Q.** Define *Inductance*.  
  **Ans.** L = NΦ/I
- **Q.** Define *Magnetic Energy*.  
  **Ans.** W = ½LI²

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Biot-Savart Law* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Currents produce magnetic fields. Biot-Savart Law — dH = I dl × aR / (4πR²); Field of Infinite Wire — H = I/(2πρ); Magnetic Field Intensity — H in amperes per metre.
- **Q.** Explain *Ampere's Circuital Law* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Line integral of H equals enclosed current. Ampere's Law — closed line integral of H·dl = Ienc; Point Form — ∇ × H = J; Solenoid Field — H = nI inside a long solenoid.
- **Q.** Explain *Magnetic Flux & Vector Potential* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Flux density and potentials describe magnetic fields. Magnetic Flux Density — B = μH; No Magnetic Monopoles — ∇·B = 0; Vector Magnetic Potential — B = ∇ × A.
- **Q.** Explain *Magnetic Forces & Inductance* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Fields exert forces and store energy. Lorentz Force — F = q(E + v × B); Inductance — L = NΦ/I; Magnetic Energy — W = ½LI².

## Unit 4: Maxwell's Equations & Plane Waves

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Faraday's Law*.  
  **Ans.** emf = −dΦ/dt
- **Q.** Define *Displacement Current Density*.  
  **Ans.** Jd = ∂D/∂t
- **Q.** Define *Lenz's Law*.  
  **Ans.** induced current opposes the change causing it
- **Q.** Define *Maxwell's Equations*.  
  **Ans.** Gauss's laws for D and B, Faraday's law, Ampere-Maxwell law
- **Q.** Define *Integral Form*.  
  **Ans.** equations over surfaces and contours
- **Q.** Define *Time-harmonic Form*.  
  **Ans.** phasor form with ∂/∂t replaced by jω
- **Q.** Define *Wave Equation*.  
  **Ans.** ∇²E = με ∂²E/∂t²
- **Q.** Define *Intrinsic Impedance*.  
  **Ans.** η = √(μ/ε); 377 Ω in free space
- **Q.** Define *Propagation Constant*.  
  **Ans.** γ = α + jβ
- **Q.** Define *Skin Depth*.  
  **Ans.** δ = 1/√(πfμσ)
- **Q.** Define *Poynting Vector*.  
  **Ans.** P = E × H, power flow density
- **Q.** Define *Polarisation*.  
  **Ans.** orientation of the E field: linear, circular, elliptical

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Faraday's Law & Displacement Current* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Time-varying fields complete Maxwell's equations. Faraday's Law — emf = −dΦ/dt; Displacement Current Density — Jd = ∂D/∂t; Lenz's Law — induced current opposes the change causing it.
- **Q.** Explain *Maxwell's Equations* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Four equations govern all electromagnetic fields. Maxwell's Equations — Gauss's laws for D and B, Faraday's law, Ampere-Maxwell law; Integral Form — equations over surfaces and contours; Time-harmonic Form — phasor form with ∂/∂t replaced by jω.
- **Q.** Explain *Uniform Plane Waves* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Waves propagate in free space and media. Wave Equation — ∇²E = με ∂²E/∂t²; Intrinsic Impedance — η = √(μ/ε); 377 Ω in free space; Propagation Constant — γ = α + jβ.
- **Q.** Explain *Wave Propagation in Media* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Losses, skin effect and polarisation. Skin Depth — δ = 1/√(πfμσ); Poynting Vector — P = E × H, power flow density; Polarisation — orientation of the E field: linear, circular, elliptical.

## Unit 5: Transmission Lines

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Characteristic Impedance*.  
  **Ans.** Z0 = √((R + jωL)/(G + jωC))
- **Q.** Define *Lossless Line*.  
  **Ans.** R = G = 0, Z0 = √(L/C)
- **Q.** Define *Velocity of Propagation*.  
  **Ans.** v = 1/√(LC)
- **Q.** Define *Reflection Coefficient*.  
  **Ans.** Γ = (ZL − Z0)/(ZL + Z0)
- **Q.** Define *VSWR*.  
  **Ans.** (1 + magnitude of Γ)/(1 − magnitude of Γ)
- **Q.** Define *Standing Wave*.  
  **Ans.** pattern of maxima and minima from reflected waves
- **Q.** Define *Input Impedance*.  
  **Ans.** depends on load, Z0 and electrical length
- **Q.** Define *Quarter-wave Transformer*.  
  **Ans.** Zin = Z0²/ZL
- **Q.** Define *Half-wave Line*.  
  **Ans.** repeats the load impedance
- **Q.** Define *Smith Chart*.  
  **Ans.** graphical plot of normalised impedance and reflection
- **Q.** Define *Single Stub Matching*.  
  **Ans.** short-circuited stub cancels susceptance
- **Q.** Define *Impedance Matching*.  
  **Ans.** making load equal Z0 to stop reflections

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Transmission Line Parameters* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Lines are modelled by distributed R, L, G, C. Characteristic Impedance — Z0 = √((R + jωL)/(G + jωC)); Lossless Line — R = G = 0, Z0 = √(L/C); Velocity of Propagation — v = 1/√(LC).
- **Q.** Explain *Reflection & Standing Waves* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Mismatched loads cause reflections. Reflection Coefficient — Γ = (ZL − Z0)/(ZL + Z0); VSWR — (1 + magnitude of Γ)/(1 − magnitude of Γ); Standing Wave — pattern of maxima and minima from reflected waves.
- **Q.** Explain *Input Impedance & Special Lines* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Line length transforms impedance. Input Impedance — depends on load, Z0 and electrical length; Quarter-wave Transformer — Zin = Z0²/ZL; Half-wave Line — repeats the load impedance.
- **Q.** Explain *Smith Chart & Matching* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Graphical tools design matching networks. Smith Chart — graphical plot of normalised impedance and reflection; Single Stub Matching — short-circuited stub cancels susceptance; Impedance Matching — making load equal Z0 to stop reflections.
