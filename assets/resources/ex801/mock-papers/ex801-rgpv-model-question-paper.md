- **Q1(a):** Electric Drive: motor, converter and controller

- **Q2(a):** Dual Converter Drive: four-quadrant operation
- **Q2(b):** Chopper Drive: DC-DC converter control | Regenerative Chopper: returns energy | Multi-quadrant Chopper: four-quadrant operation
- **Q3(a):** Static Scherbius Drive: recovers slip power to supply
- **Q3(b):** Field Oriented Control: decouples flux and torque | DTC: direct torque control | Sensorless Control: speed estimated without sensor
- **Q4(a):** SRM Drive: asymmetric bridge converter
- **Q4(b):** Open-loop Control: frequency control | Self-controlled Mode: load commutated inverter | LCI Drive: for large synchronous motors
- **Q5(a):** Dynamic Braking: dissipates in resistor
- **Q5(b):** Energy Recovery System: stores or returns braking energy | Traction Applications: trains and EVs | Lift Drives: regenerative elevators
- **Q6(a):** Heating Time Constant: governs temperature rise
- **Q6(b):** Electric Drive: motor, converter and controller | Load Torque: torque required by load | Four-quadrant Operation: forward/reverse motoring and braking
- **Q7(a):** Stator Voltage Control: AC voltage controller
- **Q7(b):** V/f Control: voltage proportional to frequency | VSI-fed Drive: voltage source inverter drive | CSI-fed Drive: current source inverter drive
- **Q8(a):** Traction Applications: trains and EVs
- **Q8(b):** DSP-based Drive: digital signal processor control | PWM Generation: digital modulation | Encoder Interface: position feedback
