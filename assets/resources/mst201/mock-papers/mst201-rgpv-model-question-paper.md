## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Plastic Moment: Mp = Zp fy
- **Q1(b):** Section Classification: plastic, compact, semi-compact, slender | Partial Safety Factors: γm0 = 1.10, γm1 = 1.25 | Design Strength: characteristic strength / γm
- **Q2(a):** Fatigue of Welds: stress range governs life
- **Q2(b):** End Plate Connection: bolted moment connection | Panel Zone: column web in joint | Stiffener: prevents local failure
- **Q3(a):** Standing Seam Roof: continuous roofing
- **Q3(b):** Space Truss: three-dimensional truss | Double Layer Grid: two layers connected by diagonals | Geodesic Dome: triangulated spherical structure
- **Q4(a):** Critical Temperature: about 550 °C
- **Q4(b):** Moment Resisting Frame: rigid joints | Braced Frame: concentric or eccentric bracing | Shear Wall-frame: combined system
- **Q5(a):** Wind Load on Tower: per IS 802
- **Q5(b):** Steel Chimney: self-supporting stack | Elevated Water Tank: steel tank on staging | Wind-induced Oscillation: vortex shedding
- **Q6(a):** Moment Amplification: second-order effect
- **Q6(b):** Plastic Moment: Mp = Zp fy | Shape Factor: Zp/Ze | Mechanism Method: finds collapse load
- **Q7(a):** Cold-formed Section: shaped at room temperature
- **Q7(b):** Portal Frame: rigid frame for sheds | Crane Gantry Girder: carries crane loads | Bracing System: resists wind and crane surge
- **Q8(a):** Elevated Water Tank: steel tank on staging
- **Q8(b):** Plate Girder Bridge: for medium spans | Truss Bridge: Pratt or Warren configuration | IRC Loading: Class AA and 70R vehicle loads
