# MST-201 Advanced Design of Steel Structures — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Plastic design, connections, light-gauge, industrial buildings, multistorey frames, towers and bridges in steel.

## Unit 1: Plastic & Limit State Design

### Plastic Analysis Review

Collapse-based design.

- **Plastic Moment:** Mp = Zp fy
- **Shape Factor:** Zp/Ze
- **Mechanism Method:** finds collapse load

### Limit State Design per IS 800

Indian code approach.

- **Section Classification:** plastic, compact, semi-compact, slender
- **Partial Safety Factors:** γm0 = 1.10, γm1 = 1.25
- **Design Strength:** characteristic strength / γm

### Local Buckling

Thin elements buckle locally.

- **Width-thickness Ratio:** controls local buckling
- **Effective Width:** reduced width for slender plates
- **Slender Section:** local buckling before yield

### Combined Forces

Members under axial and bending.

- **Interaction Equation:** P/Pd + M/Md ≤ 1
- **Beam-column:** member with axial force and moment
- **Moment Amplification:** second-order effect

## Unit 2: Connections

### Bolted Connections Advanced

High-strength bolts.

- **HSFG Bolt:** slip-critical friction connection
- **Prying Action:** extra bolt tension from flange bending
- **Bolt Group Eccentric Load:** combined shear and torsion

### Welded Connections Advanced

Weld design.

- **Eccentric Weld Group:** polar moment of weld
- **Fatigue of Welds:** stress range governs life
- **Weld Defects:** porosity, lack of fusion

### Moment Connections

Rigid beam-column joints.

- **End Plate Connection:** bolted moment connection
- **Panel Zone:** column web in joint
- **Stiffener:** prevents local failure

### Base Plates & Splices

Column bases and joints.

- **Moment-resisting Base:** anchor bolts in tension
- **Column Splice:** joins column segments
- **Shear Key:** transfers horizontal shear

## Unit 3: Light-gauge & Industrial Structures

### Cold-formed Steel

Thin-walled sections.

- **Cold-formed Section:** shaped at room temperature
- **Distortional Buckling:** cross-section distorts
- **IS 801:** code for light-gauge steel

### Industrial Buildings

Portal frames and gantries.

- **Portal Frame:** rigid frame for sheds
- **Crane Gantry Girder:** carries crane loads
- **Bracing System:** resists wind and crane surge

### Pre-engineered Buildings

Economical steel systems.

- **PEB:** factory-designed tapered sections
- **Purlins and Girts:** secondary members
- **Standing Seam Roof:** continuous roofing

### Space Structures

Large-span structures.

- **Space Truss:** three-dimensional truss
- **Double Layer Grid:** two layers connected by diagonals
- **Geodesic Dome:** triangulated spherical structure

## Unit 4: Multistorey & Tall Steel Buildings

### Lateral Load Systems

Resisting wind and earthquake.

- **Moment Resisting Frame:** rigid joints
- **Braced Frame:** concentric or eccentric bracing
- **Shear Wall-frame:** combined system

### Composite Construction

Steel and concrete together.

- **Composite Beam:** steel beam with concrete slab
- **Shear Connector:** stud transferring shear
- **Composite Column:** steel section encased or filled

### Seismic Design of Steel

Ductile steel systems.

- **Special Moment Frame:** high ductility
- **Eccentrically Braced Frame:** link yields
- **Capacity Design:** protects non-ductile elements

### Fire Resistance

Steel weakens at high temperature.

- **Critical Temperature:** about 550 °C
- **Fire Protection:** intumescent coatings and boards
- **Fire Resistance Rating:** minutes of resistance

## Unit 5: Towers & Bridges

### Transmission Towers

Lattice towers support lines.

- **Lattice Tower:** angle-section truss tower
- **Wind Load on Tower:** per IS 802
- **Broken Wire Condition:** design load case

### Chimneys & Tanks

Special steel structures.

- **Steel Chimney:** self-supporting stack
- **Elevated Water Tank:** steel tank on staging
- **Wind-induced Oscillation:** vortex shedding

### Steel Bridges

Bridges using plate girders and trusses.

- **Plate Girder Bridge:** for medium spans
- **Truss Bridge:** Pratt or Warren configuration
- **IRC Loading:** Class AA and 70R vehicle loads

### Fatigue & Inspection

Bridges face repeated loads.

- **Fatigue Life:** cycles before crack
- **S-N Curve:** stress range versus cycles
- **Inspection:** periodic checks for cracks and corrosion
