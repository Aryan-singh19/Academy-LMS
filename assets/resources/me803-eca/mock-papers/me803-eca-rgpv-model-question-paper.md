## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Primary Energy Consumption: total energy from all sources
- **Q1(b):** Energy Conservation Act 2001: law establishing BEE and efficiency programmes | BEE: Bureau of Energy Efficiency | Designated Consumer: large energy user required to appoint energy manager
- **Q2(a):** Benchmarking: comparing performance against standards
- **Q2(b):** Power Analyser: measures voltage, current, power factor | Flue Gas Analyser: measures O2, CO2 and CO | Ultrasonic Flow Meter: non-intrusive flow measurement
- **Q3(a):** Economiser: heats feed water using flue gas
- **Q3(b):** Cogeneration: simultaneous production of electricity and heat | Topping Cycle: power first, then heat | Bottoming Cycle: heat first, then power from waste heat
- **Q4(a):** Power Factor: real power / apparent power
- **Q4(b):** Energy-efficient Motor: higher efficiency class motors | Motor Loading: efficiency falls at low load | VFD: variable frequency drive adjusts motor speed
- **Q5(a):** ISO 50001: energy management system standard
- **Q5(b):** Simple Payback Period: investment / annual savings | NPV: present value of cash flows minus investment | IRR: discount rate giving NPV zero
- **Q6(a):** Demand-side Management: shaping consumer demand
- **Q6(b):** Primary Energy Consumption: total energy from all sources | Energy Intensity: energy used per unit GDP | Energy Security: reliable access to affordable energy
- **Q7(a):** Excess Air: air beyond stoichiometric; too much lowers efficiency
- **Q7(b):** Steam Trap: removes condensate without losing steam | Condensate Recovery: returning hot condensate to boiler | Insulation: reduces heat loss from pipes
- **Q8(a):** NPV: present value of cash flows minus investment
- **Q8(b):** Monitoring and Targeting: regular measurement against targets | CUSUM: cumulative sum chart tracking savings | Energy KPI: key performance indicator for energy
