# ME-803(B) Energy Conservation & Audit — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Energy scenario, audit methodology, energy efficiency in thermal and electrical systems, waste heat recovery and energy management.

## Unit 1: Energy Scenario & Conservation

### Energy Scenario

Energy use and supply shape policy.

- **Primary Energy Consumption:** total energy from all sources
- **Energy Intensity:** energy used per unit GDP
- **Energy Security:** reliable access to affordable energy

### Energy Conservation Act

Law promotes energy efficiency in India.

- **Energy Conservation Act 2001:** law establishing BEE and efficiency programmes
- **BEE:** Bureau of Energy Efficiency
- **Designated Consumer:** large energy user required to appoint energy manager

### Energy Pricing & Policy

Prices influence conservation.

- **Time-of-day Tariff:** different prices for peak and off-peak
- **PAT Scheme:** perform, achieve and trade energy saving certificates
- **Star Labelling:** BEE efficiency labels for appliances

### Principles of Conservation

Save energy by matching supply to demand.

- **Energy Conservation:** reducing energy use without reducing output
- **Energy Efficiency:** useful output per unit input
- **Demand-side Management:** shaping consumer demand

## Unit 2: Energy Audit

### Types of Energy Audit

Audits identify saving opportunities.

- **Preliminary Audit:** quick walk-through assessment
- **Detailed Audit:** comprehensive measurement and analysis
- **Energy Audit:** verification and analysis of energy use

### Audit Methodology

Audits follow a structured procedure.

- **Data Collection:** bills, logs and measurements
- **Benchmarking:** comparing performance against standards
- **Energy Saving Potential:** estimated reduction achievable

### Audit Instruments

Instruments measure energy flows.

- **Power Analyser:** measures voltage, current, power factor
- **Flue Gas Analyser:** measures O2, CO2 and CO
- **Ultrasonic Flow Meter:** non-intrusive flow measurement

### Energy Accounting

Sankey diagrams show where energy goes.

- **Sankey Diagram:** flow diagram showing energy distribution
- **Specific Energy Consumption:** energy per unit of production
- **Energy Balance:** input equals useful output plus losses

## Unit 3: Thermal Systems Efficiency

### Boilers & Furnaces

Combustion efficiency saves fuel.

- **Excess Air:** air beyond stoichiometric; too much lowers efficiency
- **Boiler Efficiency Direct Method:** output / input
- **Indirect Method:** 100 − sum of losses

### Steam Systems

Efficient steam distribution saves energy.

- **Steam Trap:** removes condensate without losing steam
- **Condensate Recovery:** returning hot condensate to boiler
- **Insulation:** reduces heat loss from pipes

### Waste Heat Recovery

Waste heat is reused.

- **Recuperator:** heat exchanger recovering heat from exhaust
- **Heat Pipe:** efficient passive heat transfer device
- **Economiser:** heats feed water using flue gas

### Cogeneration

Combined heat and power improves efficiency.

- **Cogeneration:** simultaneous production of electricity and heat
- **Topping Cycle:** power first, then heat
- **Bottoming Cycle:** heat first, then power from waste heat

## Unit 4: Electrical Systems Efficiency

### Electric Motors

Motors use most industrial electricity.

- **Energy-efficient Motor:** higher efficiency class motors
- **Motor Loading:** efficiency falls at low load
- **VFD:** variable frequency drive adjusts motor speed

### Pumps, Fans & Compressors

Flow control strongly affects energy.

- **Affinity Laws:** power ∝ speed³
- **Throttling Loss:** wasting energy by valve control
- **Compressed Air Leaks:** major source of waste

### Lighting Systems

Efficient lighting saves energy.

- **LED Lighting:** high efficacy light source
- **Luminous Efficacy:** lumens per watt
- **Occupancy Sensor:** switches lights based on presence

### Power Factor Improvement

Improved power factor reduces losses.

- **Power Factor:** real power / apparent power
- **Capacitor Bank:** improves lagging power factor
- **Maximum Demand Control:** reduces demand charges

## Unit 5: Energy Management & Economics

### Energy Management System

Organisations manage energy systematically.

- **Energy Management:** planning and operating to optimise energy use
- **ISO 50001:** energy management system standard
- **Energy Manager:** person responsible for energy performance

### Financial Analysis

Projects are evaluated economically.

- **Simple Payback Period:** investment / annual savings
- **NPV:** present value of cash flows minus investment
- **IRR:** discount rate giving NPV zero

### Monitoring & Targeting

Continuous tracking sustains savings.

- **Monitoring and Targeting:** regular measurement against targets
- **CUSUM:** cumulative sum chart tracking savings
- **Energy KPI:** key performance indicator for energy

### Green Buildings & Renewables

Buildings and renewables cut energy use.

- **Green Building:** designed for low environmental impact
- **ECBC:** energy conservation building code
- **Rooftop Solar:** on-site solar generation
