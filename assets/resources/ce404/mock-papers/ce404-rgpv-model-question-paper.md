## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Jayakar Committee: 1927 committee recommending road fund
- **Q1(b):** National Highways: connect major cities and ports | State Highways: connect district headquarters | Expressway: access-controlled high-speed road
- **Q2(a):** Overtaking Sight Distance: distance needed to pass safely
- **Q2(b):** Superelevation: e + f = V²/(127R) | Extra Widening: mechanical plus psychological widening | Transition Curve Length: based on rate of change of centrifugal acceleration
- **Q3(a):** Ductility Test: ability to stretch
- **Q3(b):** Marshall Mix Design: finds optimum bitumen content | Air Voids: voids in compacted mix | VG Grades: viscosity grading of bitumen, e.g. VG-30
- **Q4(a):** Rutting: longitudinal depressions in wheel paths
- **Q4(b):** Flexible Pavement: layered system transmitting load by grain contact | Rigid Pavement: concrete slab acting by flexure | Composite Pavement: combination of both
- **Q5(a):** Spot Speed: speed at a location
- **Q5(b):** Traffic Signal: timed control at intersections | Webster's Method: optimum signal cycle time | Rotary: roundabout intersection
- **Q6(a):** PPP: public-private partnership funding
- **Q6(b):** Jayakar Committee: 1927 committee recommending road fund | Nagpur Road Plan: first 20-year road plan 1943 | PMGSY: rural road programme
- **Q7(a):** CBR: California bearing ratio of soil
- **Q7(b):** Los Angeles Abrasion Test: measures hardness | Aggregate Impact Value: toughness against impact | Flakiness Index: percentage of flaky particles
- **Q8(a):** Webster's Method: optimum signal cycle time
- **Q8(b):** Gauge: distance between rails; broad gauge 1676 mm | Sleeper: supports rails and holds gauge | Ballast: crushed stone bed distributing load
