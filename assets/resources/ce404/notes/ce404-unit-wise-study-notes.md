# CE-404 Transportation Engineering-I — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Highway development, geometric design, materials, pavement design, construction, traffic engineering and railways basics.

## Unit 1: Highway Development & Planning

### History of Road Development

Roads evolved through planning efforts.

- **Jayakar Committee:** 1927 committee recommending road fund
- **Nagpur Road Plan:** first 20-year road plan 1943
- **PMGSY:** rural road programme

### Road Classification

Roads are classified by function.

- **National Highways:** connect major cities and ports
- **State Highways:** connect district headquarters
- **Expressway:** access-controlled high-speed road

### Highway Alignment

Alignment balances cost and safety.

- **Obligatory Points:** control points route must pass
- **Engineering Surveys:** map study, reconnaissance, preliminary and final location
- **Requirements of Alignment:** short, easy, safe and economical

### Highway Financing & Agencies

Agencies plan and fund roads.

- **NHAI:** National Highways Authority of India
- **IRC:** Indian Roads Congress setting standards
- **PPP:** public-private partnership funding

## Unit 2: Geometric Design

### Cross-section Elements

Road cross-section has several parts.

- **Camber:** transverse slope for drainage
- **Carriageway:** paved width for traffic
- **Shoulder:** strip beside carriageway

### Sight Distance

Drivers need adequate visibility.

- **Stopping Sight Distance:** lag distance + braking distance
- **Overtaking Sight Distance:** distance needed to pass safely
- **Perception-Reaction Time:** typically 2.5 s

### Horizontal Alignment

Curves require superelevation and widening.

- **Superelevation:** e + f = V²/(127R)
- **Extra Widening:** mechanical plus psychological widening
- **Transition Curve Length:** based on rate of change of centrifugal acceleration

### Vertical Alignment

Gradients and vertical curves.

- **Ruling Gradient:** design gradient for normal conditions
- **Summit Curve Length:** based on sight distance
- **Valley Curve Length:** based on headlight sight distance and comfort

## Unit 3: Highway Materials

### Soil Subgrade

Subgrade strength governs pavement design.

- **CBR:** California bearing ratio of soil
- **Subgrade:** prepared soil supporting pavement
- **Plate Load Test:** finds modulus of subgrade reaction

### Aggregates

Aggregates form pavement skeleton.

- **Los Angeles Abrasion Test:** measures hardness
- **Aggregate Impact Value:** toughness against impact
- **Flakiness Index:** percentage of flaky particles

### Bitumen

Bitumen binds flexible pavements.

- **Penetration Test:** hardness of bitumen
- **Softening Point:** ring and ball test
- **Ductility Test:** ability to stretch

### Bituminous Mixes

Mix design gives durable surfaces.

- **Marshall Mix Design:** finds optimum bitumen content
- **Air Voids:** voids in compacted mix
- **VG Grades:** viscosity grading of bitumen, e.g. VG-30

## Unit 4: Pavement Design

### Types of Pavements

Pavements are flexible or rigid.

- **Flexible Pavement:** layered system transmitting load by grain contact
- **Rigid Pavement:** concrete slab acting by flexure
- **Composite Pavement:** combination of both

### Flexible Pavement Design

IRC:37 uses traffic and CBR.

- **Design Traffic:** cumulative standard axles in msa
- **Standard Axle:** 80 kN single axle
- **Layer Thickness:** determined from design charts

### Rigid Pavement Design

IRC:58 uses Westergaard theory.

- **Westergaard Stress:** wheel load stresses at interior, edge and corner
- **Radius of Relative Stiffness:** l = (Eh³/(12(1 − μ²)k))^0.25
- **Dowel Bar:** transfers load across joints

### Pavement Failures

Distresses indicate failure modes.

- **Rutting:** longitudinal depressions in wheel paths
- **Fatigue Cracking:** alligator cracks from repeated loading
- **Pumping:** ejection of fines at rigid pavement joints

## Unit 5: Traffic Engineering & Railways

### Traffic Characteristics

Traffic studies measure flow.

- **Traffic Volume:** vehicles per unit time
- **Spot Speed:** speed at a location
- **PCU:** passenger car unit for mixed traffic

### Traffic Control

Signals and signs regulate flow.

- **Traffic Signal:** timed control at intersections
- **Webster's Method:** optimum signal cycle time
- **Rotary:** roundabout intersection

### Railway Track Components

Railways run on permanent way.

- **Gauge:** distance between rails; broad gauge 1676 mm
- **Sleeper:** supports rails and holds gauge
- **Ballast:** crushed stone bed distributing load

### Railway Geometric Design

Track geometry ensures safety.

- **Cant:** superelevation on railway curves
- **Cant Deficiency:** shortfall from equilibrium cant
- **Points and Crossings:** allow trains to change tracks
