## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Rail Section: flat-footed rail commonly used
- **Q1(b):** Sleeper Density: number of sleepers per rail length, e.g. M + 7 | Concrete Sleeper: durable prestressed sleeper | Ballast Cushion: depth of ballast below sleeper
- **Q2(a):** Marshalling Yard: sorting wagons into trains
- **Q2(b):** High-speed Rail: trains above 250 km/h | Metro Rail: urban rapid transit | Ballastless Track: slab track for high speeds
- **Q3(a):** Apron: parking area for aircraft
- **Q3(b):** Approach Lighting: guides landing aircraft | ILS: instrument landing system | Air Traffic Control: manages aircraft movement
- **Q4(a):** Ventilation: supplying fresh air
- **Q4(b):** Tunnel: underground passage | Tunnel Shape: circular, horseshoe or D-shaped | Tunnel Surveying: transferring alignment underground
- **Q5(a):** Artificial Harbour: protected by breakwaters
- **Q5(b):** Rubble Mound Breakwater: stone mound | Vertical Wall Breakwater: reflects waves | Wave Height: key design parameter
- **Q6(a):** Automatic Block System: track circuits control signals
- **Q6(b):** Rail Section: flat-footed rail commonly used | Creep of Rails: longitudinal movement of rails | Coning of Wheels: tapered wheels keep train centred
- **Q7(a):** Airport Site Selection: obstruction-free, accessible, suitable land
- **Q7(b):** Wind Rose Diagram: determines runway orientation | Basic Runway Length: corrected for elevation, temperature and gradient | Runway Configuration: single, parallel or intersecting
- **Q8(a):** Vertical Wall Breakwater: reflects waves
- **Q8(b):** Wet Dock: enclosed basin with gates | Dry Dock: for ship repair | Quay Wall: berthing structure along shore
