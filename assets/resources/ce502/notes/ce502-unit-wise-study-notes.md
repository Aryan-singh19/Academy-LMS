# CE-502 Transportation Engineering-II — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Railway engineering, airport engineering, tunnels, docks and harbours.

## Unit 1: Railway Engineering

### Permanent Way

The track system supports trains.

- **Rail Section:** flat-footed rail commonly used
- **Creep of Rails:** longitudinal movement of rails
- **Coning of Wheels:** tapered wheels keep train centred

### Sleepers & Ballast

Components distribute loads.

- **Sleeper Density:** number of sleepers per rail length, e.g. M + 7
- **Concrete Sleeper:** durable prestressed sleeper
- **Ballast Cushion:** depth of ballast below sleeper

### Railway Curves

Curves need cant and transition.

- **Equilibrium Cant:** e = GV²/(127R)
- **Negative Cant:** cant opposite on turnout branch
- **Transition Length:** based on cant gradient

### Signalling & Interlocking

Signals ensure safe train movement.

- **Semaphore Signal:** arm signal
- **Interlocking:** prevents conflicting signal settings
- **Automatic Block System:** track circuits control signals

## Unit 2: Points, Crossings & Stations

### Turnouts

Turnouts divert trains.

- **Turnout:** switch and crossing assembly
- **Tongue Rail:** movable rail of switch
- **Crossing Number:** ratio defining crossing angle

### Stations & Yards

Stations handle passengers and goods.

- **Junction Station:** meeting of lines
- **Marshalling Yard:** sorting wagons into trains
- **Platform:** raised area for boarding

### High-speed & Metro Rail

Modern rail systems.

- **High-speed Rail:** trains above 250 km/h
- **Metro Rail:** urban rapid transit
- **Ballastless Track:** slab track for high speeds

### Track Maintenance

Maintenance keeps track safe.

- **Track Geometry:** gauge, alignment, level
- **Tamping:** packing ballast under sleepers
- **Rail Welding:** long welded rails reduce joints

## Unit 3: Airport Engineering

### Airport Planning

Airports require careful site selection.

- **Airport Site Selection:** obstruction-free, accessible, suitable land
- **ICAO:** International Civil Aviation Organization
- **Airport Master Plan:** long-term development plan

### Runway Design

Runways are oriented with wind.

- **Wind Rose Diagram:** determines runway orientation
- **Basic Runway Length:** corrected for elevation, temperature and gradient
- **Runway Configuration:** single, parallel or intersecting

### Taxiways & Aprons

Aircraft move between runway and terminal.

- **Taxiway:** path connecting runway and apron
- **Exit Taxiway:** high-speed exit reducing runway occupancy
- **Apron:** parking area for aircraft

### Airport Lighting & Navigation

Visual and instrument aids guide aircraft.

- **Approach Lighting:** guides landing aircraft
- **ILS:** instrument landing system
- **Air Traffic Control:** manages aircraft movement

## Unit 4: Tunnel Engineering

### Tunnels & Classification

Tunnels pass through obstacles.

- **Tunnel:** underground passage
- **Tunnel Shape:** circular, horseshoe or D-shaped
- **Tunnel Surveying:** transferring alignment underground

### Tunnelling Methods in Soft Ground

Soft soils need support.

- **Cut and Cover:** excavation from surface
- **Shield Tunnelling:** protective shield advancing
- **NATM:** new Austrian tunnelling method

### Tunnelling in Rock

Rock tunnels use drilling and blasting.

- **Drill and Blast:** drilling holes and blasting
- **TBM:** tunnel boring machine
- **Full Face Method:** whole section excavated at once

### Tunnel Services

Tunnels need ventilation and drainage.

- **Ventilation:** supplying fresh air
- **Tunnel Lining:** supports and waterproofs
- **Dust Control:** water spraying and ventilation

## Unit 5: Docks & Harbours

### Harbours

Harbours provide shelter for ships.

- **Natural Harbour:** sheltered by land
- **Artificial Harbour:** protected by breakwaters
- **Port:** harbour with facilities for cargo

### Breakwaters

Breakwaters protect from waves.

- **Rubble Mound Breakwater:** stone mound
- **Vertical Wall Breakwater:** reflects waves
- **Wave Height:** key design parameter

### Docks & Quays

Docks handle loading and repairs.

- **Wet Dock:** enclosed basin with gates
- **Dry Dock:** for ship repair
- **Quay Wall:** berthing structure along shore

### Dredging & Navigation Aids

Channels must remain navigable.

- **Dredging:** removing sediment
- **Lighthouse:** navigation aid
- **Buoy:** floating marker for channels
