# BT-103 English for Communication — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Communication process, grammar, vocabulary, reading, writing, technical writing and presentation skills.

## Unit 1: Communication Fundamentals

### Process of Communication

Communication transfers meaning between people.

- **Communication Process:** sender, message, channel, receiver and feedback
- **Encoding:** converting ideas into a message
- **Feedback:** receiver's response completing the cycle

### Barriers to Communication

Barriers distort or block messages.

- **Semantic Barrier:** misunderstanding of words or meanings
- **Physical Barrier:** noise or distance
- **Psychological Barrier:** emotions and attitudes affecting understanding

### Verbal & Non-verbal Communication

Messages travel through words and body language.

- **Kinesics:** study of body movements and gestures
- **Proxemics:** use of personal space
- **Paralanguage:** tone, pitch and pace of voice

### Formal & Informal Channels

Organisations communicate through networks.

- **Upward Communication:** from subordinates to superiors
- **Grapevine:** informal communication network
- **Horizontal Communication:** between peers

## Unit 2: Grammar & Vocabulary

### Parts of Speech & Tenses

Grammar structures sentences correctly.

- **Present Perfect Tense:** action completed with present relevance
- **Subject-Verb Agreement:** verb agrees with subject in number
- **Article Usage:** a, an, the before nouns

### Voice & Narration

Sentences are transformed for emphasis.

- **Active Voice:** subject performs the action
- **Passive Voice:** subject receives the action
- **Indirect Speech:** reported speech with tense changes

### Vocabulary Building

Word knowledge improves expression.

- **Synonym:** word with similar meaning
- **Antonym:** word with opposite meaning
- **One-word Substitution:** single word replacing a phrase

### Common Errors

Learners avoid typical mistakes.

- **Dangling Modifier:** modifier not clearly attached to a word
- **Redundancy:** unnecessary repetition
- **Homophones:** words sounding alike with different meanings

## Unit 3: Reading & Listening

### Reading Comprehension

Reading extracts meaning efficiently.

- **Skimming:** reading quickly for the gist
- **Scanning:** searching for specific information
- **Inference:** conclusion drawn from implied information

### Note Making & Summarising

Notes condense key ideas.

- **Note Making:** recording main ideas in structured form
- **Summary:** brief restatement of main points
- **Precis Writing:** concise summary about one-third of original

### Listening Skills

Active listening improves understanding.

- **Active Listening:** fully concentrating and responding
- **Barriers to Listening:** distraction and prejudice
- **Empathetic Listening:** understanding the speaker's feelings

### Critical Reading

Critical readers evaluate texts.

- **Critical Reading:** analysing arguments and evidence
- **Bias:** one-sided presentation
- **Main Idea:** central point of a passage

## Unit 4: Writing Skills

### Paragraph & Essay Writing

Writing is organised into coherent units.

- **Topic Sentence:** states main idea of a paragraph
- **Coherence:** logical connection between sentences
- **Essay Structure:** introduction, body and conclusion

### Business Letters

Letters follow standard formats.

- **Block Format:** all lines start at left margin
- **Enquiry Letter:** asks for information
- **Complaint Letter:** reports a problem and seeks remedy

### Emails & Memos

Workplace messages are short and clear.

- **Email Etiquette:** clear subject, polite tone, concise body
- **Memo:** internal written communication
- **CC and BCC:** copy and blind copy recipients

### Job Applications & Resume

Applications present candidates professionally.

- **Resume:** summary of education, skills and experience
- **Cover Letter:** introduces the applicant
- **Objective Statement:** career goal in a resume

## Unit 5: Technical Writing & Presentation

### Technical Reports

Reports present technical information formally.

- **Technical Report:** structured document presenting findings
- **Abstract:** brief summary of report
- **Recommendations:** suggested actions based on findings

### Proposals & Minutes

Proposals request approval; minutes record meetings.

- **Proposal:** document seeking approval or funding
- **Minutes of Meeting:** official record of decisions
- **Agenda:** list of items for a meeting

### Presentation Skills

Effective presentations engage audiences.

- **Audience Analysis:** understanding listener needs
- **Visual Aids:** slides and charts supporting talk
- **Delivery:** voice, eye contact and body language

### Group Discussion & Interviews

Oral skills matter in placements.

- **Group Discussion:** evaluated discussion on a topic
- **Interview:** formal conversation assessing suitability
- **STAR Method:** situation, task, action, result answers
