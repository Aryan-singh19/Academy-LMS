## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Communication Process: sender, message, channel, receiver and feedback
- **Q1(b):** Semantic Barrier: misunderstanding of words or meanings | Physical Barrier: noise or distance | Psychological Barrier: emotions and attitudes affecting understanding
- **Q2(a):** Passive Voice: subject receives the action
- **Q2(b):** Synonym: word with similar meaning | Antonym: word with opposite meaning | One-word Substitution: single word replacing a phrase
- **Q3(a):** Empathetic Listening: understanding the speaker's feelings
- **Q3(b):** Critical Reading: analysing arguments and evidence | Bias: one-sided presentation | Main Idea: central point of a passage
- **Q4(a):** Resume: summary of education, skills and experience
- **Q4(b):** Topic Sentence: states main idea of a paragraph | Coherence: logical connection between sentences | Essay Structure: introduction, body and conclusion
- **Q5(a):** Abstract: brief summary of report
- **Q5(b):** Proposal: document seeking approval or funding | Minutes of Meeting: official record of decisions | Agenda: list of items for a meeting
- **Q6(a):** Horizontal Communication: between peers
- **Q6(b):** Communication Process: sender, message, channel, receiver and feedback | Encoding: converting ideas into a message | Feedback: receiver's response completing the cycle
- **Q7(a):** Skimming: reading quickly for the gist
- **Q7(b):** Note Making: recording main ideas in structured form | Summary: brief restatement of main points | Precis Writing: concise summary about one-third of original
- **Q8(a):** Minutes of Meeting: official record of decisions
- **Q8(b):** Audience Analysis: understanding listener needs | Visual Aids: slides and charts supporting talk | Delivery: voice, eye contact and body language
