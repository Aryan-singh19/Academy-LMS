## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Free Vibration: vibration after initial disturbance with no external force
- **Q1(b):** SHM: acceleration proportional to displacement and directed towards mean | Amplitude: maximum displacement | Time Period: time for one complete cycle
- **Q2(a):** Rayleigh's Method: max kinetic energy equals max potential energy
- **Q2(b):** Torsional Natural Frequency: ωn = √(kt/I) | Two-rotor System: node exists between rotors | Equivalent Shaft: stepped shaft reduced to uniform equivalent
- **Q3(a):** Half-power Bandwidth: measures damping near resonance
- **Q3(b):** Transmissibility: transmitted force / applied force | Isolation Condition: r > √2 for effective isolation | Isolator Materials: rubber pads, springs and cork
- **Q4(a):** Eigenvalue Problem: [K − ω²M]{X} = 0
- **Q4(b):** Principal Modes: natural modes of vibration | Mode Shape: relative amplitudes in a mode | Coupled Coordinates: coordinates linked through stiffness or mass
- **Q5(a):** Accelerometer: high natural frequency seismic instrument
- **Q5(b):** Condition Monitoring: tracking machine health from signals | FFT Analyser: displays vibration spectrum | Predictive Maintenance: maintenance based on measured condition
- **Q6(a):** Phase Angle: lead or lag between motions
- **Q6(b):** Free Vibration: vibration after initial disturbance with no external force | Forced Vibration: vibration under external periodic force | Degree of Freedom: number of independent coordinates to describe motion
- **Q7(a):** Damping Ratio: ζ = c/cc
- **Q7(b):** Magnification Factor: ratio of dynamic to static amplitude | Resonance: forcing frequency equals natural frequency | Frequency Ratio: r = ω/ωn
- **Q8(a):** FFT Analyser: displays vibration spectrum
- **Q8(b):** Sound Pressure Level: SPL = 20 log(p/p0) dB | Decibel Addition: levels add logarithmically | Frequency Weighting: A-weighting mimics human hearing
