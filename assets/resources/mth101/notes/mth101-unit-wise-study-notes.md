# MTH-101 Advanced Thermodynamics — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Exergy analysis, thermodynamic relations, real gases, mixtures, chemical equilibrium and irreversible thermodynamics.

## Unit 1: Exergy Analysis

### Exergy Concept

Exergy is the useful work potential.

- **Exergy:** maximum useful work relative to dead state
- **Dead State:** equilibrium with environment
- **Exergy Destruction:** T0 × entropy generation

### Exergy Balance

Balances locate losses.

- **Closed System Exergy Balance:** exergy in − out − destroyed = change
- **Flow Exergy:** (h − h0) − T0(s − s0) + V²/2 + gz
- **Second-law Efficiency:** exergy recovered / exergy supplied

### Exergy of Power Cycles

Second-law analysis of cycles.

- **Rankine Cycle Exergy:** losses mainly in boiler
- **Gas Turbine Exergy:** losses in combustor
- **Exergoeconomics:** cost of exergy losses

### Entropy Generation Minimisation

Design to reduce irreversibility.

- **Gouy-Stodola Theorem:** lost work = T0 Sgen
- **Entropy Generation Number:** dimensionless irreversibility
- **Optimum Design:** balances heat transfer and friction irreversibility

## Unit 2: Thermodynamic Relations

### Maxwell Relations

Relations among properties.

- **Maxwell Relation:** (∂T/∂v)s = −(∂p/∂s)v
- **Helmholtz Function:** a = u − Ts
- **Gibbs Function:** g = h − Ts

### General Property Relations

Derive unmeasured properties.

- **Clapeyron Equation:** dp/dT = hfg/(T vfg)
- **Joule-Thomson Coefficient:** μJT = (∂T/∂p)h
- **cp − cv Relation:** T v β²/κ

### Real Gases

Ideal gas law fails at high pressure.

- **Compressibility Factor:** Z = pv/(RT)
- **Van der Waals Equation:** (p + a/v²)(v − b) = RT
- **Law of Corresponding States:** Z depends on reduced properties

### Generalised Charts

Charts give real gas properties.

- **Reduced Pressure:** p/pc
- **Enthalpy Departure:** deviation from ideal gas
- **Fugacity:** effective pressure of real gas

## Unit 3: Mixtures & Psychrometry

### Ideal Gas Mixtures

Mixtures follow Dalton and Amagat laws.

- **Dalton's Law:** total pressure equals sum of partial pressures
- **Amagat's Law:** total volume equals sum of partial volumes
- **Mole Fraction:** moles of component / total

### Real Mixtures

Non-ideal mixtures need models.

- **Kay's Rule:** pseudo-critical properties of mixture
- **Partial Molar Property:** property per mole in mixture
- **Activity Coefficient:** deviation from ideal solution

### Psychrometry Advanced

Moist air thermodynamics.

- **Adiabatic Saturation:** defines thermodynamic wet bulb
- **Humidity Ratio:** mass of vapour per mass of dry air
- **Enthalpy of Moist Air:** h = 1.005T + ω(2501 + 1.88T)

### Phase Equilibrium

Phases in equilibrium.

- **Gibbs Phase Rule:** F = C − P + 2
- **Raoult's Law:** partial pressure = mole fraction × vapour pressure
- **Chemical Potential:** equal across phases at equilibrium

## Unit 4: Combustion & Chemical Equilibrium

### Combustion Thermodynamics

Energy balance for reactions.

- **Enthalpy of Formation:** enthalpy change forming compound from elements
- **Adiabatic Flame Temperature:** temperature with no heat loss
- **Heating Value:** heat released by complete combustion

### Chemical Equilibrium

Reactions reach equilibrium.

- **Equilibrium Constant:** Kp from Gibbs function change
- **Le Chatelier's Principle:** equilibrium shifts to oppose change
- **Dissociation:** breakdown of products at high temperature

### Second-law Analysis of Combustion

Combustion is highly irreversible.

- **Entropy of Reaction:** absolute entropy change
- **Exergy of Fuel:** chemical exergy
- **Combustion Irreversibility:** largest exergy loss in power plants

### Fuel Cells Thermodynamics

Direct conversion of chemical energy.

- **Reversible Cell Voltage:** −ΔG/(nF)
- **Fuel Cell Efficiency Limit:** ΔG/ΔH
- **Nernst Equation:** voltage variation with concentration

## Unit 5: Irreversible & Statistical Thermodynamics

### Irreversible Thermodynamics

Non-equilibrium processes.

- **Onsager Reciprocal Relations:** symmetric coupling coefficients
- **Thermoelectric Effects:** Seebeck, Peltier and Thomson
- **Entropy Production Rate:** sum of fluxes × forces

### Statistical Thermodynamics Basics

Microscopic origin of properties.

- **Boltzmann Entropy:** S = k ln W
- **Partition Function:** sum over states of e^(−E/kT)
- **Equipartition:** ½kT per quadratic degree of freedom

### Kinetic Theory

Molecular motion explains gas behaviour.

- **Mean Free Path:** average distance between collisions
- **Maxwell-Boltzmann Distribution:** molecular speed distribution
- **Transport Properties:** viscosity, conductivity from kinetic theory

### Advanced Cycles

Innovative power cycles.

- **Kalina Cycle:** ammonia-water mixture cycle
- **Organic Rankine Cycle:** organic fluid for low-grade heat
- **Supercritical CO2 Cycle:** high-efficiency compact cycle
