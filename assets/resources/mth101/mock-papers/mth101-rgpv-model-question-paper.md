- **Q3(b):** Gibbs Phase Rule: F = C − P + 2 | Raoult's Law: partial pressure = mole fraction × vapour pressure | Chemical Potential: equal across phases at equilibrium
- **Q4(a):** Reversible Cell Voltage: −ΔG/(nF)
- **Q4(b):** Enthalpy of Formation: enthalpy change forming compound from elements | Adiabatic Flame Temperature: temperature with no heat loss | Heating Value: heat released by complete combustion
- **Q5(a):** Thermoelectric Effects: Seebeck, Peltier and Thomson
- **Q5(b):** Boltzmann Entropy: S = k ln W | Partition Function: sum over states of e^(−E/kT) | Equipartition: ½kT per quadratic degree of freedom

- **Q7(b):** Kay's Rule: pseudo-critical properties of mixture | Partial Molar Property: property per mole in mixture | Activity Coefficient: deviation from ideal solution
