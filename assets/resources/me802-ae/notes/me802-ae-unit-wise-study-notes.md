# ME-802(A) Automobile Engineering — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Chassis and body, transmission, suspension, steering, brakes, electrical systems and emission control.

## Unit 1: Chassis & Body Engineering

### Chassis & Frame

The chassis supports all vehicle components.

- **Chassis:** frame with engine, transmission and running gear
- **Ladder Frame:** two side members joined by cross members
- **Monocoque:** body and frame integrated into one structure

### Vehicle Layouts

Engine and drive position vary.

- **Front Engine Rear Drive:** traditional layout
- **Front Engine Front Drive:** compact layout common in cars
- **Four-wheel Drive:** power to all four wheels

### Resistance to Motion

Vehicles overcome several resistances.

- **Rolling Resistance:** resistance from tyre deformation
- **Air Resistance:** Fa = ½ρCdAV²
- **Gradient Resistance:** W sin θ on slopes

### Tractive Effort

Tractive effort must exceed resistance.

- **Tractive Effort:** force at driving wheels
- **Power Required:** total resistance × speed
- **Gradeability:** maximum slope a vehicle can climb

## Unit 2: Transmission System

### Clutch

Clutch connects and disconnects engine.

- **Single Plate Clutch:** friction plate between flywheel and pressure plate
- **Diaphragm Spring Clutch:** uses conical diaphragm spring
- **Centrifugal Clutch:** engages automatically with engine speed

### Gearbox

Gearboxes vary torque and speed.

- **Sliding Mesh Gearbox:** gears slide into mesh
- **Constant Mesh Gearbox:** gears always in mesh, dog clutches engage
- **Synchromesh Gearbox:** synchronisers equalise speeds before engagement

### Automatic Transmission

Automatic boxes shift without driver.

- **Torque Converter:** fluid coupling with torque multiplication
- **Epicyclic Gearbox:** planetary gear sets
- **CVT:** continuously variable transmission

### Propeller Shaft & Differential

Power reaches wheels through driveline.

- **Propeller Shaft:** transmits power to rear axle
- **Universal Joint:** allows angular movement
- **Differential:** allows wheels to rotate at different speeds in turns

## Unit 3: Suspension & Steering

### Suspension Systems

Suspension isolates body from road shocks.

- **Leaf Spring Suspension:** used in heavy vehicles
- **Independent Suspension:** wheels move independently
- **McPherson Strut:** common front independent suspension

### Shock Absorbers

Dampers control oscillations.

- **Telescopic Shock Absorber:** hydraulic damper
- **Air Suspension:** air springs for adjustable height
- **Damping:** dissipating oscillation energy

### Steering Geometry

Geometry ensures stability and ease.

- **Ackermann Steering:** inner wheel turns more than outer
- **Camber:** tilt of wheel from vertical
- **Caster:** tilt of steering axis giving self-centring

### Steering Gears & Power Steering

Mechanisms convert steering wheel motion.

- **Rack and Pinion:** simple direct steering gear
- **Recirculating Ball:** used in heavy vehicles
- **Power Steering:** hydraulic or electric assistance

## Unit 4: Brakes, Wheels & Tyres

### Braking Systems

Brakes stop the vehicle.

- **Drum Brake:** shoes press against rotating drum
- **Disc Brake:** pads clamp a rotating disc
- **Hydraulic Brake:** Pascal's law transmits pedal force

### ABS & Advanced Braking

Electronics prevent wheel lock.

- **ABS:** anti-lock braking system
- **Brake Fade:** loss of braking due to heat
- **EBD:** electronic brake-force distribution

### Wheels & Tyres

Tyres provide grip and comfort.

- **Radial Tyre:** plies run radially; better grip and life
- **Tubeless Tyre:** no inner tube; slower deflation
- **Tyre Designation:** e.g. 195/65 R15 for width, aspect ratio, rim

### Wheel Alignment & Balancing

Alignment and balance improve handling.

- **Toe-in:** front of wheels closer than rear
- **Wheel Balancing:** removes vibration from uneven mass
- **Kingpin Inclination:** inclination of steering axis

## Unit 5: Electrical Systems & Emission Control

### Battery & Charging System

Electrical power supply in vehicles.

- **Lead Acid Battery:** 12 V battery in most cars
- **Alternator:** generates AC rectified to DC
- **Voltage Regulator:** maintains charging voltage

### Starting & Lighting Systems

Starting motor cranks the engine.

- **Starter Motor:** DC series motor with Bendix drive
- **Solenoid Switch:** engages starter
- **Headlamp:** main lighting with high and low beams

### Vehicle Electronics

Electronics control engine and safety.

- **ECU:** electronic control unit
- **CAN Bus:** controller area network for in-vehicle communication
- **Airbag:** inflatable restraint triggered by crash sensors

### Emission Control & Alternative Vehicles

Vehicles must meet emission norms.

- **Catalytic Converter:** reduces CO, HC and NOx
- **Electric Vehicle:** battery-powered vehicle with electric motor
- **Hybrid Vehicle:** combines IC engine and electric motor
