## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Chassis: frame with engine, transmission and running gear
- **Q1(b):** Front Engine Rear Drive: traditional layout | Front Engine Front Drive: compact layout common in cars | Four-wheel Drive: power to all four wheels
- **Q2(a):** Constant Mesh Gearbox: gears always in mesh, dog clutches engage
- **Q2(b):** Torque Converter: fluid coupling with torque multiplication | Epicyclic Gearbox: planetary gear sets | CVT: continuously variable transmission
- **Q3(a):** Caster: tilt of steering axis giving self-centring
- **Q3(b):** Rack and Pinion: simple direct steering gear | Recirculating Ball: used in heavy vehicles | Power Steering: hydraulic or electric assistance
- **Q4(a):** Toe-in: front of wheels closer than rear
- **Q4(b):** Drum Brake: shoes press against rotating drum | Disc Brake: pads clamp a rotating disc | Hydraulic Brake: Pascal's law transmits pedal force
- **Q5(a):** Alternator: generates AC rectified to DC
- **Q5(b):** Starter Motor: DC series motor with Bendix drive | Solenoid Switch: engages starter | Headlamp: main lighting with high and low beams
- **Q6(a):** Gradeability: maximum slope a vehicle can climb
- **Q6(b):** Chassis: frame with engine, transmission and running gear | Ladder Frame: two side members joined by cross members | Monocoque: body and frame integrated into one structure
- **Q7(a):** Leaf Spring Suspension: used in heavy vehicles
- **Q7(b):** Telescopic Shock Absorber: hydraulic damper | Air Suspension: air springs for adjustable height | Damping: dissipating oscillation energy
- **Q8(a):** Solenoid Switch: engages starter
- **Q8(b):** ECU: electronic control unit | CAN Bus: controller area network for in-vehicle communication | Airbag: inflatable restraint triggered by crash sensors
