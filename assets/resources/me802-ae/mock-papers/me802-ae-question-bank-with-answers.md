## Unit 2: Transmission System

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Single Plate Clutch*.  
  **Ans.** friction plate between flywheel and pressure plate
- **Q.** Define *Diaphragm Spring Clutch*.  
  **Ans.** uses conical diaphragm spring
- **Q.** Define *Centrifugal Clutch*.  
  **Ans.** engages automatically with engine speed
- **Q.** Define *Sliding Mesh Gearbox*.  
  **Ans.** gears slide into mesh
- **Q.** Define *Constant Mesh Gearbox*.  
  **Ans.** gears always in mesh, dog clutches engage
- **Q.** Define *Synchromesh Gearbox*.  
  **Ans.** synchronisers equalise speeds before engagement
- **Q.** Define *Torque Converter*.  
  **Ans.** fluid coupling with torque multiplication
- **Q.** Define *Epicyclic Gearbox*.  
  **Ans.** planetary gear sets
- **Q.** Define *CVT*.  
  **Ans.** continuously variable transmission
- **Q.** Define *Propeller Shaft*.  
  **Ans.** transmits power to rear axle
- **Q.** Define *Universal Joint*.  
  **Ans.** allows angular movement
- **Q.** Define *Differential*.  
  **Ans.** allows wheels to rotate at different speeds in turns

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Clutch* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Clutch connects and disconnects engine. Single Plate Clutch — friction plate between flywheel and pressure plate; Diaphragm Spring Clutch — uses conical diaphragm spring; Centrifugal Clutch — engages automatically with engine speed.
- **Q.** Explain *Gearbox* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Gearboxes vary torque and speed. Sliding Mesh Gearbox — gears slide into mesh; Constant Mesh Gearbox — gears always in mesh, dog clutches engage; Synchromesh Gearbox — synchronisers equalise speeds before engagement.
- **Q.** Explain *Automatic Transmission* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Automatic boxes shift without driver. Torque Converter — fluid coupling with torque multiplication; Epicyclic Gearbox — planetary gear sets; CVT — continuously variable transmission.
- **Q.** Explain *Propeller Shaft & Differential* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Power reaches wheels through driveline. Propeller Shaft — transmits power to rear axle; Universal Joint — allows angular movement; Differential — allows wheels to rotate at different speeds in turns.
