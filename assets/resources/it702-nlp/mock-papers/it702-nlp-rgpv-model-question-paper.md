## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** NLP: field enabling computers to process human language
- **Q1(b):** Tokenisation: splitting text into tokens such as words | Stemming: crude suffix stripping, e.g. Porter stemmer | Lemmatisation: reducing words to dictionary base forms
- **Q2(a):** Kneser-Ney Smoothing: uses continuation counts for lower-order models
- **Q2(b):** Perplexity: inverse probability of test data normalised by length | Held-out Set: data reserved for tuning and evaluation | Out-of-vocabulary Word: word not seen in training
- **Q3(a):** Dependency Parsing: finds head-dependent relations between words
- **Q3(b):** Named Entity Recognition: identifying names of people, places and organisations | BIO Tagging: begin, inside, outside labels for spans | Conditional Random Field: discriminative sequence labelling model
- **Q4(a):** Semantic Role Labelling: assigning roles like agent and patient
- **Q4(b):** Synonymy: different words with the same meaning | WordNet: lexical database of synsets and semantic relations | Word Sense Disambiguation: choosing the correct sense of a word in context
- **Q5(a):** Transformer: architecture of stacked attention and feed-forward layers
- **Q5(b):** BERT: bidirectional encoder pretrained with masked language modelling | GPT: autoregressive decoder pretrained to predict the next token | Fine-tuning: adapting a pretrained model to a specific task
- **Q6(a):** Finite-state Transducer: automaton mapping between surface and lexical forms
- **Q6(b):** NLP: field enabling computers to process human language | Ambiguity: one expression having several meanings | Corpus: large structured collection of texts
- **Q7(a):** POS Tag: label such as noun, verb or adjective
- **Q7(b):** Context-Free Grammar: rules rewriting a non-terminal into symbols | Parse Tree: tree showing how a sentence derives from the grammar | Treebank: corpus annotated with parse trees, e.g. Penn Treebank
- **Q8(a):** GPT: autoregressive decoder pretrained to predict the next token
- **Q8(b):** Seq2Seq Model: encoder-decoder mapping input to output sequences | BLEU Score: n-gram overlap metric for translation quality | Abstractive Summarisation: generating new sentences that summarise text
