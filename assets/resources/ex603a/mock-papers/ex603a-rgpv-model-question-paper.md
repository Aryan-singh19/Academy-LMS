- **Q1(a):** Resistance Heating: heat by I²R
- **Q1(b):** Heating Element: nichrome wire | Element Design: based on power and temperature | Temperature Control: thermostat or on-off switching
- **Q2(a):** Seam Welding: continuous series of spots
- **Q2(b):** DC Welding Generator: stable arc | AC Welding Transformer: economical | Electrode Coating: stabilises arc and shields weld
- **Q3(a):** Space-height Ratio: spacing of luminaires
- **Q3(b):** Street Lighting: uniform road illumination | Flood Lighting: illumination of large areas | Glare: discomfort from bright sources
- **Q4(a):** Traction Motor: DC series or three-phase induction
- **Q4(b):** 25 kV AC Traction: standard Indian Railways system | DC Traction: used in metros | Overhead Catenary: supplies traction power
- **Q5(a):** Electroplating: depositing metal coating
- **Q5(b):** Constant Current Charging: fixed current | Constant Voltage Charging: fixed voltage | Trickle Charging: maintains full charge
- **Q6(a):** Applications: plastics and wood
- **Q6(b):** Resistance Heating: heat by I²R | Induction Heating: eddy current heating | Arc Heating: heat from electric arc
- **Q7(a):** Luminous Flux: light energy in lumens
- **Q7(b):** Incandescent Lamp: heated filament | Fluorescent Lamp: gas discharge with phosphor | LED Lamp: solid-state lighting, high efficacy
- **Q8(a):** Constant Voltage Charging: fixed voltage
- **Q8(b):** IE3 Motor: premium efficiency class | VFD Savings: pump and fan energy reduction | Power Factor Improvement: capacitor banks
