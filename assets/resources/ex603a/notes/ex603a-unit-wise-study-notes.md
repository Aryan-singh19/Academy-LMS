# EX-603(A) Utilization of Electrical Energy — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Electric heating, welding, illumination, electric traction, electrolysis and energy-efficient utilisation.

## Unit 1: Electric Heating

### Methods of Heating

Electricity provides clean heat.

- **Resistance Heating:** heat by I²R
- **Induction Heating:** eddy current heating
- **Arc Heating:** heat from electric arc

### Resistance Ovens

Ovens use heating elements.

- **Heating Element:** nichrome wire
- **Element Design:** based on power and temperature
- **Temperature Control:** thermostat or on-off switching

### Induction Furnaces

Induction melts metals.

- **Core-type Induction Furnace:** transformer principle
- **Coreless Induction Furnace:** high-frequency field
- **Applications:** steel and non-ferrous melting

### Dielectric Heating

Insulators heated by RF fields.

- **Dielectric Loss:** P ∝ f V² tan δ
- **Microwave Heating:** cooking and drying
- **Applications:** plastics and wood

## Unit 2: Electric Welding

### Welding Fundamentals

Electric welding joins metals.

- **Arc Welding:** heat from arc between electrode and work
- **Resistance Welding:** heat from contact resistance
- **Welding Transformer:** low voltage high current

### Resistance Welding Types

Several resistance processes.

- **Spot Welding:** overlapping sheets
- **Seam Welding:** continuous series of spots
- **Butt Welding:** end-to-end joining

### Arc Welding Equipment

Equipment provides suitable arc.

- **DC Welding Generator:** stable arc
- **AC Welding Transformer:** economical
- **Electrode Coating:** stabilises arc and shields weld

### Modern Welding

Advanced methods.

- **TIG Welding:** tungsten inert gas
- **MIG Welding:** metal inert gas
- **Plasma Arc Welding:** constricted arc

## Unit 3: Illumination

### Laws of Illumination

Light distribution follows laws.

- **Luminous Flux:** light energy in lumens
- **Illuminance:** lux = lumen/m²
- **Inverse Square Law:** E = I/d²

### Lamps

Lamps convert electricity to light.

- **Incandescent Lamp:** heated filament
- **Fluorescent Lamp:** gas discharge with phosphor
- **LED Lamp:** solid-state lighting, high efficacy

### Lighting Design

Designs achieve required illuminance.

- **Utilisation Factor:** flux on working plane / total flux
- **Maintenance Factor:** accounts for dirt and ageing
- **Space-height Ratio:** spacing of luminaires

### Street & Flood Lighting

Outdoor lighting design.

- **Street Lighting:** uniform road illumination
- **Flood Lighting:** illumination of large areas
- **Glare:** discomfort from bright sources

## Unit 4: Electric Traction

### Traction Systems

Electric traction powers trains.

- **25 kV AC Traction:** standard Indian Railways system
- **DC Traction:** used in metros
- **Overhead Catenary:** supplies traction power

### Speed-Time Curves

Train motion is analysed.

- **Speed-Time Curve:** acceleration, free running, coasting, braking
- **Schedule Speed:** distance / total time including stops
- **Crest Speed:** maximum speed attained

### Tractive Effort

Force required for train movement.

- **Tractive Effort:** force to accelerate and overcome resistance
- **Specific Energy Consumption:** Wh per tonne-km
- **Adhesion:** friction between wheel and rail

### Traction Motors & Braking

Motors and braking for trains.

- **Traction Motor:** DC series or three-phase induction
- **Regenerative Braking:** returns energy to supply
- **Rheostatic Braking:** dissipates energy in resistors

## Unit 5: Electrolysis & Energy Efficiency

### Electrolytic Processes

Electrolysis serves industry.

- **Faraday's Laws:** mass deposited ∝ charge
- **Electroplating:** depositing metal coating
- **Electro-refining:** purifying metals

### Battery Charging

Charging of storage batteries.

- **Constant Current Charging:** fixed current
- **Constant Voltage Charging:** fixed voltage
- **Trickle Charging:** maintains full charge

### Energy-efficient Motors & Drives

Efficient utilisation saves energy.

- **IE3 Motor:** premium efficiency class
- **VFD Savings:** pump and fan energy reduction
- **Power Factor Improvement:** capacitor banks

### Domestic Utilisation

Household appliances.

- **Refrigerator:** vapour compression appliance
- **Air Conditioner:** cooling and dehumidifying
- **Star Rating:** BEE efficiency label
