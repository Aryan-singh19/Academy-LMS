## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Kinematic Link: rigid body connecting with others
- **Q1(b):** Grubler's Equation: F = 3(n − 1) − 2j − h | Mechanism: kinematic chain with one link fixed and F = 1 | Structure: F = 0, no relative motion
- **Q2(a):** Kennedy's Theorem: three ICs of three bodies lie on a straight line
- **Q2(b):** Centripetal Acceleration: ω²r towards the centre | Tangential Acceleration: αr | Coriolis Component: 2vω for sliding on a rotating link
- **Q3(a):** Involute Profile: most common tooth profile
- **Q3(b):** Interference: tip of one tooth digs into flank of another | Minimum Teeth: about 17 for 20° full-depth involute | Contact Ratio: arc of contact / circular pitch
- **Q4(a):** Friction Circle: circle of radius μr for journal bearings
- **Q4(b):** Simple Gear Train: each shaft has one gear | Compound Gear Train: intermediate shafts carry two gears | Train Value: output speed / input speed
- **Q5(a):** Watt Governor: simple pendulum governor
- **Q5(b):** Hartnell Governor: spring-loaded centrifugal governor | Sensitiveness: range of speed / mean speed | Isochronous Governor: same equilibrium speed at all radii
- **Q6(a):** Elliptical Trammel: double slider tracing an ellipse
- **Q6(b):** Kinematic Link: rigid body connecting with others | Lower Pair: surface contact, e.g. turning pair | Higher Pair: point or line contact, e.g. cam and follower
- **Q7(a):** Cam: rotating element imparting motion to follower
- **Q7(b):** Uniform Velocity: infinite acceleration at ends | SHM Follower Motion: smooth harmonic displacement | Cycloidal Motion: zero acceleration at start and end; best for high speed
- **Q8(a):** Sensitiveness: range of speed / mean speed
- **Q8(b):** Governor Effort: mean force on sleeve for a speed change | Governor Power: effort × sleeve lift | Hunting: continuous fluctuation of speed
