# MPS-202 Smart Grid Technologies — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Smart grid architecture, AMI, distribution automation, microgrids, communication, cyber security and EV integration.

## Unit 1: Smart Grid Fundamentals

### Smart Grid Concept

Modernising the grid with digital technology.

- **Smart Grid:** grid using sensing, communication and control
- **Self-healing:** automatic fault isolation and restoration
- **Prosumer:** consumer who also produces energy

### Smart Grid Architecture

Layers of a smart grid.

- **NIST Conceptual Model:** seven domains of smart grid
- **SGAM:** smart grid architecture model
- **Interoperability:** systems work together

### Smart Grid in India

National initiatives.

- **National Smart Grid Mission:** Indian smart grid programme
- **Smart Meter National Programme:** AMI rollout
- **RDSS:** revamped distribution sector scheme

### Drivers & Benefits

Why smart grids.

- **Reliability Improvement:** fewer outages
- **Renewable Integration:** accommodating variable sources
- **Loss Reduction:** lower technical and commercial losses

## Unit 2: Advanced Metering & Demand Response

### AMI

Two-way metering infrastructure.

- **Smart Meter:** digital meter with communication
- **AMI:** advanced metering infrastructure
- **MDMS:** meter data management system

### Demand Response

Consumers adjust use.

- **Demand Response:** changes in consumption for incentives
- **Time-of-Use Tariff:** price varies by time
- **Direct Load Control:** utility controls appliances

### Home Energy Management

Smart homes optimise energy.

- **HEMS:** home energy management system
- **Smart Appliance:** responds to signals
- **Load Scheduling:** shifting appliances to cheap hours

### Data Analytics

Using meter data.

- **Load Profiling:** consumer usage patterns
- **Theft Detection:** identifying abnormal consumption
- **Big Data in Utilities:** analytics on large datasets

## Unit 3: Distribution Automation & Microgrids

### Distribution Automation

Automating distribution networks.

- **FLISR:** fault location, isolation and service restoration
- **Recloser:** automatic reclosing breaker
- **Sectionaliser:** isolates faulted sections

### Distributed Energy Resources

Small generation at distribution level.

- **DER:** distributed energy resources
- **Hosting Capacity:** DER a feeder can accommodate
- **Reverse Power Flow:** from DER to substation

### Microgrids

Local grids that can island.

- **Microgrid:** local grid with sources and loads
- **Islanding:** operating disconnected from main grid
- **Microgrid Controller:** manages sources and loads

### Microgrid Control

Hierarchical control.

- **Droop Control:** decentralised power sharing
- **Secondary Control:** restores voltage and frequency
- **Energy Management:** optimal scheduling

## Unit 4: Communication & Cyber Security

### Communication Technologies

Networks for smart grid.

- **PLC:** power line communication
- **RF Mesh:** wireless mesh for meters
- **Cellular IoT:** NB-IoT and LTE-M

### Communication Standards

Protocols for grid devices.

- **IEC 61850:** substation automation standard
- **DNP3:** SCADA protocol
- **DLMS/COSEM:** metering protocol

### Cyber Security Threats

Grids are cyber targets.

- **Cyber Attack:** unauthorised access or disruption
- **False Data Injection:** manipulating measurements
- **Ransomware on Utilities:** disrupting operations

### Cyber Security Measures

Protecting the grid.

- **Defence in Depth:** layered security
- **IEC 62351:** security for power system protocols
- **Security Operations Centre:** monitoring threats

## Unit 5: EVs, Storage & Future Grids

### EV Integration

Electric vehicles as grid loads and resources.

- **Smart Charging:** controlled EV charging
- **V2G:** vehicle-to-grid power export
- **Charging Load Impact:** peak demand increase

### Energy Storage in Smart Grids

Storage provides flexibility.

- **Battery Energy Storage System:** grid-scale batteries
- **Peak Shaving:** reducing peak demand
- **Frequency Regulation:** fast storage response

### Virtual Power Plants

Aggregating DERs.

- **VPP:** aggregated DER acting as one plant
- **Aggregator:** entity managing many DERs
- **Peer-to-peer Energy Trading:** consumers trading directly

### Digital Twins & AI

Advanced analytics.

- **Digital Twin:** virtual replica of grid
- **AI for Grid Operation:** forecasting and optimisation
- **Blockchain in Energy:** transparent energy transactions
