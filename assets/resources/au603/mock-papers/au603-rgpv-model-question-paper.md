## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Rolling Resistance Coefficient: rolling resistance / normal load
- **Q1(b):** Slip Angle: angle between wheel heading and travel direction | Cornering Stiffness: lateral force per degree of slip angle | Self-aligning Torque: moment returning wheel to straight
- **Q2(a):** Brake Force Distribution: ideal front-rear proportion
- **Q2(b):** Drag Coefficient: Cd, dimensionless drag measure | Frontal Area: projected front area | Downforce: negative lift increasing grip
- **Q3(a):** Neutral Steer: K = 0
- **Q3(b):** Lateral Load Transfer: W·h·ay/(g·t) | Static Stability Factor: t/(2h) | Roll Centre: point about which body rolls
- **Q4(a):** Damping Ratio for Ride: about 0.2-0.4
- **Q4(b):** Ride Frequency: body natural frequency, about 1-1.5 Hz | Road Roughness: random road profile input | Human Sensitivity: 4-8 Hz vertical vibration most uncomfortable
- **Q5(a):** Yaw Gain: yaw rate per steer angle
- **Q5(b):** ESC: brakes individual wheels to correct yaw | Traction Control: limits wheel spin | Rollover Mitigation: reduces risk of rollover
- **Q6(a):** Aquaplaning: tyre rides on water film
- **Q6(b):** Rolling Resistance Coefficient: rolling resistance / normal load | Slip Ratio: (wheel speed − vehicle speed) / vehicle speed | Contact Patch: tyre area touching the road
- **Q7(a):** Lateral Acceleration: V²/R
- **Q7(b):** Understeer: vehicle turns less than steering demands | Oversteer: vehicle turns more than demanded | Understeer Gradient: K, positive for understeer
- **Q8(a):** Traction Control: limits wheel spin
- **Q8(b):** Active Steering: varies steering ratio | Torque Vectoring: distributes torque between wheels | Active Anti-roll: actuators reduce roll
