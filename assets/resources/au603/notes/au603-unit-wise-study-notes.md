# AU-603 Vehicle Dynamics — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Tyre mechanics, longitudinal, lateral and vertical dynamics, handling, ride and stability.

## Unit 1: Tyre Mechanics

### Tyre Forces

Tyres generate all road forces.

- **Rolling Resistance Coefficient:** rolling resistance / normal load
- **Slip Ratio:** (wheel speed − vehicle speed) / vehicle speed
- **Contact Patch:** tyre area touching the road

### Cornering Force & Slip Angle

Lateral force comes from slip angle.

- **Slip Angle:** angle between wheel heading and travel direction
- **Cornering Stiffness:** lateral force per degree of slip angle
- **Self-aligning Torque:** moment returning wheel to straight

### Tyre Models

Models predict tyre behaviour.

- **Magic Formula:** Pacejka empirical tyre model
- **Friction Circle:** combined longitudinal and lateral limit
- **Camber Thrust:** lateral force from camber

### Tyre Characteristics

Construction affects behaviour.

- **Radial Tyre Behaviour:** lower rolling resistance
- **Inflation Pressure Effect:** changes stiffness and contact patch
- **Aquaplaning:** tyre rides on water film

## Unit 2: Longitudinal Dynamics

### Tractive Performance

Acceleration depends on tractive force.

- **Tractive Force:** engine torque × gear ratio × efficiency / wheel radius
- **Traction Limit:** μ × normal load on driven wheels
- **Power-limited Acceleration:** governed by engine power

### Braking Dynamics

Braking involves load transfer.

- **Load Transfer in Braking:** W·h·a/(g·L)
- **Brake Force Distribution:** ideal front-rear proportion
- **Wheel Lock:** loss of steering when front wheels lock

### Aerodynamic Resistance

Drag dominates at high speed.

- **Drag Coefficient:** Cd, dimensionless drag measure
- **Frontal Area:** projected front area
- **Downforce:** negative lift increasing grip

### Fuel Economy & Performance

Resistances determine efficiency.

- **Road Load:** sum of rolling, aero and grade resistance
- **Acceleration Time:** 0-100 km/h time
- **Top Speed:** when tractive force equals resistance

## Unit 3: Lateral Dynamics & Handling

### Steady-state Cornering

Vehicles corner with lateral acceleration.

- **Lateral Acceleration:** V²/R
- **Ackermann Angle:** L/R steering angle at low speed
- **Bicycle Model:** two-wheel model for lateral dynamics

### Understeer & Oversteer

Handling balance depends on tyres and weight.

- **Understeer:** vehicle turns less than steering demands
- **Oversteer:** vehicle turns more than demanded
- **Understeer Gradient:** K, positive for understeer

### Critical & Characteristic Speed

Speeds define stability limits.

- **Critical Speed:** oversteer vehicle becomes unstable
- **Characteristic Speed:** understeer vehicle needs double Ackermann steer
- **Neutral Steer:** K = 0

### Rollover & Load Transfer

Lateral load transfer can cause rollover.

- **Lateral Load Transfer:** W·h·ay/(g·t)
- **Static Stability Factor:** t/(2h)
- **Roll Centre:** point about which body rolls

## Unit 4: Vertical Dynamics & Ride

### Ride Comfort

Ride depends on suspension and road.

- **Ride Frequency:** body natural frequency, about 1-1.5 Hz
- **Road Roughness:** random road profile input
- **Human Sensitivity:** 4-8 Hz vertical vibration most uncomfortable

### Quarter Car Model

Simplified model of one corner.

- **Quarter Car Model:** sprung and unsprung masses with spring and tyre
- **Wheel Hop:** unsprung mass resonance around 10 Hz
- **Transmissibility:** body motion / road input

### Pitch & Bounce

Body motions couple.

- **Bounce:** vertical body motion
- **Pitch:** rotation about lateral axis
- **Olley's Criteria:** front ride frequency lower than rear

### Suspension Tuning

Damping trades comfort and control.

- **Damping Ratio for Ride:** about 0.2-0.4
- **Suspension Travel:** available wheel movement
- **Road Holding:** tyre-road contact force variation

## Unit 5: Stability & Control Systems

### Directional Stability

Vehicles must remain controllable.

- **Yaw Rate:** rotation about vertical axis
- **Yaw Gain:** yaw rate per steer angle
- **Sideslip Angle:** vehicle body slip angle

### Electronic Stability Control

ESC corrects skids by braking.

- **ESC:** brakes individual wheels to correct yaw
- **Traction Control:** limits wheel spin
- **Rollover Mitigation:** reduces risk of rollover

### Active Chassis Systems

Active systems improve dynamics.

- **Active Steering:** varies steering ratio
- **Torque Vectoring:** distributes torque between wheels
- **Active Anti-roll:** actuators reduce roll

### Vehicle Testing

Tests evaluate dynamics.

- **Steady-state Circular Test:** measures understeer gradient
- **Lane Change Test:** ISO double lane change
- **Skid Pad:** circular track for lateral grip
