# ME-305 Manufacturing Process — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Casting, forming, welding, powder metallurgy and plastic processing.

## Unit 1: Casting Processes

### Sand Casting & Patterns

Molten metal is poured into a mould cavity.

- **Pattern:** replica of the casting used to make the mould
- **Pattern Allowance:** extra size for shrinkage, machining and draft
- **Core:** insert forming internal cavities

### Moulding Sand & Gating System

Sand properties and gating control quality.

- **Permeability:** ability of sand to let gases escape
- **Riser:** reservoir feeding molten metal to compensate shrinkage
- **Sprue:** vertical channel through which metal enters

### Special Casting Processes

Special methods give better finish and accuracy.

- **Investment Casting:** wax pattern coated in ceramic, lost wax process
- **Die Casting:** metal forced into metal dies under pressure
- **Centrifugal Casting:** mould rotated to distribute metal, e.g. pipes

### Casting Defects

Defects arise from gas, shrinkage and mould issues.

- **Blow Hole:** cavity caused by trapped gas
- **Cold Shut:** incomplete fusion of two metal streams
- **Hot Tear:** crack from restrained shrinkage

## Unit 2: Metal Forming

### Hot & Cold Working

Forming temperature affects properties.

- **Hot Working:** above recrystallisation temperature; no strain hardening
- **Cold Working:** below recrystallisation; improves strength and finish
- **Recrystallisation Temperature:** about 0.4 of melting point (K)

### Rolling

Rolling reduces thickness between rolls.

- **Rolling:** compressing metal between rotating rolls
- **Angle of Bite:** maximum angle for metal to enter rolls
- **Neutral Point:** where roll and metal velocities are equal

### Forging

Forging shapes metal by compressive forces.

- **Open Die Forging:** metal deformed between flat dies
- **Closed Die Forging:** metal fills a shaped die cavity
- **Upsetting:** increasing cross-section by reducing length

### Extrusion & Drawing

Metal is pushed or pulled through dies.

- **Direct Extrusion:** ram and product move in the same direction
- **Indirect Extrusion:** die moves towards the billet
- **Wire Drawing:** pulling wire through a die to reduce diameter

## Unit 3: Sheet Metal Operations

### Shearing Operations

Shearing cuts sheet metal.

- **Blanking:** punched piece is the product
- **Punching:** punched piece is scrap; hole is the product
- **Clearance:** gap between punch and die

### Bending & Deep Drawing

Sheets are formed into shapes.

- **Spring Back:** elastic recovery after bending
- **Deep Drawing:** forming a cup from a blank
- **Limiting Draw Ratio:** maximum blank to punch diameter ratio

### Press Tools & Presses

Presses drive dies.

- **Progressive Die:** several operations at successive stations
- **Compound Die:** several operations in one stroke at one station
- **Stripper Plate:** removes sheet from the punch

### High Energy Rate Forming

Rapid energy release forms difficult parts.

- **Explosive Forming:** explosive energy forms sheet into a die
- **Electromagnetic Forming:** magnetic pulse forms conductive parts
- **Electrohydraulic Forming:** spark discharge in liquid creates pressure

## Unit 4: Welding & Joining

### Arc Welding

Electric arc melts base and filler metals.

- **SMAW:** shielded metal arc welding with flux-coated electrode
- **Arc Length:** distance between electrode and workpiece
- **Polarity:** DCEN gives deeper penetration

### Gas Welding & Cutting

Oxy-acetylene flame welds and cuts.

- **Neutral Flame:** equal oxygen and acetylene
- **Oxidising Flame:** excess oxygen, used for brass
- **Oxy-fuel Cutting:** oxidises steel in a jet of oxygen

### TIG, MIG & Resistance Welding

Advanced processes improve quality and speed.

- **TIG:** non-consumable tungsten electrode with inert gas
- **MIG:** consumable wire electrode with inert gas shielding
- **Spot Welding:** resistance welding of overlapping sheets

### Welding Defects & Soldering

Defects and lower-temperature joining.

- **Porosity:** gas pockets in weld metal
- **Heat Affected Zone:** base metal altered by welding heat
- **Brazing:** joining with filler above 450 °C without melting base metal

## Unit 5: Powder Metallurgy & Plastics

### Powder Production & Compaction

Metal powders are made and pressed.

- **Atomisation:** molten metal broken into droplets
- **Compaction:** pressing powder into a green compact
- **Green Strength:** strength of compact before sintering

### Sintering & Applications

Heating bonds powder particles.

- **Sintering:** heating below melting point to bond particles
- **Self-lubricating Bearing:** porous sintered bearing filled with oil
- **Cemented Carbide:** tungsten carbide bonded with cobalt

### Plastic Moulding Processes

Polymers are shaped by moulding.

- **Injection Moulding:** molten plastic injected into a mould
- **Blow Moulding:** air inflates a parison into a hollow shape
- **Compression Moulding:** charge pressed in a heated mould

### Extrusion & Thermoforming of Plastics

Continuous and sheet forming of plastics.

- **Plastic Extrusion:** continuous profiles through a die
- **Thermoforming:** heated sheet formed over a mould
- **Calendering:** rolling plastic into sheets
