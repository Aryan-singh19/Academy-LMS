## Unit 1: Casting Processes

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Pattern*.  
  **Ans.** replica of the casting used to make the mould
- **Q.** Define *Pattern Allowance*.  
  **Ans.** extra size for shrinkage, machining and draft
- **Q.** Define *Core*.  
  **Ans.** insert forming internal cavities
- **Q.** Define *Permeability*.  
  **Ans.** ability of sand to let gases escape
- **Q.** Define *Riser*.  
  **Ans.** reservoir feeding molten metal to compensate shrinkage
- **Q.** Define *Sprue*.  
  **Ans.** vertical channel through which metal enters
- **Q.** Define *Investment Casting*.  
  **Ans.** wax pattern coated in ceramic, lost wax process
- **Q.** Define *Die Casting*.  
  **Ans.** metal forced into metal dies under pressure
- **Q.** Define *Centrifugal Casting*.  
  **Ans.** mould rotated to distribute metal, e.g. pipes
- **Q.** Define *Blow Hole*.  
  **Ans.** cavity caused by trapped gas
- **Q.** Define *Cold Shut*.  
  **Ans.** incomplete fusion of two metal streams
- **Q.** Define *Hot Tear*.  
  **Ans.** crack from restrained shrinkage

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Sand Casting & Patterns* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Molten metal is poured into a mould cavity. Pattern — replica of the casting used to make the mould; Pattern Allowance — extra size for shrinkage, machining and draft; Core — insert forming internal cavities.
- **Q.** Explain *Moulding Sand & Gating System* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Sand properties and gating control quality. Permeability — ability of sand to let gases escape; Riser — reservoir feeding molten metal to compensate shrinkage; Sprue — vertical channel through which metal enters.
- **Q.** Explain *Special Casting Processes* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Special methods give better finish and accuracy. Investment Casting — wax pattern coated in ceramic, lost wax process; Die Casting — metal forced into metal dies under pressure; Centrifugal Casting — mould rotated to distribute metal, e.g. pipes.
- **Q.** Explain *Casting Defects* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Defects arise from gas, shrinkage and mould issues. Blow Hole — cavity caused by trapped gas; Cold Shut — incomplete fusion of two metal streams; Hot Tear — crack from restrained shrinkage.

## Unit 2: Metal Forming

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Hot Working*.  
  **Ans.** above recrystallisation temperature; no strain hardening
- **Q.** Define *Cold Working*.  
  **Ans.** below recrystallisation; improves strength and finish
- **Q.** Define *Recrystallisation Temperature*.  
  **Ans.** about 0.4 of melting point (K)
- **Q.** Define *Rolling*.  
  **Ans.** compressing metal between rotating rolls
- **Q.** Define *Angle of Bite*.  
  **Ans.** maximum angle for metal to enter rolls
- **Q.** Define *Neutral Point*.  
  **Ans.** where roll and metal velocities are equal
- **Q.** Define *Open Die Forging*.  
  **Ans.** metal deformed between flat dies
- **Q.** Define *Closed Die Forging*.  
  **Ans.** metal fills a shaped die cavity
- **Q.** Define *Upsetting*.  
  **Ans.** increasing cross-section by reducing length
- **Q.** Define *Direct Extrusion*.  
  **Ans.** ram and product move in the same direction
- **Q.** Define *Indirect Extrusion*.  
  **Ans.** die moves towards the billet
- **Q.** Define *Wire Drawing*.  
  **Ans.** pulling wire through a die to reduce diameter

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Hot & Cold Working* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Forming temperature affects properties. Hot Working — above recrystallisation temperature; no strain hardening; Cold Working — below recrystallisation; improves strength and finish; Recrystallisation Temperature — about 0.4 of melting point (K).
- **Q.** Explain *Rolling* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Rolling reduces thickness between rolls. Rolling — compressing metal between rotating rolls; Angle of Bite — maximum angle for metal to enter rolls; Neutral Point — where roll and metal velocities are equal.
- **Q.** Explain *Forging* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Forging shapes metal by compressive forces. Open Die Forging — metal deformed between flat dies; Closed Die Forging — metal fills a shaped die cavity; Upsetting — increasing cross-section by reducing length.
- **Q.** Explain *Extrusion & Drawing* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Metal is pushed or pulled through dies. Direct Extrusion — ram and product move in the same direction; Indirect Extrusion — die moves towards the billet; Wire Drawing — pulling wire through a die to reduce diameter.

## Unit 4: Welding & Joining

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *SMAW*.  
  **Ans.** shielded metal arc welding with flux-coated electrode
- **Q.** Define *Arc Length*.  
  **Ans.** distance between electrode and workpiece
- **Q.** Define *Polarity*.  
  **Ans.** DCEN gives deeper penetration
- **Q.** Define *Neutral Flame*.  
  **Ans.** equal oxygen and acetylene
- **Q.** Define *Oxidising Flame*.  
  **Ans.** excess oxygen, used for brass
- **Q.** Define *Oxy-fuel Cutting*.  
  **Ans.** oxidises steel in a jet of oxygen
- **Q.** Define *TIG*.  
  **Ans.** non-consumable tungsten electrode with inert gas
- **Q.** Define *MIG*.  
  **Ans.** consumable wire electrode with inert gas shielding
- **Q.** Define *Spot Welding*.  
  **Ans.** resistance welding of overlapping sheets
- **Q.** Define *Porosity*.  
  **Ans.** gas pockets in weld metal
- **Q.** Define *Heat Affected Zone*.  
  **Ans.** base metal altered by welding heat
- **Q.** Define *Brazing*.  
  **Ans.** joining with filler above 450 °C without melting base metal

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Arc Welding* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Electric arc melts base and filler metals. SMAW — shielded metal arc welding with flux-coated electrode; Arc Length — distance between electrode and workpiece; Polarity — DCEN gives deeper penetration.
- **Q.** Explain *Gas Welding & Cutting* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Oxy-acetylene flame welds and cuts. Neutral Flame — equal oxygen and acetylene; Oxidising Flame — excess oxygen, used for brass; Oxy-fuel Cutting — oxidises steel in a jet of oxygen.
- **Q.** Explain *TIG, MIG & Resistance Welding* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Advanced processes improve quality and speed. TIG — non-consumable tungsten electrode with inert gas; MIG — consumable wire electrode with inert gas shielding; Spot Welding — resistance welding of overlapping sheets.
- **Q.** Explain *Welding Defects & Soldering* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Defects and lower-temperature joining. Porosity — gas pockets in weld metal; Heat Affected Zone — base metal altered by welding heat; Brazing — joining with filler above 450 °C without melting base metal.
