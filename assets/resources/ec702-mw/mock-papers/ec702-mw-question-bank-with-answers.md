# EC-702(A) Microwave Engineering — Question Bank with Answer Hints

## Unit 1: Waveguides

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *TE Mode*.  
  **Ans.** transverse electric, no E field along propagation
- **Q.** Define *Dominant Mode*.  
  **Ans.** TE10 in rectangular waveguide
- **Q.** Define *Cut-off Wavelength*.  
  **Ans.** λc = 2a for TE10
- **Q.** Define *Guide Wavelength*.  
  **Ans.** λg = λ0 / √(1 − (λ0/λc)²)
- **Q.** Define *Phase Velocity*.  
  **Ans.** greater than c in a waveguide
- **Q.** Define *Group Velocity*.  
  **Ans.** less than c; vp·vg = c²
- **Q.** Define *Circular Waveguide Dominant Mode*.  
  **Ans.** TE11
- **Q.** Define *Cavity Resonator*.  
  **Ans.** closed waveguide section resonating at specific frequencies
- **Q.** Define *Q of Cavity*.  
  **Ans.** very high, used in filters and oscillators
- **Q.** Define *Microstrip*.  
  **Ans.** strip conductor over ground plane on dielectric
- **Q.** Define *Stripline*.  
  **Ans.** conductor between two ground planes
- **Q.** Define *Effective Permittivity*.  
  **Ans.** accounts for fields in air and dielectric

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Rectangular Waveguides* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Waveguides carry microwaves with low loss. TE Mode — transverse electric, no E field along propagation; Dominant Mode — TE10 in rectangular waveguide; Cut-off Wavelength — λc = 2a for TE10.
- **Q.** Explain *Waveguide Parameters* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Propagation depends on frequency above cut-off. Guide Wavelength — λg = λ0 / √(1 − (λ0/λc)²); Phase Velocity — greater than c in a waveguide; Group Velocity — less than c; vp·vg = c².
- **Q.** Explain *Circular Waveguides & Cavities* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Other guides and resonators. Circular Waveguide Dominant Mode — TE11; Cavity Resonator — closed waveguide section resonating at specific frequencies; Q of Cavity — very high, used in filters and oscillators.
- **Q.** Explain *Planar Transmission Lines* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Printed lines suit integrated circuits. Microstrip — strip conductor over ground plane on dielectric; Stripline — conductor between two ground planes; Effective Permittivity — accounts for fields in air and dielectric.

## Unit 2: Microwave Components & S-parameters

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *S-parameter*.  
  **Ans.** ratio of reflected or transmitted wave to incident wave
- **Q.** Define *S11*.  
  **Ans.** input reflection coefficient
- **Q.** Define *Lossless Network*.  
  **Ans.** S matrix is unitary
- **Q.** Define *E-plane Tee*.  
  **Ans.** series tee; outputs out of phase
- **Q.** Define *H-plane Tee*.  
  **Ans.** shunt tee; outputs in phase
- **Q.** Define *Magic Tee*.  
  **Ans.** hybrid with isolated E and H arms
- **Q.** Define *Coupling Factor*.  
  **Ans.** C = 10 log(P1/P3)
- **Q.** Define *Directivity*.  
  **Ans.** 10 log(P3/P4)
- **Q.** Define *Isolation*.  
  **Ans.** coupling plus directivity
- **Q.** Define *Isolator*.  
  **Ans.** passes power one way, absorbs reverse
- **Q.** Define *Circulator*.  
  **Ans.** routes power port 1 to 2, 2 to 3, 3 to 1
- **Q.** Define *Faraday Rotation*.  
  **Ans.** rotation of polarisation in magnetised ferrite

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Scattering Parameters* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** S-parameters describe microwave networks. S-parameter — ratio of reflected or transmitted wave to incident wave; S11 — input reflection coefficient; Lossless Network — S matrix is unitary.
- **Q.** Explain *Tee Junctions* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Tees split or combine power. E-plane Tee — series tee; outputs out of phase; H-plane Tee — shunt tee; outputs in phase; Magic Tee — hybrid with isolated E and H arms.
- **Q.** Explain *Directional Couplers* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Couplers sample power in one direction. Coupling Factor — C = 10 log(P1/P3); Directivity — 10 log(P3/P4); Isolation — coupling plus directivity.
- **Q.** Explain *Ferrite Devices* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Ferrites provide non-reciprocal behaviour. Isolator — passes power one way, absorbs reverse; Circulator — routes power port 1 to 2, 2 to 3, 3 to 1; Faraday Rotation — rotation of polarisation in magnetised ferrite.

## Unit 3: Microwave Tubes

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Transit Time Effect*.  
  **Ans.** electron transit comparable to RF period
- **Q.** Define *Lead Inductance*.  
  **Ans.** limits high-frequency operation
- **Q.** Define *Interelectrode Capacitance*.  
  **Ans.** shunts signal at high frequency
- **Q.** Define *Two-cavity Klystron*.  
  **Ans.** amplifier using buncher and catcher cavities
- **Q.** Define *Reflex Klystron*.  
  **Ans.** single-cavity oscillator with repeller
- **Q.** Define *Velocity Modulation*.  
  **Ans.** varying electron speed to form bunches
- **Q.** Define *Cavity Magnetron*.  
  **Ans.** crossed E and B fields generate microwaves
- **Q.** Define *π-mode*.  
  **Ans.** adjacent cavities 180° out of phase
- **Q.** Define *Application*.  
  **Ans.** radar and microwave ovens
- **Q.** Define *TWT*.  
  **Ans.** electron beam interacts with slowed wave on a helix
- **Q.** Define *Slow-wave Structure*.  
  **Ans.** helix reduces wave velocity to match beam
- **Q.** Define *Broadband Gain*.  
  **Ans.** TWT amplifies over an octave

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Limitations of Conventional Tubes* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Ordinary tubes fail at microwave frequencies. Transit Time Effect — electron transit comparable to RF period; Lead Inductance — limits high-frequency operation; Interelectrode Capacitance — shunts signal at high frequency.
- **Q.** Explain *Klystrons* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Velocity modulation and bunching amplify microwaves. Two-cavity Klystron — amplifier using buncher and catcher cavities; Reflex Klystron — single-cavity oscillator with repeller; Velocity Modulation — varying electron speed to form bunches.
- **Q.** Explain *Magnetron* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Crossed-field tube for high-power oscillation. Cavity Magnetron — crossed E and B fields generate microwaves; π-mode — adjacent cavities 180° out of phase; Application — radar and microwave ovens.
- **Q.** Explain *Travelling Wave Tube* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** TWT gives broadband amplification. TWT — electron beam interacts with slowed wave on a helix; Slow-wave Structure — helix reduces wave velocity to match beam; Broadband Gain — TWT amplifies over an octave.
