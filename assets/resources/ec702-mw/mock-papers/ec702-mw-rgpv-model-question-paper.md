## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** TE Mode: transverse electric, no E field along propagation
- **Q1(b):** Guide Wavelength: λg = λ0 / √(1 − (λ0/λc)²) | Phase Velocity: greater than c in a waveguide | Group Velocity: less than c; vp·vg = c²
- **Q2(a):** H-plane Tee: shunt tee; outputs in phase
- **Q2(b):** Coupling Factor: C = 10 log(P1/P3) | Directivity: 10 log(P3/P4) | Isolation: coupling plus directivity
- **Q3(a):** Application: radar and microwave ovens
- **Q3(b):** TWT: electron beam interacts with slowed wave on a helix | Slow-wave Structure: helix reduces wave velocity to match beam | Broadband Gain: TWT amplifies over an octave
- **Q4(a):** PIN Diode: used as microwave switch and attenuator
- **Q4(b):** GaAs MESFET: metal-semiconductor FET for microwave amplifiers | HEMT: high electron mobility transistor with low noise | HBT: heterojunction bipolar transistor
- **Q5(a):** Thermistor Mount: power meter sensor
- **Q5(b):** Slotted Line: probe samples standing wave along a line | Double Minimum Method: measures high VSWR | Network Analyser: measures S-parameters directly
- **Q6(a):** Effective Permittivity: accounts for fields in air and dielectric
- **Q6(b):** TE Mode: transverse electric, no E field along propagation | Dominant Mode: TE10 in rectangular waveguide | Cut-off Wavelength: λc = 2a for TE10
- **Q7(a):** Transit Time Effect: electron transit comparable to RF period
- **Q7(b):** Two-cavity Klystron: amplifier using buncher and catcher cavities | Reflex Klystron: single-cavity oscillator with repeller | Velocity Modulation: varying electron speed to form bunches
- **Q8(a):** Double Minimum Method: measures high VSWR
- **Q8(b):** Cavity Wavemeter: absorption dip at resonance | Attenuation: ratio of input to output power in dB | Insertion Loss: loss when a device is inserted
