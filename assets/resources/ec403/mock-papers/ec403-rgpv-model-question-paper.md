## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Modulation Index: m = Am/Ac; overmodulation when m > 1
- **Q1(b):** Square-law Modulator: uses non-linear device to produce AM | Collector Modulation: high-level AM in the final stage | Switching Modulator: diode switching to generate AM
- **Q2(a):** Carson's Rule: BW ≈ 2(Δf + fm)
- **Q2(b):** Direct Method: varactor varies oscillator frequency | Armstrong Method: indirect FM via narrowband PM and frequency multiplication | Frequency Multiplier: multiplies carrier and deviation
- **Q3(a):** Mixer: combines signal and local oscillator to produce IF
- **Q3(b):** AGC: automatic gain control adjusting gain with signal strength | Fidelity: faithful reproduction of all modulating frequencies | De-emphasis: restores audio balance after pre-emphasis
- **Q4(a):** FM Noise Advantage: output SNR grows with β²
- **Q4(b):** Thermal Noise: Pn = kTB | Shot Noise: random arrival of charge carriers | Atmospheric Noise: noise from lightning and natural sources
- **Q5(a):** Flat-top Sampling: sample-and-hold pulses
- **Q5(b):** PWM: pulse width proportional to sample value | PPM: pulse position varies with sample value | PPM Generation: derived from PWM trailing edges
- **Q6(a):** VSB: vestigial sideband used for TV video
- **Q6(b):** Modulation Index: m = Am/Ac; overmodulation when m > 1 | AM Spectrum: carrier plus upper and lower sidebands | Total Power: Pt = Pc(1 + m²/2)
- **Q7(a):** Low-level Modulation: modulation before final amplification
- **Q7(b):** TRF Receiver: cascaded tuned RF amplifiers | Selectivity Problem: bandwidth varies with tuning | Sensitivity: minimum input for a specified output
- **Q8(a):** PPM: pulse position varies with sample value
- **Q8(b):** FDM: signals placed in separate frequency bands | Guard Band: unused band separating channels | Group and Supergroup: FDM hierarchy in telephony
