## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Orthogonal Cutting: cutting edge perpendicular to cutting velocity
- **Q1(b):** Continuous Chip: ductile material at high speed | Discontinuous Chip: brittle materials like cast iron | Built-up Edge: welded material on tool tip at low speed
- **Q2(a):** Reaming: finishing a drilled hole to accurate size
- **Q2(b):** Shaper: tool reciprocates over stationary work | Planer: work reciprocates under stationary tool | Quick Return Ratio: cutting stroke time / return stroke time
- **Q3(a):** Buffing: polishing to a lustrous finish
- **Q3(b):** Ra Value: arithmetic mean roughness | Lay: direction of dominant surface pattern | Surface Finish Symbol: drawing notation for roughness
- **Q4(a):** USM: ultrasonic machining with abrasive slurry
- **Q4(b):** Unconventional Machining: removes material without traditional chips | Hard Materials: carbides and ceramics difficult to cut | Energy Classification: mechanical, thermal, chemical and electrochemical
- **Q5(a):** Fixture: holds work without guiding the tool
- **Q5(b):** Tolerance: permissible variation in size | Clearance Fit: hole always larger than shaft | Interference Fit: shaft always larger than hole
- **Q6(a):** Crater Wear: wear on rake face
- **Q6(b):** Orthogonal Cutting: cutting edge perpendicular to cutting velocity | Oblique Cutting: cutting edge inclined to velocity | Chip Thickness Ratio: r = t / tc
- **Q7(a):** Grinding Wheel: bonded abrasive grains
- **Q7(b):** Abrasive Type: aluminium oxide for steel, silicon carbide for cast iron | Grade: hardness of the bond | Structure: spacing of grains in the wheel
- **Q8(a):** Clearance Fit: hole always larger than shaft
- **Q8(b):** Vernier Caliper: measures with least count 0.02 mm | Micrometer: screw gauge with least count 0.01 mm | Sine Bar: measures angles using slip gauges
