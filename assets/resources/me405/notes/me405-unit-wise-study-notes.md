# ME-405 Manufacturing Technology — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Metal cutting theory, machine tools, grinding, non-conventional machining, jigs and fixtures and metrology.

## Unit 1: Theory of Metal Cutting

### Orthogonal & Oblique Cutting

Cutting geometry affects forces and chips.

- **Orthogonal Cutting:** cutting edge perpendicular to cutting velocity
- **Oblique Cutting:** cutting edge inclined to velocity
- **Chip Thickness Ratio:** r = t / tc

### Chip Formation

Chip type depends on material and conditions.

- **Continuous Chip:** ductile material at high speed
- **Discontinuous Chip:** brittle materials like cast iron
- **Built-up Edge:** welded material on tool tip at low speed

### Merchant's Circle

Force analysis of orthogonal cutting.

- **Merchant's Circle:** force diagram relating cutting forces
- **Shear Angle Relation:** 2φ + β − α = 90°
- **Cutting Force:** force component in direction of cutting velocity

### Tool Life & Wear

Tools wear and must be replaced.

- **Taylor's Tool Life Equation:** VT^n = C
- **Flank Wear:** wear on clearance face
- **Crater Wear:** wear on rake face

## Unit 2: Machine Tools

### Lathe

Lathe produces cylindrical surfaces.

- **Lathe:** machine rotating work against a single-point tool
- **Taper Turning:** by compound rest, tailstock offset or attachment
- **Thread Cutting:** lead screw synchronises carriage with spindle

### Drilling & Boring

Holes are made and enlarged.

- **Drilling:** making holes with a twist drill
- **Reaming:** finishing a drilled hole to accurate size
- **Boring:** enlarging an existing hole with a single-point tool

### Shaper, Planer & Slotter

Reciprocating tools make flat surfaces.

- **Shaper:** tool reciprocates over stationary work
- **Planer:** work reciprocates under stationary tool
- **Quick Return Ratio:** cutting stroke time / return stroke time

### Milling

Multi-tooth cutters machine many shapes.

- **Up Milling:** cutter rotates against feed direction
- **Down Milling:** cutter rotates with feed direction
- **Indexing:** dividing head for equally spaced features

## Unit 3: Grinding & Finishing

### Grinding Process

Abrasives remove small amounts of material.

- **Grinding Wheel:** bonded abrasive grains
- **Grit Size:** abrasive particle size number
- **Wheel Dressing:** restoring sharp grains on a wheel

### Grinding Wheel Specification

Wheels are specified by standard code.

- **Abrasive Type:** aluminium oxide for steel, silicon carbide for cast iron
- **Grade:** hardness of the bond
- **Structure:** spacing of grains in the wheel

### Superfinishing Processes

Finishing improves surface quality.

- **Honing:** abrasive stones finish bores
- **Lapping:** loose abrasive between lap and work
- **Buffing:** polishing to a lustrous finish

### Surface Roughness

Surface quality is quantified.

- **Ra Value:** arithmetic mean roughness
- **Lay:** direction of dominant surface pattern
- **Surface Finish Symbol:** drawing notation for roughness

## Unit 4: Non-conventional Machining

### Need for Unconventional Processes

Hard materials and complex shapes need new methods.

- **Unconventional Machining:** removes material without traditional chips
- **Hard Materials:** carbides and ceramics difficult to cut
- **Energy Classification:** mechanical, thermal, chemical and electrochemical

### EDM & Wire EDM

Sparks erode conductive materials.

- **EDM:** electric discharge machining using sparks in dielectric
- **Dielectric Fluid:** kerosene or deionised water
- **Wire EDM:** travelling wire cuts complex profiles

### ECM & Chemical Machining

Electrochemical dissolution removes metal.

- **ECM:** electrochemical machining, reverse of electroplating
- **Electrolyte:** NaCl solution commonly used
- **Chemical Milling:** selective etching with masks

### USM, LBM & AJM

Other unconventional processes.

- **USM:** ultrasonic machining with abrasive slurry
- **LBM:** laser beam machining by melting and vaporising
- **AJM:** abrasive jet machining with high-velocity particles

## Unit 5: Jigs, Fixtures & Metrology

### Jigs & Fixtures

Work-holding devices improve accuracy.

- **Jig:** holds work and guides the tool
- **Fixture:** holds work without guiding the tool
- **3-2-1 Principle:** six points locate a body

### Limits, Fits & Tolerances

Interchangeability needs controlled sizes.

- **Tolerance:** permissible variation in size
- **Clearance Fit:** hole always larger than shaft
- **Interference Fit:** shaft always larger than hole

### Linear & Angular Measurement

Instruments measure dimensions accurately.

- **Vernier Caliper:** measures with least count 0.02 mm
- **Micrometer:** screw gauge with least count 0.01 mm
- **Sine Bar:** measures angles using slip gauges

### Comparators & Gauges

Gauges check limits quickly.

- **Slip Gauges:** precision blocks for length standards
- **Limit Gauge:** GO and NO-GO gauges
- **Comparator:** magnifies deviation from a standard
