# CE-802(D) Earthquake Resistant Design of Structures — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Seismology, structural dynamics, IS 1893 seismic analysis, ductile detailing and seismic design of buildings.

## Unit 1: Engineering Seismology

### Earthquake Basics

Earthquakes release stored strain energy.

- **Elastic Rebound Theory:** strain energy release along faults
- **Plate Tectonics:** movement of lithospheric plates
- **Hypocentre:** point of rupture origin

### Seismic Waves

Waves propagate earthquake energy.

- **P-wave:** primary compressional wave, fastest
- **S-wave:** secondary shear wave
- **Surface Waves:** Love and Rayleigh waves causing damage

### Magnitude & Intensity

Measures describe earthquakes.

- **Magnitude:** energy released, e.g. Richter scale
- **Intensity:** damage at a location, e.g. MSK scale
- **Moment Magnitude:** based on seismic moment

### Seismic Zoning of India

India is divided into zones.

- **Seismic Zones:** Zone II to V as per IS 1893
- **Zone Factor:** 0.10, 0.16, 0.24, 0.36
- **Zone V:** highest seismic risk, e.g. North-East

## Unit 2: Structural Dynamics

### SDOF Systems

Single degree of freedom models.

- **SDOF System:** mass, spring and damper
- **Natural Period:** T = 2π√(m/k)
- **Damping:** energy dissipation, typically 5% for RCC

### Response to Ground Motion

Structures respond to base excitation.

- **Ground Acceleration:** input motion during earthquake
- **Resonance:** amplified response near natural period
- **Duhamel's Integral:** response to arbitrary loading

### Response Spectrum

Spectra summarise peak response.

- **Response Spectrum:** peak response versus period
- **Design Spectrum:** smoothed spectrum for design
- **Spectral Acceleration:** Sa/g from IS 1893

### MDOF Systems

Buildings have many modes.

- **Mode Shape:** vibration pattern
- **Modal Analysis:** combines modal responses
- **SRSS:** square root of sum of squares combination

## Unit 3: Seismic Analysis as per IS 1893

### Design Lateral Force

Codes give design base shear.

- **Design Base Shear:** VB = Ah W
- **Design Horizontal Coefficient:** Ah = (Z/2)(I/R)(Sa/g)
- **Importance Factor:** 1.0 or 1.5 for important buildings

### Response Reduction Factor

Ductility reduces design forces.

- **Response Reduction Factor:** R, e.g. 5 for SMRF
- **SMRF:** special moment resisting frame
- **OMRF:** ordinary moment resisting frame

### Equivalent Static Method

Base shear is distributed by height.

- **Vertical Distribution:** Qi = VB Wi hi² / Σ Wj hj²
- **Fundamental Period:** Ta = 0.075 h^0.75 for RC frames
- **Storey Drift Limit:** 0.004 times storey height

### Irregularities

Irregular buildings perform poorly.

- **Soft Storey:** stiffness much less than storey above
- **Torsional Irregularity:** uneven lateral stiffness distribution
- **Re-entrant Corner:** L or T shaped plans

## Unit 4: Ductile Detailing

### Ductility Concepts

Ductility allows energy dissipation.

- **Ductility:** ability to deform inelastically without collapse
- **Strong Column Weak Beam:** columns stronger than beams
- **Capacity Design:** forces hierarchy of failure

### IS 13920 Beam Detailing

Beams are detailed for ductility.

- **IS 13920:** ductile detailing code
- **Hoops in Beams:** closely spaced near supports
- **Minimum Tension Steel:** at top and bottom throughout

### IS 13920 Column Detailing

Columns need confinement.

- **Special Confining Reinforcement:** closely spaced ties at ends
- **Lap Splice Location:** in middle half of column
- **135° Hook:** anchors ties into core

### Beam-Column Joints

Joints must remain intact.

- **Joint Shear:** shear in beam-column joint
- **Confinement of Joint:** ties through joint
- **Anchorage of Beam Bars:** development into joint

## Unit 5: Seismic Design of Buildings

### Shear Walls

Walls resist lateral loads.

- **Shear Wall:** RC wall resisting lateral forces
- **Boundary Element:** confined ends of wall
- **Coupled Shear Wall:** walls connected by beams

### Masonry Buildings

Masonry needs seismic bands.

- **Seismic Band:** RC band at plinth, lintel and roof
- **Vertical Reinforcement:** at corners and junctions
- **IS 4326:** earthquake-resistant masonry code

### Base Isolation & Dampers

Modern techniques reduce response.

- **Base Isolation:** decouples building from ground
- **Lead Rubber Bearing:** common isolator
- **Viscous Damper:** dissipates energy

### Retrofitting

Existing buildings are strengthened.

- **Jacketing:** enlarging member with new concrete or steel
- **FRP Wrapping:** confines columns
- **Seismic Evaluation:** rapid visual screening
