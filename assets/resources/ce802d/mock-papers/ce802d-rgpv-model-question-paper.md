## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Elastic Rebound Theory: strain energy release along faults
- **Q1(b):** P-wave: primary compressional wave, fastest | S-wave: secondary shear wave | Surface Waves: Love and Rayleigh waves causing damage
- **Q2(a):** Resonance: amplified response near natural period
- **Q2(b):** Response Spectrum: peak response versus period | Design Spectrum: smoothed spectrum for design | Spectral Acceleration: Sa/g from IS 1893
- **Q3(a):** Storey Drift Limit: 0.004 times storey height
- **Q3(b):** Soft Storey: stiffness much less than storey above | Torsional Irregularity: uneven lateral stiffness distribution | Re-entrant Corner: L or T shaped plans
- **Q4(a):** Joint Shear: shear in beam-column joint
- **Q4(b):** Ductility: ability to deform inelastically without collapse | Strong Column Weak Beam: columns stronger than beams | Capacity Design: forces hierarchy of failure
- **Q5(a):** Boundary Element: confined ends of wall
- **Q5(b):** Seismic Band: RC band at plinth, lintel and roof | Vertical Reinforcement: at corners and junctions | IS 4326: earthquake-resistant masonry code
- **Q6(a):** Zone V: highest seismic risk, e.g. North-East
- **Q6(b):** Elastic Rebound Theory: strain energy release along faults | Plate Tectonics: movement of lithospheric plates | Hypocentre: point of rupture origin
- **Q7(a):** Design Base Shear: VB = Ah W
- **Q7(b):** Response Reduction Factor: R, e.g. 5 for SMRF | SMRF: special moment resisting frame | OMRF: ordinary moment resisting frame
- **Q8(a):** Vertical Reinforcement: at corners and junctions
- **Q8(b):** Base Isolation: decouples building from ground | Lead Rubber Bearing: common isolator | Viscous Damper: dissipates energy
