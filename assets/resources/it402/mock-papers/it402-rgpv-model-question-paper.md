## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Periodic Signal: repeats every fixed period T
- **Q1(b):** Fourier Series: periodic signal expressed as sum of harmonics | Fourier Transform: frequency spectrum of a non-periodic signal | Bandwidth: range of frequencies a signal occupies
- **Q2(a):** SSB: single sideband transmission halving the bandwidth
- **Q2(b):** FM: carrier frequency varied with the message | Frequency Deviation: maximum shift of carrier frequency from its centre | Carson's Rule: FM bandwidth ≈ 2(Δf + fm)
- **Q3(a):** Companding: compressing then expanding to equalise SNR, e.g. μ-law
- **Q3(b):** Delta Modulation: transmits whether the signal rose or fell | Slope Overload: staircase cannot follow a rapidly changing signal | Granular Noise: hunting of the staircase around a slowly varying signal
- **Q4(a):** FDM: frequency division multiplexing
- **Q4(b):** ASK: amplitude shift keying | FSK: frequency shift keying using two frequencies | BPSK: binary phase shift keying with phases 0° and 180°
- **Q5(a):** Entropy: average information per symbol, H = −Σ p log2 p
- **Q5(b):** Shannon-Hartley Theorem: C = B log2(1 + S/N) | Channel Capacity: maximum reliable information rate of a channel | Bandwidth-SNR Trade-off: bandwidth can be exchanged for signal power
- **Q6(a):** Noise Figure: degradation of SNR caused by a device
- **Q6(b):** Periodic Signal: repeats every fixed period T | Energy Signal: finite total energy, zero average power | Power Signal: finite non-zero average power, e.g. periodic signals
- **Q7(a):** Nyquist Rate: sampling at least twice the highest frequency, fs ≥ 2fm
- **Q7(b):** PAM: pulse amplitude modulation | PWM: pulse width varied with the message | PPM: pulse position varied with the message
- **Q8(a):** Channel Capacity: maximum reliable information rate of a channel
- **Q8(b):** Shannon-Fano Coding: splits symbols into equally probable groups | Huffman Coding: optimal prefix code from least probable merges | Code Efficiency: entropy divided by average code length
