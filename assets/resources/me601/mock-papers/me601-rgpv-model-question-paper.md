## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Fire-tube Boiler: hot gases pass through tubes surrounded by water
- **Q1(b):** La Mont Boiler: forced circulation boiler | Benson Boiler: supercritical once-through boiler | Supercritical Boiler: operates above critical pressure; no boiling
- **Q2(a):** De Laval Turbine: single-stage impulse turbine
- **Q2(b):** Velocity Compounding: Curtis stage with moving and fixed blade rows | Pressure Compounding: Rateau stages of nozzles and blades | Pressure-Velocity Compounding: combination of both
- **Q3(a):** Approach: outlet water temperature minus wet-bulb temperature
- **Q3(b):** Rankine Cycle: ideal steam power cycle | Reheat: steam reheated after partial expansion | Regeneration: feed water heated by bled steam
- **Q4(a):** Slip Factor: ratio of actual to ideal whirl velocity
- **Q4(b):** Isothermal Efficiency: isothermal work / actual work | Clearance Volume: reduces volumetric efficiency | Volumetric Efficiency: 1 + C − C(p2/p1)^(1/n)
- **Q5(a):** Speed of Sound: a = √(γRT)
- **Q5(b):** Area-Velocity Relation: dA/A = (M² − 1)dV/V | Convergent-Divergent Nozzle: needed to reach supersonic speeds | Choked Flow: maximum mass flow at M = 1 at throat
- **Q6(a):** Natural Draught: chimney creates draught by density difference
- **Q6(b):** Fire-tube Boiler: hot gases pass through tubes surrounded by water | Water-tube Boiler: water flows in tubes heated externally; high pressure | Babcock and Wilcox Boiler: inclined water-tube boiler
- **Q7(a):** Surface Condenser: steam and cooling water do not mix
- **Q7(b):** Vacuum Efficiency: actual vacuum / ideal vacuum | Dalton's Law: total pressure is the sum of partial pressures of steam and air | Air Pump: removes air from condenser
- **Q8(a):** Convergent-Divergent Nozzle: needed to reach supersonic speeds
- **Q8(b):** Normal Shock: discontinuity converting supersonic to subsonic flow | Prandtl Relation: V1·V2 = a*² | Entropy Rise: entropy increases across a shock
