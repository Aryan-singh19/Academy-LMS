## Unit 2: Steam Nozzles & Turbines

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Nozzle Exit Velocity*.  
  **Ans.** V = 44.72√(Δh) with Δh in kJ/kg
- **Q.** Define *Critical Pressure Ratio*.  
  **Ans.** about 0.546 for superheated steam
- **Q.** Define *Supersaturated Flow*.  
  **Ans.** steam expands without condensing (metastable)
- **Q.** Define *Impulse Turbine*.  
  **Ans.** pressure drop occurs only in nozzles
- **Q.** Define *De Laval Turbine*.  
  **Ans.** single-stage impulse turbine
- **Q.** Define *Blade Efficiency*.  
  **Ans.** maximum when blade speed ratio is cos α/2
- **Q.** Define *Velocity Compounding*.  
  **Ans.** Curtis stage with moving and fixed blade rows
- **Q.** Define *Pressure Compounding*.  
  **Ans.** Rateau stages of nozzles and blades
- **Q.** Define *Pressure-Velocity Compounding*.  
  **Ans.** combination of both
- **Q.** Define *Reaction Turbine*.  
  **Ans.** pressure drop in fixed and moving blades
- **Q.** Define *Degree of Reaction*.  
  **Ans.** enthalpy drop in moving blades / stage drop
- **Q.** Define *Parsons Turbine*.  
  **Ans.** 50% reaction turbine

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Flow Through Nozzles* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Nozzles convert enthalpy into kinetic energy. Nozzle Exit Velocity — V = 44.72√(Δh) with Δh in kJ/kg; Critical Pressure Ratio — about 0.546 for superheated steam; Supersaturated Flow — steam expands without condensing (metastable).
- **Q.** Explain *Impulse Turbines* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Impulse turbines expand steam in nozzles only. Impulse Turbine — pressure drop occurs only in nozzles; De Laval Turbine — single-stage impulse turbine; Blade Efficiency — maximum when blade speed ratio is cos α/2.
- **Q.** Explain *Compounding of Turbines* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Compounding reduces rotor speed. Velocity Compounding — Curtis stage with moving and fixed blade rows; Pressure Compounding — Rateau stages of nozzles and blades; Pressure-Velocity Compounding — combination of both.
- **Q.** Explain *Reaction Turbines* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Steam expands in both fixed and moving blades. Reaction Turbine — pressure drop in fixed and moving blades; Degree of Reaction — enthalpy drop in moving blades / stage drop; Parsons Turbine — 50% reaction turbine.

- **Q.** Define *Jet Condenser*.  
  **Ans.** steam mixes with cooling water

- **Q.** Define *Vacuum Efficiency*.  
  **Ans.** actual vacuum / ideal vacuum
- **Q.** Define *Dalton's Law*.

- **Q.** Define *Reheat*.

- **Q.** Define *Regeneration*.

- **Q.** Explain *Cooling Towers* in detail with a neat diagram.

## Unit 4: Air Compressors

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Isothermal Efficiency*.  
  **Ans.** isothermal work / actual work
- **Q.** Define *Clearance Volume*.  
  **Ans.** reduces volumetric efficiency
- **Q.** Define *Volumetric Efficiency*.  
  **Ans.** 1 + C − C(p2/p1)^(1/n)
- **Q.** Define *Intercooling*.  
  **Ans.** cooling air between stages
- **Q.** Define *Perfect Intercooling*.  
  **Ans.** air cooled to initial temperature
- **Q.** Define *Optimum Pressure Ratio*.  
  **Ans.** equal ratio per stage, √(p3/p1) for two stages
- **Q.** Define *Centrifugal Compressor*.  
  **Ans.** impeller imparts kinetic energy converted in diffuser
- **Q.** Define *Axial Flow Compressor*.  
  **Ans.** rows of rotor and stator blades
- **Q.** Define *Surging*.  
  **Ans.** unstable flow reversal at low flow rates
- **Q.** Define *Slip Factor*.  
  **Ans.** ratio of actual to ideal whirl velocity
- **Q.** Define *Choking*.  
  **Ans.** maximum flow when Mach 1 is reached
- **Q.** Define *Pressure Coefficient*.  
  **Ans.** actual / isentropic pressure rise

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Reciprocating Compressors* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Pistons compress air in cylinders. Isothermal Efficiency — isothermal work / actual work; Clearance Volume — reduces volumetric efficiency; Volumetric Efficiency — 1 + C − C(p2/p1)^(1/n).
- **Q.** Explain *Multistage Compression* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Staging with intercooling saves work. Intercooling — cooling air between stages; Perfect Intercooling — air cooled to initial temperature; Optimum Pressure Ratio — equal ratio per stage, √(p3/p1) for two stages.
- **Q.** Explain *Rotary Compressors* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Rotary machines deliver continuous flow. Centrifugal Compressor — impeller imparts kinetic energy converted in diffuser; Axial Flow Compressor — rows of rotor and stator blades; Surging — unstable flow reversal at low flow rates.
- **Q.** Explain *Compressor Performance* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Performance maps show operating limits. Slip Factor — ratio of actual to ideal whirl velocity; Choking — maximum flow when Mach 1 is reached; Pressure Coefficient — actual / isentropic pressure rise.

## Unit 5: Gas Dynamics

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Mach Number*.  
  **Ans.** M = V/a
- **Q.** Define *Speed of Sound*.  
  **Ans.** a = √(γRT)
- **Q.** Define *Stagnation Properties*.  
  **Ans.** properties when flow is brought to rest isentropically
- **Q.** Define *Area-Velocity Relation*.  
  **Ans.** dA/A = (M² − 1)dV/V
- **Q.** Define *Convergent-Divergent Nozzle*.  
  **Ans.** needed to reach supersonic speeds
- **Q.** Define *Choked Flow*.  
  **Ans.** maximum mass flow at M = 1 at throat
- **Q.** Define *Normal Shock*.  
  **Ans.** discontinuity converting supersonic to subsonic flow
- **Q.** Define *Prandtl Relation*.  
  **Ans.** V1·V2 = a*²
- **Q.** Define *Entropy Rise*.  
  **Ans.** entropy increases across a shock
- **Q.** Define *Fanno Flow*.  
  **Ans.** adiabatic flow with friction
- **Q.** Define *Rayleigh Flow*.  
  **Ans.** frictionless flow with heat transfer
- **Q.** Define *Choking Length*.  
  **Ans.** duct length driving flow to M = 1

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Compressible Flow Basics* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Density changes matter at high speeds. Mach Number — M = V/a; Speed of Sound — a = √(γRT); Stagnation Properties — properties when flow is brought to rest isentropically.
- **Q.** Explain *Isentropic Flow in Nozzles* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Area changes accelerate subsonic and supersonic flow differently. Area-Velocity Relation — dA/A = (M² − 1)dV/V; Convergent-Divergent Nozzle — needed to reach supersonic speeds; Choked Flow — maximum mass flow at M = 1 at throat.
- **Q.** Explain *Normal Shock Waves* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Shocks are abrupt irreversible changes. Normal Shock — discontinuity converting supersonic to subsonic flow; Prandtl Relation — V1·V2 = a*²; Entropy Rise — entropy increases across a shock.
- **Q.** Explain *Fanno & Rayleigh Flow* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Friction and heat transfer affect duct flow. Fanno Flow — adiabatic flow with friction; Rayleigh Flow — frictionless flow with heat transfer; Choking Length — duct length driving flow to M = 1.
