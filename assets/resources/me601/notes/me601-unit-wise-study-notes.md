# ME-601 Thermal Engineering & Gas Dynamics — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Boilers, steam nozzles and turbines, condensers, compressors and compressible flow.

## Unit 1: Steam Generators & Boilers

### Classification of Boilers

Boilers generate steam for power and process.

- **Fire-tube Boiler:** hot gases pass through tubes surrounded by water
- **Water-tube Boiler:** water flows in tubes heated externally; high pressure
- **Babcock and Wilcox Boiler:** inclined water-tube boiler

### High-pressure Boilers

Modern boilers operate at very high pressures.

- **La Mont Boiler:** forced circulation boiler
- **Benson Boiler:** supercritical once-through boiler
- **Supercritical Boiler:** operates above critical pressure; no boiling

### Boiler Mountings & Accessories

Fittings ensure safety and efficiency.

- **Safety Valve:** releases excess pressure
- **Economiser:** preheats feed water using flue gases
- **Superheater:** raises steam temperature above saturation

### Boiler Performance & Draught

Performance is rated by evaporation and efficiency.

- **Equivalent Evaporation:** water evaporated from and at 100 °C
- **Boiler Efficiency:** heat used in steam / heat supplied by fuel
- **Natural Draught:** chimney creates draught by density difference

## Unit 2: Steam Nozzles & Turbines

### Flow Through Nozzles

Nozzles convert enthalpy into kinetic energy.

- **Nozzle Exit Velocity:** V = 44.72√(Δh) with Δh in kJ/kg
- **Critical Pressure Ratio:** about 0.546 for superheated steam
- **Supersaturated Flow:** steam expands without condensing (metastable)

### Impulse Turbines

Impulse turbines expand steam in nozzles only.

- **Impulse Turbine:** pressure drop occurs only in nozzles
- **De Laval Turbine:** single-stage impulse turbine
- **Blade Efficiency:** maximum when blade speed ratio is cos α/2

### Compounding of Turbines

Compounding reduces rotor speed.

- **Velocity Compounding:** Curtis stage with moving and fixed blade rows
- **Pressure Compounding:** Rateau stages of nozzles and blades
- **Pressure-Velocity Compounding:** combination of both

### Reaction Turbines

Steam expands in both fixed and moving blades.

- **Reaction Turbine:** pressure drop in fixed and moving blades
- **Degree of Reaction:** enthalpy drop in moving blades / stage drop
- **Parsons Turbine:** 50% reaction turbine

## Unit 3: Condensers & Cooling Towers

### Steam Condensers

Condensers lower back pressure and recover water.

- **Surface Condenser:** steam and cooling water do not mix
- **Jet Condenser:** steam mixes with cooling water
- **Condenser Vacuum:** lower pressure increases turbine work

### Condenser Efficiency

Air leakage reduces performance.

- **Vacuum Efficiency:** actual vacuum / ideal vacuum
- **Dalton's Law:** total pressure is the sum of partial pressures of steam and air
- **Air Pump:** removes air from condenser

### Cooling Towers

Cooling towers reject heat to atmosphere.

- **Natural Draught Cooling Tower:** hyperbolic tower using buoyancy
- **Range:** inlet minus outlet water temperature
- **Approach:** outlet water temperature minus wet-bulb temperature

### Rankine Cycle Improvements

Plant efficiency is raised by modifications.

- **Rankine Cycle:** ideal steam power cycle
- **Reheat:** steam reheated after partial expansion
- **Regeneration:** feed water heated by bled steam

## Unit 4: Air Compressors

### Reciprocating Compressors

Pistons compress air in cylinders.

- **Isothermal Efficiency:** isothermal work / actual work
- **Clearance Volume:** reduces volumetric efficiency
- **Volumetric Efficiency:** 1 + C − C(p2/p1)^(1/n)

### Multistage Compression

Staging with intercooling saves work.

- **Intercooling:** cooling air between stages
- **Perfect Intercooling:** air cooled to initial temperature
- **Optimum Pressure Ratio:** equal ratio per stage, √(p3/p1) for two stages

### Rotary Compressors

Rotary machines deliver continuous flow.

- **Centrifugal Compressor:** impeller imparts kinetic energy converted in diffuser
- **Axial Flow Compressor:** rows of rotor and stator blades
- **Surging:** unstable flow reversal at low flow rates

### Compressor Performance

Performance maps show operating limits.

- **Slip Factor:** ratio of actual to ideal whirl velocity
- **Choking:** maximum flow when Mach 1 is reached
- **Pressure Coefficient:** actual / isentropic pressure rise

## Unit 5: Gas Dynamics

### Compressible Flow Basics

Density changes matter at high speeds.

- **Mach Number:** M = V/a
- **Speed of Sound:** a = √(γRT)
- **Stagnation Properties:** properties when flow is brought to rest isentropically

### Isentropic Flow in Nozzles

Area changes accelerate subsonic and supersonic flow differently.

- **Area-Velocity Relation:** dA/A = (M² − 1)dV/V
- **Convergent-Divergent Nozzle:** needed to reach supersonic speeds
- **Choked Flow:** maximum mass flow at M = 1 at throat

### Normal Shock Waves

Shocks are abrupt irreversible changes.

- **Normal Shock:** discontinuity converting supersonic to subsonic flow
- **Prandtl Relation:** V1·V2 = a*²
- **Entropy Rise:** entropy increases across a shock

### Fanno & Rayleigh Flow

Friction and heat transfer affect duct flow.

- **Fanno Flow:** adiabatic flow with friction
- **Rayleigh Flow:** frictionless flow with heat transfer
- **Choking Length:** duct length driving flow to M = 1
