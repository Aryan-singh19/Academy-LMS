## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Artificial Intelligence: study of agents that act rationally
- **Q1(b):** Agent: entity perceiving through sensors and acting through actuators | Rational Agent: selects actions maximising expected performance | PEAS: performance, environment, actuators and sensors description
- **Q2(a):** A* Search: expands node with smallest f(n) = g(n) + h(n)
- **Q2(b):** Hill Climbing: moves to the best neighbour; may stick at local maxima | Simulated Annealing: allows worse moves with decreasing probability | Genetic Algorithm: evolves a population by selection, crossover and mutation
- **Q3(a):** Ontology: formal specification of concepts and relationships in a domain
- **Q3(b):** STRIPS: planning language with preconditions and effects | Partial-order Planning: commits only to necessary action orderings | Goal Stack Planning: solves goals by pushing subgoals on a stack
- **Q4(a):** Supervised Learning: learning a mapping from labelled examples
- **Q4(b):** Bayes' Theorem: P(A given B) = P(B given A)·P(A)/P(B) | Conditional Independence: two variables independent given a third | Prior Probability: belief before observing evidence
- **Q5(a):** Knowledge Base: facts and rules of the domain
- **Q5(b):** Tokenisation: splitting text into words or subwords | Parsing: analysing grammatical structure of a sentence | Sentiment Analysis: detecting opinion polarity in text
- **Q6(a):** Utility-based Agent: maximises a utility measure of desirability
- **Q6(b):** Artificial Intelligence: study of agents that act rationally | Turing Test: test of whether a machine's behaviour is indistinguishable from a human's | Strong vs Weak AI: general human-level intelligence versus task-specific intelligence
- **Q7(a):** First-Order Logic: logic with objects, predicates and quantifiers
- **Q7(b):** Forward Chaining: data-driven reasoning from facts to conclusions | Backward Chaining: goal-driven reasoning from the query back to facts | Horn Clause: clause with at most one positive literal
- **Q8(a):** Parsing: analysing grammatical structure of a sentence
- **Q8(b):** Perceptron: single-layer linear classifier | Backpropagation: gradient computation through layers by the chain rule | Deep Learning: neural networks with many hidden layers
