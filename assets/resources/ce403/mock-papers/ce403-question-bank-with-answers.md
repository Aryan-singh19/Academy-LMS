## Unit 3: Energy Methods

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Strain Energy in Bending*.  
  **Ans.** ∫ M²/(2EI) dx
- **Q.** Define *Strain Energy in Axial Load*.  
  **Ans.** P²L/(2AE)
- **Q.** Define *Complementary Energy*.  
  **Ans.** energy used in non-linear analysis
- **Q.** Define *Castigliano's First Theorem*.  
  **Ans.** ∂U/∂Δ = P
- **Q.** Define *Castigliano's Second Theorem*.  
  **Ans.** ∂U/∂P = Δ
- **Q.** Define *Dummy Load Method*.  
  **Ans.** apply fictitious load to find deflection
- **Q.** Define *Virtual Work Principle*.  
  **Ans.** external virtual work equals internal virtual work
- **Q.** Define *Unit Load Method*.  
  **Ans.** δ = ∫ Mm/(EI) dx
- **Q.** Define *Truss Deflection*.  
  **Ans.** δ = Σ PuL/(AE)
- **Q.** Define *Maxwell's Reciprocal Theorem*.  
  **Ans.** deflection at A due to load at B equals deflection at B due to load at A
- **Q.** Define *Betti's Theorem*.  
  **Ans.** generalised reciprocal work theorem
- **Q.** Define *Application*.  
  **Ans.** influence lines by Müller-Breslau

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Strain Energy* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Work done is stored as strain energy. Strain Energy in Bending — ∫ M²/(2EI) dx; Strain Energy in Axial Load — P²L/(2AE); Complementary Energy — energy used in non-linear analysis.
- **Q.** Explain *Castigliano's Theorems* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Derivatives of strain energy give deflections. Castigliano's First Theorem — ∂U/∂Δ = P; Castigliano's Second Theorem — ∂U/∂P = Δ; Dummy Load Method — apply fictitious load to find deflection.
- **Q.** Explain *Unit Load Method* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Virtual work finds deflections. Virtual Work Principle — external virtual work equals internal virtual work; Unit Load Method — δ = ∫ Mm/(EI) dx; Truss Deflection — δ = Σ PuL/(AE).
- **Q.** Explain *Maxwell & Betti Theorems* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Reciprocal theorems relate deflections. Maxwell's Reciprocal Theorem — deflection at A due to load at B equals deflection at B due to load at A; Betti's Theorem — generalised reciprocal work theorem; Application — influence lines by Müller-Breslau.

## Unit 5: Influence Lines & Rolling Loads

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Influence Line*.  
  **Ans.** response at a point as unit load moves
- **Q.** Define *IL for Reaction*.  
  **Ans.** straight line for simply supported beam
- **Q.** Define *Müller-Breslau Principle*.  
  **Ans.** IL shape equals deflected shape from released constraint
- **Q.** Define *Single Concentrated Load*.  
  **Ans.** maximum moment when load is at section
- **Q.** Define *UDL Longer than Span*.  
  **Ans.** covers whole span for maximum
- **Q.** Define *Maximum Bending Moment*.  
  **Ans.** occurs under a load near mid-span
- **Q.** Define *Absolute Maximum Moment*.  
  **Ans.** largest moment anywhere in span
- **Q.** Define *Resultant Rule*.  
  **Ans.** centre of span bisects distance between resultant and nearest load
- **Q.** Define *Equivalent UDL*.  
  **Ans.** uniform load giving same maximum effect
- **Q.** Define *Propped Cantilever*.  
  **Ans.** one degree indeterminate
- **Q.** Define *Fixed Beam*.  
  **Ans.** fixing moments at both ends; wL²/12 for UDL
- **Q.** Define *Clapeyron's Three Moment Equation*.  
  **Ans.** analyses continuous beams

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Influence Lines* in detail with a neat diagram.

- **Q.** Explain *Rolling Loads* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Moving loads produce maximum effects. Single Concentrated Load — maximum moment when load is at section; UDL Longer than Span — covers whole span for maximum; Maximum Bending Moment — occurs under a load near mid-span.
- **Q.** Explain *Absolute Maximum Moment* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Moving load trains create peak effects. Absolute Maximum Moment — largest moment anywhere in span; Resultant Rule — centre of span bisects distance between resultant and nearest load; Equivalent UDL — uniform load giving same maximum effect.
- **Q.** Explain *Indeterminate Beams* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Propped and fixed beams. Propped Cantilever — one degree indeterminate; Fixed Beam — fixing moments at both ends; wL²/12 for UDL; Clapeyron's Three Moment Equation — analyses continuous beams.
