## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Determinate Structure: solvable by equilibrium alone
- **Q1(b):** Static Indeterminacy: excess unknown forces | Kinematic Indeterminacy: unknown joint displacements | Stability: structure resists loads without collapse
- **Q2(a):** Second Theorem: deflection equals moment of M/EI area
- **Q2(b):** Conjugate Beam: beam loaded by M/EI diagram | Shear in Conjugate Beam: slope in real beam | Moment in Conjugate Beam: deflection in real beam
- **Q3(a):** Truss Deflection: δ = Σ PuL/(AE)
- **Q3(b):** Maxwell's Reciprocal Theorem: deflection at A due to load at B equals deflection at B due to load at A | Betti's Theorem: generalised reciprocal work theorem | Application: influence lines by Müller-Breslau
- **Q4(a):** Stiffening Girder: spreads loads in suspension bridges
- **Q4(b):** Three-hinged Arch: determinate with hinge at crown | Horizontal Thrust: H = wL²/(8h) for parabolic arch under UDL | Normal Thrust: axial force along arch
- **Q5(a):** IL for Reaction: straight line for simply supported beam
- **Q5(b):** Single Concentrated Load: maximum moment when load is at section | UDL Longer than Span: covers whole span for maximum | Maximum Bending Moment: occurs under a load near mid-span
- **Q6(a):** Small Deflection Assumption: geometry unchanged by loading
- **Q6(b):** Determinate Structure: solvable by equilibrium alone | Indeterminate Structure: needs compatibility in addition to equilibrium | Degree of Indeterminacy: unknown reactions minus equilibrium equations
- **Q7(a):** Strain Energy in Bending: ∫ M²/(2EI) dx
- **Q7(b):** Castigliano's First Theorem: ∂U/∂Δ = P | Castigliano's Second Theorem: ∂U/∂P = Δ | Dummy Load Method: apply fictitious load to find deflection
- **Q8(a):** UDL Longer than Span: covers whole span for maximum
- **Q8(b):** Absolute Maximum Moment: largest moment anywhere in span | Resultant Rule: centre of span bisects distance between resultant and nearest load | Equivalent UDL: uniform load giving same maximum effect
