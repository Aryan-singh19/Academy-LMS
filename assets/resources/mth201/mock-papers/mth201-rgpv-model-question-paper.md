## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Thermal System: interacting components transferring energy
- **Q1(b):** Design Variables: quantities the designer chooses | Constraints: limits on variables | Objective Function: quantity to minimise or maximise
- **Q2(a):** Thermal Capacitance: energy storage term
- **Q2(b):** Information Flow Diagram: shows variables passed between components | Sequential Modular Approach: solve units in sequence | Equation-oriented Approach: solve all equations together
- **Q3(a):** Six-tenths Rule: cost scales with capacity^0.6
- **Q3(b):** Exergy Cost: cost per unit exergy | Specific Exergy Costing: SPECO method | Cost of Exergy Destruction: economic value of losses
- **Q4(a):** Genetic Algorithm: population-based evolutionary search
- **Q4(b):** Calculus Method: set derivatives to zero | Lagrange Multipliers: constrained optimisation | Search Methods: numerical search for optimum
- **Q5(a):** Sizing Problem: area for given duty
- **Q5(b):** System Curve: head versus flow of piping | Operating Point: intersection of pump and system curves | Economic Pipe Diameter: balances pipe and pumping cost
- **Q6(a):** Compressor Model: performance map
- **Q6(b):** Thermal System: interacting components transferring energy | Workable Design: satisfies requirements | Optimal Design: best design by chosen criterion
- **Q7(a):** Present Worth: future amount discounted to today
- **Q7(b):** Life Cycle Cost: total cost over lifetime | Payback Period: time to recover investment | Internal Rate of Return: discount rate giving zero NPV
- **Q8(a):** Operating Point: intersection of pump and system curves
- **Q8(b):** Pinch Analysis: targets minimum utility use | Cogeneration Design: combined heat and power | Waste Heat Recovery: using exhaust heat
