## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Precipitation: rain, snow and hail
- **Q1(b):** Symons Rain Gauge: non-recording gauge | Average Rainfall: arithmetic, Thiessen polygon or isohyetal method | Double Mass Curve: checks consistency of records
- **Q2(a):** Delta: total depth of water for crop
- **Q2(b):** Field Capacity: water held after drainage | Permanent Wilting Point: moisture where plants wilt | Available Water: field capacity minus wilting point
- **Q3(a):** Benefits of Lining: reduces waterlogging
- **Q3(b):** Waterlogging: saturated root zone | Tile Drain: subsurface drainage | Salinity: salt accumulation in soil
- **Q4(a):** Ogee Spillway: shaped overflow spillway
- **Q4(b):** Gravity Dam: resists by own weight | Earth Dam: embankment of soil | Arch Dam: transfers load to abutments
- **Q5(a):** Barrage: gated structure across river
- **Q5(b):** Canal Fall: drop structure on steep ground | Aqueduct: carries canal over drainage | Cross Regulator: controls canal levels
- **Q6(a):** Gumbel's Method: statistical flood frequency analysis
- **Q6(b):** Precipitation: rain, snow and hail | Infiltration: water entering soil | Runoff: water flowing over surface to streams
- **Q7(a):** Main Canal: takes water from headworks
- **Q7(b):** Kennedy's Theory: critical velocity V0 = 0.55 m D^0.64 | Lacey's Theory: regime channel theory | Silt Factor: f = 1.76√d
- **Q8(a):** Aqueduct: carries canal over drainage
- **Q8(b):** Watershed: area draining to a point | Check Dam: small dam reducing erosion | Contour Bunding: embankments along contours
