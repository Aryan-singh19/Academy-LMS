## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Ferrous Metal: contains iron, e.g. steel
- **Q1(b):** Ductility: ability to be drawn into wire | Malleability: ability to be hammered into sheets | Toughness: energy absorbed before fracture
- **Q2(a):** Headstock: holds spindle and drive
- **Q2(b):** Drilling Machine: makes holes using twist drill | Milling Machine: multi-point cutter removes metal | Shaper: reciprocating tool for flat surfaces
- **Q3(a):** Venturimeter: measures flow rate
- **Q3(b):** Pump: adds energy to fluid | Turbine: extracts energy from fluid | Hydraulic Press: force multiplication using Pascal's law
- **Q4(a):** Heat Engine: device converting heat into work
- **Q4(b):** Thermodynamic System: quantity of matter under study | Property: measurable characteristic like p, V, T | Thermal Equilibrium: no temperature difference
- **Q5(a):** Dry Saturated Steam: no water particles
- **Q5(b):** Fire-tube Boiler: hot gases in tubes, e.g. Cochran | Water-tube Boiler: water in tubes, e.g. Babcock and Wilcox | Boiler Mountings: safety valves and gauges
- **Q6(a):** Tempering: reheating to reduce brittleness
- **Q6(b):** Ferrous Metal: contains iron, e.g. steel | Non-ferrous Metal: no iron, e.g. copper and aluminium | Composite: combination of two or more materials
- **Q7(a):** Density: mass per unit volume
- **Q7(b):** Pascal's Law: pressure transmitted equally in all directions | Manometer: liquid column instrument | Bourdon Gauge: mechanical pressure gauge
- **Q8(a):** Water-tube Boiler: water in tubes, e.g. Babcock and Wilcox
- **Q8(b):** Four-stroke Engine: suction, compression, power, exhaust | Two-stroke Engine: power stroke every revolution | Petrol vs Diesel: spark ignition versus compression ignition
