# BT-203 Basic Mechanical Engineering — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Materials, measurement, machine tools, fluid basics, thermodynamics, steam boilers and IC engines.

## Unit 1: Engineering Materials

### Classification of Materials

Materials are metals, non-metals and composites.

- **Ferrous Metal:** contains iron, e.g. steel
- **Non-ferrous Metal:** no iron, e.g. copper and aluminium
- **Composite:** combination of two or more materials

### Mechanical Properties

Properties describe response to loads.

- **Ductility:** ability to be drawn into wire
- **Malleability:** ability to be hammered into sheets
- **Toughness:** energy absorbed before fracture

### Stress-Strain Diagram

Tensile behaviour of mild steel.

- **Elastic Limit:** stress up to which material regains shape
- **Yield Point:** stress where plastic deformation begins
- **Ultimate Stress:** maximum stress in the test

### Heat Treatment Basics

Heating and cooling change properties.

- **Annealing:** softening by slow cooling
- **Hardening:** quenching to increase hardness
- **Tempering:** reheating to reduce brittleness

## Unit 2: Measurement & Machine Tools

### Measuring Instruments

Precision instruments measure dimensions.

- **Vernier Caliper:** least count 0.02 mm
- **Micrometer:** least count 0.01 mm
- **Dial Gauge:** measures small deviations

### Lathe Machine

Lathe makes cylindrical parts.

- **Lathe Operations:** turning, facing, threading and taper turning
- **Headstock:** holds spindle and drive
- **Tailstock:** supports long work

### Drilling & Milling

Holes and flat surfaces are machined.

- **Drilling Machine:** makes holes using twist drill
- **Milling Machine:** multi-point cutter removes metal
- **Shaper:** reciprocating tool for flat surfaces

### Basic Manufacturing Processes

Shaping methods for metals.

- **Casting:** pouring molten metal into mould
- **Welding:** joining by fusion
- **Forging:** shaping by compressive force

## Unit 3: Fluid Mechanics Basics

### Fluid Properties

Fluids are characterised by properties.

- **Density:** mass per unit volume
- **Viscosity:** resistance to flow
- **Specific Gravity:** density relative to water

### Pressure Measurement

Pressure is measured by manometers and gauges.

- **Pascal's Law:** pressure transmitted equally in all directions
- **Manometer:** liquid column instrument
- **Bourdon Gauge:** mechanical pressure gauge

### Bernoulli's Equation

Energy conservation in fluid flow.

- **Bernoulli's Equation:** p/ρg + v²/2g + z = constant
- **Continuity Equation:** A1v1 = A2v2
- **Venturimeter:** measures flow rate

### Hydraulic Machines

Machines convert fluid energy.

- **Pump:** adds energy to fluid
- **Turbine:** extracts energy from fluid
- **Hydraulic Press:** force multiplication using Pascal's law

## Unit 4: Thermodynamics

### Basic Concepts

Systems exchange heat and work.

- **Thermodynamic System:** quantity of matter under study
- **Property:** measurable characteristic like p, V, T
- **Thermal Equilibrium:** no temperature difference

### Laws of Thermodynamics

Laws govern energy conversion.

- **Zeroth Law:** basis of temperature measurement
- **First Law:** energy conservation, Q − W = ΔU
- **Second Law:** heat cannot fully convert into work in a cycle

### Ideal Gas Processes

Gases follow simple relations.

- **Ideal Gas Equation:** pV = mRT
- **Isothermal Process:** constant temperature
- **Adiabatic Process:** no heat transfer; pV^γ = constant

### Heat Engines & Efficiency

Engines convert heat into work.

- **Heat Engine:** device converting heat into work
- **Carnot Efficiency:** 1 − T2/T1
- **Refrigerator:** transfers heat from cold to hot using work

## Unit 5: Steam Boilers & IC Engines

### Properties of Steam

Steam is a common working fluid.

- **Wet Steam:** mixture of water and vapour
- **Dry Saturated Steam:** no water particles
- **Superheated Steam:** heated above saturation temperature

### Steam Boilers

Boilers produce steam.

- **Fire-tube Boiler:** hot gases in tubes, e.g. Cochran
- **Water-tube Boiler:** water in tubes, e.g. Babcock and Wilcox
- **Boiler Mountings:** safety valves and gauges

### IC Engines

Engines burn fuel inside the cylinder.

- **Four-stroke Engine:** suction, compression, power, exhaust
- **Two-stroke Engine:** power stroke every revolution
- **Petrol vs Diesel:** spark ignition versus compression ignition

### Engine Performance Basics

Engines are rated by power and efficiency.

- **Indicated Power:** power in cylinder
- **Brake Power:** output at shaft
- **Mechanical Efficiency:** BP/IP
