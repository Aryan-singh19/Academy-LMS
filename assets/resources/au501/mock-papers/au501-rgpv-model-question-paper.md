## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Inline Engine: cylinders arranged in a single row
- **Q1(b):** Cylinder Block: casting containing cylinders and coolant passages | Cylinder Head: closes cylinders and holds valves | Cylinder Liner: replaceable wet or dry sleeve
- **Q2(a):** Lambda Sensor: measures exhaust oxygen for closed-loop control
- **Q2(b):** Inline Pump: separate plunger for each cylinder | Distributor Pump: single plunger distributing to cylinders | CRDI: common rail direct injection with electronic injectors
- **Q3(a):** OBD-II: on-board diagnostics standard for fault codes
- **Q3(b):** VVT: variable valve timing changing cam phase | VTEC: Honda system switching cam profiles | Cylinder Deactivation: shutting cylinders at light load
- **Q4(a):** Downsizing: smaller engines with turbocharging
- **Q4(b):** Torque Curve: engine torque versus speed | Brake Power: BP = 2πNT/60000 kW | Specific Power: power per litre of displacement
- **Q5(a):** Water Pump: circulates coolant
- **Q5(b):** Wet Sump: oil stored in engine sump | Dry Sump: separate oil tank for racing engines | SAE Grade: viscosity rating such as 10W-40
- **Q6(a):** Hydraulic Lifter: self-adjusting tappet removing valve clearance
- **Q6(b):** Inline Engine: cylinders arranged in a single row | V-Engine: two cylinder banks forming a V | Boxer Engine: horizontally opposed cylinders
- **Q7(a):** Detonation: autoignition of end gas
- **Q7(b):** Ignition Delay Period: time before combustion starts | Rapid Combustion: uncontrolled burning of accumulated fuel | Controlled Combustion: burning as fuel is injected
- **Q8(a):** Dry Sump: separate oil tank for racing engines
- **Q8(b):** Exhaust Manifold: collects gases from cylinders | Muffler: reduces exhaust noise | DPF: diesel particulate filter
