## Unit 1: Engine Construction

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Inline Engine*.  
  **Ans.** cylinders arranged in a single row
- **Q.** Define *V-Engine*.  
  **Ans.** two cylinder banks forming a V
- **Q.** Define *Boxer Engine*.  
  **Ans.** horizontally opposed cylinders
- **Q.** Define *Cylinder Block*.  
  **Ans.** casting containing cylinders and coolant passages
- **Q.** Define *Cylinder Head*.  
  **Ans.** closes cylinders and holds valves
- **Q.** Define *Cylinder Liner*.  
  **Ans.** replaceable wet or dry sleeve
- **Q.** Define *Piston*.  
  **Ans.** transmits gas force to connecting rod
- **Q.** Define *Compression Ring*.  
  **Ans.** seals combustion gases
- **Q.** Define *Oil Control Ring*.  
  **Ans.** scrapes excess oil from cylinder wall
- **Q.** Define *Crankshaft*.  
  **Ans.** converts reciprocating motion into rotation
- **Q.** Define *Overhead Camshaft*.  
  **Ans.** camshaft placed in the cylinder head
- **Q.** Define *Hydraulic Lifter*.  
  **Ans.** self-adjusting tappet removing valve clearance

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Engine Classification for Vehicles* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Automotive engines are classified by layout and cycle. Inline Engine — cylinders arranged in a single row; V-Engine — two cylinder banks forming a V; Boxer Engine — horizontally opposed cylinders.
- **Q.** Explain *Cylinder Block & Head* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Main structural parts house the combustion process. Cylinder Block — casting containing cylinders and coolant passages; Cylinder Head — closes cylinders and holds valves; Cylinder Liner — replaceable wet or dry sleeve.
- **Q.** Explain *Piston, Rings & Connecting Rod* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Reciprocating parts transmit gas force. Piston — transmits gas force to connecting rod; Compression Ring — seals combustion gases; Oil Control Ring — scrapes excess oil from cylinder wall.
- **Q.** Explain *Crankshaft, Camshaft & Valve Train* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Rotating parts convert and time motion. Crankshaft — converts reciprocating motion into rotation; Overhead Camshaft — camshaft placed in the cylinder head; Hydraulic Lifter — self-adjusting tappet removing valve clearance.

  **Ans.** removes contaminants
- **Q.** Define *Fuel Pressure Regulator*.  
  **Ans.** keeps injector pressure constant
- **Q.** Define *MAP Sensor*.  
  **Ans.** measures intake manifold pressure
- **Q.** Define *Lambda Sensor*.  
  **Ans.** measures exhaust oxygen for closed-loop control
- **Q.** Define *Throttle Body Injection*.  
  **Ans.** single injector above throttle
- **Q.** Define *Inline Pump*.  
  **Ans.** separate plunger for each cylinder
- **Q.** Define *Distributor Pump*.  
  **Ans.** single plunger distributing to cylinders
- **Q.** Define *CRDI*.  
  **Ans.** common rail direct injection with electronic injectors
- **Q.** Define *CNG Kit*.  
  **Ans.** cylinder, regulator and mixer or injectors
- **Q.** Define *LPG System*.  
  **Ans.** liquefied petroleum gas vaporiser and mixer
- **Q.** Define *Dual Fuel*.  
  **Ans.** diesel pilot ignites gaseous fuel

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Petrol Fuel Supply* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Fuel is stored, pumped and metered. Fuel Pump — delivers fuel from tank to engine; Fuel Filter — removes contaminants; Fuel Pressure Regulator — keeps injector pressure constant.
- **Q.** Explain *Electronic Fuel Injection* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Sensors and ECU meter fuel precisely. MAP Sensor — measures intake manifold pressure; Lambda Sensor — measures exhaust oxygen for closed-loop control; Throttle Body Injection — single injector above throttle.
- **Q.** Explain *Diesel Fuel Systems* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Diesel engines inject fuel at high pressure. Inline Pump — separate plunger for each cylinder; Distributor Pump — single plunger distributing to cylinders; CRDI — common rail direct injection with electronic injectors.
- **Q.** Explain *Alternative Fuel Systems* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Gaseous fuels need special systems. CNG Kit — cylinder, regulator and mixer or injectors; LPG System — liquefied petroleum gas vaporiser and mixer; Dual Fuel — diesel pilot ignites gaseous fuel.
