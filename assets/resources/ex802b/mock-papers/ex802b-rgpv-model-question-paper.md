## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Stepper Motor: converts pulses into steps
- **Q1(b):** Variable Reluctance Stepper: toothed soft-iron rotor | Permanent Magnet Stepper: magnet rotor | Hybrid Stepper: combines both
- **Q2(a):** Aligned Position: minimum reluctance
- **Q2(b):** Asymmetric Bridge Converter: two switches per phase | Commutation: switching phases in sequence | Current Chopping: controls phase current
- **Q3(a):** Interior PM: magnets embedded, adds reluctance torque
- **Q3(b):** PMSG: wind turbine generator | Axial Flux Machine: disc-shaped machine | Cogging Torque: torque ripple from slotting
- **Q4(a):** AC Servo Motor: two-phase control motor
- **Q4(b):** LIM: unrolled induction motor | End Effect: losses at entry and exit | Applications: maglev and conveyors
- **Q5(a):** Observer: estimates rotor position
- **Q5(b):** Traction Motor: PMSM or induction motor | Field Weakening: extends speed range | In-wheel Motor: motor inside wheel
- **Q6(a):** Resonance: vibration at certain step rates
- **Q6(b):** Stepper Motor: converts pulses into steps | Step Angle: 360°/(steps per revolution) | Holding Torque: torque at standstill when energised
- **Q7(a):** NdFeB Magnet: high energy rare-earth magnet
- **Q7(b):** BLDC Motor: trapezoidal back EMF | Hall Sensor: detects rotor position | Six-step Commutation: switching sequence
- **Q8(a):** Field Weakening: extends speed range
- **Q8(b):** More Electric Aircraft: electric actuators | Fault-tolerant Machine: continues operating with faults | Medical Drives: precision motors
