- **Q1(a):** Exactness Condition: ∂M/∂y = ∂N/∂x
- **Q1(b):** Linear Equation: dy/dx + Py = Q | Integrating Factor of Linear: e^(∫P dx) | Bernoulli Equation: dy/dx + Py = Qy^n
- **Q2(a):** PI for e^(ax): e^(ax)/f(a) if f(a) ≠ 0
- **Q2(b):** Variation of Parameters: uses Wronskian to find PI | Wronskian: W = y1y2' − y2y1' | Method of Undetermined Coefficients: guess PI form
- **Q3(a):** Standard Form f(p,q): 0 = z = ax + by + c
- **Q3(b):** Separation of Variables: assume u = X(x)T(t) | Heat Equation: ∂u/∂t = c²∂²u/∂x² | Wave Equation: ∂²u/∂t² = c²∂²u/∂x²
- **Q4(a):** Residue: coefficient of 1/(z − a) in Laurent series
- **Q4(b):** Analytic Function: differentiable in a neighbourhood | Cauchy-Riemann Equations: ux = vy, uy = −vx | Harmonic Function: satisfies Laplace's equation
- **Q5(a):** Divergence: ∇·F, measures outflow
- **Q5(b):** Directional Derivative: ∇φ·â | Maximum Directional Derivative: magnitude of gradient | Solenoidal Field: divergence zero
- **Q6(a):** RL Circuit: L di/dt + Ri = E
- **Q6(b):** Exactness Condition: ∂M/∂y = ∂N/∂x | Integrating Factor: multiplier making equation exact | Solution: ∫M dx + ∫(terms of N without x) dy = c
- **Q7(a):** PDE: equation with partial derivatives
- **Q7(b):** Lagrange's Equation: Pp + Qq = R | Subsidiary Equations: dx/P = dy/Q = dz/R | General Solution: φ(u, v) = 0
