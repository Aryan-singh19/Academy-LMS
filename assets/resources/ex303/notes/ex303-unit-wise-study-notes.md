# EX-303 Electrical Measurements & Measuring Instruments — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Galvanometers, indicating instruments, wattmeters, energy meters, bridges, instrument transformers and potentiometers.

## Unit 1: Measurement Fundamentals & Galvanometers

### Measurement Basics

Instruments are classified by operation and output.

- **Absolute Instrument:** gives value from constants, e.g. tangent galvanometer
- **Secondary Instrument:** calibrated against absolute instrument
- **Deflecting Torque:** torque moving the pointer

### Torques in Instruments

Three torques act in indicating instruments.

- **Controlling Torque:** spring or gravity torque opposing deflection
- **Damping Torque:** brings pointer to rest quickly
- **Eddy Current Damping:** damping by induced currents

### D'Arsonval Galvanometer

Moving coil galvanometer detects small currents.

- **D'Arsonval Galvanometer:** moving coil in permanent magnet field
- **Current Sensitivity:** deflection per unit current
- **Critical Damping Resistance:** resistance for critical damping

### Ballistic Galvanometer

Measures charge in short pulses.

- **Ballistic Galvanometer:** measures quantity of charge
- **Ballistic Throw:** first maximum deflection
- **Flux Meter:** special ballistic galvanometer for flux

## Unit 2: Indicating Instruments

### PMMC Instrument

Accurate DC measurement.

- **PMMC:** permanent magnet moving coil, linear scale
- **Torque Equation:** Td = NBAI
- **Swamping Resistance:** reduces temperature error

### Moving Iron Instruments

Rugged instruments for AC and DC.

- **Attraction Type:** iron drawn into coil
- **Repulsion Type:** two irons repel each other
- **Square Law Scale:** deflection proportional to I²

### Electrodynamometer Instruments

Fixed and moving coils interact.

- **Electrodynamometer:** no iron, accurate for AC
- **Transfer Instrument:** calibrated on DC, used on AC
- **Wattmeter Application:** current and pressure coils

### Extending Ranges

Shunts and multipliers extend range.

- **Shunt Resistance:** Rsh = Rm/(m − 1)
- **Multiplier:** Rs = Rm(m − 1)
- **Universal Shunt:** Ayrton shunt for multi-range ammeters

## Unit 3: Power & Energy Measurement

### Wattmeters

Wattmeters measure power.

- **Dynamometer Wattmeter:** current and potential coils
- **Low Power Factor Wattmeter:** for small power factors
- **Wattmeter Errors:** pressure coil inductance and connection errors

### Three-phase Power Measurement

Methods measure three-phase power.

- **Two Wattmeter Method:** P = W1 + W2 for three-wire systems
- **Power Factor from Two Wattmeters:** tan φ = √3(W1 − W2)/(W1 + W2)
- **Three Wattmeter Method:** for four-wire systems

### Energy Meters

Energy meters integrate power over time.

- **Induction Type Energy Meter:** rotating disc meter
- **Creeping:** disc rotation at no load
- **Meter Constant:** revolutions per kWh

### Electronic Energy Meters

Modern meters are digital.

- **Static Energy Meter:** electronic metering
- **Smart Meter:** two-way communication meter
- **Maximum Demand Indicator:** records peak demand

## Unit 4: Bridges & Potentiometers

### Measurement of Resistance

Methods depend on resistance range.

- **Wheatstone Bridge:** medium resistance
- **Kelvin Double Bridge:** low resistance
- **Megger:** insulation resistance tester

### AC Bridges

AC bridges measure L and C.

- **Maxwell's Bridge:** inductance measurement
- **Schering Bridge:** capacitance and dielectric loss
- **Wien's Bridge:** frequency measurement

### DC Potentiometer

Null method for accurate voltage.

- **DC Potentiometer:** compares unknown emf with standard
- **Standardisation:** calibrating against standard cell
- **Crompton Potentiometer:** laboratory DC potentiometer

### AC Potentiometer

Measures magnitude and phase.

- **Polar Potentiometer:** reads magnitude and angle
- **Coordinate Potentiometer:** reads in-phase and quadrature components
- **Drysdale Potentiometer:** polar type AC potentiometer

## Unit 5: Instrument Transformers & Digital Instruments

### Current Transformers

CTs step down current for meters.

- **Current Transformer:** primary in series with line
- **Ratio Error:** deviation from nominal ratio
- **Open-circuit Danger:** never open CT secondary under load

### Potential Transformers

PTs step down voltage.

- **Potential Transformer:** primary across line
- **Phase Angle Error:** angle between primary and reversed secondary
- **Burden:** load on secondary in VA

### Digital Instruments

Digital meters show numerical readings.

- **Digital Voltmeter:** ADC-based voltmeter
- **Digital Frequency Meter:** counts cycles in time gate
- **Resolution:** smallest change detectable

### Oscilloscope & Recorders

Waveforms are displayed and recorded.

- **CRO:** displays voltage waveforms
- **DSO:** digital storage oscilloscope
- **X-Y Recorder:** plots one variable against another
