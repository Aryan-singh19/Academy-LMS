## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Free Space Model: Friis equation Pr = PtGtGrλ²/(4πd)²
- **Q1(b):** Flat Fading: signal bandwidth smaller than coherence bandwidth | Frequency-selective Fading: bandwidth larger than coherence bandwidth | Fast Fading: channel changes within a symbol period
- **Q2(a):** Equal Gain Combining: co-phases and adds branches
- **Q2(b):** Linear Equaliser: FIR filter reversing channel | Decision Feedback Equaliser: uses past decisions to cancel ISI | MLSE: maximum-likelihood sequence estimation
- **Q3(a):** Spatial Multiplexing Gain: parallel data streams
- **Q3(b):** Alamouti Code: 2×1 space-time block code giving full diversity | Diversity Gain: slope improvement of BER curve | Massive MIMO: very large antenna arrays at base station
- **Q4(a):** NOMA: non-orthogonal multiple access using power domain
- **Q4(b):** FDMA: separate frequencies | TDMA: separate time slots | OFDMA: separate subcarrier groups
- **Q5(a):** 802.11ax: Wi-Fi 6 with OFDMA
- **Q5(b):** Bluetooth: 2.4 GHz WPAN with frequency hopping | Zigbee: low-power mesh networking | UWB: ultra-wideband short-range high-rate radio
- **Q6(a):** Jakes Model: classical Doppler spectrum model
- **Q6(b):** Free Space Model: Friis equation Pr = PtGtGrλ²/(4πd)² | Log-distance Model: path loss grows with 10n log(d) | Shadowing: log-normal variation due to obstacles
- **Q7(a):** OFDM: parallel orthogonal subcarriers implemented by IFFT/FFT
- **Q7(b):** PAPR: high peak-to-average power ratio | Carrier Frequency Offset: breaks subcarrier orthogonality | SC-FDMA: lower-PAPR uplink scheme in LTE
- **Q8(a):** Zigbee: low-power mesh networking
- **Q8(b):** LTE: 4G OFDMA-based standard | 5G NR: new radio supporting mmWave and low latency | Network Slicing: virtual networks tailored to services
