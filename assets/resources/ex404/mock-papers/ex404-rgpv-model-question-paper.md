- **Q2(b):** Capacitance: C = 2πε/ln(D/r) per conductor | Effect of Earth: increases capacitance | Bundled Conductors: reduce reactance and corona
- **Q3(a):** SIL: surge impedance loading V²/Zc
- **Q3(b):** Ferranti Effect: receiving voltage exceeds sending at no load | Shunt Reactor: compensates line charging | Series Capacitor: reduces line reactance
- **Q4(a):** Corona: ionisation when field exceeds breakdown
- **Q4(b):** Pin Insulator: up to 33 kV | Suspension Insulator: string of discs for high voltage | String Efficiency: V/(n × V of disc nearest conductor)
- **Q5(a):** Secondary Distribution: 415/230 V
- **Q5(b):** Radial System: single feed path | Ring Main System: closed loop for reliability | Interconnected System: multiple sources
- **Q6(a):** Two-part Tariff: fixed plus energy charges
- **Q6(b):** Power System: generation, transmission, distribution and load | Grid: interconnected network | Load Curve: load versus time
- **Q7(a):** Short Line: series impedance only
- **Q7(b):** Nominal T Method: capacitance at midpoint | Nominal π Method: capacitance split at ends | ABCD Constants: relate sending and receiving quantities
- **Q8(a):** Ring Main System: closed loop for reliability
- **Q8(b):** Uniformly Loaded Distributor: voltage drop at centre or end | Fed at Both Ends: reduces drop | Kelvin's Law: economical conductor size
