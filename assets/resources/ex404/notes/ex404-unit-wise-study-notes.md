# EX-404 Power System-I — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Power generation, transmission line parameters, performance, insulators, cables, corona and distribution.

## Unit 1: Power Generation

### Structure of Power System

Generation, transmission and distribution.

- **Power System:** generation, transmission, distribution and load
- **Grid:** interconnected network
- **Load Curve:** load versus time

### Thermal & Hydro Plants

Conventional generation sources.

- **Thermal Power Plant:** coal-fired steam plant
- **Hydro Power:** P = 9.81 Q H η kW
- **Pumped Storage:** stores energy by pumping water

### Nuclear & Renewable Generation

Alternative sources supply power.

- **Nuclear Plant:** fission heat produces steam
- **Solar PV Plant:** direct conversion of sunlight
- **Wind Farm:** group of wind turbines

### Load Factors & Tariffs

Economics of generation.

- **Load Factor:** average load / maximum demand
- **Diversity Factor:** sum of maximum demands / system maximum
- **Two-part Tariff:** fixed plus energy charges

## Unit 2: Transmission Line Parameters

### Resistance & Skin Effect

Conductor resistance depends on current distribution.

- **Skin Effect:** AC current concentrates near surface
- **ACSR:** aluminium conductor steel reinforced
- **Proximity Effect:** nearby conductors alter distribution

### Inductance

Lines have inductance due to flux linkage.

- **Inductance of Single-phase Line:** L = 4×10⁻⁷ ln(D/r') H/m
- **GMR:** geometric mean radius r' = 0.7788 r
- **Transposition:** equalises inductance of phases

### Capacitance

Lines have shunt capacitance.

- **Capacitance:** C = 2πε/ln(D/r) per conductor
- **Effect of Earth:** increases capacitance
- **Bundled Conductors:** reduce reactance and corona

### GMD & Composite Conductors

Geometric means simplify calculations.

- **GMD:** geometric mean distance
- **Composite Conductor:** stranded conductor
- **Double Circuit Line:** two circuits on same tower

## Unit 3: Transmission Line Performance

### Short Transmission Lines

Lines up to 80 km neglect capacitance.

- **Short Line:** series impedance only
- **Regulation:** (VS − VR)/VR
- **Efficiency:** output power / input power

### Medium Transmission Lines

Lines 80-250 km use lumped capacitance.

- **Nominal T Method:** capacitance at midpoint
- **Nominal π Method:** capacitance split at ends
- **ABCD Constants:** relate sending and receiving quantities

### Long Transmission Lines

Distributed parameters needed.

- **Propagation Constant:** γ = √(zy)
- **Surge Impedance:** Zc = √(z/y)
- **SIL:** surge impedance loading V²/Zc

### Ferranti Effect & Compensation

Light loads raise receiving voltage.

- **Ferranti Effect:** receiving voltage exceeds sending at no load
- **Shunt Reactor:** compensates line charging
- **Series Capacitor:** reduces line reactance

## Unit 4: Insulators, Cables & Corona

### Overhead Line Insulators

Insulators support conductors.

- **Pin Insulator:** up to 33 kV
- **Suspension Insulator:** string of discs for high voltage
- **String Efficiency:** V/(n × V of disc nearest conductor)

### Grading & Sag

Improving string efficiency and line clearance.

- **Guard Ring:** equalises voltage across discs
- **Sag:** W L²/(8T) for equal supports
- **Effect of Wind and Ice:** increases loading on conductor

### Underground Cables

Cables are used in cities.

- **Cable Construction:** conductor, insulation, sheath and armour
- **Insulation Resistance:** R = (ρ/2πl) ln(R/r)
- **Capacitance Grading:** layers of different permittivity

### Corona

Ionisation of air around conductors.

- **Corona:** ionisation when field exceeds breakdown
- **Disruptive Critical Voltage:** voltage at which corona starts
- **Corona Loss:** Peek's formula estimates loss

## Unit 5: Distribution Systems

### Distribution Classification

Distribution delivers power to consumers.

- **Primary Distribution:** 11 kV feeders
- **Secondary Distribution:** 415/230 V
- **Feeder:** line from substation without tappings

### Radial & Ring Systems

Layouts trade cost and reliability.

- **Radial System:** single feed path
- **Ring Main System:** closed loop for reliability
- **Interconnected System:** multiple sources

### DC & AC Distributors

Voltage drop calculations.

- **Uniformly Loaded Distributor:** voltage drop at centre or end
- **Fed at Both Ends:** reduces drop
- **Kelvin's Law:** economical conductor size

### Substations

Substations transform and switch power.

- **Substation:** equipment for voltage transformation
- **Busbar Arrangement:** single, double or ring bus
- **Earthing:** protection against fault currents
