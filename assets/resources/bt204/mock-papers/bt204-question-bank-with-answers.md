## Unit 4: Engineering Mechanics: Forces

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Coplanar Forces*.  
  **Ans.** forces in the same plane
- **Q.** Define *Resultant*.  
  **Ans.** single force equivalent to a system
- **Q.** Define *Parallelogram Law*.  
  **Ans.** resultant of two forces by diagonal
- **Q.** Define *Lami's Theorem*.  
  **Ans.** P/sin α = Q/sin β = R/sin γ for three concurrent forces
- **Q.** Define *Equilibrium Conditions*.  
  **Ans.** ΣFx = 0, ΣFy = 0, ΣM = 0
- **Q.** Define *Free Body Diagram*.  
  **Ans.** isolated body with all forces
- **Q.** Define *Moment*.  
  **Ans.** force × perpendicular distance
- **Q.** Define *Varignon's Theorem*.  
  **Ans.** moment of resultant equals sum of moments
- **Q.** Define *Couple*.  
  **Ans.** two equal, opposite, parallel forces
- **Q.** Define *Simple Support*.  
  **Ans.** provides vertical reaction
- **Q.** Define *Method of Joints*.  
  **Ans.** equilibrium at each truss joint
- **Q.** Define *Method of Sections*.  
  **Ans.** cutting truss to find member forces

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *System of Forces* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Forces are vectors acting on bodies. Coplanar Forces — forces in the same plane; Resultant — single force equivalent to a system; Parallelogram Law — resultant of two forces by diagonal.
- **Q.** Explain *Lami's Theorem & Equilibrium* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Bodies in equilibrium have zero resultant. Lami's Theorem — P/sin α = Q/sin β = R/sin γ for three concurrent forces; Equilibrium Conditions — ΣFx = 0, ΣFy = 0, ΣM = 0; Free Body Diagram — isolated body with all forces.
- **Q.** Explain *Moments & Couples* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Forces cause rotation. Moment — force × perpendicular distance; Varignon's Theorem — moment of resultant equals sum of moments; Couple — two equal, opposite, parallel forces.
- **Q.** Explain *Support Reactions & Trusses* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Beams and trusses are analysed for reactions. Simple Support — provides vertical reaction; Method of Joints — equilibrium at each truss joint; Method of Sections — cutting truss to find member forces.

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Coefficient of Friction*.  
  **Ans.** μ = F/N
- **Q.** Define *Angle of Friction*.  
  **Ans.** tan φ = μ
- **Q.** Define *Limiting Friction*.  
  **Ans.** maximum static friction
- **Q.** Define *Ladder Friction*.  
  **Ans.** equilibrium of ladder against wall
- **Q.** Define *Wedge*.  
  **Ans.** uses friction to lift loads
- **Q.** Define *Angle of Repose*.  
  **Ans.** equals angle of friction
- **Q.** Define *Centroid*.  
  **Ans.** point where area is concentrated
- **Q.** Define *Centroid of Triangle*.  
  **Ans.** h/3 from base
- **Q.** Define *Composite Area Centroid*.  
  **Ans.** x̄ = ΣAx/ΣA
- **Q.** Define *Moment of Inertia*.  
  **Ans.** ∫ y² dA
- **Q.** Define *Rectangle I*.  
  **Ans.** bd³/12
- **Q.** Define *Parallel Axis Theorem*.  
  **Ans.** I = Ig + Ah²

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Laws of Friction* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Friction opposes relative motion. Coefficient of Friction — μ = F/N; Angle of Friction — tan φ = μ; Limiting Friction — maximum static friction.
- **Q.** Explain *Applications of Friction* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Friction problems include ladders and wedges. Ladder Friction — equilibrium of ladder against wall; Wedge — uses friction to lift loads; Angle of Repose — equals angle of friction.
- **Q.** Explain *Centroid & Centre of Gravity* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Centroid locates the geometric centre. Centroid — point where area is concentrated; Centroid of Triangle — h/3 from base; Composite Area Centroid — x̄ = ΣAx/ΣA.
- **Q.** Explain *Moment of Inertia* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Area moment of inertia measures resistance to bending. Moment of Inertia — ∫ y² dA; Rectangle I — bd³/12; Parallel Axis Theorem — I = Ig + Ah².
