## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** First Class Brick: well burnt, standard size, water absorption below 20%
- **Q1(b):** OPC: ordinary Portland cement | Water-Cement Ratio: governs concrete strength | Concrete Grade: M20 means 20 N/mm² characteristic strength
- **Q2(a):** Whole Circle Bearing: measured clockwise from north 0-360°
- **Q2(b):** Benchmark: point of known elevation | Reduced Level: elevation relative to datum | Height of Instrument Method: RL = HI − staff reading
- **Q3(a):** Staircase: steps connecting floors
- **Q3(b):** Orientation: positioning building for sun and wind | FAR: floor area ratio | Setback: open space between building and boundary
- **Q4(a):** Simple Support: provides vertical reaction
- **Q4(b):** Coplanar Forces: forces in the same plane | Resultant: single force equivalent to a system | Parallelogram Law: resultant of two forces by diagonal
- **Q5(a):** Angle of Friction: tan φ = μ
- **Q5(b):** Ladder Friction: equilibrium of ladder against wall | Wedge: uses friction to lift loads | Angle of Repose: equals angle of friction
- **Q6(a):** Setting Time Test: Vicat apparatus for cement
- **Q6(b):** First Class Brick: well burnt, standard size, water absorption below 20% | Stone: natural material for masonry | Lime: binding material from limestone
- **Q7(a):** Shallow Foundation: depth less than width, e.g. isolated footing
- **Q7(b):** English Bond: alternate courses of headers and stretchers | Load-bearing Wall: carries loads from above | Damp Proof Course: layer preventing moisture rise
- **Q8(a):** Wedge: uses friction to lift loads
- **Q8(b):** Centroid: point where area is concentrated | Centroid of Triangle: h/3 from base | Composite Area Centroid: x̄ = ΣAx/ΣA
