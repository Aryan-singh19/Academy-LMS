## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Torque Capacity: clutch must transmit maximum engine torque
- **Q1(b):** Coil Spring Clutch: multiple coil springs press plate | Diaphragm Clutch: single diaphragm spring; compact | Multi-plate Wet Clutch: used in motorcycles
- **Q2(a):** Constant Mesh: dog clutches engage gears
- **Q2(b):** Geometric Progression: ratios in constant step | Top Gear Ratio: often 1:1 direct drive | Reverse Gear: idler gear reverses rotation
- **Q3(a):** Ravigneaux Gear Set: compound planetary set
- **Q3(b):** Valve Body: hydraulic control unit | TCU: transmission control unit | Shift Schedule: map of shift points by speed and throttle
- **Q4(a):** Single-speed Reduction: common in EVs
- **Q4(b):** CVT: continuously variable ratio transmission | Variable Pulley: pulley sheaves change effective diameter | Push Belt: steel belt transmitting torque
- **Q5(a):** Slip Joint: allows length change
- **Q5(b):** Hooke's Joint: universal joint with speed fluctuation | Constant Velocity Joint: transmits uniform speed at angles | Rzeppa Joint: ball-type CV joint
- **Q6(a):** Clutch Judder: vibration during engagement
- **Q6(b):** Torque Capacity: clutch must transmit maximum engine torque | Clutch Free Play: pedal travel before disengagement | Heat Dissipation: clutch must shed friction heat
- **Q7(a):** Fluid Coupling: impeller and turbine in oil
- **Q7(b):** Torque Converter: pump, turbine and stator | Stall Ratio: torque multiplication at turbine stall | Lock-up Clutch: mechanically locks converter at cruise
- **Q8(a):** Constant Velocity Joint: transmits uniform speed at angles
- **Q8(b):** Transfer Case: splits power to front and rear | Part-time 4WD: driver selects 4WD | AWD: all-wheel drive with centre differential
