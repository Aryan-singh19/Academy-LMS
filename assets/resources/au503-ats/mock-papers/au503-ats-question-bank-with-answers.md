## Unit 1: Clutches

- **Q.** Define *Torque Capacity*.  
  **Ans.** clutch must transmit maximum engine torque
- **Q.** Define *Clutch Free Play*.  
  **Ans.** pedal travel before disengagement
- **Q.** Define *Heat Dissipation*.  
  **Ans.** clutch must shed friction heat
- **Q.** Define *Coil Spring Clutch*.  
  **Ans.** multiple coil springs press plate
- **Q.** Define *Diaphragm Clutch*.  
  **Ans.** single diaphragm spring; compact
- **Q.** Define *Multi-plate Wet Clutch*.  
  **Ans.** used in motorcycles
- **Q.** Define *Clutch Torque*.  
  **Ans.** T = nμWR
- **Q.** Define *Uniform Wear Mean Radius*.  
  **Ans.** (r1 + r2)/2
- **Q.** Define *Friction Lining*.  
  **Ans.** material on clutch plate faces
- **Q.** Define *Hydraulic Clutch Actuation*.  
  **Ans.** master and slave cylinders
- **Q.** Define *Clutch Slip*.  
  **Ans.** clutch fails to transmit full torque
- **Q.** Define *Clutch Judder*.  
  **Ans.** vibration during engagement

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Clutch Requirements* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Clutch smoothly connects engine to gearbox. Torque Capacity — clutch must transmit maximum engine torque; Clutch Free Play — pedal travel before disengagement; Heat Dissipation — clutch must shed friction heat.

  **Key points:** Different clutches suit different vehicles. Coil Spring Clutch — multiple coil springs press plate; Diaphragm Clutch — single diaphragm spring; compact; Multi-plate Wet Clutch — used in motorcycles.
