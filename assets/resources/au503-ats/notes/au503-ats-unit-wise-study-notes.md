# AU-503(A) Automotive Transmission System — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Clutches, manual and automatic gearboxes, fluid drives, CVT, driveline and hybrid transmissions.

## Unit 1: Clutches

### Clutch Requirements

Clutch smoothly connects engine to gearbox.

- **Torque Capacity:** clutch must transmit maximum engine torque
- **Clutch Free Play:** pedal travel before disengagement
- **Heat Dissipation:** clutch must shed friction heat

### Clutch Types

Different clutches suit different vehicles.

- **Coil Spring Clutch:** multiple coil springs press plate
- **Diaphragm Clutch:** single diaphragm spring; compact
- **Multi-plate Wet Clutch:** used in motorcycles

### Clutch Torque Calculation

Torque depends on friction and pressure.

- **Clutch Torque:** T = nμWR
- **Uniform Wear Mean Radius:** (r1 + r2)/2
- **Friction Lining:** material on clutch plate faces

### Clutch Operation & Faults

Actuation and common problems.

- **Hydraulic Clutch Actuation:** master and slave cylinders
- **Clutch Slip:** clutch fails to transmit full torque
- **Clutch Judder:** vibration during engagement

## Unit 2: Manual Gearbox

### Need for Gearbox

Gearboxes match engine output to road load.

- **Gear Ratio:** input speed / output speed
- **Overdrive:** output faster than input
- **Tractive Effort Curve:** varies with gear selected

### Gearbox Types

Gear engagement methods evolved.

- **Sliding Mesh:** gears slide into engagement
- **Constant Mesh:** dog clutches engage gears
- **Synchromesh:** cone clutches synchronise speeds

### Gear Ratio Design

Ratios follow progression.

- **Geometric Progression:** ratios in constant step
- **Top Gear Ratio:** often 1:1 direct drive
- **Reverse Gear:** idler gear reverses rotation

### Gear Shift Mechanism

Linkages select gears.

- **Selector Fork:** moves sleeve or gear
- **Interlock:** prevents two gears engaging at once
- **Detent:** holds gear in position

## Unit 3: Fluid Drives & Automatic Transmission

### Fluid Coupling

Fluid coupling transmits torque without wear.

- **Fluid Coupling:** impeller and turbine in oil
- **Coupling Slip:** turbine runs slower than impeller
- **Coupling Efficiency:** turbine speed / impeller speed

### Torque Converter

Torque converter multiplies torque.

- **Torque Converter:** pump, turbine and stator
- **Stall Ratio:** torque multiplication at turbine stall
- **Lock-up Clutch:** mechanically locks converter at cruise

### Epicyclic Gear Sets

Planetary sets give multiple ratios.

- **Planetary Gear Set:** sun, planets, ring and carrier
- **Simpson Gear Set:** two planetary sets sharing sun
- **Ravigneaux Gear Set:** compound planetary set

### Automatic Transmission Control

Hydraulics and electronics shift gears.

- **Valve Body:** hydraulic control unit
- **TCU:** transmission control unit
- **Shift Schedule:** map of shift points by speed and throttle

## Unit 4: CVT, DCT & AMT

### Continuously Variable Transmission

CVT gives stepless ratios.

- **CVT:** continuously variable ratio transmission
- **Variable Pulley:** pulley sheaves change effective diameter
- **Push Belt:** steel belt transmitting torque

### Dual Clutch Transmission

DCT shifts quickly without power loss.

- **DCT:** two clutches for odd and even gears
- **Pre-selection:** next gear engaged in advance
- **Wet vs Dry DCT:** oil-cooled or dry clutches

### Automated Manual Transmission

AMT automates clutch and shift.

- **AMT:** manual gearbox with electro-hydraulic actuators
- **Torque Interruption:** brief power cut during shift
- **Cost Advantage:** cheaper than full automatic

### Electric & Hybrid Transmissions

Electrified drivetrains need new transmissions.

- **Single-speed Reduction:** common in EVs
- **Power-split Device:** planetary set in hybrids
- **e-CVT:** electrically controlled CVT in hybrids

## Unit 5: Driveline

### Propeller Shaft

Shaft transmits torque to axle.

- **Propeller Shaft:** tubular shaft with joints
- **Slip Joint:** allows length change
- **Whirling:** critical speed of long shafts

### Universal & CV Joints

Joints allow angular movement.

- **Hooke's Joint:** universal joint with speed fluctuation
- **Constant Velocity Joint:** transmits uniform speed at angles
- **Rzeppa Joint:** ball-type CV joint

### Transfer Case & 4WD

4WD distributes torque to both axles.

- **Transfer Case:** splits power to front and rear
- **Part-time 4WD:** driver selects 4WD
- **AWD:** all-wheel drive with centre differential

### Driveline Vibration & Losses

Driveline must be smooth and efficient.

- **Driveline Efficiency:** power at wheels / engine power
- **Torsional Damper:** reduces driveline vibration
- **Dual Mass Flywheel:** isolates engine torsional vibration
