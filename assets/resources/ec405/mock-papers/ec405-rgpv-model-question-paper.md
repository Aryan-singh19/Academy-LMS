## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Cascade Gain: overall gain is the product of stage gains
- **Q1(b):** Lower Cut-off Frequency: fL = 1/(2πRC) of dominant capacitor | Bypass Capacitor: shunts emitter resistor for AC | Half-power Point: frequency where gain drops by 3 dB
- **Q2(a):** Current-Shunt Feedback: decreases input, increases output impedance
- **Q2(b):** Bandwidth Improvement: bandwidth multiplied by (1 + Aβ) | Distortion Reduction: non-linear distortion divided by (1 + Aβ) | Noise Reduction: internal noise reduced by feedback
- **Q3(a):** Clapp Oscillator: Colpitts with series capacitor for stability
- **Q3(b):** Piezoelectric Effect: mechanical stress produces voltage and vice versa | Series Resonance: crystal acts as low impedance | High Q: crystals have Q of thousands, giving stability
- **Q4(a):** Single-tuned Amplifier: one LC tank load
- **Q4(b):** Class A: conducts 360°; max efficiency 25% (50% transformer coupled) | Class B: conducts 180°; max efficiency 78.5% | Class C: conducts less than 180°; used in RF
- **Q5(a):** CMRR: ratio of differential gain to common-mode gain
- **Q5(b):** Inverting Amplifier: gain −Rf/R1 | Non-inverting Amplifier: gain 1 + Rf/R1 | Voltage Follower: unity-gain buffer
- **Q6(a):** Bootstrapping: raises input impedance using positive feedback
- **Q6(b):** Cascade Gain: overall gain is the product of stage gains | RC Coupling: stages coupled by capacitors for audio amplifiers | Direct Coupling: no coupling capacitors, passes DC
- **Q7(a):** Oscillator: amplifier with positive feedback sustaining oscillation
- **Q7(b):** RC Phase Shift Oscillator: three RC sections give 180° shift; f = 1/(2πRC√6) | Wien Bridge Oscillator: f = 1/(2πRC); needs gain of 3 | Audio Frequency Range: RC oscillators suit low frequencies
- **Q8(a):** Non-inverting Amplifier: gain 1 + Rf/R1
- **Q8(b):** Integrator: output proportional to integral of input | Differentiator: output proportional to derivative of input | Instrumentation Amplifier: high-CMRR differential amplifier using three op-amps
