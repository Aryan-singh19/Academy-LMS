## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Fossil Fuel Depletion: finite crude oil reserves
- **Q1(b):** Calorific Value: energy released per kg of fuel | Flash Point: lowest temperature where vapour ignites | Stoichiometric Ratio: chemically correct air-fuel ratio
- **Q2(a):** Cold Starting Problem: high latent heat of alcohols
- **Q2(b):** Biodiesel: fatty acid methyl esters from oils | Transesterification: reaction of oil with alcohol and catalyst | B20: 20% biodiesel blend with diesel
- **Q3(a):** Scrubbing: removes CO2 and H2S from biogas
- **Q3(b):** PEM Fuel Cell: proton exchange membrane cell for vehicles | FCEV: fuel cell electric vehicle | Fuel Cell Efficiency: higher than IC engines
- **Q4(a):** CO Toxicity: binds with haemoglobin
- **Q4(b):** CO Formation: incomplete combustion in rich mixtures | HC Formation: unburned fuel from crevices and wall quenching | NOx Formation: high-temperature reaction of N2 and O2
- **Q5(a):** EGR: recirculated exhaust lowers combustion temperature
- **Q5(b):** Catalytic Converter: platinum, palladium and rhodium catalysts | DOC: diesel oxidation catalyst | SCR with AdBlue: urea solution reduces NOx
- **Q6(a):** CNG Programme: city gas distribution networks
- **Q6(b):** Fossil Fuel Depletion: finite crude oil reserves | Energy Security: reducing oil import dependence | Fuel Properties: calorific value, octane or cetane, density
- **Q7(a):** CNG: compressed natural gas, mainly methane, stored at about 200 bar
- **Q7(b):** Hydrogen: zero-carbon fuel with wide flammability | Hydrogen Storage: compressed, liquid or metal hydride | Backfire: flashback problem in hydrogen engines
- **Q8(a):** DOC: diesel oxidation catalyst
- **Q8(b):** NDIR Analyser: measures CO and CO2 | FID: flame ionisation detector for hydrocarbons | Chemiluminescence Analyser: measures NOx
