# AU-503(C) Alternate Automotive Fuels & Emissions — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Alternative fuels, their properties and engine adaptation, emission formation, measurement and control, norms.

## Unit 1: Need for Alternative Fuels

### Energy Crisis & Fuel Scenario

Oil dependence drives alternative fuels.

- **Fossil Fuel Depletion:** finite crude oil reserves
- **Energy Security:** reducing oil import dependence
- **Fuel Properties:** calorific value, octane or cetane, density

### Fuel Quality Parameters

Properties determine suitability.

- **Calorific Value:** energy released per kg of fuel
- **Flash Point:** lowest temperature where vapour ignites
- **Stoichiometric Ratio:** chemically correct air-fuel ratio

### Classification of Alternative Fuels

Fuels are liquid or gaseous.

- **Liquid Alternative Fuels:** alcohols and biodiesel
- **Gaseous Fuels:** CNG, LPG, hydrogen and biogas
- **Renewable Fuel:** produced from biomass

### Fuel Policy in India

Policies promote alternatives.

- **Ethanol Blending Programme:** blending ethanol with petrol, e.g. E20
- **National Biofuel Policy:** promotes biofuels
- **CNG Programme:** city gas distribution networks

## Unit 2: Alcohols & Biodiesel

### Alcohol Fuels

Ethanol and methanol suit SI engines.

- **Ethanol:** alcohol fuel from sugarcane or grains
- **Methanol:** alcohol from natural gas or coal
- **High Octane:** alcohols resist knock

### Alcohol in Engines

Engines need modification for alcohols.

- **Flex Fuel Vehicle:** runs on variable ethanol blends
- **Cold Starting Problem:** high latent heat of alcohols
- **Material Compatibility:** alcohols corrode some metals and rubbers

### Biodiesel

Vegetable oils converted for diesel engines.

- **Biodiesel:** fatty acid methyl esters from oils
- **Transesterification:** reaction of oil with alcohol and catalyst
- **B20:** 20% biodiesel blend with diesel

### Vegetable Oils & Biodiesel Issues

Straight oils have problems.

- **High Viscosity:** poor atomisation of raw vegetable oil
- **Cold Flow Properties:** biodiesel gels in cold
- **Lower NOx Trade-off:** biodiesel may raise NOx

## Unit 3: Gaseous Fuels

### CNG & LPG

Gaseous fuels are cleaner than liquids.

- **CNG:** compressed natural gas, mainly methane, stored at about 200 bar
- **LPG:** mixture of propane and butane stored as liquid
- **Bi-fuel Vehicle:** runs on petrol or gas

### Hydrogen as Fuel

Hydrogen burns without carbon.

- **Hydrogen:** zero-carbon fuel with wide flammability
- **Hydrogen Storage:** compressed, liquid or metal hydride
- **Backfire:** flashback problem in hydrogen engines

### Biogas & Producer Gas

Gases from biomass.

- **Biogas:** methane and CO2 from digestion
- **Producer Gas:** CO-rich gas from biomass gasification
- **Scrubbing:** removes CO2 and H2S from biogas

### Fuel Cells for Vehicles

Fuel cells convert hydrogen directly to electricity.

- **PEM Fuel Cell:** proton exchange membrane cell for vehicles
- **FCEV:** fuel cell electric vehicle
- **Fuel Cell Efficiency:** higher than IC engines

## Unit 4: Emission Formation

### Pollutants from SI Engines

SI engines emit CO, HC and NOx.

- **CO Formation:** incomplete combustion in rich mixtures
- **HC Formation:** unburned fuel from crevices and wall quenching
- **NOx Formation:** high-temperature reaction of N2 and O2

### Pollutants from CI Engines

Diesel emits soot and NOx.

- **Particulate Matter:** carbon soot with adsorbed hydrocarbons
- **Smoke:** visible particulates
- **NOx-PM Trade-off:** reducing one tends to increase the other

### Evaporative & Crankcase Emissions

Non-exhaust emissions exist.

- **Evaporative Emissions:** fuel vapour from tank
- **Blow-by Gases:** gases leaking past rings into crankcase
- **PCV Valve:** positive crankcase ventilation

### Effects of Pollutants

Pollutants harm health and environment.

- **CO Toxicity:** binds with haemoglobin
- **Photochemical Smog:** from NOx and HC in sunlight
- **Greenhouse Gas:** CO2 contributes to warming

## Unit 5: Emission Control & Norms

### Engine Modifications

Design changes reduce emissions.

- **Lean Burn:** excess air lowers CO and HC
- **EGR:** recirculated exhaust lowers combustion temperature
- **Injection Timing Retard:** reduces NOx in diesel engines

### Aftertreatment Devices

Exhaust treatment removes pollutants.

- **Catalytic Converter:** platinum, palladium and rhodium catalysts
- **DOC:** diesel oxidation catalyst
- **SCR with AdBlue:** urea solution reduces NOx

### Emission Measurement

Instruments measure exhaust pollutants.

- **NDIR Analyser:** measures CO and CO2
- **FID:** flame ionisation detector for hydrocarbons
- **Chemiluminescence Analyser:** measures NOx

### Emission Norms

Standards limit vehicle emissions.

- **Bharat Stage Norms:** Indian emission standards
- **BS-VI:** equivalent to Euro VI, effective 2020
- **Driving Cycle:** standard test cycle, e.g. MIDC
