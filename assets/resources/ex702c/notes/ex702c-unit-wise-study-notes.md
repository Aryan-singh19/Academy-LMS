# EX-702(C) High Voltage Engineering — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Breakdown in gases, liquids and solids, generation and measurement of high voltages, overvoltages and HV testing.

## Unit 1: Breakdown in Gases

### Ionisation Processes

Gases break down by ionisation.

- **Townsend's First Ionisation Coefficient:** electrons produced per unit length
- **Townsend's Second Coefficient:** secondary emission
- **Townsend Criterion:** γ(e^(αd) − 1) = 1

### Paschen's Law

Breakdown voltage depends on pd.

- **Paschen's Law:** Vb = f(pd)
- **Paschen Minimum:** lowest breakdown voltage point
- **Streamer Theory:** breakdown in long gaps

### Corona Discharge

Partial breakdown near conductors.

- **Corona:** localised ionisation
- **Positive Corona:** from positive electrode
- **Corona Inception Voltage:** start of corona

### SF6 & Vacuum Insulation

Special media insulate equipment.

- **SF6 Gas:** electronegative insulating gas
- **Vacuum Breakdown:** field emission mechanisms
- **GIS:** gas insulated substation

## Unit 2: Breakdown in Liquids & Solids

### Liquid Dielectrics

Oils insulate transformers.

- **Transformer Oil:** mineral insulating oil
- **Suspended Particle Theory:** impurities bridge gap
- **Cavitation Theory:** bubbles cause breakdown

### Solid Dielectrics

Solids fail by several mechanisms.

- **Intrinsic Breakdown:** electronic breakdown in pure solids
- **Thermal Breakdown:** heating from dielectric loss
- **Electromechanical Breakdown:** compression by field

### Treeing & Tracking

Long-term degradation.

- **Electrical Treeing:** branching channels in insulation
- **Tracking:** conductive path on surface
- **Partial Discharge:** local breakdown in voids

### Composite Insulation

Combinations of materials.

- **Composite Insulator:** polymer housing with FRP core
- **Insulation Ageing:** degradation over time
- **Dielectric Strength:** maximum field without breakdown

## Unit 3: Generation of High Voltages

### HV AC Generation

Transformers produce HV AC.

- **Cascaded Transformers:** series connection for high AC
- **Resonant Transformer:** uses resonance to produce HV
- **Tesla Coil:** high-frequency high voltage

### HV DC Generation

Multipliers produce HV DC.

- **Cockcroft-Walton Multiplier:** voltage multiplier circuit
- **Van de Graaff Generator:** electrostatic generator
- **Ripple:** variation in DC output

### Impulse Voltage Generation

Impulses simulate lightning.

- **Standard Lightning Impulse:** 1.2/50 µs waveform
- **Marx Generator:** charges capacitors in parallel, discharges in series
- **Switching Impulse:** 250/2500 µs waveform

### Impulse Current Generation

Large currents test arresters.

- **Impulse Current Generator:** capacitor bank discharge
- **8/20 µs Wave:** standard current impulse
- **Applications:** testing surge arresters

## Unit 4: Measurement of High Voltages

### Sphere Gaps

Sphere gaps measure peak voltage.

- **Sphere Gap:** breakdown voltage depends on gap and diameter
- **Air Density Correction:** correction for temperature and pressure
- **Accuracy:** within 3%

### Voltage Dividers

Dividers scale down voltages.

- **Resistive Divider:** for DC and AC
- **Capacitive Divider:** for AC and impulses
- **Mixed Divider:** for impulse measurement

### Electrostatic Voltmeter

Measures RMS voltage directly.

- **Electrostatic Voltmeter:** force between electrodes
- **Generating Voltmeter:** rotating electrode type
- **Peak Voltmeter:** measures crest value

### Current & PD Measurement

HV currents and discharges are measured.

- **Rogowski Coil:** measures impulse currents
- **Partial Discharge Measurement:** detects insulation defects
- **Shunt:** low resistance current measurement

## Unit 5: Overvoltages & HV Testing

### Lightning Overvoltages

Lightning strikes induce surges.

- **Direct Stroke:** lightning hits conductor
- **Induced Stroke:** nearby lightning induces voltage
- **Shielding Wire:** earth wire protecting conductors

### Switching Overvoltages

Switching causes transients.

- **Switching Surge:** due to breaker operations
- **Travelling Waves:** surges propagating along lines
- **Reflection Coefficient:** at line discontinuities

### Insulation Coordination

Matching insulation to protection.

- **BIL:** basic insulation level
- **Protective Margin:** BIL above arrester protective level
- **Surge Arrester:** metal oxide arrester

### HV Testing

Equipment is tested before service.

- **Power Frequency Withstand Test:** one-minute AC test
- **Impulse Test:** lightning impulse withstand
- **Type Test:** tests on representative sample
