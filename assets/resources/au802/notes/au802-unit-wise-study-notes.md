# AU-802 Automotive Safety & Legislation — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Active and passive safety, crash dynamics, occupant protection, motor vehicle law, homologation and type approval.

## Unit 1: Introduction to Vehicle Safety

### Road Safety Scenario

Road accidents are a major public health issue.

- **Road Accident Statistics:** India records over 1.5 lakh road deaths a year
- **Haddon Matrix:** pre-crash, crash and post-crash factors
- **Safe System Approach:** designing roads and vehicles to forgive mistakes

### Active vs Passive Safety

Safety systems prevent or mitigate crashes.

- **Active Safety:** systems preventing accidents, e.g. ABS
- **Passive Safety:** systems reducing injury, e.g. airbags
- **Tertiary Safety:** post-crash measures such as eCall

### Human Factors

Driver behaviour causes most crashes.

- **Reaction Time:** time to respond to hazard
- **Driver Fatigue:** reduced alertness
- **Distracted Driving:** attention diverted from road

### Vehicle Conspicuity

Vehicles must be visible.

- **Daytime Running Lights:** improve visibility
- **Retro-reflective Tape:** for commercial vehicles
- **Conspicuity:** ease of being seen

## Unit 2: Active Safety Systems

### Braking Safety Systems

Advanced brakes improve control.

- **ABS:** maintains steering control in braking
- **EBD:** balances brake force
- **Brake Assist:** increases braking in emergencies

### Stability & Traction

Systems keep vehicle stable.

- **ESC:** prevents skidding
- **TCS:** traction control system
- **Hill Hold Assist:** prevents rollback

### ADAS

Driver assistance prevents collisions.

- **AEB:** autonomous emergency braking
- **Lane Departure Warning:** alerts lane drift
- **Blind Spot Detection:** warns of vehicles in blind spots

### Tyre & Visibility Safety

Tyres and lights matter.

- **TPMS:** tyre pressure monitoring system
- **Adaptive Lighting:** headlamps follow curves
- **Rain Sensing Wipers:** automatic wiping

## Unit 3: Passive Safety & Crashworthiness

### Crash Dynamics

Crashes involve rapid deceleration.

- **Crash Pulse:** deceleration versus time
- **Energy Absorption:** structure absorbs kinetic energy
- **Occupant Ride-down:** slowing occupant with vehicle

### Crashworthy Structure

Structures protect occupants.

- **Crumple Zone:** deforms to absorb energy
- **Safety Cell:** rigid occupant compartment
- **Load Path:** route of crash forces through structure

### Occupant Protection

Restraints reduce injury.

- **Seat Belt:** primary restraint
- **Load Limiter:** limits belt force on chest
- **Side Curtain Airbag:** protects head in side impact

### Injury Criteria

Injury risk is quantified.

- **HIC:** head injury criterion
- **Crash Test Dummy:** anthropomorphic test device
- **Chest Deflection:** measure of thoracic injury

## Unit 4: Motor Vehicle Legislation

### Motor Vehicles Act

Law governs vehicles and drivers in India.

- **Motor Vehicles Act 1988:** main road transport law in India
- **MV Amendment Act 2019:** increased penalties and safety rules
- **Driving Licence:** authorisation to drive specific vehicle classes

### Central Motor Vehicle Rules

Rules set technical requirements.

- **CMVR:** central motor vehicle rules
- **Registration:** mandatory vehicle registration
- **Fitness Requirement:** periodic fitness for transport vehicles

### Emission & Safety Regulations

Regulations mandate standards.

- **AIS Standards:** automotive industry standards
- **Mandatory Airbags:** driver and passenger airbags required
- **Speed Limiting Device:** mandatory in certain commercial vehicles

### Insurance & Liability

Insurance protects road users.

- **Third-party Insurance:** mandatory insurance covering others
- **Comprehensive Insurance:** covers own vehicle too
- **Motor Accident Claims Tribunal:** adjudicates compensation

## Unit 5: Homologation & Testing

### Homologation

Vehicles must be certified before sale.

- **Homologation:** certification that vehicle meets regulations
- **Type Approval:** approval of a vehicle model
- **COP:** conformity of production checks

### Testing Agencies

Agencies certify vehicles in India.

- **ARAI:** Automotive Research Association of India
- **ICAT:** International Centre for Automotive Technology
- **NATRAX:** high-speed test track in Indore

### International Standards

Global standards harmonise rules.

- **UNECE Regulations:** international vehicle regulations
- **FMVSS:** US federal motor vehicle safety standards
- **Euro NCAP:** European safety rating

### Recall & Product Liability

Defects must be fixed.

- **Vehicle Recall:** manufacturer fixes safety defect
- **Product Liability:** manufacturer responsibility for defects
- **Voluntary Recall Code:** industry recall policy
