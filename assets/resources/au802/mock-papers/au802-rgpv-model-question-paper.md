- **Q3(a):** Side Curtain Airbag: protects head in side impact
- **Q3(b):** HIC: head injury criterion | Crash Test Dummy: anthropomorphic test device | Chest Deflection: measure of thoracic injury
- **Q4(a):** Third-party Insurance: mandatory insurance covering others
- **Q4(b):** Motor Vehicles Act 1988: main road transport law in India | MV Amendment Act 2019: increased penalties and safety rules | Driving Licence: authorisation to drive specific vehicle classes
- **Q5(a):** Type Approval: approval of a vehicle model
- **Q5(b):** ARAI: Automotive Research Association of India | ICAT: International Centre for Automotive Technology | NATRAX: high-speed test track in Indore
- **Q6(a):** Conspicuity: ease of being seen
- **Q6(b):** Road Accident Statistics: India records over 1.5 lakh road deaths a year | Haddon Matrix: pre-crash, crash and post-crash factors | Safe System Approach: designing roads and vehicles to forgive mistakes
