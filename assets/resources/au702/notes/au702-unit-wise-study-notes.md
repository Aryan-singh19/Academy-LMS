# AU-703 Vehicle Body Engineering & Aerodynamics — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Car body design, materials, safety, aerodynamics, bus and commercial vehicle bodies.

## Unit 1: Car Body Design

### Body Types

Car bodies vary by purpose.

- **Sedan:** three-box body with separate boot
- **Hatchback:** rear door including window
- **SUV:** sport utility vehicle with high ground clearance

### Body Construction

Bodies are built from pressed panels.

- **Body-in-White:** welded body shell before painting
- **Monocoque Construction:** body carries all loads
- **Crumple Zone:** designed to absorb crash energy

### Visibility & Ergonomics

Design ensures driver comfort and vision.

- **H-point:** hip point of seated occupant
- **Driver Visibility:** field of view and blind spots
- **Ergonomic Layout:** placement of controls for reach

### Body Materials

Materials balance weight and cost.

- **High Strength Steel:** reduces weight while keeping strength
- **Aluminium Body:** lighter but costlier
- **Composite Panels:** plastics and fibre composites

## Unit 2: Vehicle Aerodynamics

### Aerodynamic Forces

Air exerts drag, lift and side forces.

- **Drag Force:** ½ρV²ACd
- **Lift Force:** vertical aerodynamic force
- **Side Force:** crosswind force

### Drag Reduction

Shape optimisation reduces drag.

- **Pressure Drag:** due to flow separation
- **Boat Tailing:** tapering rear to reduce wake
- **Underbody Panel:** smooths underbody flow

### Aerodynamic Devices

Devices modify flow.

- **Spoiler:** disrupts airflow to reduce lift
- **Rear Wing:** generates downforce
- **Air Dam:** reduces underbody airflow

### Wind Tunnel Testing

Testing validates design.

- **Wind Tunnel:** facility for aerodynamic tests
- **Scale Model Testing:** reduced-scale models
- **CFD:** computational fluid dynamics simulation

## Unit 3: Body Safety

### Passive Safety

Structures protect occupants in crashes.

- **Passive Safety:** protection during a crash
- **Side Impact Beam:** door beam reducing intrusion
- **Safety Cage:** rigid passenger compartment

### Crash Testing

Crash tests rate safety.

- **Frontal Offset Test:** crash with partial overlap
- **NCAP:** new car assessment programme
- **Bharat NCAP:** Indian crash rating programme

### Restraint Systems

Restraints hold occupants.

- **Seat Belt Pretensioner:** tightens belt in crash
- **Airbag:** inflatable cushion
- **ISOFIX:** child seat anchor system

### Pedestrian Safety

Design reduces pedestrian injuries.

- **Soft Bumper:** reduces leg injury
- **Pop-up Bonnet:** raises bonnet for head protection
- **Bonnet Clearance:** space above engine

## Unit 4: Bus & Commercial Vehicle Bodies

### Bus Body Types

Buses vary by application.

- **City Bus:** low floor for easy boarding
- **Coach:** long-distance comfort bus
- **Mini Bus:** small capacity bus

### Bus Body Construction

Buses use frames and panels.

- **Bus Chassis:** ladder frame with body built on it
- **Integral Bus:** self-supporting body structure
- **Bus Body Code:** AIS-052 standard in India

### Commercial Vehicle Bodies

Trucks have specialised bodies.

- **Tipper Body:** tilting body for unloading
- **Container Body:** enclosed cargo box
- **Tanker Body:** carries liquids

### Driver Cabin Design

Cabins ensure comfort and safety.

- **Cab-over Design:** cabin above engine
- **Conventional Cab:** engine ahead of cabin
- **Sleeper Cab:** cabin with sleeping berth

## Unit 5: Body Manufacturing & Finishing

### Panel Manufacturing

Panels are pressed and joined.

- **Press Forming:** stamping body panels
- **Spot Welding:** primary joining method
- **Laser Welding:** precise high-speed joining

### Painting Process

Paint protects and decorates.

- **Pretreatment:** cleaning and phosphating
- **E-coat:** electrophoretic primer coating
- **Clear Coat:** top protective layer

### Corrosion Protection

Bodies must resist rust.

- **Galvanising:** zinc coating on steel
- **Underbody Coating:** protects from stones and moisture
- **Cavity Wax:** protects hollow sections

### Interior Trim & NVH

Interior design addresses noise.

- **NVH:** noise, vibration and harshness
- **Sound Deadening:** materials reducing noise
- **Interior Trim:** dashboard, seats and panels
