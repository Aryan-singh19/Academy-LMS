## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Normal Stress: σ = P/A
- **Q1(b):** Young's Modulus: ratio of normal stress to strain | Modulus of Rigidity: G = shear stress / shear strain | Relation of Constants: E = 2G(1 + ν) = 3K(1 − 2ν)
- **Q2(a):** Bending Moment: algebraic sum of moments on one side
- **Q2(b):** Point of Contraflexure: location where bending moment is zero and changes sign | Maximum Bending Moment: occurs where shear force is zero | Overhanging Beam: beam extending beyond its support
- **Q3(a):** Circular Section: maximum shear 4/3 times average
- **Q3(b):** Eccentric Load: load offset from centroid | Combined Stress: σ = P/A ± M/Z | Middle Third Rule: no tension if load lies within middle third
- **Q4(a):** Moment Area Method: slope change equals area of M/EI diagram
- **Q4(b):** Torsion Equation: T/J = τ/r = Gθ/L | Polar Moment of Inertia: J = πd⁴/32 for solid shaft | Power Transmitted: P = 2πNT/60
- **Q5(a):** Effective Length: depends on end conditions; Le = L for both ends hinged
- **Q5(b):** Rankine's Formula: 1/P = 1/Pc + 1/Pe | Crushing Load: σc·A for short columns | Rankine Constant: α depends on material
- **Q6(a):** Impact Loading: suddenly applied load gives twice the static stress
- **Q6(b):** Normal Stress: σ = P/A | Hooke's Law: σ = Eε within the elastic limit | Poisson's Ratio: lateral strain / longitudinal strain
- **Q7(a):** Bending Equation: M/I = σ/y = E/R
- **Q7(b):** Rectangle I: bd³/12 about centroidal axis | Parallel Axis Theorem: I = Ig + Ah² | Circular Section I: πd⁴/64
- **Q8(a):** Crushing Load: σc·A for short columns
- **Q8(b):** Hoop Stress: σh = pd/(2t) for thin cylinder | Longitudinal Stress: σl = pd/(4t) | Lame's Equations: stresses in thick cylinders
