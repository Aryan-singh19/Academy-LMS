# BT-101 Engineering Chemistry — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Water treatment, fuels, lubricants, polymers, corrosion, spectroscopy and instrumental methods.

## Unit 1: Water Analysis & Treatment

### Hardness of Water

Dissolved calcium and magnesium salts make water hard.

- **Temporary Hardness:** due to bicarbonates; removed by boiling
- **Permanent Hardness:** due to chlorides and sulphates of Ca and Mg
- **Hardness Unit:** expressed as mg/L (ppm) of CaCO3 equivalent

### EDTA Method & Alkalinity

Titrations measure hardness and alkalinity.

- **EDTA Titration:** complexometric method using Eriochrome Black-T indicator
- **Alkalinity:** due to OH⁻, CO3²⁻ and HCO3⁻ ions
- **Phenolphthalein Alkalinity:** alkalinity measured up to pH 8.3

### Boiler Troubles

Hard water causes problems in boilers.

- **Scale and Sludge:** deposits reducing heat transfer
- **Priming and Foaming:** carry-over of water droplets with steam
- **Caustic Embrittlement:** boiler metal cracking due to NaOH

### Softening Methods

Hardness is removed by chemical or ion exchange processes.

- **Lime-Soda Process:** adds lime and soda to precipitate hardness
- **Zeolite Process:** sodium zeolite exchanges Na⁺ for Ca²⁺ and Mg²⁺
- **Ion Exchange:** cation and anion resins give demineralised water

## Unit 2: Fuels & Combustion

### Classification of Fuels

Fuels release heat by combustion.

- **Calorific Value:** heat released by complete combustion of unit mass
- **HCV vs LCV:** gross value includes latent heat of steam formed
- **Bomb Calorimeter:** measures calorific value of solid and liquid fuels

### Coal Analysis

Coal quality is judged by analysis.

- **Proximate Analysis:** moisture, volatile matter, ash and fixed carbon
- **Ultimate Analysis:** C, H, N, S and O percentages
- **Carbonisation:** heating coal without air to make coke

### Petroleum & Cracking

Crude oil is refined into fuels.

- **Fractional Distillation:** separates crude oil by boiling range
- **Cracking:** breaking large hydrocarbons into smaller ones
- **Octane Number:** knock resistance of petrol

### Combustion Calculations

Air needed for combustion is calculated.

- **Theoretical Air:** minimum air for complete combustion
- **Excess Air:** air supplied beyond theoretical
- **Flue Gas Analysis:** Orsat apparatus measures CO2, CO and O2

## Unit 3: Lubricants & Polymers

### Lubricants & Mechanisms

Lubricants reduce friction and wear.

- **Fluid Film Lubrication:** thick film separates surfaces
- **Boundary Lubrication:** thin film when load is high
- **Extreme Pressure Lubrication:** additives form protective films

### Properties of Lubricants

Tests characterise lubricants.

- **Viscosity Index:** change of viscosity with temperature
- **Flash and Fire Point:** temperatures where vapours ignite
- **Cloud and Pour Point:** low-temperature flow properties

### Polymers

Polymers are long chain molecules.

- **Addition Polymerisation:** monomers add without by-product, e.g. polyethylene
- **Condensation Polymerisation:** monomers join with loss of small molecules, e.g. nylon
- **Thermosetting Polymer:** sets permanently on heating, e.g. bakelite

### Rubber & Composites

Elastomers and engineered polymers.

- **Vulcanisation:** cross-linking rubber with sulphur
- **Conducting Polymer:** polymer that conducts electricity, e.g. polyaniline
- **Biodegradable Polymer:** decomposes naturally

## Unit 4: Corrosion & Electrochemistry

### Electrochemical Cells

Chemical energy is converted into electricity.

- **Galvanic Cell:** spontaneous redox reaction produces current
- **Nernst Equation:** E = E° − (0.0591/n) log Q at 25 °C
- **Standard Electrode Potential:** potential versus standard hydrogen electrode

### Batteries & Fuel Cells

Practical electrochemical energy sources.

- **Primary Cell:** non-rechargeable, e.g. dry cell
- **Secondary Cell:** rechargeable, e.g. lead-acid and Li-ion
- **Hydrogen-Oxygen Fuel Cell:** produces electricity and water

### Corrosion Mechanisms

Metals degrade by reaction with environment.

- **Dry Corrosion:** direct chemical attack by gases
- **Wet Corrosion:** electrochemical corrosion in moisture
- **Galvanic Corrosion:** when two dissimilar metals contact in electrolyte

### Corrosion Prevention

Corrosion is controlled by design and coatings.

- **Cathodic Protection:** making the metal a cathode
- **Galvanising:** coating iron with zinc
- **Sacrificial Anode:** more active metal corrodes instead

## Unit 5: Instrumental Methods

### Spectroscopy Basics

Matter interacts with electromagnetic radiation.

- **Beer-Lambert Law:** A = εcl
- **UV-Visible Spectroscopy:** electronic transitions in molecules
- **IR Spectroscopy:** identifies functional groups by vibrations

### Chromatography

Mixtures are separated by differential migration.

- **Chromatography:** separation between mobile and stationary phases
- **Rf Value:** distance by solute / distance by solvent
- **Gas Chromatography:** separation of volatile compounds

### Thermal Analysis

Heating reveals material properties.

- **TGA:** thermogravimetric analysis measures mass change
- **DTA:** differential thermal analysis
- **DSC:** differential scanning calorimetry

### Green Chemistry & Nanomaterials

Sustainable chemistry and nanoscale materials.

- **Green Chemistry:** designing processes to reduce hazardous substances
- **Atom Economy:** fraction of reactant atoms in desired product
- **Nanomaterial:** material with dimensions 1-100 nm
