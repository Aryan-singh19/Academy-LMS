# EX-402 Electrical Machine-I — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Magnetic circuits, single-phase and three-phase transformers, autotransformers and three-phase induction motors.

## Unit 1: Magnetic Circuits & Transformer Basics

### Magnetic Circuits

Magnetic circuits guide flux.

- **Reluctance:** S = l/(μA)
- **Hysteresis Loss:** Ph = η Bmax^1.6 f V
- **Eddy Current Loss:** Pe ∝ B² f² t²

### Transformer Principle

Mutual induction transfers energy.

- **Transformer:** static device changing AC voltage
- **EMF Equation:** E = 4.44 f N Φm
- **Transformation Ratio:** K = N2/N1

### Ideal & Practical Transformer

Practical transformers have losses and leakage.

- **Ideal Transformer:** no losses or leakage
- **Leakage Reactance:** due to flux not linking both windings
- **Magnetising Current:** current producing core flux

### Construction

Core and winding arrangements.

- **Core Type:** windings surround core limbs
- **Shell Type:** core surrounds windings
- **Transformer Oil:** cooling and insulation

## Unit 2: Transformer Performance

### Equivalent Circuit

Circuit models predict performance.

- **Equivalent Circuit:** referred to primary or secondary
- **Referred Impedance:** Z' = Z/K²
- **Approximate Equivalent Circuit:** shunt branch moved to terminals

### Open & Short Circuit Tests

Tests find parameters.

- **Open Circuit Test:** gives core loss and shunt parameters
- **Short Circuit Test:** gives copper loss and series impedance
- **Sumpner's Test:** back-to-back test for heat run

### Voltage Regulation

Output voltage changes with load.

- **Voltage Regulation:** (E2 − V2)/V2
- **Regulation Approximation:** I(R cos φ ± X sin φ)/V
- **Zero Regulation:** possible at leading power factor

### Efficiency

Losses decide efficiency.

- **Efficiency:** output/(output + losses)
- **Maximum Efficiency Condition:** copper loss equals iron loss
- **All-day Efficiency:** energy output / energy input over 24 h

## Unit 3: Three-phase Transformers & Autotransformers

### Three-phase Connections

Windings are connected in star or delta.

- **Star-Delta Connection:** used for step-down at substations
- **Vector Group:** phase displacement notation, e.g. Dyn11
- **Open Delta:** V-V connection with 57.7% capacity

### Parallel Operation

Transformers share load in parallel.

- **Conditions for Parallel Operation:** same ratio, polarity, phase sequence and vector group
- **Load Sharing:** inversely proportional to impedance
- **Circulating Current:** flows when ratios differ

### Autotransformer

Single winding with tap.

- **Autotransformer:** single winding transformer
- **Copper Saving:** (1 − K) fraction saved
- **Application:** motor starting and voltage regulation

### Special Transformers

Transformers for special purposes.

- **Scott Connection:** three-phase to two-phase
- **Tap Changer:** adjusts turns ratio
- **Welding Transformer:** high current low voltage

## Unit 4: Three-phase Induction Motor

### Construction & Principle

Rotating field induces rotor currents.

- **Rotating Magnetic Field:** produced by three-phase currents
- **Squirrel Cage Rotor:** bars short-circuited by end rings
- **Slip Ring Rotor:** wound rotor with external resistance

### Slip & Rotor Quantities

Rotor frequency depends on slip.

- **Slip:** s = (Ns − N)/Ns
- **Rotor Frequency:** f2 = s f
- **Rotor EMF:** sE2

### Torque Equation

Torque depends on slip and rotor parameters.

- **Torque:** T ∝ s E2² R2/(R2² + (sX2)²)
- **Maximum Torque Condition:** R2 = sX2
- **Starting Torque:** maximum when R2 = X2

### Power Flow

Power splits into losses and output.

- **Rotor Input:** Pg
- **Rotor Copper Loss:** s × Pg
- **Mechanical Power:** (1 − s) Pg

## Unit 5: Induction Motor Performance & Control

### Equivalent Circuit & Tests

Tests find motor parameters.

- **No-load Test:** finds core and mechanical losses
- **Blocked Rotor Test:** finds short-circuit parameters
- **Circle Diagram:** graphical performance prediction

### Starting Methods

Starters limit starting current.

- **DOL Starter:** direct on line for small motors
- **Star-Delta Starter:** reduces starting current to 1/3
- **Rotor Resistance Starter:** for slip ring motors

### Speed Control

Speed is varied by several methods.

- **Stator Voltage Control:** changes torque-speed curve
- **Frequency Control:** V/f control
- **Pole Changing:** changes synchronous speed

### Induction Generator & Braking

Machine can generate and brake.

- **Induction Generator:** runs above synchronous speed
- **Plugging:** reversing phase sequence to brake
- **Cogging and Crawling:** abnormal starting phenomena
