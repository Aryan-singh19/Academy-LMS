- **Q4(a):** Rotor Input: Pg

- **Q5(a):** Blocked Rotor Test: finds short-circuit parameters
- **Q5(b):** DOL Starter: direct on line for small motors | Star-Delta Starter: reduces starting current to 1/3 | Rotor Resistance Starter: for slip ring motors

- **Q6(b):** Reluctance: S = l/(μA) | Hysteresis Loss: Ph = η Bmax^1.6 f V | Eddy Current Loss: Pe ∝ B² f² t²
