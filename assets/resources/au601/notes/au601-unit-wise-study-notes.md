# AU-601 Automotive Electrical & Electronics — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Batteries, charging, starting, ignition, lighting, sensors, actuators, ECUs and in-vehicle networks.

## Unit 1: Batteries & Charging

### Lead-acid Battery

The starting battery supplies electrical energy.

- **Lead-acid Battery:** PbO2 positive, Pb negative plates in sulphuric acid
- **Specific Gravity Test:** measures state of charge
- **Ampere-hour Capacity:** current × hours a battery can supply

### Maintenance-free & Advanced Batteries

Newer batteries reduce maintenance.

- **MF Battery:** sealed battery with calcium alloy grids
- **AGM Battery:** absorbed glass mat for start-stop vehicles
- **Lithium-ion Battery:** high energy density battery for EVs

### Alternator & Charging System

Alternators recharge the battery.

- **Alternator:** AC generator with rectifier
- **Rectifier Bridge:** diodes converting AC to DC
- **Voltage Regulator:** keeps output near 14 V

### Charging System Faults

Faults cause flat batteries.

- **Undercharging:** battery not fully recharged
- **Overcharging:** excessive voltage damages battery
- **Charging Warning Lamp:** indicates charging failure

## Unit 2: Starting & Ignition Systems

### Starting System

Starter motor cranks the engine.

- **Starter Motor:** DC series motor with high starting torque
- **Bendix Drive:** inertia drive engaging pinion
- **Pre-engaged Starter:** solenoid engages pinion before rotation

### Conventional Ignition

Coil ignition produces high voltage.

- **Ignition Coil:** transformer stepping up to kilovolts
- **Contact Breaker:** switches primary current
- **Condenser:** reduces arcing at contact points

### Electronic & Distributorless Ignition

Electronics improve spark timing.

- **Transistorised Ignition:** transistor switches coil current
- **DIS:** distributorless ignition with coil packs
- **Coil-on-plug:** separate coil on each spark plug

### Spark Plugs

Spark plugs ignite the mixture.

- **Spark Plug Gap:** electrode gap setting
- **Heat Range:** ability to dissipate heat
- **Plug Fouling:** deposits preventing sparking

## Unit 3: Lighting & Accessories

### Lighting System

Lights ensure visibility and signalling.

- **Headlamp:** high and low beam lighting
- **LED Headlamp:** efficient long-life lighting
- **Adaptive Headlamp:** beam turns with steering

### Signalling & Instrumentation

Indicators and gauges inform driver.

- **Flasher Unit:** makes turn indicators blink
- **Instrument Cluster:** speedometer, tachometer and warning lamps
- **Fuel Gauge:** indicates fuel level using sender unit

### Wiring & Protection

Wiring harness distributes power safely.

- **Wiring Harness:** bundle of vehicle wires
- **Fuse:** protects circuit by melting at overload
- **Relay:** small current switches large current

### Comfort Accessories

Electrical accessories improve convenience.

- **Power Window:** motor-driven window regulator
- **Central Locking:** locks all doors together
- **Wiper Motor:** drives windscreen wipers

## Unit 4: Sensors & Actuators

### Engine Sensors

Sensors feed the ECU.

- **Crankshaft Position Sensor:** gives engine speed and position
- **Coolant Temperature Sensor:** thermistor measuring coolant
- **Throttle Position Sensor:** potentiometer on throttle

### Exhaust & Air Sensors

Sensors measure air and exhaust conditions.

- **Oxygen Sensor:** measures oxygen in exhaust
- **MAF Sensor:** measures mass of intake air
- **Knock Sensor:** piezoelectric sensor detecting knock

### Chassis Sensors

Sensors support safety systems.

- **Wheel Speed Sensor:** used by ABS
- **Yaw Rate Sensor:** used by stability control
- **Steering Angle Sensor:** measures steering input

### Actuators

Actuators carry out ECU commands.

- **Fuel Injector:** solenoid valve spraying fuel
- **Idle Air Control Valve:** controls idle speed
- **Electronic Throttle:** motor-driven throttle body

## Unit 5: Vehicle Electronics & Networks

### Electronic Control Unit

ECUs process sensor data.

- **ECU:** microcontroller-based controller
- **Look-up Table:** maps stored in ECU
- **Limp Home Mode:** reduced function on sensor failure

### In-vehicle Networks

ECUs communicate over buses.

- **CAN Bus:** robust serial bus for vehicle ECUs
- **LIN Bus:** low-cost bus for simple devices
- **FlexRay:** high-speed deterministic bus

### Safety Electronics

Electronics enhance safety.

- **ABS:** prevents wheel lock-up
- **ESC:** electronic stability control
- **Airbag Control Unit:** deploys airbags on crash

### Diagnostics & ADAS

Diagnostics and driver assistance.

- **OBD Scanner:** reads diagnostic trouble codes
- **ADAS:** advanced driver assistance systems
- **Adaptive Cruise Control:** maintains distance using radar
