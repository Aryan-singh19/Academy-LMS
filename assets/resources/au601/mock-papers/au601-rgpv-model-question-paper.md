## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Lead-acid Battery: PbO2 positive, Pb negative plates in sulphuric acid
- **Q1(b):** MF Battery: sealed battery with calcium alloy grids | AGM Battery: absorbed glass mat for start-stop vehicles | Lithium-ion Battery: high energy density battery for EVs
- **Q2(a):** Contact Breaker: switches primary current
- **Q2(b):** Transistorised Ignition: transistor switches coil current | DIS: distributorless ignition with coil packs | Coil-on-plug: separate coil on each spark plug
- **Q3(a):** Relay: small current switches large current
- **Q3(b):** Power Window: motor-driven window regulator | Central Locking: locks all doors together | Wiper Motor: drives windscreen wipers
- **Q4(a):** Fuel Injector: solenoid valve spraying fuel
- **Q4(b):** Crankshaft Position Sensor: gives engine speed and position | Coolant Temperature Sensor: thermistor measuring coolant | Throttle Position Sensor: potentiometer on throttle
- **Q5(a):** Look-up Table: maps stored in ECU
- **Q5(b):** CAN Bus: robust serial bus for vehicle ECUs | LIN Bus: low-cost bus for simple devices | FlexRay: high-speed deterministic bus
- **Q6(a):** Charging Warning Lamp: indicates charging failure
- **Q6(b):** Lead-acid Battery: PbO2 positive, Pb negative plates in sulphuric acid | Specific Gravity Test: measures state of charge | Ampere-hour Capacity: current × hours a battery can supply
- **Q7(a):** Headlamp: high and low beam lighting
- **Q7(b):** Flasher Unit: makes turn indicators blink | Instrument Cluster: speedometer, tachometer and warning lamps | Fuel Gauge: indicates fuel level using sender unit
- **Q8(a):** LIN Bus: low-cost bus for simple devices
- **Q8(b):** ABS: prevents wheel lock-up | ESC: electronic stability control | Airbag Control Unit: deploys airbags on crash
