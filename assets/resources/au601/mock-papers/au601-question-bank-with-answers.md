- **Q.** Define *Transistorised Ignition*.

- **Q.** Define *Spark Plug Gap*.

- **Q.** Define *Heat Range*.

- **Q.** Define *Plug Fouling*.

- **Q.** Explain *Starting System* in detail with a neat diagram.
