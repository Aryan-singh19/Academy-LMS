- **Q2(b):** Stokes' First Problem: suddenly started plate | Stokes' Second Problem: oscillating plate | Penetration Depth: grows as √(νt)

- **Q3(b):** Thermal Boundary Layer: temperature varies near wall | Prandtl Number Effect: ratio of momentum to thermal diffusivity | Reynolds Analogy: links friction and heat transfer

- **Q4(b):** Turbulence: irregular, diffusive, dissipative flow | Transition: laminar to turbulent change | Energy Cascade: energy passes from large to small eddies
