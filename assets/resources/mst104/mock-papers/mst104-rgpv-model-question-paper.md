- **Q3(a):** Effective Modal Mass: mass participating in mode
- **Q3(b):** Rayleigh Damping: C = αM + βK | Modal Damping: damping ratio per mode | Non-classical Damping: cannot be diagonalised
- **Q4(a):** SSI: interaction between structure and soil
