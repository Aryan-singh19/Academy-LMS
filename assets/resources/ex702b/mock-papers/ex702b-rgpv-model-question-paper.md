## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** HVDC: high-voltage direct current transmission
- **Q1(b):** Monopolar Link: single conductor with earth return | Bipolar Link: two conductors of opposite polarity | Back-to-back Link: converters at same site
- **Q2(a):** Inverter Operation: extinction angle γ controlled
- **Q2(b):** Constant Current Control: at rectifier | Constant Extinction Angle Control: at inverter | Power Reversal: by reversing DC voltage
- **Q3(a):** TCR: thyristor controlled reactor
- **Q3(b):** TCSC: thyristor controlled series capacitor | SSSC: static synchronous series compensator | Subsynchronous Resonance: risk with series compensation
- **Q4(a):** Characteristic Harmonics: 12k ± 1 for 12-pulse
- **Q4(b):** UPFC: unified power flow controller | Shunt and Series Converters: share DC link | Independent P and Q Control: key UPFC feature
- **Q5(a):** Green Energy Corridor: Indian RE transmission
- **Q5(b):** MTDC: multi-terminal DC system | DC Circuit Breaker: interrupts DC fault currents | DC Grid: meshed DC network
- **Q6(a):** Raigarh-Pugalur: large VSC-HVDC
- **Q6(b):** HVDC: high-voltage direct current transmission | Break-even Distance: about 600-800 km for overhead lines | Asynchronous Link: connects grids of different frequency
- **Q7(a):** Reactive Power: Q = V I sin φ
- **Q7(b):** FACTS: flexible AC transmission systems | Power Transfer Equation: P = (V1V2/X) sin δ | Controllable Parameters: voltage, impedance and angle
- **Q8(a):** DC Circuit Breaker: interrupts DC fault currents
- **Q8(b):** DSTATCOM: distribution STATCOM | DVR: dynamic voltage restorer | Custom Power: power quality improvement devices
