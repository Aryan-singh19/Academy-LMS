## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** SI Engine: spark ignition engine working on Otto cycle
- **Q1(b):** Bore: cylinder diameter | Stroke: distance piston travels between dead centres | Compression Ratio: (Vs + Vc)/Vc
- **Q2(a):** GDI: gasoline direct injection into cylinder
- **Q2(b):** Fuel Injection Pump: delivers metered high-pressure fuel | Common Rail: high-pressure accumulator feeding injectors | Injector Nozzle: atomises fuel into spray
- **Q3(a):** Higher Octane: allows higher compression ratio
- **Q3(b):** Squish: radial inward air motion at TDC | Swirl: rotational air motion improving mixing | Direct Injection Chamber: open chamber for CI engines
- **Q4(a):** Supercharging: compressing intake air with engine-driven compressor
- **Q4(b):** Indicated Power: power developed in cylinder | Brake Power: power at the crankshaft | Mechanical Efficiency: BP / IP
- **Q5(a):** Pressure Lubrication: oil pump forces oil to bearings
- **Q5(b):** Air Cooling: fins dissipate heat | Water Cooling: coolant circulated through jackets and radiator | Thermostat: controls coolant flow to maintain temperature
- **Q6(a):** Exhaust Blowdown Loss: loss when exhaust valve opens early
- **Q6(b):** SI Engine: spark ignition engine working on Otto cycle | CI Engine: compression ignition engine working on Diesel cycle | Two-stroke Engine: one power stroke per revolution
- **Q7(a):** Ignition Lag: delay between spark and flame development
- **Q7(b):** Ignition Delay: time between injection and start of combustion | Diesel Knock: sudden combustion of accumulated fuel | Cetane Number: ignition quality of diesel fuel
- **Q8(a):** Water Cooling: coolant circulated through jackets and radiator
- **Q8(b):** CO: carbon monoxide from rich mixtures | NOx: oxides of nitrogen at high temperatures | Particulate Matter: soot mainly from diesel engines
