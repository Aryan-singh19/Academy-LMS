## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Viscosity: resistance to shear; τ = μ du/dy
- **Q1(b):** Pascal's Law: pressure at a point is equal in all directions | Hydrostatic Law: dp/dz = −ρg | U-tube Manometer: measures pressure using liquid columns
- **Q2(a):** Continuity Equation: A1V1 = A2V2 for incompressible flow
- **Q2(b):** Velocity Potential: φ with u = −∂φ/∂x | Stream Function: ψ, constant along streamlines | Flow Net: grid of streamlines and equipotential lines
- **Q3(a):** Jet Impact on Plate: F = ρaV² on a fixed flat plate
- **Q3(b):** Rectangular Notch: Q = (2/3)Cd·L·√(2g)·H^(3/2) | V-notch: Q = (8/15)Cd·tan(θ/2)√(2g)H^(5/2) | Weir: notch on a large scale
- **Q4(a):** Water Hammer: pressure rise from sudden stopping of flow
- **Q4(b):** Hagen-Poiseuille Equation: Δp = 32μVL/D² | Reynolds Number: Re = ρVD/μ; laminar below about 2000 | Velocity Profile: parabolic in laminar pipe flow
- **Q5(a):** Boundary Layer Thickness: distance where u = 0.99U
- **Q5(b):** Flow Separation: boundary layer detaches from surface | Drag Force: FD = CD·½ρV²A | Lift Force: force perpendicular to flow direction
- **Q6(a):** Metacentre: point about which a floating body oscillates
- **Q6(b):** Viscosity: resistance to shear; τ = μ du/dy | Newtonian Fluid: shear stress proportional to velocity gradient | Surface Tension: force per unit length at a liquid surface
- **Q7(a):** Euler's Equation: dp/ρ + V dV + g dz = 0
- **Q7(b):** Venturimeter: measures discharge using pressure difference | Pitot Tube: measures velocity from stagnation pressure | Orifice Meter: simple plate meter with higher losses
- **Q8(a):** Drag Force: FD = CD·½ρV²A
- **Q8(b):** Buckingham π Theorem: n variables and m dimensions give n − m π groups | Rayleigh's Method: finds relation using exponents of variables | Dimensional Homogeneity: each term has same dimensions
