# BT-201 Engineering Physics — Question Bank with Answer Hints

## Unit 1: Quantum Physics

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *de Broglie Wavelength*.  
  **Ans.** λ = h/p
- **Q.** Define *Photoelectric Effect*.  
  **Ans.** emission of electrons by light above threshold frequency
- **Q.** Define *Compton Effect*.  
  **Ans.** increase in wavelength of X-rays scattered by electrons
- **Q.** Define *Uncertainty Principle*.  
  **Ans.** Δx·Δp ≥ h/(4π)
- **Q.** Define *Energy-Time Uncertainty*.  
  **Ans.** ΔE·Δt ≥ h/(4π)
- **Q.** Define *Consequence*.  
  **Ans.** electrons cannot exist inside nucleus
- **Q.** Define *Time-independent Schrödinger Equation*.  
  **Ans.** −(ħ²/2m)∇²ψ + Vψ = Eψ
- **Q.** Define *Wave Function*.  
  **Ans.** ψ, with probability density given by its square
- **Q.** Define *Normalisation*.  
  **Ans.** total probability equals 1
- **Q.** Define *Particle in 1D Box*.  
  **Ans.** En = n²h²/(8mL²)
- **Q.** Define *Quantisation*.  
  **Ans.** energy has discrete values
- **Q.** Define *Zero-point Energy*.  
  **Ans.** lowest energy is non-zero

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Wave-Particle Duality* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Matter and radiation show both wave and particle nature. de Broglie Wavelength — λ = h/p; Photoelectric Effect — emission of electrons by light above threshold frequency; Compton Effect — increase in wavelength of X-rays scattered by electrons.
- **Q.** Explain *Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Position and momentum cannot both be exact. Uncertainty Principle — Δx·Δp ≥ h/(4π); Energy-Time Uncertainty — ΔE·Δt ≥ h/(4π); Consequence — electrons cannot exist inside nucleus.
- **Q.** Explain *Schrödinger Wave Equation* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Wave function describes quantum systems. Time-independent Schrödinger Equation — −(ħ²/2m)∇²ψ + Vψ = Eψ; Wave Function — ψ, with probability density given by its square; Normalisation — total probability equals 1.
- **Q.** Explain *Particle in a Box* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Quantum confinement gives discrete energies. Particle in 1D Box — En = n²h²/(8mL²); Quantisation — energy has discrete values; Zero-point Energy — lowest energy is non-zero.

## Unit 2: Wave Optics

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Coherent Sources*.  
  **Ans.** constant phase difference
- **Q.** Define *Young's Double Slit*.  
  **Ans.** fringe width β = λD/d
- **Q.** Define *Constructive Interference*.  
  **Ans.** path difference nλ
- **Q.** Define *Thin Film Interference*.  
  **Ans.** reflections from film surfaces interfere
- **Q.** Define *Newton's Rings*.  
  **Ans.** dark ring diameter D² = 4nλR
- **Q.** Define *Wedge-shaped Film*.  
  **Ans.** straight equally spaced fringes
- **Q.** Define *Fraunhofer Diffraction*.  
  **Ans.** source and screen at infinity
- **Q.** Define *Single Slit Minima*.  
  **Ans.** a sin θ = nλ
- **Q.** Define *Diffraction Grating*.  
  **Ans.** (a + b) sin θ = nλ
- **Q.** Define *Malus's Law*.  
  **Ans.** I = I0 cos²θ
- **Q.** Define *Brewster's Law*.  
  **Ans.** tan θp = μ
- **Q.** Define *Double Refraction*.  
  **Ans.** splitting into ordinary and extraordinary rays

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Interference* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Superposition of coherent waves gives fringes. Coherent Sources — constant phase difference; Young's Double Slit — fringe width β = λD/d; Constructive Interference — path difference nλ.
- **Q.** Explain *Thin Film & Newton's Rings* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Thin films produce colours and rings. Thin Film Interference — reflections from film surfaces interfere; Newton's Rings — dark ring diameter D² = 4nλR; Wedge-shaped Film — straight equally spaced fringes.
- **Q.** Explain *Diffraction* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Bending of light around obstacles. Fraunhofer Diffraction — source and screen at infinity; Single Slit Minima — a sin θ = nλ; Diffraction Grating — (a + b) sin θ = nλ.
- **Q.** Explain *Polarisation* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Light vibrations restricted to one plane. Malus's Law — I = I0 cos²θ; Brewster's Law — tan θp = μ; Double Refraction — splitting into ordinary and extraordinary rays.
