## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** de Broglie Wavelength: λ = h/p
- **Q1(b):** Uncertainty Principle: Δx·Δp ≥ h/(4π) | Energy-Time Uncertainty: ΔE·Δt ≥ h/(4π) | Consequence: electrons cannot exist inside nucleus
- **Q2(a):** Newton's Rings: dark ring diameter D² = 4nλR
- **Q2(b):** Fraunhofer Diffraction: source and screen at infinity | Single Slit Minima: a sin θ = nλ | Diffraction Grating: (a + b) sin θ = nλ
- **Q3(a):** LIDAR: laser-based distance measurement
- **Q3(b):** Numerical Aperture: NA = √(n1² − n2²) | Acceptance Angle: maximum angle for guided light | Step-index Fibre: abrupt change of refractive index
- **Q4(a):** Intrinsic Semiconductor: pure semiconductor
- **Q4(b):** Unit Cell: smallest repeating unit | Bravais Lattices: 14 types of lattices | Miller Indices: notation for crystal planes
- **Q5(a):** Mass Defect: difference between nucleon mass sum and nuclear mass
- **Q5(b):** Half-life: time for half the nuclei to decay | Decay Law: N = N0 e^(−λt) | Alpha, Beta, Gamma: types of radioactive emission
- **Q6(a):** Zero-point Energy: lowest energy is non-zero
- **Q6(b):** de Broglie Wavelength: λ = h/p | Photoelectric Effect: emission of electrons by light above threshold frequency | Compton Effect: increase in wavelength of X-rays scattered by electrons
- **Q7(a):** Stimulated Emission: incident photon triggers identical photon
- **Q7(b):** Ruby Laser: three-level solid-state laser | He-Ne Laser: four-level gas laser at 632.8 nm | Semiconductor Laser: diode laser
- **Q8(a):** Decay Law: N = N0 e^(−λt)
- **Q8(b):** Fission: splitting of heavy nucleus | Fusion: combining light nuclei, powering the Sun | Chain Reaction: self-sustaining fission
