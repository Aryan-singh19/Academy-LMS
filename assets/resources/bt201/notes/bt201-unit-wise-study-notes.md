# BT-201 Engineering Physics — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Quantum mechanics, wave optics, lasers, fibre optics, solid state physics and nuclear physics.

## Unit 1: Quantum Physics

### Wave-Particle Duality

Matter and radiation show both wave and particle nature.

- **de Broglie Wavelength:** λ = h/p
- **Photoelectric Effect:** emission of electrons by light above threshold frequency
- **Compton Effect:** increase in wavelength of X-rays scattered by electrons

### Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle

Position and momentum cannot both be exact.

- **Uncertainty Principle:** Δx·Δp ≥ h/(4π)
- **Energy-Time Uncertainty:** ΔE·Δt ≥ h/(4π)
- **Consequence:** electrons cannot exist inside nucleus

### Schrödinger Wave Equation

Wave function describes quantum systems.

- **Time-independent Schrödinger Equation:** −(ħ²/2m)∇²ψ + Vψ = Eψ
- **Wave Function:** ψ, with probability density given by its square
- **Normalisation:** total probability equals 1

### Particle in a Box

Quantum confinement gives discrete energies.

- **Particle in 1D Box:** En = n²h²/(8mL²)
- **Quantisation:** energy has discrete values
- **Zero-point Energy:** lowest energy is non-zero

## Unit 2: Wave Optics

### Interference

Superposition of coherent waves gives fringes.

- **Coherent Sources:** constant phase difference
- **Young's Double Slit:** fringe width β = λD/d
- **Constructive Interference:** path difference nλ

### Thin Film & Newton's Rings

Thin films produce colours and rings.

- **Thin Film Interference:** reflections from film surfaces interfere
- **Newton's Rings:** dark ring diameter D² = 4nλR
- **Wedge-shaped Film:** straight equally spaced fringes

### Diffraction

Bending of light around obstacles.

- **Fraunhofer Diffraction:** source and screen at infinity
- **Single Slit Minima:** a sin θ = nλ
- **Diffraction Grating:** (a + b) sin θ = nλ

### Polarisation

Light vibrations restricted to one plane.

- **Malus's Law:** I = I0 cos²θ
- **Brewster's Law:** tan θp = μ
- **Double Refraction:** splitting into ordinary and extraordinary rays

## Unit 3: Lasers & Fibre Optics

### Laser Principles

Lasers produce coherent amplified light.

- **Stimulated Emission:** incident photon triggers identical photon
- **Population Inversion:** more atoms in excited state
- **Einstein Coefficients:** A and B coefficients relating emission and absorption

### Types of Lasers

Lasers use different active media.

- **Ruby Laser:** three-level solid-state laser
- **He-Ne Laser:** four-level gas laser at 632.8 nm
- **Semiconductor Laser:** diode laser

### Applications of Lasers

Lasers serve industry and medicine.

- **Holography:** recording 3D images using interference
- **Laser Cutting:** precise material processing
- **LIDAR:** laser-based distance measurement

### Optical Fibres

Fibres guide light by total internal reflection.

- **Numerical Aperture:** NA = √(n1² − n2²)
- **Acceptance Angle:** maximum angle for guided light
- **Step-index Fibre:** abrupt change of refractive index

## Unit 4: Solid State Physics

### Crystal Structure

Atoms arrange in lattices.

- **Unit Cell:** smallest repeating unit
- **Bravais Lattices:** 14 types of lattices
- **Miller Indices:** notation for crystal planes

### X-ray Diffraction

X-rays reveal crystal structure.

- **Bragg's Law:** 2d sin θ = nλ
- **Interplanar Spacing:** d = a/√(h² + k² + l²) for cubic
- **Powder Method:** XRD of polycrystalline samples

### Band Theory

Energy bands explain conductivity.

- **Valence Band:** highest filled band
- **Conduction Band:** band allowing conduction
- **Band Gap:** separates insulators, semiconductors and conductors

### Semiconductors & Hall Effect

Semiconductors have tunable conductivity.

- **Intrinsic Semiconductor:** pure semiconductor
- **Fermi Level:** energy with 50% occupation probability
- **Hall Effect:** transverse voltage in magnetic field gives carrier type

## Unit 5: Nuclear Physics & Modern Materials

### Nuclear Structure

Nucleus is held by strong force.

- **Binding Energy:** energy to break nucleus into nucleons
- **Mass Defect:** difference between nucleon mass sum and nuclear mass
- **Nuclear Force:** short-range strong attraction

### Radioactivity

Unstable nuclei decay.

- **Half-life:** time for half the nuclei to decay
- **Decay Law:** N = N0 e^(−λt)
- **Alpha, Beta, Gamma:** types of radioactive emission

### Nuclear Fission & Fusion

Nuclear reactions release energy.

- **Fission:** splitting of heavy nucleus
- **Fusion:** combining light nuclei, powering the Sun
- **Chain Reaction:** self-sustaining fission

### Superconductors & Nanomaterials

Special materials show unusual properties.

- **Superconductivity:** zero resistance below critical temperature
- **Meissner Effect:** expulsion of magnetic field from superconductor
- **Nanotechnology:** control of matter at 1-100 nm
