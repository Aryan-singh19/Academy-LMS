## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Igneous Rock: formed by cooling of magma, e.g. granite; hard and durable
- **Q1(b):** Crushing Strength Test: compressive strength of a stone cube | Water Absorption Test: percentage of water absorbed in 24 hours | Attrition Test: resistance of stone to wear by rubbing
- **Q2(a):** C3S: responsible for early strength
- **Q2(b):** PPC: Portland pozzolana cement with fly ash | Rapid Hardening Cement: gains strength quickly | Vicat Apparatus: finds initial and final setting time
- **Q3(a):** Characteristic Strength: strength below which only 5% results fall
- **Q3(b):** Plasticiser: improves workability at same water content | Accelerator: speeds setting, e.g. calcium chloride | Self-compacting Concrete: flows and compacts under its own weight
- **Q4(a):** Aluminium: light, corrosion resistant
- **Q4(b):** Seasoning: reducing moisture content of timber | Defects in Timber: knots, shakes and decay | Plywood: layers of veneer glued with grain at right angles
- **Q5(a):** PVC: polymer for pipes and fittings
- **Q5(b):** Paint Constituents: base, vehicle, pigment, drier and thinner | Distemper: water-based coating for interior walls | Varnish: transparent protective coating
- **Q6(a):** Vitrified Tile: dense low-porosity tile fired at high temperature
- **Q6(b):** Igneous Rock: formed by cooling of magma, e.g. granite; hard and durable | Sedimentary Rock: formed by deposition, e.g. sandstone and limestone | Dressing of Stones: shaping quarried stone to required size and finish
- **Q7(a):** Fine Aggregate: particles passing 4.75 mm sieve
- **Q7(b):** Workability: ease of mixing, placing and compacting | Slump Test: measures consistency of fresh concrete | Segregation: separation of coarse aggregate from the mix
- **Q8(a):** Distemper: water-based coating for interior walls
- **Q8(b):** Fly Ash Brick: brick made with fly ash, lime and gypsum | Bamboo: renewable structural material | Recycled Aggregate: aggregate from demolition waste
