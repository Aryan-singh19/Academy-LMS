# CE-302 Construction Materials — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Stones, bricks, lime, cement, concrete, timber, metals, polymers and modern construction materials.

## Unit 1: Stones, Bricks & Tiles

### Building Stones

Natural stones are classified and tested for construction use.

- **Igneous Rock:** formed by cooling of magma, e.g. granite; hard and durable
- **Sedimentary Rock:** formed by deposition, e.g. sandstone and limestone
- **Dressing of Stones:** shaping quarried stone to required size and finish

### Tests on Stones

Tests decide whether a stone suits a structure.

- **Crushing Strength Test:** compressive strength of a stone cube
- **Water Absorption Test:** percentage of water absorbed in 24 hours
- **Attrition Test:** resistance of stone to wear by rubbing

### Bricks

Burnt clay bricks are the most common masonry unit.

- **Standard Brick Size:** 190 × 90 × 90 mm (modular) in India
- **Brick Earth Constituents:** silica, alumina, lime, iron oxide and magnesia
- **Efflorescence:** white salt deposits caused by soluble salts in bricks

### Tiles & Clay Products

Burnt clay products include tiles and pipes.

- **Roofing Tile:** clay tile such as Mangalore pattern
- **Terracotta:** burnt clay used for ornamental work
- **Vitrified Tile:** dense low-porosity tile fired at high temperature

## Unit 2: Lime, Cement & Mortar

### Lime

Lime is a traditional binding material.

- **Fat Lime:** high-calcium lime that slakes vigorously
- **Hydraulic Lime:** sets under water due to clay content
- **Slaking:** adding water to quicklime to form hydrated lime

### Portland Cement

Cement hydrates to bind aggregates.

- **Bogue Compounds:** C3S, C2S, C3A and C4AF
- **C3S:** responsible for early strength
- **Heat of Hydration:** heat released when cement reacts with water

### Types & Tests of Cement

Different cements suit different works.

- **PPC:** Portland pozzolana cement with fly ash
- **Rapid Hardening Cement:** gains strength quickly
- **Vicat Apparatus:** finds initial and final setting time

### Mortar

Mortar binds masonry units.

- **Cement Mortar:** cement, sand and water
- **Mix Proportion:** e.g. 1:6 cement to sand for brickwork
- **Lime Mortar:** lime and sand, slower setting

## Unit 3: Concrete

### Constituents of Concrete

Concrete is cement, aggregates and water.

- **Fine Aggregate:** particles passing 4.75 mm sieve
- **Coarse Aggregate:** particles retained on 4.75 mm sieve
- **Fineness Modulus:** index of aggregate coarseness from sieve analysis

### Fresh Concrete Properties

Workability governs placing and compaction.

- **Workability:** ease of mixing, placing and compacting
- **Slump Test:** measures consistency of fresh concrete
- **Segregation:** separation of coarse aggregate from the mix

### Hardened Concrete Properties

Strength and durability of concrete.

- **Water-Cement Ratio Law:** strength falls as w/c ratio rises (Abrams' law)
- **Curing:** maintaining moisture for hydration
- **Characteristic Strength:** strength below which only 5% results fall

### Admixtures & Special Concretes

Admixtures modify concrete properties.

- **Plasticiser:** improves workability at same water content
- **Accelerator:** speeds setting, e.g. calcium chloride
- **Self-compacting Concrete:** flows and compacts under its own weight

## Unit 4: Timber & Metals

### Timber

Timber is a natural building material.

- **Seasoning:** reducing moisture content of timber
- **Defects in Timber:** knots, shakes and decay
- **Plywood:** layers of veneer glued with grain at right angles

### Preservation of Timber

Treatments protect timber.

- **ASCU Treatment:** arsenic-copper preservative
- **Creosote:** oil-based preservative
- **Fire Proofing:** coatings that delay ignition

### Ferrous Metals

Steel is the main structural metal.

- **Mild Steel:** low carbon steel for structures
- **TMT Bars:** thermo-mechanically treated reinforcement bars
- **HYSD Bars:** high yield strength deformed bars

### Non-ferrous Metals

Aluminium and copper are also used.

- **Aluminium:** light, corrosion resistant
- **Copper:** good conductor for wiring and plumbing
- **Corrosion Protection:** painting and galvanising

## Unit 5: Modern & Miscellaneous Materials

### Glass & Plastics

Glass and polymers find wide use.

- **Float Glass:** flat glass of uniform thickness
- **PVC:** polymer for pipes and fittings
- **FRP:** fibre reinforced polymer for strengthening

### Paints & Varnishes

Coatings protect and decorate surfaces.

- **Paint Constituents:** base, vehicle, pigment, drier and thinner
- **Distemper:** water-based coating for interior walls
- **Varnish:** transparent protective coating

### Eco-friendly Materials

Sustainable materials reduce environmental impact.

- **Fly Ash Brick:** brick made with fly ash, lime and gypsum
- **Bamboo:** renewable structural material
- **Recycled Aggregate:** aggregate from demolition waste

### Geosynthetics & Smart Materials

New materials improve performance.

- **Geotextile:** permeable fabric used with soil
- **Ferrocement:** thin mortar with closely spaced mesh
- **Smart Material:** responds to stimuli, e.g. shape memory alloys
