## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Slope Deflection Equation: M = Mf + (2EI/L)(2θa + θb − 3Δ/L)
- **Q1(b):** Continuous Beam: beam over more than two supports | Support Settlement: adds sway term in equations | Unknown Rotations: solved from joint equations
- **Q2(a):** Balancing: distributing unbalanced moments
- **Q2(b):** Sway Correction: separate analysis for sway | Shear Condition: horizontal equilibrium of frame | Final Moments: sum of non-sway and sway moments
- **Q3(a):** Inflection Point: point of zero moment
- **Q3(b):** Müller-Breslau Principle: IL equals deflected shape | Qualitative IL: sketches for loading patterns | Propped Cantilever IL: curved influence line
- **Q4(a):** STAAD Pro: structural analysis software
- **Q4(b):** Flexibility Matrix: displacements due to unit forces | Compatibility Equation: [F]{X} = {Δ} | Redundant: chosen extra unknown force
- **Q5(a):** Plastic Moment: Mp = fy × Zp
- **Q5(b):** Beam Mechanism: hinges in a single span | Sway Mechanism: hinges in columns | Combined Mechanism: beam and sway together
- **Q6(a):** Sidesway Correction: accounts for lateral movement
- **Q6(b):** Slope Deflection Equation: M = Mf + (2EI/L)(2θa + θb − 3Δ/L) | Fixed End Moment: moment in fully fixed beam | Joint Equilibrium: sum of moments at joint is zero
- **Q7(a):** Column Analogy: analogous column loaded by M/EI
- **Q7(b):** Portal Method: assumes inflection points at mid-height | Cantilever Method: axial forces proportional to distance from centroid | Substitute Frame: analyses a floor separately
- **Q8(a):** Sway Mechanism: hinges in columns
- **Q8(b):** Static Theorem: lower bound on collapse load | Kinematic Theorem: upper bound on collapse load | Uniqueness Theorem: satisfies equilibrium, mechanism and yield
