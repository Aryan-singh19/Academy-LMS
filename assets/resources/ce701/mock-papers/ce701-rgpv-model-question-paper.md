## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Void Ratio: volume of voids / volume of solids
- **Q1(b):** Specific Gravity: ratio of soil solids density to water | Liquid Limit: water content at transition to liquid | Plasticity Index: liquid limit minus plastic limit
- **Q2(a):** Effect of Grain Size: Hazen's k = C D10²
- **Q2(b):** Flow Net: flow lines and equipotential lines | Seepage Discharge: q = kH Nf/Nd | Laplace Equation: governs 2D seepage
- **Q3(a):** Coefficient of Consolidation: cv from time-settlement curve
- **Q3(b):** Compression Index: slope of e-log σ' curve | Primary Settlement: due to pore pressure dissipation | Overconsolidated Clay: past pressure exceeds present
- **Q4(a):** Sensitivity: undisturbed / remoulded strength
- **Q4(b):** Mohr-Coulomb Equation: τ = c + σ tan φ | Cohesion: shear strength at zero normal stress | Angle of Internal Friction: frictional resistance parameter
- **Q5(a):** Passive Earth Pressure: Kp = (1 + sin φ)/(1 − sin φ)
- **Q5(b):** Rankine's Theory: smooth vertical wall | Coulomb's Wedge Theory: considers wall friction | Culmann's Method: graphical earth pressure
- **Q6(a):** Well-graded Soil: wide range of sizes
- **Q6(b):** Void Ratio: volume of voids / volume of solids | Porosity: volume of voids / total volume | Degree of Saturation: volume of water / volume of voids
- **Q7(a):** Standard Proctor Test: finds maximum dry density and OMC
- **Q7(b):** Sheep Foot Roller: for clayey soils | Vibratory Roller: for granular soils | Field Density Test: sand replacement or core cutter
- **Q8(a):** Coulomb's Wedge Theory: considers wall friction
- **Q8(b):** Gravity Retaining Wall: resists by weight | Cantilever Retaining Wall: RCC stem and base | Stability Checks: sliding, overturning and bearing
