# CE-602 Environmental Engineering-I — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Water demand, sources, conveyance, quality, treatment and distribution systems.

## Unit 1: Water Demand & Sources

### Water Demand

Demand determines design capacity.

- **Per Capita Demand:** 135 lpcd for urban areas as per CPHEEO
- **Fire Demand:** water for firefighting
- **Peak Factor:** maximum to average demand ratio

### Population Forecasting

Design needs future population.

- **Arithmetic Increase Method:** constant increment per decade
- **Geometric Increase Method:** constant percentage growth
- **Incremental Increase Method:** increment changes uniformly

### Surface Sources

Rivers and lakes supply water.

- **Impounding Reservoir:** dam storing river water
- **Intake Structure:** draws water from source
- **River Intake:** located upstream of towns

### Groundwater Sources

Wells and springs tap aquifers.

- **Open Well:** shallow dug well
- **Tube Well:** deep bored well
- **Specific Yield:** water drained per unit volume of aquifer

## Unit 2: Water Quality

### Physical Parameters

Physical tests characterise water.

- **Turbidity:** cloudiness measured in NTU
- **Colour:** due to dissolved organic matter
- **Taste and Odour:** due to dissolved gases and organics

### Chemical Parameters

Chemical tests check safety.

- **pH:** 6.5 to 8.5 for drinking water
- **Hardness:** due to Ca and Mg salts
- **Fluoride Limit:** 1.0 mg/L desirable as per IS 10500

### Biological Parameters

Microbes indicate contamination.

- **Coliform Bacteria:** indicator of faecal contamination
- **MPN:** most probable number test
- **E. coli:** specific faecal indicator

### Drinking Water Standards

Standards define safe limits.

- **IS 10500:** Indian drinking water specification
- **WHO Guidelines:** international guideline values
- **Permissible Limit:** maximum in absence of alternate source

## Unit 3: Water Treatment

### Screening & Plain Sedimentation

Removal of floating and settleable solids.

- **Screening:** removes floating matter
- **Plain Sedimentation:** settling without chemicals
- **Stokes' Law:** settling velocity of discrete particles

### Coagulation & Flocculation

Chemicals aggregate fine particles.

- **Coagulation:** destabilising colloids with alum
- **Jar Test:** determines optimum coagulant dose
- **Flocculation:** gentle mixing to form flocs

### Filtration

Filters remove remaining particles.

- **Slow Sand Filter:** biological layer, low rate
- **Rapid Sand Filter:** high rate with backwashing
- **Filter Backwashing:** reversing flow to clean filter

### Disinfection & Softening

Final treatment ensures safety.

- **Chlorination:** killing pathogens with chlorine
- **Breakpoint Chlorination:** dose beyond which free residual rises
- **Residual Chlorine:** 0.2 mg/L at consumer end

## Unit 4: Conveyance & Distribution

### Pipes & Conveyance

Water is conveyed through pipes.

- **Hazen-Williams Formula:** V = 0.849 C R^0.63 S^0.54
- **Pipe Materials:** CI, DI, PVC, HDPE
- **Water Hammer:** pressure surge from sudden valve closure

### Distribution Systems

Layouts deliver water to consumers.

- **Dead End System:** branching layout
- **Grid Iron System:** interconnected loops
- **Ring System:** main around area

### Storage & Pumping

Reservoirs balance supply and demand.

- **Service Reservoir:** storage near distribution
- **Mass Curve Method:** finds storage capacity
- **Pump Selection:** based on head and discharge

### Network Analysis

Hydraulic analysis sizes pipes.

- **Hardy Cross Method:** iterative loop analysis
- **Equivalent Pipe:** replaces pipe system
- **Leak Detection:** reducing water losses

## Unit 5: Rural & Advanced Water Supply

### Rural Water Supply

Small systems serve villages.

- **Hand Pump:** simple groundwater supply
- **Jal Jeevan Mission:** tap water to rural households
- **Rainwater Harvesting:** collecting rooftop runoff

### Advanced Treatment

New technologies treat difficult water.

- **Reverse Osmosis:** membrane removing dissolved salts
- **Activated Carbon:** adsorbs organics
- **Defluoridation:** Nalgonda technique removing fluoride

### Water Conservation

Saving water reduces demand.

- **Water Audit:** accounting for water use
- **Leakage Control:** reducing distribution losses
- **Recycling:** reuse of treated wastewater

### Plumbing in Buildings

Internal systems deliver water.

- **House Connection:** service pipe from main
- **Overhead Tank:** storage on building
- **Cross Connection:** contamination risk between systems
