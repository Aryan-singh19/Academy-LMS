## Unit 3: Water Treatment

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Screening*.

- **Q.** Define *Plain Sedimentation*.  
  **Ans.** settling without chemicals
- **Q.** Define *Stokes' Law*.  
  **Ans.** settling velocity of discrete particles
- **Q.** Define *Coagulation*.  
  **Ans.** destabilising colloids with alum
- **Q.** Define *Jar Test*.  
  **Ans.** determines optimum coagulant dose
- **Q.** Define *Flocculation*.  
  **Ans.** gentle mixing to form flocs
- **Q.** Define *Slow Sand Filter*.  
  **Ans.** biological layer, low rate
- **Q.** Define *Rapid Sand Filter*.  
  **Ans.** high rate with backwashing
- **Q.** Define *Filter Backwashing*.  
  **Ans.** reversing flow to clean filter
- **Q.** Define *Chlorination*.  
  **Ans.** killing pathogens with chlorine
- **Q.** Define *Breakpoint Chlorination*.  
  **Ans.** dose beyond which free residual rises
- **Q.** Define *Residual Chlorine*.  
  **Ans.** 0.2 mg/L at consumer end

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Screening & Plain Sedimentation* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Removal of floating and settleable solids. Screening — removes floating matter; Plain Sedimentation — settling without chemicals; Stokes' Law — settling velocity of discrete particles.
- **Q.** Explain *Coagulation & Flocculation* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Chemicals aggregate fine particles. Coagulation — destabilising colloids with alum; Jar Test — determines optimum coagulant dose; Flocculation — gentle mixing to form flocs.
- **Q.** Explain *Filtration* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Filters remove remaining particles. Slow Sand Filter — biological layer, low rate; Rapid Sand Filter — high rate with backwashing; Filter Backwashing — reversing flow to clean filter.
