## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Per Capita Demand: 135 lpcd for urban areas as per CPHEEO
- **Q1(b):** Arithmetic Increase Method: constant increment per decade | Geometric Increase Method: constant percentage growth | Incremental Increase Method: increment changes uniformly
- **Q2(a):** Hardness: due to Ca and Mg salts
- **Q2(b):** Coliform Bacteria: indicator of faecal contamination | MPN: most probable number test | E. coli: specific faecal indicator
- **Q3(a):** Filter Backwashing: reversing flow to clean filter
- **Q3(b):** Chlorination: killing pathogens with chlorine | Breakpoint Chlorination: dose beyond which free residual rises | Residual Chlorine: 0.2 mg/L at consumer end
- **Q4(a):** Hardy Cross Method: iterative loop analysis
- **Q4(b):** Hazen-Williams Formula: V = 0.849 C R^0.63 S^0.54 | Pipe Materials: CI, DI, PVC, HDPE | Water Hammer: pressure surge from sudden valve closure
- **Q5(a):** Jal Jeevan Mission: tap water to rural households
- **Q5(b):** Reverse Osmosis: membrane removing dissolved salts | Activated Carbon: adsorbs organics | Defluoridation: Nalgonda technique removing fluoride
- **Q6(a):** Specific Yield: water drained per unit volume of aquifer
- **Q6(b):** Per Capita Demand: 135 lpcd for urban areas as per CPHEEO | Fire Demand: water for firefighting | Peak Factor: maximum to average demand ratio
- **Q7(a):** Screening: removes floating matter
- **Q7(b):** Coagulation: destabilising colloids with alum | Jar Test: determines optimum coagulant dose | Flocculation: gentle mixing to form flocs
- **Q8(a):** Activated Carbon: adsorbs organics
- **Q8(b):** Water Audit: accounting for water use | Leakage Control: reducing distribution losses | Recycling: reuse of treated wastewater
