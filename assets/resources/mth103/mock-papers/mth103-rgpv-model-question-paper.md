## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Laplace Equation for Temperature: ∇²T = 0
- **Q1(b):** Biot Number: hL/k | Fourier Number: αt/L² | Semi-infinite Solid: erf solution for surface temperature change
- **Q2(a):** Fully Developed Laminar Flow Nu: 3.66 constant wall temperature
- **Q2(b):** Rayleigh Number: Ra = Gr Pr | Boussinesq Approximation: density varies only in buoyancy term | Enclosure Convection: cavities heated from side or below
- **Q3(a):** Non-condensable Gases: reduce condensation
- **Q3(b):** Heat Pipe: evaporator, adiabatic and condenser sections | Wick: returns liquid by capillarity | Capillary Limit: maximum heat transport
- **Q4(a):** Solar Irradiance: flux reaching surface
- **Q4(b):** Spectral Emissivity: emissivity at given wavelength | Grey Surface: emissivity independent of wavelength | Kirchhoff's Law: ελ = αλ at equilibrium
- **Q5(a):** Equimolar Counter Diffusion: equal opposite fluxes
- **Q5(b):** Sherwood Number: hm L/D | Schmidt Number: ν/D | Chilton-Colburn Analogy: jH = jD
- **Q6(a):** Overall Surface Efficiency: includes fin and base
- **Q6(b):** Laplace Equation for Temperature: ∇²T = 0 | Separation of Variables: analytical 2D solution | Shape Factor: q = Sk ΔT
- **Q7(a):** Nukiyama Curve: boiling curve of heat flux versus superheat
- **Q7(b):** Flow Patterns: bubbly, slug, annular flow | Dryout: liquid film disappears | Two-phase Multiplier: pressure drop enhancement
- **Q8(a):** Schmidt Number: ν/D
- **Q8(b):** ε-NTU Method: effectiveness from NTU and capacity ratio | Fouling Factor: resistance from deposits | Kern Method: shell-side design method
