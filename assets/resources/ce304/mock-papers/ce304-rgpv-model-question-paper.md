## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Aspect: arrangement of doors and windows to get sun and breeze
- **Q1(b):** Roominess: efficient use of space without waste | Horizontal Circulation: passages and corridors | Vertical Circulation: stairs, lifts and ramps
- **Q2(a):** Ground Coverage: built-up area as percentage of plot
- **Q2(b):** Habitable Room: minimum area about 9.5 m² | Ceiling Height: minimum about 2.75 m for habitable rooms | Lighting and Ventilation: openings at least one-tenth of floor area
- **Q3(a):** North-light Roof: gives uniform daylight in factories
- **Q3(b):** Plan: horizontal section at window level | Elevation: view of exterior face | Section: vertical cut showing internal details
- **Q4(a):** Landscape Architecture: design of outdoor spaces
- **Q4(b):** Proportion: relation of parts to the whole | Scale: size relative to human body | Rhythm: repetition of elements
- **Q5(a):** Sanitary Fittings: WC, wash basin and sinks
- **Q5(b):** Wiring System: conduit, casing-capping | Earthing: connecting to ground for safety | Lighting Design: illumination levels in lux
- **Q6(a):** Sanitation: planning for hygiene and drainage
- **Q6(b):** Aspect: arrangement of doors and windows to get sun and breeze | Prospect: view from the building | Privacy: screening of rooms from outside and inside
- **Q7(a):** Bungalow: detached single-family house
- **Q7(b):** School Planning: classrooms, labs and play areas | Hospital Planning: zoning for wards, OT and services | Office Building: open plan or cellular offices
- **Q8(a):** Earthing: connecting to ground for safety
- **Q8(b):** Reverberation Time: Sabine's formula T = 0.16V/A | Sound Insulation: reduces noise transmission | Echo: reflected sound heard distinctly
