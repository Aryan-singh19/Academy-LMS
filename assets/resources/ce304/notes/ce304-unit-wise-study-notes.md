# CE-304 Building Planning & Architecture — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Principles of planning, building bye-laws, functional design, orientation, architecture history and building services.

## Unit 1: Principles of Planning

### Planning Principles

Good planning ensures function and comfort.

- **Aspect:** arrangement of doors and windows to get sun and breeze
- **Prospect:** view from the building
- **Privacy:** screening of rooms from outside and inside

### Grouping & Circulation

Rooms are grouped by function.

- **Roominess:** efficient use of space without waste
- **Horizontal Circulation:** passages and corridors
- **Vertical Circulation:** stairs, lifts and ramps

### Orientation

Orientation affects light and heat.

- **Building Orientation:** placing building relative to sun and wind
- **Sun Path Diagram:** shows sun position through the year
- **Cross Ventilation:** openings on opposite walls for air flow

### Economy & Flexibility

Efficient designs reduce cost.

- **Economy in Planning:** minimum wall area for floor area
- **Flexibility:** rooms adaptable for different uses
- **Sanitation:** planning for hygiene and drainage

## Unit 2: Building Bye-laws & Regulations

### Building Bye-laws

Bye-laws regulate construction.

- **Building Bye-laws:** local rules for safe orderly construction
- **National Building Code:** NBC of India guiding design and construction
- **Building Permit:** approval before construction

### FAR & Ground Coverage

Density is controlled by ratios.

- **FAR:** total floor area divided by plot area
- **Ground Coverage:** built-up area as percentage of plot
- **Setback:** mandatory open space from plot boundary

### Minimum Room Sizes

Codes specify minimum dimensions.

- **Habitable Room:** minimum area about 9.5 m²
- **Ceiling Height:** minimum about 2.75 m for habitable rooms
- **Lighting and Ventilation:** openings at least one-tenth of floor area

### Fire & Accessibility Norms

Buildings must be safe and accessible.

- **Fire Exit:** escape route to safe place
- **Ramp Gradient:** about 1:12 for wheelchair access
- **Fire Rating:** time a component resists fire

## Unit 3: Functional Planning of Buildings

### Residential Buildings

Houses are planned around family needs.

- **Bungalow:** detached single-family house
- **Apartment:** multi-dwelling building
- **Kitchen Work Triangle:** sink, stove and fridge arrangement

### Public Buildings

Schools and hospitals need special planning.

- **School Planning:** classrooms, labs and play areas
- **Hospital Planning:** zoning for wards, OT and services
- **Office Building:** open plan or cellular offices

### Industrial Buildings

Factories follow process flow.

- **Process Layout:** machines grouped by function
- **Product Layout:** machines arranged in sequence
- **North-light Roof:** gives uniform daylight in factories

### Building Drawing

Drawings communicate the design.

- **Plan:** horizontal section at window level
- **Elevation:** view of exterior face
- **Section:** vertical cut showing internal details

## Unit 4: Architecture

### Elements of Architecture

Design uses basic visual elements.

- **Proportion:** relation of parts to the whole
- **Scale:** size relative to human body
- **Rhythm:** repetition of elements

### History of Architecture

Styles evolved across eras.

- **Indian Temple Architecture:** Nagara and Dravida styles
- **Mughal Architecture:** domes, arches and gardens, e.g. Taj Mahal
- **Modern Architecture:** function over ornament, e.g. Le Corbusier

### Green Building Design

Sustainable buildings save resources.

- **Green Building:** reduces energy, water and material use
- **GRIHA:** Indian green building rating system
- **Passive Solar Design:** uses sun for heating and lighting

### Landscape & Interior

Spaces around and within buildings.

- **Landscape Architecture:** design of outdoor spaces
- **Interior Design:** arrangement of interior spaces
- **Ergonomics:** design for human comfort

## Unit 5: Building Services

### Water Supply & Drainage

Services supply water and remove waste.

- **Plumbing:** pipes for water supply
- **Sanitary Fittings:** WC, wash basin and sinks
- **Septic Tank:** on-site treatment of sewage

### Electrical Services

Electrical layouts power the building.

- **Wiring System:** conduit, casing-capping
- **Earthing:** connecting to ground for safety
- **Lighting Design:** illumination levels in lux

### Acoustics

Acoustics controls sound in buildings.

- **Reverberation Time:** Sabine's formula T = 0.16V/A
- **Sound Insulation:** reduces noise transmission
- **Echo:** reflected sound heard distinctly

### HVAC & Lifts

Mechanical services ensure comfort.

- **HVAC:** heating, ventilation and air conditioning
- **Lift Planning:** based on traffic and waiting time
- **Escalator:** moving staircase for heavy traffic
