# CE-801 Design of Steel Structures — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Limit state design of steel tension, compression, flexural members, connections, plate girders and roof trusses as per IS 800.

## Unit 1: Introduction & Connections

### Steel as Structural Material

Steel is strong and ductile.

- **Structural Steel:** Fe 410 grade commonly used
- **IS 800:2007:** Indian code for steel design
- **Rolled Sections:** I, channel, angle sections

### Limit State Design of Steel

Partial safety factors ensure safety.

- **Partial Safety Factor γm0:** 1.10 for yielding
- **γm1:** 1.25 for ultimate stress
- **Load Combinations:** dead, live, wind and earthquake

### Bolted Connections

Bolts transfer forces.

- **Bearing Type Bolt:** transfers load by bearing and shear
- **HSFG Bolt:** high strength friction grip bolt
- **Bolt Strength:** least of shear and bearing capacity

### Welded Connections

Welds join members permanently.

- **Fillet Weld:** triangular weld at corner
- **Butt Weld:** full penetration weld
- **Effective Throat:** 0.7 × size of fillet weld

## Unit 2: Tension & Compression Members

### Tension Members

Tension members are checked for three modes.

- **Gross Section Yielding:** Tdg = Ag fy / γm0
- **Net Section Rupture:** Tdn = 0.9 An fu / γm1
- **Block Shear:** tearing along shear and tension planes

### Compression Members

Struts and columns buckle.

- **Slenderness Ratio:** KL/r
- **Buckling Class:** a, b, c, d based on section
- **Design Compressive Stress:** from IS 800 buckling curves

### Built-up Columns

Laced and battened columns.

- **Lacing:** diagonal bars connecting components
- **Battening:** plates connecting components
- **Built-up Column:** two or more sections combined

### Column Bases

Bases transfer load to foundation.

- **Slab Base:** thick plate under column
- **Gusseted Base:** with gusset plates
- **Base Plate Thickness:** from bearing pressure

## Unit 3: Flexural Members

### Laterally Supported Beams

Beams with restrained compression flange.

- **Plastic Section:** develops full plastic moment
- **Design Bending Strength:** Md = βb Zp fy / γm0
- **Shear Strength:** Vd = Av fy/(√3 γm0)

### Laterally Unsupported Beams

Beams may buckle laterally.

- **Lateral Torsional Buckling:** twisting and lateral bending
- **Effective Length:** depends on restraint
- **Elastic Critical Moment:** Mcr for LTB

### Web Buckling & Crippling

Concentrated loads affect web.

- **Web Buckling:** compression failure of web
- **Web Crippling:** local yielding under load
- **Bearing Stiffener:** prevents web failure

### Purlins

Purlins support roof sheeting.

- **Purlin:** beam spanning between trusses
- **Angle Purlin:** simple design for light roofs
- **Channel Purlin:** heavier roofs

## Unit 4: Plate Girders

### Plate Girder Components

Plate girders carry heavy loads.

- **Plate Girder:** built-up I section
- **Web Plate:** carries shear
- **Flange Plate:** resists bending

### Design of Plate Girder

Proportions are chosen for economy.

- **Economical Depth:** based on moment and web slenderness
- **Tension Field Action:** post-buckling strength of web
- **Web Thickness:** based on serviceability and buckling

### Stiffeners

Stiffeners prevent buckling.

- **Intermediate Stiffener:** vertical stiffener preventing web buckling
- **Bearing Stiffener:** at supports and loads
- **Longitudinal Stiffener:** for deep webs

### Gantry Girders

Gantry girders carry cranes.

- **Gantry Girder:** supports crane rails
- **Impact Factor:** dynamic allowance for crane
- **Lateral Load:** surge from crane trolley

## Unit 5: Roof Trusses & Industrial Buildings

### Roof Trusses

Trusses span industrial sheds.

- **Fink Truss:** common roof truss
- **Pratt Truss:** diagonals in tension
- **Pitch:** rise to span ratio

### Loads on Trusses

Trusses carry several loads.

- **Dead Load:** sheeting, purlins and self weight
- **Live Load:** as per IS 875 Part 2
- **Wind Load:** as per IS 875 Part 3

### Design of Truss Members

Members designed as struts and ties.

- **Rafter:** top chord in compression
- **Tie Member:** bottom chord in tension
- **Gusset Plate:** connects truss members

### Industrial Building Frames

Portal frames and bracing.

- **Portal Frame:** rigid frame for sheds
- **Bracing:** resists lateral loads
- **Pre-engineered Building:** economical steel system
