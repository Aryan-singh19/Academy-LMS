## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Flexibility Coefficient: displacement due to unit force
- **Q1(b):** Kinematic Indeterminacy: number of unknown joint displacements | Static Indeterminacy: number of redundant forces | Restrained DOF: displacement known at supports
- **Q2(a):** Fixed End Actions: equivalent nodal loads
- **Q2(b):** Assembly: adding element matrices to global matrix | Boundary Condition Imposition: removing restrained DOF | Banded Matrix: efficient storage
- **Q3(a):** Torsion in Grids: significant in grid behaviour
- **Q3(b):** Temperature Loads: equivalent nodal forces | Support Settlement: prescribed displacement | Elastic Supports: spring stiffness added to diagonal
- **Q4(a):** Collapse Load: load causing mechanism
- **Q4(b):** Geometric Non-linearity: large displacements | Material Non-linearity: plasticity | P-Delta Effect: secondary moments from axial load and sway
- **Q5(a):** Critical Load Factor: lowest eigenvalue
- **Q5(b):** Effective Length Factor: from stability analysis | Sway Buckling: frames without bracing | Non-sway Buckling: braced frames
- **Q6(a):** Minimum Potential Energy: equilibrium condition
- **Q6(b):** Flexibility Coefficient: displacement due to unit force | Stiffness Coefficient: force due to unit displacement | Relation: stiffness matrix is inverse of flexibility matrix
- **Q7(a):** Plane Frame Element: 6×6 stiffness matrix
- **Q7(b):** Space Frame Element: 12×12 stiffness matrix | Torsional Stiffness: GJ/L | Member Orientation: defined by reference vector
- **Q8(a):** Sway Buckling: frames without bracing
- **Q8(b):** Consistent Mass Matrix: derived from shape functions | Lumped Mass Matrix: masses at nodes | Eigenvalue Problem: [K − ω²M]{φ} = 0
