## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Stress Tensor: nine components at a point
- **Q1(b):** Principal Stress: normal stress on plane with zero shear | Stress Invariants: I1, I2, I3 independent of axes | Characteristic Equation: σ³ − I1σ² + I2σ − I3 = 0
- **Q2(a):** Saint-Venant Compatibility: six compatibility equations
- **Q2(b):** Generalised Hooke's Law: stress linearly related to strain | Isotropic Material: two independent constants | Lame's Constants: λ and μ
- **Q3(a):** Rotating Disc: centrifugal stresses
- **Q3(b):** Saint-Venant's Principle: statically equivalent loads give same distant stresses | Semi-inverse Method: assume part of solution | Boussinesq Problem: point load on half-space
- **Q4(a):** Elliptical Section: exact Prandtl solution
- **Q4(b):** Warping: out-of-plane displacement of cross-section | Prandtl Stress Function: φ for torsion | Torsion Equation: ∇²φ = −2Gθ
- **Q5(a):** Strain Hardening: increase of yield stress with plastic strain
- **Q5(b):** Tresca Criterion: maximum shear stress | von Mises Criterion: distortion energy | Yield Surface: boundary of elastic region in stress space
- **Q6(a):** Octahedral Shear Stress: related to distortion energy
- **Q6(b):** Stress Tensor: nine components at a point | Cauchy's Formula: traction = stress tensor × normal | Symmetry of Stress: τxy = τyx
- **Q7(a):** Plane Stress: σz = 0 for thin plates
- **Q7(b):** Airy Stress Function: σx = ∂²φ/∂y² | Biharmonic Equation: ∇⁴φ = 0 | Polynomial Solutions: for beams
- **Q8(a):** von Mises Criterion: distortion energy
- **Q8(b):** Normality Rule: plastic strain normal to yield surface | Levy-Mises Equations: rigid-plastic flow rule | Prandtl-Reuss Equations: elastic-plastic flow rule
