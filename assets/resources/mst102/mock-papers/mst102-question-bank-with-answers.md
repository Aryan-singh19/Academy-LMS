## Unit 1: Analysis of Stress

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Stress Tensor*.  
  **Ans.** nine components at a point
- **Q.** Define *Cauchy's Formula*.  
  **Ans.** traction = stress tensor × normal
- **Q.** Define *Symmetry of Stress*.  
  **Ans.** τxy = τyx
- **Q.** Define *Principal Stress*.  
  **Ans.** normal stress on plane with zero shear
- **Q.** Define *Stress Invariants*.  
  **Ans.** I1, I2, I3 independent of axes
- **Q.** Define *Characteristic Equation*.  
  **Ans.** σ³ − I1σ² + I2σ − I3 = 0
- **Q.** Define *Equilibrium Equation*.  
  **Ans.** ∂σx/∂x + ∂τxy/∂y + ∂τxz/∂z + Bx = 0
- **Q.** Define *Body Force*.  
  **Ans.** force per unit volume
- **Q.** Define *Mohr's Circle in 3D*.  
  **Ans.** three circles for principal stresses
- **Q.** Define *Hydrostatic Stress*.  
  **Ans.** mean normal stress
- **Q.** Define *Deviatoric Stress*.  
  **Ans.** total minus hydrostatic
- **Q.** Define *Octahedral Shear Stress*.  
  **Ans.** related to distortion energy

## Unit 2: Analysis of Strain & Constitutive Relations

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Strain-displacement Relations*.  
  **Ans.** εx = ∂u/∂x, γxy = ∂u/∂y + ∂v/∂x
- **Q.** Define *Principal Strains*.  
  **Ans.** extreme normal strains
- **Q.** Define *Volumetric Strain*.  
  **Ans.** sum of normal strains
- **Q.** Define *Compatibility Equation*.  
  **Ans.** ∂²εx/∂y² + ∂²εy/∂x² = ∂²γxy/∂x∂y
- **Q.** Define *Saint-Venant Compatibility*.  
  **Ans.** six compatibility equations
- **Q.** Define *Single-valued Displacements*.  
  **Ans.** ensured by compatibility
- **Q.** Define *Generalised Hooke's Law*.

- **Q.** Define *Lame's Constants*.  
  **Ans.** λ and μ
- **Q.** Define *Strain Energy Density*.

- **Q.** Define *Clapeyron's Theorem*.  
  **Ans.** strain energy equals half work of loads
- **Q.** Define *Uniqueness Theorem*.  
  **Ans.** elasticity solution is unique
