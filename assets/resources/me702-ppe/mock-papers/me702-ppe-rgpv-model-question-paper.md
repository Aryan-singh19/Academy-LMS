## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Base Load Plant: runs continuously at near full load
- **Q1(b):** Coal and Ash Circuit: handling coal to boiler and ash to disposal | Feed Water Circuit: condensate pumped back to boiler | Cooling Water Circuit: removes heat from condenser
- **Q2(a):** Penstock: pipe carrying water to turbines
- **Q2(b):** Run-of-river Plant: little or no storage | Pumped Storage Plant: pumps water uphill at off-peak times | High Head Plant: uses Pelton turbines
- **Q3(a):** Regenerator: recovers exhaust heat
- **Q3(b):** Combined Cycle: gas turbine exhaust raises steam for steam turbine | HRSG: heat recovery steam generator | Combined Cycle Efficiency: can exceed 55%
- **Q4(a):** Geothermal Plant: uses Earth's heat
- **Q4(b):** Diesel Power Plant: diesel engines driving generators | Applications: standby, peak load and remote locations | Fuel System: storage, filters and injection
- **Q5(a):** Load Duration Curve: loads arranged in descending order
- **Q5(b):** Load Factor: average load / peak load | Demand Factor: maximum demand / connected load | Diversity Factor: sum of individual maximum demands / maximum demand of system
- **Q6(a):** Electrostatic Precipitator: removes fly ash from flue gas
- **Q6(b):** Base Load Plant: runs continuously at near full load | Peak Load Plant: supplies demand peaks | Installed Capacity: total rated capacity of plants
- **Q7(a):** Nuclear Fission: splitting of heavy nucleus releasing energy and neutrons
- **Q7(b):** PWR: pressurised water reactor | BWR: boiling water reactor producing steam directly | PHWR: pressurised heavy water reactor used widely in India
- **Q8(a):** Demand Factor: maximum demand / connected load
- **Q8(b):** Fixed Cost: interest, depreciation and insurance | Operating Cost: fuel, labour and maintenance | Depreciation: loss of value over time
