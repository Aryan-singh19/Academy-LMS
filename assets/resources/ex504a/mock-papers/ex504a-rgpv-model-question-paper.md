- **Q1(a):** SCR: unidirectional controlled switch
- **Q1(b):** UJT Relaxation Oscillator: generates trigger pulses | Pulse Transformer: isolates gate circuit | Opto-isolator: optical isolation of control
- **Q2(a):** Off-delay Timer: output persists after de-energising
- **Q2(b):** Industrial Counter: counts products | Sequencer: steps through operations | Interlocking: prevents unsafe sequences
- **Q3(a):** Synchronous Timer: synchronised with supply
- **Q3(b):** Arc Furnace: melting by electric arc | Electrode Control: maintains arc length | Power Factor in Furnaces: typically low
- **Q4(a):** Overload Relay: trips on sustained overcurrent
- **Q4(b):** Phase-controlled DC Drive: SCR rectifier feeding motor | Chopper Drive: DC-DC converter control | Closed-loop Speed Control: tachogenerator feedback
- **Q5(a):** Scan Cycle: input scan, program execution, output update
- **Q5(b):** Ladder Diagram: relay-style program | Timer and Counter Instructions: TON, TOF, CTU | Latching Circuit: holds output on
- **Q6(a):** UPS: uninterruptible power supply
- **Q6(b):** SCR: unidirectional controlled switch | TRIAC: bidirectional thyristor | DIAC: bidirectional trigger diode
- **Q7(a):** Induction Heating: heating by induced eddy currents
- **Q7(b):** Dielectric Heating: heating non-conductors by dielectric loss | Applications: wood gluing and plastic welding | Frequency Range: 1-100 MHz
