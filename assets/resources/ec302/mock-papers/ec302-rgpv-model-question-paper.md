## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Accuracy: closeness of a reading to the true value
- **Q1(b):** Gross Error: human mistake in reading or recording | Systematic Error: repeatable error from instrument or environment | Random Error: unpredictable error reduced by averaging
- **Q2(a):** Electrodynamometer: fixed and moving coils used as a wattmeter
- **Q2(b):** Ramp-type DVM: counts clock pulses during a linear ramp | Dual-slope DVM: integrating converter with good noise rejection | Successive Approximation DVM: binary search conversion
- **Q3(a):** De Sauty Bridge: simple capacitance comparison bridge
- **Q3(b):** Q-meter: measures Q using series resonance | Quality Factor: Q = ωL/R | Distributed Capacitance: self-capacitance of a coil found with a Q-meter
- **Q4(a):** Function Generator: produces sine, square and triangular waves
- **Q4(b):** CRT: cathode ray tube with electron gun, deflection plates and screen | Time Base: sawtooth sweep moving the beam horizontally | Deflection Sensitivity: beam deflection per volt
- **Q5(a):** Passive Transducer: needs external excitation, e.g. strain gauge
- **Q5(b):** Strain Gauge: resistance changes with strain | Gauge Factor: (ΔR/R)/(Δl/l) | LVDT: linear variable differential transformer for displacement
- **Q6(a):** Probable Error: 0.6745 × standard deviation
- **Q6(b):** Accuracy: closeness of a reading to the true value | Precision: closeness of repeated readings to each other | Resolution: smallest input change an instrument can detect
- **Q7(a):** Wheatstone Bridge: balance condition R1·R4 = R2·R3 for medium resistance
- **Q7(b):** Maxwell Bridge: measures medium-Q inductance with a capacitor | Hay's Bridge: suits high-Q coils | Anderson Bridge: accurate inductance measurement using a modified Maxwell bridge
- **Q8(a):** Gauge Factor: (ΔR/R)/(Δl/l)
- **Q8(b):** Thermocouple: Seebeck-effect sensor generating voltage with temperature | RTD: resistance temperature detector, usually platinum | Thermistor: semiconductor with large negative temperature coefficient
