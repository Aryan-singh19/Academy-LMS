## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Turbomachine: device transferring energy between rotor and fluid
- **Q1(b):** Euler's Equation: E = (U1Vw1 − U2Vw2)/g | Velocity Triangle: vectors of blade, absolute and relative velocity | Whirl Velocity: tangential component of absolute velocity
- **Q2(a):** Manometric Efficiency: manometric head / Euler head
- **Q2(b):** Cavitation: formation and collapse of vapour bubbles | NPSH: net positive suction head | Thoma Cavitation Factor: σ = NPSH / H
- **Q3(a):** High Specific Speed: Kaplan suits large flows at low heads
- **Q3(b):** Unit Quantities: performance at unit head | Main Characteristic Curves: performance at constant head | Governing: adjusting flow to keep speed constant
- **Q4(a):** Surge: flow reversal oscillation in compressors
- **Q4(b):** Centrifugal Compressor: radial flow compressor with diffuser | Slip Factor: accounts for flow deviation at impeller exit | Pre-whirl: guide vanes reduce inlet relative Mach number
- **Q5(a):** Variable Displacement Pump: output adjustable at constant speed
- **Q5(b):** Hydraulic Intensifier: increases fluid pressure | Hydraulic Accumulator: stores energy in pressurised fluid | Hydraulic Press: multiplies force by area ratio
- **Q6(a):** Affinity Laws: Q ∝ N, H ∝ N², P ∝ N³
- **Q6(b):** Turbomachine: device transferring energy between rotor and fluid | Power-absorbing Machine: pump or compressor | Power-producing Machine: turbine
- **Q7(a):** Pelton Wheel: impulse turbine with buckets struck by jets
- **Q7(b):** Francis Turbine: inward mixed flow reaction turbine | Guide Vanes: control flow and direction into runner | Draft Tube: recovers kinetic energy at exit
- **Q8(a):** Hydraulic Accumulator: stores energy in pressurised fluid
- **Q8(b):** Hydraulic Crane: lifts loads using hydraulic cylinders | Hydraulic Lift: elevator powered by hydraulics | Hydraulic Ram: uses water hammer to lift water
