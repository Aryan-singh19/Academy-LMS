## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Industrial Robot: reprogrammable multifunctional manipulator
- **Q1(b):** Cartesian Robot: three linear joints | SCARA: selective compliance robot arm for assembly | Articulated Robot: rotary joints like a human arm
- **Q2(a):** DH Parameters: link length, twist, offset and joint angle
- **Q2(b):** Inverse Kinematics: finding joint variables for a desired pose | Multiple Solutions: several joint sets may reach the same pose | Singularity: configuration losing a degree of freedom
- **Q3(a):** Path vs Trajectory: path is geometric, trajectory adds timing
- **Q3(b):** PID Joint Control: independent joint control | Computed Torque Control: cancels non-linear dynamics | Force Control: regulates contact forces
- **Q4(a):** Servo Motor: closed-loop motor for joints
- **Q4(b):** Encoder: measures joint position | Resolver: analog angular position sensor | Tachometer: measures joint velocity
- **Q5(a):** Teach Pendant: handheld device for programming
- **Q5(b):** VAL: early robot programming language | Motion Commands: MOVE, APPROACH and DEPART | ROS: robot operating system framework
- **Q6(a):** Tool Changer: allows automatic tool swapping
- **Q6(b):** Industrial Robot: reprogrammable multifunctional manipulator | Degrees of Freedom: number of independent joint motions | Work Envelope: space reachable by the end effector
- **Q7(a):** Lagrange-Euler Formulation: dynamics from kinetic and potential energy
- **Q7(b):** Joint-space Trajectory: interpolating joint angles | Cubic Polynomial Trajectory: satisfies position and velocity at ends | Via Points: intermediate points along a path
- **Q8(a):** Motion Commands: MOVE, APPROACH and DEPART
- **Q8(b):** Spot Welding Robots: used in automobile body shops | Painting Robots: spray painting with consistent quality | Material Handling: pick-and-place and palletising
