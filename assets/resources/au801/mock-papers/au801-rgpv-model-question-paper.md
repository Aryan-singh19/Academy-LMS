## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Preventive Maintenance: scheduled servicing to avoid failures
- **Q1(b):** Periodic Service: service at fixed km or time | Oil Change Interval: recommended engine oil replacement | Service Record: history of maintenance
- **Q2(a):** Crankshaft Grinding: regrinding journals
- **Q2(b):** Injector Cleaning: removes deposits | Fuel Pressure Test: checks pump and regulator | Smoke Colour Diagnosis: black rich, blue oil, white coolant
- **Q3(a):** Shock Absorber Test: bounce test for damping
- **Q3(b):** Tyre Rotation: swapping positions for even wear | Inflation Check: correct pressure for wear and economy | Tread Depth: minimum legal depth 1.6 mm
- **Q4(a):** Blown Fuse: indicates overload or short
- **Q4(b):** Load Test: checks battery under load | Hydrometer: measures electrolyte specific gravity | Alternator Output Test: checks charging voltage
- **Q5(a):** DTC: diagnostic trouble code, e.g. P0300 misfire
- **Q5(b):** Scan Tool: reads codes and live sensor data | Automotive Oscilloscope: views sensor and ignition waveforms | Exhaust Gas Analyser: measures emissions
- **Q6(a):** Jack Stand: supports raised vehicle safely
- **Q6(b):** Preventive Maintenance: scheduled servicing to avoid failures | Breakdown Maintenance: repair after failure | Predictive Maintenance: based on condition monitoring
- **Q7(a):** Clutch Slipping: worn lining or weak springs
- **Q7(b):** Brake Bleeding: removing air from hydraulic lines | Pad Replacement: when lining wears to limit | Brake Pull: uneven braking to one side
- **Q8(a):** Automotive Oscilloscope: views sensor and ignition waveforms
- **Q8(b):** PUC Certificate: pollution under control certificate in India | Fitness Certificate: roadworthiness certificate for commercial vehicles | Pre-delivery Inspection: check before customer delivery
