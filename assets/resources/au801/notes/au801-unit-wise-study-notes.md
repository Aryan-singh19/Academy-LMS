# AU-801 Vehicle Maintenance & Fault Diagnosis — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Maintenance types, workshop practice, engine and chassis diagnosis, electrical troubleshooting and diagnostic tools.

## Unit 1: Maintenance Fundamentals

### Types of Maintenance

Maintenance keeps vehicles reliable.

- **Preventive Maintenance:** scheduled servicing to avoid failures
- **Breakdown Maintenance:** repair after failure
- **Predictive Maintenance:** based on condition monitoring

### Service Schedules

Manufacturers specify service intervals.

- **Periodic Service:** service at fixed km or time
- **Oil Change Interval:** recommended engine oil replacement
- **Service Record:** history of maintenance

### Workshop Layout & Tools

Workshops are organised for efficiency.

- **Workshop Layout:** arrangement of bays and equipment
- **Vehicle Hoist:** lifts vehicle for underbody work
- **Torque Wrench:** tightens bolts to specified torque

### Workshop Safety

Safety prevents accidents.

- **PPE:** personal protective equipment
- **Fire Extinguisher:** for fuel and electrical fires
- **Jack Stand:** supports raised vehicle safely

## Unit 2: Engine Diagnosis & Overhaul

### Engine Fault Diagnosis

Symptoms indicate engine faults.

- **Compression Test:** checks cylinder sealing
- **Leak-down Test:** locates compression leaks
- **Vacuum Gauge Test:** indicates engine condition

### Engine Overhaul

Overhaul restores worn engines.

- **Reboring:** machining worn cylinders oversize
- **Crankshaft Grinding:** regrinding journals
- **Valve Lapping:** seating valves with abrasive

### Fuel System Diagnosis

Fuel faults affect performance.

- **Injector Cleaning:** removes deposits
- **Fuel Pressure Test:** checks pump and regulator
- **Smoke Colour Diagnosis:** black rich, blue oil, white coolant

### Cooling & Lubrication Faults

Overheating and oil problems are common.

- **Overheating:** due to low coolant, thermostat or pump failure
- **Radiator Pressure Test:** finds leaks
- **Low Oil Pressure:** due to worn bearings or pump

## Unit 3: Chassis Maintenance

### Clutch & Transmission Faults

Driveline faults affect driving.

- **Clutch Slipping:** worn lining or weak springs
- **Hard Gear Shifting:** clutch drag or worn synchronisers
- **Transmission Noise:** worn bearings or gears

### Brake Maintenance

Brakes need regular inspection.

- **Brake Bleeding:** removing air from hydraulic lines
- **Pad Replacement:** when lining wears to limit
- **Brake Pull:** uneven braking to one side

### Suspension & Steering Faults

Faults affect handling and comfort.

- **Wheel Alignment:** adjusts camber, caster and toe
- **Steering Play:** excessive free movement
- **Shock Absorber Test:** bounce test for damping

### Tyre Maintenance

Tyres need proper care.

- **Tyre Rotation:** swapping positions for even wear
- **Inflation Check:** correct pressure for wear and economy
- **Tread Depth:** minimum legal depth 1.6 mm

## Unit 4: Electrical Diagnosis

### Battery & Charging Tests

Electrical tests find power faults.

- **Load Test:** checks battery under load
- **Hydrometer:** measures electrolyte specific gravity
- **Alternator Output Test:** checks charging voltage

### Starting System Diagnosis

Starting faults stop the vehicle.

- **Voltage Drop Test:** finds high resistance connections
- **Starter Draw Test:** current drawn by starter
- **Solenoid Click:** indicates low battery or solenoid fault

### Wiring Faults

Circuits are traced with meters.

- **Open Circuit:** break in the wire
- **Short Circuit:** unintended path to ground
- **Multimeter:** measures voltage, current and resistance

### Lighting & Accessory Faults

Common electrical complaints.

- **Blown Fuse:** indicates overload or short
- **Relay Testing:** checks coil and contacts
- **Earth Fault:** poor ground connection

## Unit 5: Diagnostic Tools & Inspection

### On-board Diagnostics

OBD reads fault codes.

- **OBD-II:** standard diagnostic interface
- **DTC:** diagnostic trouble code, e.g. P0300 misfire
- **Freeze Frame Data:** conditions when fault occurred

### Scan Tools & Oscilloscopes

Tools analyse live data.

- **Scan Tool:** reads codes and live sensor data
- **Automotive Oscilloscope:** views sensor and ignition waveforms
- **Exhaust Gas Analyser:** measures emissions

### Vehicle Inspection & Certification

Vehicles are inspected for roadworthiness.

- **PUC Certificate:** pollution under control certificate in India
- **Fitness Certificate:** roadworthiness certificate for commercial vehicles
- **Pre-delivery Inspection:** check before customer delivery

### Fleet Maintenance Management

Fleets track maintenance systematically.

- **Fleet Management:** maintenance planning for many vehicles
- **MTBF:** mean time between failures
- **Maintenance Cost Analysis:** tracking cost per km
