# CE-402 Construction Technology — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Foundations, masonry, floors, roofs, stairs, formwork, damp proofing, construction equipment and modern methods.

## Unit 1: Foundations & Masonry

### Types of Foundations

Foundations transfer loads to soil.

- **Spread Footing:** enlarged base for column or wall
- **Raft Foundation:** single slab under whole building
- **Pile Foundation:** transfers load to deep strata

### Brick Masonry

Bonds give strength to walls.

- **English Bond:** alternate header and stretcher courses
- **Flemish Bond:** headers and stretchers in each course
- **Header:** brick laid with length across the wall

### Stone Masonry

Stone walls use rubble or ashlar.

- **Rubble Masonry:** undressed or roughly dressed stones
- **Ashlar Masonry:** finely dressed stones with thin joints
- **Through Stone:** stone extending across wall thickness

### Composite & Hollow Block Masonry

Other masonry types.

- **Hollow Concrete Block:** lightweight masonry unit
- **Cavity Wall:** two leaves with gap for insulation
- **Reinforced Masonry:** steel bars in masonry for strength

## Unit 2: Floors, Roofs & Stairs

### Floors

Floors give level surfaces.

- **Ground Floor:** laid on prepared base
- **Terrazzo Floor:** marble chips in cement
- **Flooring Selection:** durability, cleanliness and cost

### Roofs

Roofs protect buildings from weather.

- **Pitched Roof:** sloping roof with trusses
- **Flat Roof:** RCC slab with slope for drainage
- **King Post Truss:** timber truss for spans up to 8 m

### Staircases

Stairs connect floors.

- **Tread:** horizontal part of step
- **Riser:** vertical part of step
- **Dog-legged Stair:** two flights with half landing

### Doors & Windows

Openings for access and ventilation.

- **Panelled Door:** frame with panels
- **Casement Window:** side-hinged window
- **Lintel:** beam over opening carrying load

## Unit 3: Damp Proofing & Finishes

### Dampness in Buildings

Moisture causes deterioration.

- **Rising Damp:** moisture rising by capillarity
- **DPC:** damp proof course preventing rising damp
- **Efflorescence:** salt deposits from moisture

### Waterproofing

Roofs and basements need waterproofing.

- **Membrane Waterproofing:** bituminous or polymer sheet
- **Integral Waterproofing:** admixture in concrete
- **Grouting:** injecting material to seal cracks

### Plastering & Pointing

Finishes protect walls.

- **Plastering:** covering walls with mortar
- **Pointing:** finishing mortar joints
- **Defects in Plaster:** cracking, blistering and efflorescence

### Painting & Finishing

Coatings protect and decorate.

- **Primer:** first coat improving adhesion
- **Emulsion Paint:** water-based paint
- **Texture Finish:** decorative surface finish

## Unit 4: Formwork & Scaffolding

### Formwork

Formwork shapes fresh concrete.

- **Formwork:** temporary mould for concrete
- **Shuttering:** formwork for slabs and walls
- **Removal of Formwork:** after specified curing period

### Scaffolding & Shoring

Temporary structures support work.

- **Scaffolding:** temporary platform for workers
- **Shoring:** support for unsafe structures
- **Underpinning:** strengthening existing foundations

### Slip & Modular Formwork

Modern formwork speeds construction.

- **Slip Form:** continuously moving formwork for tall structures
- **Aluminium Formwork:** reusable system formwork
- **Table Form:** large formwork for slabs

### Expansion & Construction Joints

Joints control movements.

- **Expansion Joint:** gap allowing thermal movement
- **Construction Joint:** joint between successive pours
- **Contraction Joint:** controls shrinkage cracks

## Unit 5: Construction Equipment & Methods

### Earth Moving Equipment

Machines excavate and move soil.

- **Bulldozer:** tractor with blade for pushing earth
- **Excavator:** digs with bucket on arm
- **Scraper:** excavates and transports soil

### Concreting Equipment

Machines mix and place concrete.

- **Transit Mixer:** truck mixing concrete in transit
- **Concrete Pump:** pumps concrete to placement
- **Vibrator:** compacts concrete

### Hoisting Equipment

Cranes lift materials.

- **Tower Crane:** tall crane for high-rise construction
- **Mobile Crane:** truck-mounted crane
- **Hoist:** vertical lifting of materials

### Prefabrication & Fast-track Construction

Industrialised methods save time.

- **Precast Construction:** elements cast off-site
- **Modular Construction:** factory-built modules
- **Pre-engineered Building:** steel buildings with standard components
