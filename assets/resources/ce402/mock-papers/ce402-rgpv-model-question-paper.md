## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Spread Footing: enlarged base for column or wall
- **Q1(b):** English Bond: alternate header and stretcher courses | Flemish Bond: headers and stretchers in each course | Header: brick laid with length across the wall
- **Q2(a):** Flat Roof: RCC slab with slope for drainage
- **Q2(b):** Tread: horizontal part of step | Riser: vertical part of step | Dog-legged Stair: two flights with half landing
- **Q3(a):** Defects in Plaster: cracking, blistering and efflorescence
- **Q3(b):** Primer: first coat improving adhesion | Emulsion Paint: water-based paint | Texture Finish: decorative surface finish
- **Q4(a):** Expansion Joint: gap allowing thermal movement
- **Q4(b):** Formwork: temporary mould for concrete | Shuttering: formwork for slabs and walls | Removal of Formwork: after specified curing period
- **Q5(a):** Excavator: digs with bucket on arm
- **Q5(b):** Transit Mixer: truck mixing concrete in transit | Concrete Pump: pumps concrete to placement | Vibrator: compacts concrete
- **Q6(a):** Reinforced Masonry: steel bars in masonry for strength
- **Q6(b):** Spread Footing: enlarged base for column or wall | Raft Foundation: single slab under whole building | Pile Foundation: transfers load to deep strata
- **Q7(a):** Rising Damp: moisture rising by capillarity
- **Q7(b):** Membrane Waterproofing: bituminous or polymer sheet | Integral Waterproofing: admixture in concrete | Grouting: injecting material to seal cracks
- **Q8(a):** Concrete Pump: pumps concrete to placement
- **Q8(b):** Tower Crane: tall crane for high-rise construction | Mobile Crane: truck-mounted crane | Hoist: vertical lifting of materials
