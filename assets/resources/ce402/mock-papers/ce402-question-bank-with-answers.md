## Unit 1: Foundations & Masonry

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Spread Footing*.  
  **Ans.** enlarged base for column or wall
- **Q.** Define *Raft Foundation*.  
  **Ans.** single slab under whole building
- **Q.** Define *Pile Foundation*.  
  **Ans.** transfers load to deep strata
- **Q.** Define *English Bond*.  
  **Ans.** alternate header and stretcher courses
- **Q.** Define *Flemish Bond*.  
  **Ans.** headers and stretchers in each course
- **Q.** Define *Header*.  
  **Ans.** brick laid with length across the wall
- **Q.** Define *Rubble Masonry*.  
  **Ans.** undressed or roughly dressed stones
- **Q.** Define *Ashlar Masonry*.  
  **Ans.** finely dressed stones with thin joints
- **Q.** Define *Through Stone*.  
  **Ans.** stone extending across wall thickness
- **Q.** Define *Hollow Concrete Block*.  
  **Ans.** lightweight masonry unit
- **Q.** Define *Cavity Wall*.  
  **Ans.** two leaves with gap for insulation
- **Q.** Define *Reinforced Masonry*.  
  **Ans.** steel bars in masonry for strength

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Types of Foundations* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Foundations transfer loads to soil. Spread Footing — enlarged base for column or wall; Raft Foundation — single slab under whole building; Pile Foundation — transfers load to deep strata.
- **Q.** Explain *Brick Masonry* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Bonds give strength to walls. English Bond — alternate header and stretcher courses; Flemish Bond — headers and stretchers in each course; Header — brick laid with length across the wall.
- **Q.** Explain *Stone Masonry* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Stone walls use rubble or ashlar. Rubble Masonry — undressed or roughly dressed stones; Ashlar Masonry — finely dressed stones with thin joints; Through Stone — stone extending across wall thickness.
- **Q.** Explain *Composite & Hollow Block Masonry* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Other masonry types. Hollow Concrete Block — lightweight masonry unit; Cavity Wall — two leaves with gap for insulation; Reinforced Masonry — steel bars in masonry for strength.
