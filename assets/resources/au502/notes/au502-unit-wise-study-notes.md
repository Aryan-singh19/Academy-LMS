# AU-502 Automotive Chassis System — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Frames, front axle and steering, suspension, rear axle and final drive, wheels, tyres and brakes.

## Unit 1: Frames & Body

### Types of Frames

Frames carry loads and mount components.

- **Conventional Frame:** separate ladder frame with body bolted on
- **Integral Frame:** unitary body construction
- **Semi-integral Frame:** partial integration of body and frame

### Frame Materials & Loads

Frames resist bending and torsion.

- **Frame Loads:** static weight, braking, cornering and torsion
- **Cross Member:** connects side members and adds stiffness
- **Frame Material:** pressed steel channel sections

### Vehicle Dimensions

Dimensions affect stability and handling.

- **Wheelbase:** distance between front and rear axles
- **Track Width:** distance between left and right wheels
- **Ground Clearance:** lowest point height from road

### Weight Distribution

Load sharing affects handling.

- **Kerb Weight:** vehicle weight with fluids but no load
- **GVW:** gross vehicle weight including payload
- **Centre of Gravity:** point where vehicle weight acts

## Unit 2: Front Axle & Steering

### Front Axle Types

Front axles carry steering wheels.

- **Dead Axle:** does not transmit power
- **Live Front Axle:** transmits power in 4WD
- **Stub Axle:** short axle carrying wheel, pivoted on kingpin

### Steering Geometry

Angles improve stability and tyre life.

- **Camber Angle:** wheel tilt from vertical
- **Castor Angle:** backward tilt of kingpin giving self-centring
- **Toe-out on Turns:** inner wheel turns more in a corner

### Steering Mechanisms

Mechanisms achieve correct steering.

- **Ackermann Mechanism:** approximates true rolling in turns
- **Davis Mechanism:** exact steering using sliding pairs
- **Turning Radius:** radius of circle traced by outer wheel

### Steering Gearboxes & Power Steering

Gearboxes multiply driver effort.

- **Worm and Sector:** early steering gear type
- **Rack and Pinion:** direct steering gear in cars
- **EPS:** electric power steering with motor assistance

## Unit 3: Suspension

### Suspension Requirements

Suspension provides comfort and control.

- **Sprung Mass:** mass supported by springs
- **Unsprung Mass:** wheels, axles and brakes below springs
- **Ride Comfort:** isolation from road irregularities

### Springs

Springs absorb road shocks.

- **Leaf Spring:** laminated steel spring
- **Coil Spring:** helical spring in independent suspensions
- **Torsion Bar:** twisting bar acting as a spring

### Independent Suspension Types

Each wheel moves separately.

- **Double Wishbone:** two control arms per wheel
- **MacPherson Strut:** strut combining spring and damper
- **Multi-link Suspension:** several links controlling wheel motion

### Dampers & Stabilisers

Dampers and bars control motion.

- **Shock Absorber:** converts oscillation energy to heat
- **Anti-roll Bar:** reduces body roll in turns
- **Active Suspension:** electronically adjusts damping or height

## Unit 4: Rear Axle & Final Drive

### Final Drive

Final drive reduces speed and turns power 90°.

- **Final Drive Ratio:** crown wheel teeth / pinion teeth
- **Hypoid Gear:** offset pinion allowing lower floor
- **Crown Wheel and Pinion:** bevel gear pair in rear axle

### Differential

Differential allows wheel speed difference.

- **Differential Gear:** planetary bevel gears dividing torque
- **Limited Slip Differential:** limits wheel spin on slippery roads
- **Differential Lock:** locks both wheels together

### Rear Axle Types

Axles support load and drive wheels.

- **Semi-floating Axle:** axle shaft carries vehicle load and torque
- **Full-floating Axle:** shaft carries only torque
- **Three-quarter Floating Axle:** intermediate design

### Rear Axle Drives

Torque reactions are handled by drives.

- **Hotchkiss Drive:** leaf springs absorb torque reaction
- **Torque Tube Drive:** enclosed propeller shaft takes torque
- **Axle Housing:** encloses differential and shafts

## Unit 5: Wheels, Tyres & Brakes

### Wheels

Wheels support the tyre and vehicle.

- **Disc Wheel:** pressed steel wheel
- **Alloy Wheel:** light aluminium alloy wheel
- **Wire Wheel:** spoked wheel

### Tyres

Tyres provide traction and cushioning.

- **Cross-ply Tyre:** plies at angles; stiff sidewall
- **Radial Ply Tyre:** radial plies with belts
- **Tyre Wear Patterns:** indicate alignment or inflation faults

### Brake Systems

Brakes convert kinetic energy to heat.

- **Mechanical Brake:** cable or rod operated
- **Hydraulic Brake:** master and wheel cylinders
- **Air Brake:** compressed air brakes in heavy vehicles

### Brake Performance

Stopping distance depends on friction and speed.

- **Stopping Distance:** v²/(2μg) ignoring reaction time
- **Weight Transfer:** load shifts to front during braking
- **Brake Efficiency:** braking force / vehicle weight
