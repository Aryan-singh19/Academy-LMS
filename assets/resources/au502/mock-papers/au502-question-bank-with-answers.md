- **Q.** Define *Live Front Axle*.  
  **Ans.** transmits power in 4WD
- **Q.** Define *Stub Axle*.

- **Q.** Define *Camber Angle*.  
  **Ans.** wheel tilt from vertical
- **Q.** Define *Castor Angle*.

- **Q.** Explain *Front Axle Types* in detail with a neat diagram.
