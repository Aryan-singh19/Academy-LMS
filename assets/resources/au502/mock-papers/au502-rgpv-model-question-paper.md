## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Conventional Frame: separate ladder frame with body bolted on
- **Q1(b):** Frame Loads: static weight, braking, cornering and torsion | Cross Member: connects side members and adds stiffness | Frame Material: pressed steel channel sections
- **Q2(a):** Castor Angle: backward tilt of kingpin giving self-centring
- **Q2(b):** Ackermann Mechanism: approximates true rolling in turns | Davis Mechanism: exact steering using sliding pairs | Turning Radius: radius of circle traced by outer wheel
- **Q3(a):** Multi-link Suspension: several links controlling wheel motion
- **Q3(b):** Shock Absorber: converts oscillation energy to heat | Anti-roll Bar: reduces body roll in turns | Active Suspension: electronically adjusts damping or height
- **Q4(a):** Hotchkiss Drive: leaf springs absorb torque reaction
- **Q4(b):** Final Drive Ratio: crown wheel teeth / pinion teeth | Hypoid Gear: offset pinion allowing lower floor | Crown Wheel and Pinion: bevel gear pair in rear axle
- **Q5(a):** Alloy Wheel: light aluminium alloy wheel
- **Q5(b):** Cross-ply Tyre: plies at angles; stiff sidewall | Radial Ply Tyre: radial plies with belts | Tyre Wear Patterns: indicate alignment or inflation faults
- **Q6(a):** Centre of Gravity: point where vehicle weight acts
- **Q6(b):** Conventional Frame: separate ladder frame with body bolted on | Integral Frame: unitary body construction | Semi-integral Frame: partial integration of body and frame
- **Q7(a):** Sprung Mass: mass supported by springs
- **Q7(b):** Leaf Spring: laminated steel spring | Coil Spring: helical spring in independent suspensions | Torsion Bar: twisting bar acting as a spring
- **Q8(a):** Radial Ply Tyre: radial plies with belts
- **Q8(b):** Mechanical Brake: cable or rod operated | Hydraulic Brake: master and wheel cylinders | Air Brake: compressed air brakes in heavy vehicles
