# ME-801 Refrigeration & Air Conditioning — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Refrigeration cycles, refrigerants, vapour absorption, psychrometry, air conditioning systems and load calculation.

## Unit 1: Refrigeration Fundamentals

### Refrigeration Basics

Refrigeration removes heat from a low-temperature space.

- **Refrigeration:** process of removing heat from a space below ambient
- **Ton of Refrigeration:** 3.5 kW, heat to freeze 1 ton of water in 24 h
- **COP:** refrigerating effect / work input

### Reversed Carnot Cycle

The ideal refrigeration cycle.

- **Reversed Carnot COP:** T2/(T1 − T2)
- **Limitations:** Carnot cycle is impractical due to wet compression
- **Heat Pump COP:** T1/(T1 − T2)

### Air Refrigeration

Air cycles serve aircraft cooling.

- **Bell-Coleman Cycle:** reversed Brayton air refrigeration cycle
- **Aircraft Refrigeration:** simple, bootstrap and regenerative systems
- **Low COP:** air cycles have lower COP than vapour cycles

### Refrigerants

Working fluids are chosen for properties and safety.

- **R-134a:** HFC refrigerant with zero ozone depletion
- **ODP:** ozone depletion potential
- **GWP:** global warming potential

## Unit 2: Vapour Compression Refrigeration

### Simple VCR Cycle

VCR is the most common system.

- **Vapour Compression Cycle:** compression, condensation, expansion and evaporation
- **Refrigerating Effect:** h1 − h4
- **Compressor Work:** h2 − h1

### p-h & T-s Diagrams

Charts analyse VCR cycles.

- **p-h Diagram:** pressure-enthalpy chart for refrigerants
- **Throttling Process:** constant enthalpy expansion
- **Saturation Dome:** region of wet vapour

### Subcooling & Superheating

Modifications improve the cycle.

- **Subcooling:** cooling liquid below saturation increases refrigerating effect
- **Superheating:** vapour heated above saturation at evaporator exit
- **Volumetric Efficiency:** affects compressor capacity

### Multistage & Cascade Systems

Low temperatures need staging.

- **Multistage Compression:** reduces work for large pressure ratios
- **Flash Chamber:** separates flash vapour between stages
- **Cascade System:** two cycles with different refrigerants

## Unit 3: Vapour Absorption & Other Systems

### Vapour Absorption System

Heat drives the absorption cycle.

- **Vapour Absorption System:** absorber, generator and pump replace compressor
- **NH3-Water System:** ammonia refrigerant, water absorbent
- **LiBr-Water System:** water refrigerant, lithium bromide absorbent

### Electrolux Refrigerator

Three-fluid system without pump.

- **Electrolux Refrigerator:** uses ammonia, water and hydrogen
- **No Moving Parts:** silent operation
- **Partial Pressure Principle:** hydrogen allows evaporation at low partial pressure

### Steam Jet & Thermoelectric Refrigeration

Other methods for niche uses.

- **Steam Jet Refrigeration:** uses steam ejector with water refrigerant
- **Thermoelectric Refrigeration:** Peltier effect
- **Vortex Tube:** separates compressed air into hot and cold streams

### Refrigeration Components

Hardware of refrigeration systems.

- **Reciprocating Compressor:** common in small systems
- **Thermostatic Expansion Valve:** maintains constant superheat
- **Evaporator:** coil where refrigerant absorbs heat

## Unit 4: Psychrometry

### Psychrometric Properties

Moist air properties govern comfort.

- **Specific Humidity:** mass of water vapour per kg of dry air
- **Relative Humidity:** vapour pressure / saturation vapour pressure
- **Dew Point:** temperature at which condensation starts

### Psychrometric Chart

Chart displays moist air properties.

- **Dry Bulb Temperature:** air temperature by ordinary thermometer
- **Wet Bulb Temperature:** temperature from wetted-bulb thermometer
- **Enthalpy of Moist Air:** h = 1.005t + ω(2500 + 1.88t)

### Psychrometric Processes

Air conditioning changes air state.

- **Sensible Heating:** temperature rise without moisture change
- **Cooling and Dehumidification:** cooling below dew point removes moisture
- **Adiabatic Mixing:** mixing two air streams

### Bypass Factor & Sensible Heat Factor

Coil performance and load ratio.

- **Bypass Factor:** fraction of air not contacting the coil
- **Sensible Heat Factor:** sensible heat / total heat
- **ADP:** apparatus dew point of cooling coil

## Unit 5: Air Conditioning Systems

### Human Comfort

Comfort depends on temperature, humidity and air motion.

- **Effective Temperature:** index combining temperature, humidity and air velocity
- **Comfort Zone:** conditions where most people feel comfortable
- **Metabolic Heat:** heat produced by human body

### Cooling Load Calculation

Loads determine system size.

- **Sensible Heat Load:** load from temperature difference
- **Latent Heat Load:** load from moisture
- **Solar Heat Gain:** heat through windows and walls

### Air Conditioning Systems

Systems differ in layout and application.

- **Window AC:** unitary system for single rooms
- **Split AC:** indoor and outdoor units connected by piping
- **Central AC:** chilled water or ducted systems for buildings

### Duct Design & Automotive AC

Air distribution and vehicle cooling.

- **Duct Design:** equal friction or velocity reduction method
- **AHU:** air handling unit filtering and conditioning air
- **Automotive AC:** belt-driven compressor system in vehicles
