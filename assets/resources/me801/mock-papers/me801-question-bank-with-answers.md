# ME-801 Refrigeration & Air Conditioning — Question Bank with Answer Hints

## Unit 1: Refrigeration Fundamentals

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Refrigeration*.  
  **Ans.** process of removing heat from a space below ambient
- **Q.** Define *Ton of Refrigeration*.  
  **Ans.** 3.5 kW, heat to freeze 1 ton of water in 24 h
- **Q.** Define *COP*.  
  **Ans.** refrigerating effect / work input
- **Q.** Define *Reversed Carnot COP*.  
  **Ans.** T2/(T1 − T2)
- **Q.** Define *Limitations*.  
  **Ans.** Carnot cycle is impractical due to wet compression
- **Q.** Define *Heat Pump COP*.  
  **Ans.** T1/(T1 − T2)
- **Q.** Define *Bell-Coleman Cycle*.  
  **Ans.** reversed Brayton air refrigeration cycle
- **Q.** Define *Aircraft Refrigeration*.  
  **Ans.** simple, bootstrap and regenerative systems
- **Q.** Define *Low COP*.  
  **Ans.** air cycles have lower COP than vapour cycles
- **Q.** Define *R-134a*.  
  **Ans.** HFC refrigerant with zero ozone depletion
- **Q.** Define *ODP*.  
  **Ans.** ozone depletion potential
- **Q.** Define *GWP*.  
  **Ans.** global warming potential

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Refrigeration Basics* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Refrigeration removes heat from a low-temperature space. Refrigeration — process of removing heat from a space below ambient; Ton of Refrigeration — 3.5 kW, heat to freeze 1 ton of water in 24 h; COP — refrigerating effect / work input.
- **Q.** Explain *Reversed Carnot Cycle* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** The ideal refrigeration cycle. Reversed Carnot COP — T2/(T1 − T2); Limitations — Carnot cycle is impractical due to wet compression; Heat Pump COP — T1/(T1 − T2).
- **Q.** Explain *Air Refrigeration* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Air cycles serve aircraft cooling. Bell-Coleman Cycle — reversed Brayton air refrigeration cycle; Aircraft Refrigeration — simple, bootstrap and regenerative systems; Low COP — air cycles have lower COP than vapour cycles.
- **Q.** Explain *Refrigerants* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Working fluids are chosen for properties and safety. R-134a — HFC refrigerant with zero ozone depletion; ODP — ozone depletion potential; GWP — global warming potential.

## Unit 2: Vapour Compression Refrigeration

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Vapour Compression Cycle*.  
  **Ans.** compression, condensation, expansion and evaporation
- **Q.** Define *Refrigerating Effect*.  
  **Ans.** h1 − h4
- **Q.** Define *Compressor Work*.  
  **Ans.** h2 − h1
- **Q.** Define *p-h Diagram*.  
  **Ans.** pressure-enthalpy chart for refrigerants
- **Q.** Define *Throttling Process*.  
  **Ans.** constant enthalpy expansion
- **Q.** Define *Saturation Dome*.  
  **Ans.** region of wet vapour
- **Q.** Define *Subcooling*.  
  **Ans.** cooling liquid below saturation increases refrigerating effect
- **Q.** Define *Superheating*.  
  **Ans.** vapour heated above saturation at evaporator exit
- **Q.** Define *Volumetric Efficiency*.  
  **Ans.** affects compressor capacity
- **Q.** Define *Multistage Compression*.  
  **Ans.** reduces work for large pressure ratios
- **Q.** Define *Flash Chamber*.  
  **Ans.** separates flash vapour between stages
- **Q.** Define *Cascade System*.  
  **Ans.** two cycles with different refrigerants

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Simple VCR Cycle* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** VCR is the most common system. Vapour Compression Cycle — compression, condensation, expansion and evaporation; Refrigerating Effect — h1 − h4; Compressor Work — h2 − h1.
- **Q.** Explain *p-h & T-s Diagrams* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Charts analyse VCR cycles. p-h Diagram — pressure-enthalpy chart for refrigerants; Throttling Process — constant enthalpy expansion; Saturation Dome — region of wet vapour.
- **Q.** Explain *Subcooling & Superheating* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Modifications improve the cycle. Subcooling — cooling liquid below saturation increases refrigerating effect; Superheating — vapour heated above saturation at evaporator exit; Volumetric Efficiency — affects compressor capacity.
- **Q.** Explain *Multistage & Cascade Systems* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Low temperatures need staging. Multistage Compression — reduces work for large pressure ratios; Flash Chamber — separates flash vapour between stages; Cascade System — two cycles with different refrigerants.

## Unit 4: Psychrometry

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Specific Humidity*.  
  **Ans.** mass of water vapour per kg of dry air
- **Q.** Define *Relative Humidity*.  
  **Ans.** vapour pressure / saturation vapour pressure
- **Q.** Define *Dew Point*.  
  **Ans.** temperature at which condensation starts
- **Q.** Define *Dry Bulb Temperature*.  
  **Ans.** air temperature by ordinary thermometer
- **Q.** Define *Wet Bulb Temperature*.  
  **Ans.** temperature from wetted-bulb thermometer
- **Q.** Define *Enthalpy of Moist Air*.  
  **Ans.** h = 1.005t + ω(2500 + 1.88t)
- **Q.** Define *Sensible Heating*.  
  **Ans.** temperature rise without moisture change
- **Q.** Define *Cooling and Dehumidification*.  
  **Ans.** cooling below dew point removes moisture
- **Q.** Define *Adiabatic Mixing*.  
  **Ans.** mixing two air streams
- **Q.** Define *Bypass Factor*.  
  **Ans.** fraction of air not contacting the coil
- **Q.** Define *Sensible Heat Factor*.  
  **Ans.** sensible heat / total heat
- **Q.** Define *ADP*.  
  **Ans.** apparatus dew point of cooling coil

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Psychrometric Properties* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Moist air properties govern comfort. Specific Humidity — mass of water vapour per kg of dry air; Relative Humidity — vapour pressure / saturation vapour pressure; Dew Point — temperature at which condensation starts.
- **Q.** Explain *Psychrometric Chart* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Chart displays moist air properties. Dry Bulb Temperature — air temperature by ordinary thermometer; Wet Bulb Temperature — temperature from wetted-bulb thermometer; Enthalpy of Moist Air — h = 1.005t + ω(2500 + 1.88t).
- **Q.** Explain *Psychrometric Processes* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Air conditioning changes air state. Sensible Heating — temperature rise without moisture change; Cooling and Dehumidification — cooling below dew point removes moisture; Adiabatic Mixing — mixing two air streams.
- **Q.** Explain *Bypass Factor & Sensible Heat Factor* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Coil performance and load ratio. Bypass Factor — fraction of air not contacting the coil; Sensible Heat Factor — sensible heat / total heat; ADP — apparatus dew point of cooling coil.
