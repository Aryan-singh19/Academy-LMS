## Unit 3: Queuing & Game Theory

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

  **Ans.** λ customers per unit time
- **Q.** Define *Service Rate*.  
  **Ans.** μ customers per unit time
- **Q.** Define *Kendall Notation*.  
  **Ans.** A/B/c/K/N/D describes a queue
- **Q.** Define *Utilisation*.  
  **Ans.** ρ = λ/μ
- **Q.** Define *Average Number in System*.  
  **Ans.** Ls = λ/(μ − λ)
- **Q.** Define *Average Waiting Time in Queue*.  
  **Ans.** Wq = λ/(μ(μ − λ))
- **Q.** Define *Two-person Zero-sum Game*.  
  **Ans.** one's gain is the other's loss
- **Q.** Define *Saddle Point*.  
  **Ans.** maximin equals minimax
- **Q.** Define *Mixed Strategy*.  
  **Ans.** players randomise among strategies
- **Q.** Define *Maximin Criterion*.  
  **Ans.** choose best of worst outcomes
- **Q.** Define *Hurwicz Criterion*.  
  **Ans.** weighted optimism-pessimism
- **Q.** Define *Expected Monetary Value*.  
  **Ans.** probability-weighted payoff

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Queuing Fundamentals* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Queuing models study waiting lines. Arrival Rate — λ customers per unit time; Service Rate — μ customers per unit time; Kendall Notation — A/B/c/K/N/D describes a queue.
- **Q.** Explain *M/M/1 Model* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Single-server Poisson queue. Utilisation — ρ = λ/μ; Average Number in System — Ls = λ/(μ − λ); Average Waiting Time in Queue — Wq = λ/(μ(μ − λ)).
- **Q.** Explain *Game Theory* in detail with a neat diagram.

- **Q.** Explain *Decision Theory* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Decisions under uncertainty use criteria. Maximin Criterion — choose best of worst outcomes; Hurwicz Criterion — weighted optimism-pessimism; Expected Monetary Value — probability-weighted payoff.
