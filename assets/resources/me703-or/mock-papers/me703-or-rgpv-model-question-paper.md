## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Linear Programming: optimising linear objective subject to linear constraints
- **Q1(b):** Corner Point Theorem: optimum lies at a vertex of the feasible region | Unbounded Solution: objective can increase indefinitely | Infeasible Problem: no point satisfies all constraints
- **Q2(a):** Degeneracy: fewer than m + n − 1 allocations
- **Q2(b):** Assignment Problem: one-to-one allocation minimising cost | Hungarian Method: row and column reduction to find optimum | Prohibited Assignment: given very high cost
- **Q3(a):** Mixed Strategy: players randomise among strategies
- **Q3(b):** Maximin Criterion: choose best of worst outcomes | Hurwicz Criterion: weighted optimism-pessimism | Expected Monetary Value: probability-weighted payoff
- **Q4(a):** PERT Expected Time: te = (to + 4tm + tp)/6
- **Q4(b):** EOQ: √(2DS/H) | Reorder Point: demand during lead time | Safety Stock: buffer against demand variation
- **Q5(a):** Supply Chain Management: coordinating flows of material, information and money
- **Q5(b):** Bullwhip Effect: demand fluctuations amplify up the supply chain | Information Sharing: reduces bullwhip effect | Vendor Managed Inventory: supplier manages customer stock
- **Q6(a):** Sensitivity Analysis: effect of parameter changes on optimum
- **Q6(b):** Linear Programming: optimising linear objective subject to linear constraints | Decision Variables: quantities to be determined | Feasible Region: set of points satisfying all constraints
- **Q7(a):** Arrival Rate: λ customers per unit time
- **Q7(b):** Utilisation: ρ = λ/μ | Average Number in System: Ls = λ/(μ − λ) | Average Waiting Time in Queue: Wq = λ/(μ(μ − λ))
- **Q8(a):** Information Sharing: reduces bullwhip effect
- **Q8(b):** Logistics: planning transport and storage | Cross-docking: direct transfer from inbound to outbound | Third-party Logistics: outsourced logistics services
