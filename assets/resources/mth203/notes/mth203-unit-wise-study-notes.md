# MTH-203 Advanced Refrigeration & Cryogenics — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Advanced vapour compression and absorption systems, refrigerants, cryogenic liquefaction, separation and storage.

## Unit 1: Advanced Vapour Compression

### Multistage Systems

Staging improves low-temperature performance.

- **Two-stage Compression:** with flash intercooling
- **Optimum Intermediate Pressure:** √(p1 p2) approximately
- **Flash Gas Removal:** improves efficiency

### Cascade Refrigeration

Two cycles reach very low temperatures.

- **Cascade System:** high-temperature and low-temperature cycles
- **Cascade Condenser:** links two cycles
- **Refrigerant Selection:** suitable for each temperature range

### Component Matching

Components must balance.

- **Compressor Capacity:** must match evaporator load
- **Balance Point:** equilibrium of components
- **Capillary Tube:** fixed expansion device

### Transcritical CO2 Cycles

Natural refrigerant systems.

- **Transcritical Cycle:** heat rejection above critical point
- **Gas Cooler:** replaces condenser
- **Ejector:** recovers expansion work

## Unit 2: Absorption & Alternative Refrigeration

### Vapour Absorption Advanced

Heat-driven refrigeration.

- **Double-effect Absorption:** uses heat twice
- **LiBr-Water System:** for air conditioning
- **Crystallisation:** risk in LiBr systems

### Adsorption Refrigeration

Solid adsorbents.

- **Adsorption Chiller:** silica gel-water pair
- **Low-grade Heat:** drives adsorption cycles
- **Intermittent Operation:** adsorption and desorption cycles

### Ejector & Thermoelectric

Other methods.

- **Steam Ejector Refrigeration:** jet pump compression
- **Thermoelectric Cooling:** Peltier effect
- **Figure of Merit ZT:** thermoelectric material performance

### Magnetic & Vortex Cooling

Emerging technologies.

- **Magnetocaloric Effect:** temperature change with magnetisation
- **Vortex Tube:** separates hot and cold air
- **Pulse Tube Cooler:** cryocooler without moving parts at cold end

## Unit 3: Refrigerants & Environment

### Refrigerant Properties

Selecting refrigerants.

- **ODP:** ozone depletion potential
- **GWP:** global warming potential
- **Glide:** temperature change during phase change in blends

### Refrigerant Phase-out

Regulations drive change.

- **Montreal Protocol:** phased out CFCs
- **Kigali Amendment:** phase-down of HFCs
- **HFO Refrigerants:** low-GWP alternatives such as R1234yf

### Natural Refrigerants

Environment-friendly options.

- **Ammonia R717:** efficient industrial refrigerant
- **CO2 R744:** natural refrigerant with GWP 1
- **Hydrocarbons:** R290 propane, R600a isobutane

### Refrigerant Safety

Flammability and toxicity.

- **Safety Classification:** A1 to B3 classes
- **Leak Detection:** sensors and soap tests
- **Charge Limits:** restrict flammable refrigerant quantity

## Unit 4: Cryogenics

### Cryogenic Fundamentals

Very low temperatures below 123 K.

- **Cryogenics:** science of temperatures below about 123 K
- **Liquid Nitrogen:** boils at 77 K
- **Liquid Helium:** boils at 4.2 K

### Gas Liquefaction

Producing cryogenic liquids.

- **Linde-Hampson Cycle:** Joule-Thomson expansion liquefaction
- **Claude Cycle:** expansion engine plus JT valve
- **Inversion Temperature:** above which JT expansion heats gas

### Gas Separation

Separating air components.

- **Air Separation Unit:** distillation of liquid air
- **Rectification Column:** separates nitrogen and oxygen
- **Pressure Swing Adsorption:** non-cryogenic separation

### Cryocoolers

Small cryogenic refrigerators.

- **Stirling Cryocooler:** regenerative cycle cooler
- **Gifford-McMahon Cooler:** for MRI and research
- **Regenerator:** periodic heat storage matrix

## Unit 5: Cryogenic Systems & Applications

### Cryogenic Insulation

Minimising heat leak.

- **Multilayer Insulation:** reflective layers in vacuum
- **Vacuum Jacket:** eliminates convection
- **Dewar Vessel:** vacuum-insulated container

### Storage & Transfer

Handling cryogenic liquids.

- **Boil-off:** evaporation due to heat leak
- **Transfer Line:** vacuum-insulated piping
- **Cool-down:** bringing equipment to cryogenic temperature

### Material Properties at Low Temperature

Materials change behaviour.

- **Ductile-brittle Transition:** carbon steels become brittle
- **Austenitic Stainless Steel:** suitable for cryogenic use
- **Thermal Contraction:** large shrinkage on cooling

### Applications

Cryogenics in industry and science.

- **MRI Magnets:** superconducting magnets cooled by helium
- **Rocket Propellants:** liquid hydrogen and oxygen
- **Cryopreservation:** biological storage at low temperature
