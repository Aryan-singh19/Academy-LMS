## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Electric Vehicle: vehicle propelled by electric motor from battery
- **Q1(b):** Battery Pack: energy storage | Traction Motor: drives wheels | Power Electronics Controller: controls motor power
- **Q2(a):** Slip: (Ns − N)/Ns
- **Q2(b):** PMSM: permanent magnet synchronous motor | BLDC Motor: brushless DC motor with electronic commutation | Rare Earth Magnets: neodymium magnets used in PM motors
- **Q3(a):** Thermal Runaway: uncontrolled heating leading to fire
- **Q3(b):** Supercapacitor: high power density storage | Flywheel Storage: kinetic energy storage | Hybrid Energy Storage: battery with supercapacitor
- **Q4(a):** Charging Station: public EV charging point
- **Q4(b):** DC-DC Converter: changes DC voltage levels | Inverter: converts DC to AC for motor | Bidirectional Converter: allows regenerative flow
- **Q5(a):** Degree of Hybridisation: motor power / total power
- **Q5(b):** Series Hybrid: engine charges battery; motor drives wheels | Range Extender: small engine generating electricity | Advantage: engine runs at optimal point
- **Q6(a):** Energy Consumption: Wh per km
- **Q6(b):** Electric Vehicle: vehicle propelled by electric motor from battery | Zero Tailpipe Emission: EVs emit no exhaust | Well-to-wheel Efficiency: efficiency from energy source to wheels
- **Q7(a):** Li-ion Battery: high energy density; common in EVs
- **Q7(b):** State of Charge: remaining capacity as percentage | Depth of Discharge: fraction of capacity used | C-rate: charge or discharge current relative to capacity
- **Q8(a):** Range Extender: small engine generating electricity
- **Q8(b):** Parallel Hybrid: mechanical coupling of engine and motor | Torque Coupling: combining torque from both sources | Advantage: fewer energy conversions
