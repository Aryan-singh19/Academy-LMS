  **Ans.** high starting torque
- **Q.** Define *Brushed DC Motor*.  
  **Ans.** commutator and brushes
- **Q.** Define *Speed Control*.  
  **Ans.** varying armature voltage
- **Q.** Define *Induction Motor*.  
  **Ans.** rotor current induced by stator field
- **Q.** Define *Slip*.  
  **Ans.** (Ns − N)/Ns
- **Q.** Define *Field-oriented Control*.  
  **Ans.** vector control of induction motors
- **Q.** Define *PMSM*.  
  **Ans.** permanent magnet synchronous motor
- **Q.** Define *BLDC Motor*.  
  **Ans.** brushless DC motor with electronic commutation
- **Q.** Define *Rare Earth Magnets*.  
  **Ans.** neodymium magnets used in PM motors
- **Q.** Define *SRM*.  
  **Ans.** switched reluctance motor
- **Q.** Define *Torque Ripple*.  
  **Ans.** drawback of SRM
- **Q.** Define *Rugged Rotor*.  
  **Ans.** no windings or magnets on rotor

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *DC Motors* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** DC motors were used in early EVs. DC Series Motor — high starting torque; Brushed DC Motor — commutator and brushes; Speed Control — varying armature voltage.
- **Q.** Explain *Induction Motors* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Induction motors are rugged. Induction Motor — rotor current induced by stator field; Slip — (Ns − N)/Ns; Field-oriented Control — vector control of induction motors.
- **Q.** Explain *Permanent Magnet Motors* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** PM motors are efficient and compact. PMSM — permanent magnet synchronous motor; BLDC Motor — brushless DC motor with electronic commutation; Rare Earth Magnets — neodymium magnets used in PM motors.
- **Q.** Explain *Switched Reluctance Motors* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** SRM avoids magnets. SRM — switched reluctance motor; Torque Ripple — drawback of SRM; Rugged Rotor — no windings or magnets on rotor.

- **Q.** Define *Degree of Hybridisation*.  
  **Ans.** motor power / total power
- **Q.** Define *Mild Hybrid*.  
  **Ans.** small motor assisting engine
- **Q.** Define *Series Hybrid*.  
  **Ans.** engine charges battery; motor drives wheels
- **Q.** Define *Range Extender*.  
  **Ans.** small engine generating electricity
- **Q.** Define *Advantage*.  
  **Ans.** engine runs at optimal point
- **Q.** Define *Parallel Hybrid*.  
  **Ans.** mechanical coupling of engine and motor
- **Q.** Define *Torque Coupling*.  
  **Ans.** combining torque from both sources
- **Q.** Define *Advantage*.  
  **Ans.** fewer energy conversions
- **Q.** Define *Power-split Hybrid*.  
  **Ans.** planetary gear splits engine power
- **Q.** Define *Energy Management Strategy*.  
  **Ans.** decides power split
- **Q.** Define *Toyota Hybrid System*.  
  **Ans.** well-known power-split design

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Hybrid Concepts* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Hybrids combine engine and motor. HEV — hybrid electric vehicle; Degree of Hybridisation — motor power / total power; Mild Hybrid — small motor assisting engine.
- **Q.** Explain *Series Hybrid* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Engine drives generator only. Series Hybrid — engine charges battery; motor drives wheels; Range Extender — small engine generating electricity; Advantage — engine runs at optimal point.
- **Q.** Explain *Parallel Hybrid* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Engine and motor both drive wheels. Parallel Hybrid — mechanical coupling of engine and motor; Torque Coupling — combining torque from both sources; Advantage — fewer energy conversions.
- **Q.** Explain *Series-Parallel Hybrid & Control* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Power-split systems combine both. Power-split Hybrid — planetary gear splits engine power; Energy Management Strategy — decides power split; Toyota Hybrid System — well-known power-split design.
