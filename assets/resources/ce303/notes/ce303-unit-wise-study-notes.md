# CE-303 Surveying — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Chain, compass, levelling, plane table, theodolite, tacheometry, curves and modern surveying instruments.

## Unit 1: Chain & Compass Surveying

### Principles of Surveying

Surveying fixes relative positions of points.

- **Plane Surveying:** Earth's curvature neglected
- **Geodetic Surveying:** curvature considered for large areas
- **Working from Whole to Part:** limits accumulation of errors

### Chain Surveying

Distances are measured with chain or tape.

- **Survey Station:** point at the end of a chain line
- **Offset:** lateral measurement to locate details
- **Ranging:** establishing intermediate points on a line

### Compass Surveying

Compass measures bearings of lines.

- **Prismatic Compass:** reads whole circle bearing
- **Magnetic Declination:** angle between true and magnetic meridian
- **Local Attraction:** disturbance of compass needle by nearby iron

### Traversing

Traverses link survey stations.

- **Closed Traverse:** starts and ends at same point
- **Closing Error:** misclosure of traverse
- **Bowditch Rule:** distributes closing error in proportion to line lengths

## Unit 2: Levelling & Contouring

### Levelling Fundamentals

Levelling finds difference in elevation.

- **Benchmark:** point of known reduced level
- **Backsight:** first reading after setting up instrument
- **Foresight:** last reading before shifting instrument

### Reduction of Levels

Readings are converted to reduced levels.

- **Height of Collimation Method:** RL = HI − staff reading
- **Rise and Fall Method:** compares consecutive readings
- **Arithmetic Check:** ΣBS − ΣFS = last RL − first RL

### Errors & Special Levelling

Curvature and refraction affect long sights.

- **Curvature Correction:** −0.0785 d² m with d in km
- **Reciprocal Levelling:** eliminates instrument and curvature errors
- **Profile Levelling:** levels along a route for a section

### Contouring

Contours show relief on maps.

- **Contour Line:** line joining points of equal elevation
- **Contour Interval:** vertical distance between contours
- **Contour Characteristics:** closely spaced contours indicate steep slope

## Unit 3: Plane Table & Theodolite

### Plane Table Surveying

Plotting is done in the field.

- **Radiation Method:** locating points from one station
- **Intersection Method:** locating points by rays from two stations
- **Resection:** locating the plane table station itself

### Theodolite

Theodolite measures horizontal and vertical angles.

- **Transit Theodolite:** telescope can be transited
- **Face Left and Face Right:** observations to eliminate errors
- **Temporary Adjustments:** setting up, centring, levelling and focusing

### Measurement of Angles

Methods improve accuracy.

- **Repetition Method:** measures angle several times cumulatively
- **Reiteration Method:** measures several angles from one station
- **Vertical Angle:** angle from horizontal to line of sight

### Traverse Computation

Coordinates are computed from angles and lengths.

- **Latitude:** L cos θ
- **Departure:** L sin θ
- **Consecutive Coordinates:** latitude and departure of each line

## Unit 4: Tacheometry & Curves

### Tacheometry

Distances measured optically.

- **Tacheometric Constant:** multiplying constant usually 100
- **Stadia Method:** distance from staff intercept
- **Horizontal Distance:** D = ks + C for horizontal sight

### Simple Circular Curves

Curves connect straights on roads.

- **Degree of Curve:** angle subtended by 30 m chord
- **Tangent Length:** R tan(Δ/2)
- **Rankine's Method:** setting out curve by deflection angles

### Transition Curves

Gradual change of curvature improves comfort.

- **Transition Curve:** curvature increases gradually
- **Clothoid:** spiral with curvature proportional to length
- **Superelevation:** raising outer edge on curves

### Vertical Curves

Curves join different gradients.

- **Summit Curve:** vertical curve with convexity upward
- **Valley Curve:** vertical curve with concavity upward
- **Parabolic Curve:** commonly used vertical curve

## Unit 5: Modern Surveying

### Electronic Distance Measurement

EDM uses electromagnetic waves.

- **EDM:** measures distance by phase of modulated wave
- **Total Station:** electronic theodolite with EDM
- **Prism Reflector:** target returning EDM signal

### GPS & GNSS

Satellites give positions.

- **GNSS:** global navigation satellite systems such as GPS and NavIC
- **DGPS:** differential GPS improving accuracy
- **RTK:** real-time kinematic centimetre-level positioning

### Remote Sensing in Surveying

Aerial and satellite imagery map large areas.

- **Aerial Photogrammetry:** measurements from overlapping photographs
- **Stereoscopic Vision:** depth from overlapping images
- **LiDAR:** laser scanning to create elevation models

### GIS Basics

GIS stores and analyses spatial data.

- **GIS:** geographic information system
- **Vector Data:** points, lines and polygons
- **Raster Data:** grid of cells with values
