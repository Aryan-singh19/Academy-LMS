# CE-303 Surveying — Question Bank with Answer Hints

## Unit 1: Chain & Compass Surveying

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Plane Surveying*.  
  **Ans.** Earth's curvature neglected
- **Q.** Define *Geodetic Surveying*.  
  **Ans.** curvature considered for large areas
- **Q.** Define *Working from Whole to Part*.  
  **Ans.** limits accumulation of errors
- **Q.** Define *Survey Station*.  
  **Ans.** point at the end of a chain line
- **Q.** Define *Offset*.  
  **Ans.** lateral measurement to locate details
- **Q.** Define *Ranging*.  
  **Ans.** establishing intermediate points on a line
- **Q.** Define *Prismatic Compass*.  
  **Ans.** reads whole circle bearing
- **Q.** Define *Magnetic Declination*.  
  **Ans.** angle between true and magnetic meridian
- **Q.** Define *Local Attraction*.  
  **Ans.** disturbance of compass needle by nearby iron
- **Q.** Define *Closed Traverse*.  
  **Ans.** starts and ends at same point
- **Q.** Define *Closing Error*.  
  **Ans.** misclosure of traverse
- **Q.** Define *Bowditch Rule*.  
  **Ans.** distributes closing error in proportion to line lengths

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Principles of Surveying* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Surveying fixes relative positions of points. Plane Surveying — Earth's curvature neglected; Geodetic Surveying — curvature considered for large areas; Working from Whole to Part — limits accumulation of errors.
- **Q.** Explain *Chain Surveying* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Distances are measured with chain or tape. Survey Station — point at the end of a chain line; Offset — lateral measurement to locate details; Ranging — establishing intermediate points on a line.
- **Q.** Explain *Compass Surveying* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Compass measures bearings of lines. Prismatic Compass — reads whole circle bearing; Magnetic Declination — angle between true and magnetic meridian; Local Attraction — disturbance of compass needle by nearby iron.
- **Q.** Explain *Traversing* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Traverses link survey stations. Closed Traverse — starts and ends at same point; Closing Error — misclosure of traverse; Bowditch Rule — distributes closing error in proportion to line lengths.

## Unit 2: Levelling & Contouring

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Benchmark*.  
  **Ans.** point of known reduced level
- **Q.** Define *Backsight*.  
  **Ans.** first reading after setting up instrument
- **Q.** Define *Foresight*.  
  **Ans.** last reading before shifting instrument
- **Q.** Define *Height of Collimation Method*.  
  **Ans.** RL = HI − staff reading
- **Q.** Define *Rise and Fall Method*.  
  **Ans.** compares consecutive readings
- **Q.** Define *Arithmetic Check*.  
  **Ans.** ΣBS − ΣFS = last RL − first RL
- **Q.** Define *Curvature Correction*.  
  **Ans.** −0.0785 d² m with d in km
- **Q.** Define *Reciprocal Levelling*.  
  **Ans.** eliminates instrument and curvature errors
- **Q.** Define *Profile Levelling*.  
  **Ans.** levels along a route for a section
- **Q.** Define *Contour Line*.  
  **Ans.** line joining points of equal elevation
- **Q.** Define *Contour Interval*.  
  **Ans.** vertical distance between contours
- **Q.** Define *Contour Characteristics*.  
  **Ans.** closely spaced contours indicate steep slope

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Levelling Fundamentals* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Levelling finds difference in elevation. Benchmark — point of known reduced level; Backsight — first reading after setting up instrument; Foresight — last reading before shifting instrument.
- **Q.** Explain *Reduction of Levels* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Readings are converted to reduced levels. Height of Collimation Method — RL = HI − staff reading; Rise and Fall Method — compares consecutive readings; Arithmetic Check — ΣBS − ΣFS = last RL − first RL.
- **Q.** Explain *Errors & Special Levelling* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Curvature and refraction affect long sights. Curvature Correction — −0.0785 d² m with d in km; Reciprocal Levelling — eliminates instrument and curvature errors; Profile Levelling — levels along a route for a section.
- **Q.** Explain *Contouring* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Contours show relief on maps. Contour Line — line joining points of equal elevation; Contour Interval — vertical distance between contours; Contour Characteristics — closely spaced contours indicate steep slope.

- **Q.** Define *Valley Curve*.

### Long answer (7 marks)

## Unit 5: Modern Surveying

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *EDM*.  
  **Ans.** measures distance by phase of modulated wave
- **Q.** Define *Total Station*.  
  **Ans.** electronic theodolite with EDM
- **Q.** Define *Prism Reflector*.  
  **Ans.** target returning EDM signal
- **Q.** Define *GNSS*.  
  **Ans.** global navigation satellite systems such as GPS and NavIC
- **Q.** Define *DGPS*.  
  **Ans.** differential GPS improving accuracy
- **Q.** Define *RTK*.  
  **Ans.** real-time kinematic centimetre-level positioning
- **Q.** Define *Aerial Photogrammetry*.  
  **Ans.** measurements from overlapping photographs
- **Q.** Define *Stereoscopic Vision*.  
  **Ans.** depth from overlapping images
- **Q.** Define *LiDAR*.  
  **Ans.** laser scanning to create elevation models
- **Q.** Define *GIS*.  
  **Ans.** geographic information system
- **Q.** Define *Vector Data*.  
  **Ans.** points, lines and polygons
- **Q.** Define *Raster Data*.  
  **Ans.** grid of cells with values

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Electronic Distance Measurement* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** EDM uses electromagnetic waves. EDM — measures distance by phase of modulated wave; Total Station — electronic theodolite with EDM; Prism Reflector — target returning EDM signal.
- **Q.** Explain *GPS & GNSS* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Satellites give positions. GNSS — global navigation satellite systems such as GPS and NavIC; DGPS — differential GPS improving accuracy; RTK — real-time kinematic centimetre-level positioning.
- **Q.** Explain *Remote Sensing in Surveying* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Aerial and satellite imagery map large areas. Aerial Photogrammetry — measurements from overlapping photographs; Stereoscopic Vision — depth from overlapping images; LiDAR — laser scanning to create elevation models.
- **Q.** Explain *GIS Basics* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** GIS stores and analyses spatial data. GIS — geographic information system; Vector Data — points, lines and polygons; Raster Data — grid of cells with values.
