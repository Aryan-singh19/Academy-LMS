## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Plane Surveying: Earth's curvature neglected
- **Q1(b):** Survey Station: point at the end of a chain line | Offset: lateral measurement to locate details | Ranging: establishing intermediate points on a line
- **Q2(a):** Rise and Fall Method: compares consecutive readings
- **Q2(b):** Curvature Correction: −0.0785 d² m with d in km | Reciprocal Levelling: eliminates instrument and curvature errors | Profile Levelling: levels along a route for a section
- **Q3(a):** Vertical Angle: angle from horizontal to line of sight
- **Q3(b):** Latitude: L cos θ | Departure: L sin θ | Consecutive Coordinates: latitude and departure of each line
- **Q4(a):** Summit Curve: vertical curve with convexity upward
- **Q4(b):** Tacheometric Constant: multiplying constant usually 100 | Stadia Method: distance from staff intercept | Horizontal Distance: D = ks + C for horizontal sight
- **Q5(a):** Total Station: electronic theodolite with EDM
- **Q5(b):** GNSS: global navigation satellite systems such as GPS and NavIC | DGPS: differential GPS improving accuracy | RTK: real-time kinematic centimetre-level positioning
- **Q6(a):** Bowditch Rule: distributes closing error in proportion to line lengths
- **Q6(b):** Plane Surveying: Earth's curvature neglected | Geodetic Surveying: curvature considered for large areas | Working from Whole to Part: limits accumulation of errors
- **Q7(a):** Radiation Method: locating points from one station
- **Q7(b):** Transit Theodolite: telescope can be transited | Face Left and Face Right: observations to eliminate errors | Temporary Adjustments: setting up, centring, levelling and focusing
- **Q8(a):** DGPS: differential GPS improving accuracy
- **Q8(b):** Aerial Photogrammetry: measurements from overlapping photographs | Stereoscopic Vision: depth from overlapping images | LiDAR: laser scanning to create elevation models
