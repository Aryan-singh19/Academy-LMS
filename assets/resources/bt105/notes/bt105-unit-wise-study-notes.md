# BT-105 Engineering Graphics — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Drawing instruments, scales, curves, projections of points, lines, planes and solids, sections, developments and isometric views.

## Unit 1: Drawing Fundamentals

### Drawing Instruments & Conventions

Standard tools and conventions ensure clarity.

- **BIS SP-46:** Indian code for engineering drawing practice
- **Line Types:** continuous thick for visible, dashed for hidden edges
- **Lettering:** standard single-stroke letters

### Dimensioning

Dimensions specify size and location.

- **Aligned System:** dimensions read from bottom or right
- **Unidirectional System:** all dimensions read from bottom
- **Dimension Line:** thin line with arrowheads

### Scales

Scales represent objects at reduced or enlarged size.

- **Representative Fraction:** drawing size / actual size
- **Plain Scale:** reads two units, e.g. m and dm
- **Diagonal Scale:** reads three units using diagonals

### Vernier Scale

Vernier scales read small fractions.

- **Vernier Scale:** main and vernier scale for precise reading
- **Least Count:** one main division minus one vernier division
- **Scale of Chords:** used to set off angles

## Unit 2: Engineering Curves

### Conic Sections

Conics are sections of a cone.

- **Ellipse:** eccentricity less than 1
- **Parabola:** eccentricity equal to 1
- **Hyperbola:** eccentricity greater than 1

### Construction of Conics

Conics are drawn by standard methods.

- **Eccentricity Method:** distance from focus / distance from directrix
- **Concentric Circle Method:** draws ellipse from two circles
- **Rectangle Method:** draws ellipse or parabola in a rectangle

### Cycloidal Curves

Rolling circles generate cycloids.

- **Cycloid:** locus of a point on a circle rolling on a straight line
- **Epicycloid:** circle rolls outside another circle
- **Hypocycloid:** circle rolls inside another circle

### Involutes & Spirals

Involutes are used in gear teeth.

- **Involute:** locus of end of a string unwound from a circle
- **Archimedean Spiral:** radius increases uniformly with angle
- **Helix:** curve on a cylinder with uniform pitch

## Unit 3: Projections of Points, Lines & Planes

### Orthographic Projection

3D objects are shown in 2D views.

- **First Angle Projection:** object between observer and plane; used in India
- **Third Angle Projection:** plane between observer and object
- **Reference Line:** XY line separating views

### Projection of Points

Points are located by distances from planes.

- **Front View:** projection on vertical plane
- **Top View:** projection on horizontal plane
- **Quadrant:** position of point relative to HP and VP

### Projection of Lines

Inclined lines appear shortened.

- **True Length:** actual length of the line
- **Apparent Inclination:** inclination seen in a view
- **Traces:** points where line meets reference planes

### Projection of Planes

Planes are drawn in stages of inclination.

- **Plane Perpendicular to One Plane:** appears as a line in that view
- **Inclined Plane:** drawn in two or three stages
- **True Shape:** seen when plane is parallel to projection plane

## Unit 4: Projections & Sections of Solids

### Types of Solids

Solids are polyhedra or solids of revolution.

- **Prism:** two equal end faces joined by rectangles
- **Pyramid:** polygon base with triangular faces meeting at apex
- **Cone:** circular base tapering to apex

### Projection of Solids

Solids are projected in simple and inclined positions.

- **Axis Perpendicular to HP:** base seen as true shape in top view
- **Axis Inclined:** drawn in two stages
- **Generator:** line from apex to base edge of cone

### Sections of Solids

Cutting planes reveal interior shape.

- **Section Plane:** imaginary cutting plane
- **True Shape of Section:** seen on auxiliary plane parallel to section
- **Hatching:** thin lines at 45° indicating cut surface

### Development of Surfaces

Surfaces are unfolded into flat patterns.

- **Parallel Line Development:** used for prisms and cylinders
- **Radial Line Development:** used for pyramids and cones
- **Application:** sheet metal work such as ducts and funnels

## Unit 5: Isometric & Computer Graphics

### Isometric Projection

Isometric views show three faces.

- **Isometric Axes:** three axes at 120°
- **Isometric Scale:** 0.816 of true length
- **Isometric View:** drawn with true lengths

### Conversion of Views

Orthographic and isometric views are interconverted.

- **Orthographic from Isometric:** draw front, top and side views
- **Isometric from Orthographic:** build up using box method
- **Hidden Lines:** shown dashed in orthographic views

### Introduction to CAD

CAD software creates drawings digitally.

- **CAD:** computer aided design
- **AutoCAD:** popular 2D and 3D drafting software
- **Layers:** organise drawing elements

### CAD Commands

Basic commands create and edit geometry.

- **LINE Command:** draws straight lines
- **TRIM Command:** cuts objects at boundaries
- **Coordinate Entry:** absolute, relative and polar inputs
