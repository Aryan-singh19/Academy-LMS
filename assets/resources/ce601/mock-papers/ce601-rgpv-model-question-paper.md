- **Q1(a):** Working Stress Method: elastic stresses within permissible limits

- **Q2(a):** Additional Moment: Mu − Mu,lim taken by compression steel
- **Q2(b):** Effective Flange Width: lo/6 + bw + 6Df for T-beam | Neutral Axis in Flange: rectangular beam behaviour | T-beam: monolithic beam and slab
- **Q3(a):** Cantilever Slab: balcony and chajja design
- **Q3(b):** Basic Span/Depth Ratio: 20 for simply supported | Modification Factor: depends on steel stress | Cracking: limited by bar spacing
- **Q4(a):** Combined Footing: supports two columns
- **Q4(b):** Short Column: effective length / lateral dimension < 12 | Axial Load Capacity: Pu = 0.4 fck Ac + 0.67 fy Asc | Minimum Eccentricity: l/500 + D/30, not less than 20 mm
- **Q5(a):** Waist Slab: inclined slab of stair
- **Q5(b):** Clear Cover: 20-75 mm depending on exposure | Lap Length: overlap for bar continuity | Bar Bending Schedule: list of bar shapes and lengths
- **Q6(a):** Steel Design Stress: 0.87 fy
- **Q6(b):** Working Stress Method: elastic stresses within permissible limits | Limit State Method: safety against collapse and serviceability | IS 456:2000: Indian code for plain and reinforced concrete
- **Q7(a):** One-way Slab: ly/lx > 2
