## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Antenna: transducer between guided and free-space waves
- **Q1(b):** Radiation Pattern: plot of radiated power versus direction | HPBW: half-power beamwidth between −3 dB points | Side Lobe: minor lobe outside the main beam
- **Q2(a):** Directivity of Dipole: 1.64 (2.15 dBi)
- **Q2(b):** Quarter-wave Monopole: λ/4 over ground, about 36.5 Ω | Image Theory: ground plane creates image doubling the antenna | Small Loop: magnetic-dipole-like radiator used in receivers
- **Q3(a):** Grating Lobe: unwanted lobe when spacing exceeds λ
- **Q3(b):** Yagi-Uda: driven dipole with reflector and directors | Reflector: slightly longer parasitic element behind | Director: shorter elements in front increasing gain
- **Q4(a):** Anechoic Chamber: room lined with absorber for measurement
- **Q4(b):** Horn Antenna: flared waveguide for microwave radiation | Pyramidal Horn: flared in both planes | Optimum Horn: flare chosen for maximum gain with acceptable phase error
- **Q5(a):** Surface Wave Attenuation: increases with frequency
- **Q5(b):** Ionosphere: ionised layers D, E, F1, F2 | Critical Frequency: highest frequency reflected at vertical incidence | MUF: maximum usable frequency = fc / cos θ
- **Q6(a):** Polarisation: orientation of radiated E-field
- **Q6(b):** Antenna: transducer between guided and free-space waves | Near Field: reactive region close to the antenna | Far Field: region beyond 2D²/λ where pattern is stable
- **Q7(a):** Array Factor: pattern of isotropic elements in the array
- **Q7(b):** Broadside Array: maximum perpendicular to array axis; zero phase difference | End-fire Array: maximum along the array axis | Hansen-Woodyard Condition: increased directivity for end-fire
- **Q8(a):** Critical Frequency: highest frequency reflected at vertical incidence
- **Q8(b):** Line-of-sight Range: d ≈ 4.12(√h1 + √h2) km with heights in metres | Radio Horizon: 4/3 Earth radius model for refraction | Duct Propagation: trapping in atmospheric layers
