## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Primary Sensing Element: first element receiving the measurand
- **Q1(b):** Sensitivity: output change per unit input change | Linearity: closeness of calibration curve to a straight line | Hysteresis: different output for increasing and decreasing input
- **Q2(a):** Wheatstone Bridge Circuit: converts resistance change to voltage
- **Q2(b):** Load Cell: elastic element with strain gauges | Proving Ring: elastic ring used to calibrate force | Hydraulic Load Cell: force measured by fluid pressure
- **Q3(a):** Coefficient of Discharge: actual flow / theoretical flow
- **Q3(b):** Rotameter: variable-area meter with float | Hot-wire Anemometer: measures velocity by heat loss | Electromagnetic Flowmeter: measures conductive fluid flow by induced emf
- **Q4(a):** Radiation Pyrometer: measures emitted thermal radiation
- **Q4(b):** Bimetallic Strip: two metals bend differently with temperature | Liquid-in-glass Thermometer: liquid expands in a capillary | Pressure Thermometer: gas or liquid pressure in a bulb
- **Q5(a):** Closed-loop Control: output compared with set point
- **Q5(b):** Transfer Function: Laplace output / input | Block Diagram: graphical representation of system | Closed-loop Gain: G/(1 + GH)
- **Q6(a):** Uncertainty Analysis: combining errors to estimate overall uncertainty
- **Q6(b):** Primary Sensing Element: first element receiving the measurand | Signal Conditioning: amplifying and filtering the signal | Data Presentation: display or record of the measurement
- **Q7(a):** Manometer: liquid column measuring pressure difference
- **Q7(b):** McLeod Gauge: compresses gas to measure vacuum | Pirani Gauge: thermal conductivity vacuum gauge | Ionisation Gauge: measures very high vacuum
- **Q8(a):** Block Diagram: graphical representation of system
- **Q8(b):** On-Off Control: two-position control with differential gap | Proportional Control: output proportional to error, leaves offset | PID Control: proportional, integral and derivative actions combined
