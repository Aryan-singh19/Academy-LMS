# ME-701 Heat & Mass Transfer — Question Bank with Answer Hints

## Unit 1: Conduction

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Fourier's Law*.  
  **Ans.** q = −kA dT/dx
- **Q.** Define *Newton's Law of Cooling*.  
  **Ans.** q = hA(Ts − T∞)
- **Q.** Define *Stefan-Boltzmann Law*.  
  **Ans.** E = σT⁴
- **Q.** Define *General Conduction Equation*.  
  **Ans.** ∇²T + q/k = (1/α)∂T/∂t
- **Q.** Define *Thermal Diffusivity*.  
  **Ans.** α = k/(ρc)
- **Q.** Define *Steady State*.  
  **Ans.** temperature independent of time
- **Q.** Define *Plane Wall Resistance*.  
  **Ans.** R = L/(kA)
- **Q.** Define *Cylinder Resistance*.  
  **Ans.** R = ln(r2/r1)/(2πkL)
- **Q.** Define *Composite Wall*.  
  **Ans.** resistances in series add
- **Q.** Define *Critical Radius for Cylinder*.  
  **Ans.** rc = k/h
- **Q.** Define *Critical Radius for Sphere*.  
  **Ans.** rc = 2k/h
- **Q.** Define *Overall Heat Transfer Coefficient*.  
  **Ans.** U = 1/ΣR per unit area

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Modes of Heat Transfer* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Heat moves by conduction, convection and radiation. Fourier's Law — q = −kA dT/dx; Newton's Law of Cooling — q = hA(Ts − T∞); Stefan-Boltzmann Law — E = σT⁴.
- **Q.** Explain *Heat Conduction Equation* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** General equation governs temperature fields. General Conduction Equation — ∇²T + q/k = (1/α)∂T/∂t; Thermal Diffusivity — α = k/(ρc); Steady State — temperature independent of time.
- **Q.** Explain *Plane Wall, Cylinder & Sphere* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** 1D steady conduction uses thermal resistance. Plane Wall Resistance — R = L/(kA); Cylinder Resistance — R = ln(r2/r1)/(2πkL); Composite Wall — resistances in series add.
- **Q.** Explain *Critical Radius of Insulation* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Insulation can increase heat loss below a radius. Critical Radius for Cylinder — rc = k/h; Critical Radius for Sphere — rc = 2k/h; Overall Heat Transfer Coefficient — U = 1/ΣR per unit area.

## Unit 2: Extended Surfaces & Transient Conduction

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Fin*.  
  **Ans.** extended surface enhancing convection
- **Q.** Define *Fin Parameter*.  
  **Ans.** m = √(hP/(kA))
- **Q.** Define *Long Fin Heat Transfer*.  
  **Ans.** q = √(hPkA)·θ0
- **Q.** Define *Fin Efficiency*.  
  **Ans.** actual heat transfer / ideal heat transfer at base temperature
- **Q.** Define *Fin Effectiveness*.  
  **Ans.** heat transfer with fin / without fin
- **Q.** Define *Fin Justification*.  
  **Ans.** useful when effectiveness ≥ 2
- **Q.** Define *Biot Number*.  
  **Ans.** Bi = hLc/k
- **Q.** Define *Lumped Condition*.  
  **Ans.** Bi < 0.1
- **Q.** Define *Lumped Solution*.  
  **Ans.** (T − T∞)/(Ti − T∞) = e^(−hAt/(ρVc))
- **Q.** Define *Heisler Charts*.  
  **Ans.** centre temperature for slabs, cylinders and spheres
- **Q.** Define *Fourier Number*.  
  **Ans.** Fo = αt/L²
- **Q.** Define *Semi-infinite Solid*.  
  **Ans.** body extending infinitely in one direction

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Fins* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Fins increase surface area for heat transfer. Fin — extended surface enhancing convection; Fin Parameter — m = √(hP/(kA)); Long Fin Heat Transfer — q = √(hPkA)·θ0.
- **Q.** Explain *Fin Efficiency & Effectiveness* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Measures of fin performance. Fin Efficiency — actual heat transfer / ideal heat transfer at base temperature; Fin Effectiveness — heat transfer with fin / without fin; Fin Justification — useful when effectiveness ≥ 2.
- **Q.** Explain *Lumped Capacitance Method* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Small bodies have uniform temperature. Biot Number — Bi = hLc/k; Lumped Condition — Bi < 0.1; Lumped Solution — (T − T∞)/(Ti − T∞) = e^(−hAt/(ρVc)).
- **Q.** Explain *Transient Charts* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Heisler charts solve larger bodies. Heisler Charts — centre temperature for slabs, cylinders and spheres; Fourier Number — Fo = αt/L²; Semi-infinite Solid — body extending infinitely in one direction.

## Unit 3: Convection

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Forced Convection*.  
  **Ans.** flow driven externally by pump or fan
- **Q.** Define *Natural Convection*.  
  **Ans.** flow driven by buoyancy
- **Q.** Define *Thermal Boundary Layer*.  
  **Ans.** region of temperature variation near a wall
- **Q.** Define *Nusselt Number*.  
  **Ans.** Nu = hL/k
- **Q.** Define *Prandtl Number*.  
  **Ans.** Pr = ν/α
- **Q.** Define *Grashof Number*.  
  **Ans.** Gr = gβΔTL³/ν²
- **Q.** Define *Dittus-Boelter Equation*.  
  **Ans.** Nu = 0.023Re^0.8Pr^n for turbulent pipe flow
- **Q.** Define *Laminar Flat Plate*.  
  **Ans.** Nu = 0.664Re^0.5Pr^(1/3)
- **Q.** Define *Reynolds Analogy*.  
  **Ans.** relates heat transfer and friction
- **Q.** Define *Pool Boiling Curve*.  
  **Ans.** nucleate, transition and film boiling
- **Q.** Define *Critical Heat Flux*.  
  **Ans.** maximum heat flux in nucleate boiling
- **Q.** Define *Dropwise Condensation*.  
  **Ans.** higher h than filmwise condensation

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Convection Fundamentals* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Convection combines conduction and fluid motion. Forced Convection — flow driven externally by pump or fan; Natural Convection — flow driven by buoyancy; Thermal Boundary Layer — region of temperature variation near a wall.
- **Q.** Explain *Dimensionless Numbers* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Correlations use dimensionless groups. Nusselt Number — Nu = hL/k; Prandtl Number — Pr = ν/α; Grashof Number — Gr = gβΔTL³/ν².
- **Q.** Explain *Forced Convection Correlations* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Empirical correlations give h. Dittus-Boelter Equation — Nu = 0.023Re^0.8Pr^n for turbulent pipe flow; Laminar Flat Plate — Nu = 0.664Re^0.5Pr^(1/3); Reynolds Analogy — relates heat transfer and friction.
- **Q.** Explain *Boiling & Condensation* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Phase change gives high heat transfer. Pool Boiling Curve — nucleate, transition and film boiling; Critical Heat Flux — maximum heat flux in nucleate boiling; Dropwise Condensation — higher h than filmwise condensation.

## Unit 4: Radiation

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Black Body*.  
  **Ans.** ideal absorber and emitter
- **Q.** Define *Emissivity*.  
  **Ans.** ratio of emission to black body emission
- **Q.** Define *Kirchhoff's Law*.  
  **Ans.** emissivity equals absorptivity at equilibrium
- **Q.** Define *Planck's Law*.  
  **Ans.** spectral distribution of black body radiation
- **Q.** Define *Wien's Displacement Law*.  
  **Ans.** λmax·T = 2898 µm·K
- **Q.** Define *Grey Body*.  
  **Ans.** emissivity independent of wavelength
- **Q.** Define *View Factor*.  
  **Ans.** fraction of radiation leaving one surface reaching another
- **Q.** Define *Reciprocity Relation*.  
  **Ans.** A1F12 = A2F21
- **Q.** Define *Summation Rule*.  
  **Ans.** sum of view factors from a surface is 1
- **Q.** Define *Radiation Network*.  
  **Ans.** surface and space resistances
- **Q.** Define *Radiation Shield*.  
  **Ans.** thin sheet reducing heat exchange
- **Q.** Define *Two Parallel Plates*.  
  **Ans.** q = σ(T1⁴ − T2⁴)/(1/ε1 + 1/ε2 − 1)

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Radiation Fundamentals* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** All bodies emit thermal radiation. Black Body — ideal absorber and emitter; Emissivity — ratio of emission to black body emission; Kirchhoff's Law — emissivity equals absorptivity at equilibrium.
- **Q.** Explain *Planck's & Wien's Laws* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Spectral emission depends on temperature. Planck's Law — spectral distribution of black body radiation; Wien's Displacement Law — λmax·T = 2898 µm·K; Grey Body — emissivity independent of wavelength.
- **Q.** Explain *View Factors* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Geometry determines radiation exchange. View Factor — fraction of radiation leaving one surface reaching another; Reciprocity Relation — A1F12 = A2F21; Summation Rule — sum of view factors from a surface is 1.
- **Q.** Explain *Radiation Exchange & Shields* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Surfaces exchange radiation through networks. Radiation Network — surface and space resistances; Radiation Shield — thin sheet reducing heat exchange; Two Parallel Plates — q = σ(T1⁴ − T2⁴)/(1/ε1 + 1/ε2 − 1).

## Unit 5: Heat Exchangers & Mass Transfer

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Parallel Flow*.  
  **Ans.** both fluids flow in same direction
- **Q.** Define *Counter Flow*.  
  **Ans.** fluids flow in opposite directions; most effective
- **Q.** Define *Shell and Tube Exchanger*.  
  **Ans.** tubes inside a shell with baffles
- **Q.** Define *LMTD*.  
  **Ans.** (ΔT1 − ΔT2)/ln(ΔT1/ΔT2)
- **Q.** Define *Heat Duty*.  
  **Ans.** Q = UA·LMTD
- **Q.** Define *Correction Factor*.  
  **Ans.** applied for cross-flow and multipass exchangers
- **Q.** Define *Effectiveness*.  
  **Ans.** actual heat transfer / maximum possible
- **Q.** Define *NTU*.  
  **Ans.** UA/Cmin
- **Q.** Define *Capacity Ratio*.  
  **Ans.** Cmin/Cmax
- **Q.** Define *Fick's Law*.  
  **Ans.** J = −D dC/dx
- **Q.** Define *Mass Transfer Coefficient*.  
  **Ans.** analogous to heat transfer coefficient
- **Q.** Define *Schmidt Number*.  
  **Ans.** Sc = ν/D

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Heat Exchanger Types* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Exchangers transfer heat between fluids. Parallel Flow — both fluids flow in same direction; Counter Flow — fluids flow in opposite directions; most effective; Shell and Tube Exchanger — tubes inside a shell with baffles.
- **Q.** Explain *LMTD Method* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Log mean temperature difference sizes exchangers. LMTD — (ΔT1 − ΔT2)/ln(ΔT1/ΔT2); Heat Duty — Q = UA·LMTD; Correction Factor — applied for cross-flow and multipass exchangers.
- **Q.** Explain *NTU-Effectiveness Method* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Effectiveness method rates exchangers. Effectiveness — actual heat transfer / maximum possible; NTU — UA/Cmin; Capacity Ratio — Cmin/Cmax.
- **Q.** Explain *Mass Transfer* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Concentration differences drive mass transfer. Fick's Law — J = −D dC/dx; Mass Transfer Coefficient — analogous to heat transfer coefficient; Schmidt Number — Sc = ν/D.
