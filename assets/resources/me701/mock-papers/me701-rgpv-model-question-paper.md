## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Fourier's Law: q = −kA dT/dx
- **Q1(b):** General Conduction Equation: ∇²T + q/k = (1/α)∂T/∂t | Thermal Diffusivity: α = k/(ρc) | Steady State: temperature independent of time
- **Q2(a):** Fin Effectiveness: heat transfer with fin / without fin
- **Q2(b):** Biot Number: Bi = hLc/k | Lumped Condition: Bi < 0.1 | Lumped Solution: (T − T∞)/(Ti − T∞) = e^(−hAt/(ρVc))
- **Q3(a):** Reynolds Analogy: relates heat transfer and friction
- **Q3(b):** Pool Boiling Curve: nucleate, transition and film boiling | Critical Heat Flux: maximum heat flux in nucleate boiling | Dropwise Condensation: higher h than filmwise condensation
- **Q4(a):** Radiation Network: surface and space resistances
- **Q4(b):** Black Body: ideal absorber and emitter | Emissivity: ratio of emission to black body emission | Kirchhoff's Law: emissivity equals absorptivity at equilibrium
- **Q5(a):** Counter Flow: fluids flow in opposite directions; most effective
- **Q5(b):** LMTD: (ΔT1 − ΔT2)/ln(ΔT1/ΔT2) | Heat Duty: Q = UA·LMTD | Correction Factor: applied for cross-flow and multipass exchangers
- **Q6(a):** Overall Heat Transfer Coefficient: U = 1/ΣR per unit area
- **Q6(b):** Fourier's Law: q = −kA dT/dx | Newton's Law of Cooling: q = hA(Ts − T∞) | Stefan-Boltzmann Law: E = σT⁴
- **Q7(a):** Forced Convection: flow driven externally by pump or fan
- **Q7(b):** Nusselt Number: Nu = hL/k | Prandtl Number: Pr = ν/α | Grashof Number: Gr = gβΔTL³/ν²
- **Q8(a):** Heat Duty: Q = UA·LMTD
- **Q8(b):** Effectiveness: actual heat transfer / maximum possible | NTU: UA/Cmin | Capacity Ratio: Cmin/Cmax
