## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Fuel-air Cycle: accounts for variable specific heats
- **Q1(b):** Zero-dimensional Model: single zone in cylinder | Two-zone Model: burned and unburned zones | Wiebe Function: burn fraction profile
- **Q2(a):** Premixed Combustion Phase: rapid heat release
- **Q2(b):** Spray Penetration: distance travelled by fuel jet | Atomisation: break-up into droplets | Swirl Ratio: rotational air motion
- **Q3(a):** Lean Burn: excess air improves efficiency
- **Q3(b):** Downsizing: smaller boosted engines | Turbocharging: exhaust-driven compressor | Miller Cycle: late intake valve closing
- **Q4(a):** BS-VI: Indian emission standard
- **Q4(b):** Thermal NOx: Zeldovich mechanism | Soot Formation: in fuel-rich zones | Unburned HC: crevice and wall quenching
- **Q5(a):** Biodiesel: FAME from vegetable oils
- **Q5(b):** CNG: compressed natural gas | Hydrogen Engine: zero carbon combustion | Ammonia Fuel: carbon-free hydrogen carrier
- **Q6(a):** Knock Limited Heat Release: in SI engines
- **Q6(b):** Fuel-air Cycle: accounts for variable specific heats | Actual Cycle Losses: heat, time, exhaust and pumping losses | Indicated Efficiency: indicated work / fuel energy
- **Q7(a):** HCCI: premixed charge autoignition
- **Q7(b):** PCCI: premixed charge compression ignition | RCCI: dual fuels of different reactivity | Reactivity Stratification: controls ignition
- **Q8(a):** Hydrogen Engine: zero carbon combustion
- **Q8(b):** Parallel Hybrid: engine and motor both drive | Engine Load Shifting: operates engine efficiently | Atkinson Cycle Engine: efficiency-focused hybrid engine
