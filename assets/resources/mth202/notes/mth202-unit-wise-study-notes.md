# MTH-202 Advanced IC Engines & Emissions — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Engine thermodynamics, combustion modelling, advanced combustion modes, emissions formation and aftertreatment, alternative fuels.

## Unit 1: Engine Thermodynamics

### Fuel-air Cycle Analysis

Realistic cycle analysis.

- **Fuel-air Cycle:** accounts for variable specific heats
- **Actual Cycle Losses:** heat, time, exhaust and pumping losses
- **Indicated Efficiency:** indicated work / fuel energy

### Thermodynamic Engine Models

Models predict performance.

- **Zero-dimensional Model:** single zone in cylinder
- **Two-zone Model:** burned and unburned zones
- **Wiebe Function:** burn fraction profile

### Gas Exchange

Breathing determines power.

- **Volumetric Efficiency:** air inducted / swept volume
- **Valve Timing:** influences gas exchange
- **Scavenging:** clearing burnt gases in two-strokes

### Heat Transfer in Engines

Heat loss affects efficiency.

- **Woschni Correlation:** in-cylinder heat transfer coefficient
- **Thermal Barrier Coating:** reduces heat loss
- **Knock Limited Heat Release:** in SI engines

## Unit 2: Combustion

### SI Combustion

Flame propagation and knock.

- **Turbulent Flame Speed:** enhanced by turbulence
- **Knock:** autoignition of end gas
- **Octane Number:** knock resistance

### CI Combustion

Diffusion-controlled combustion.

- **Ignition Delay:** physical and chemical delay
- **Premixed Combustion Phase:** rapid heat release
- **Mixing-controlled Phase:** diffusion combustion

### Spray & Mixing

Fuel injection controls combustion.

- **Spray Penetration:** distance travelled by fuel jet
- **Atomisation:** break-up into droplets
- **Swirl Ratio:** rotational air motion

### Combustion Diagnostics

Measuring combustion.

- **In-cylinder Pressure Measurement:** basis for analysis
- **Heat Release Rate:** from pressure data
- **Optical Engine:** visual access to combustion

## Unit 3: Advanced Combustion Modes

### HCCI

Homogeneous charge compression ignition.

- **HCCI:** premixed charge autoignition
- **Low Temperature Combustion:** low NOx and soot
- **Combustion Phasing Control:** main HCCI challenge

### PCCI & RCCI

Partially premixed and reactivity controlled.

- **PCCI:** premixed charge compression ignition
- **RCCI:** dual fuels of different reactivity
- **Reactivity Stratification:** controls ignition

### GDI & Lean Burn

Direct injection petrol engines.

- **GDI:** gasoline direct injection
- **Stratified Charge:** rich near plug, lean overall
- **Lean Burn:** excess air improves efficiency

### Downsizing & Boosting

Higher specific output.

- **Downsizing:** smaller boosted engines
- **Turbocharging:** exhaust-driven compressor
- **Miller Cycle:** late intake valve closing

## Unit 4: Emissions Formation & Control

### Pollutant Formation

Mechanisms of emissions.

- **Thermal NOx:** Zeldovich mechanism
- **Soot Formation:** in fuel-rich zones
- **Unburned HC:** crevice and wall quenching

### In-cylinder Control

Reducing emissions at source.

- **EGR:** lowers combustion temperature
- **Injection Rate Shaping:** controls heat release
- **Water Injection:** reduces NOx

### Aftertreatment

Exhaust treatment devices.

- **Three-way Catalyst:** for stoichiometric SI engines
- **DPF:** diesel particulate filter
- **SCR:** urea-based NOx reduction

### Emission Regulations & Testing

Standards and test cycles.

- **BS-VI:** Indian emission standard
- **RDE:** real driving emissions testing
- **WLTP:** worldwide harmonised light vehicle test procedure

## Unit 5: Alternative Fuels & Electrification

### Biofuels

Renewable liquid fuels.

- **Ethanol Blends:** E10, E20 petrol blends
- **Biodiesel:** FAME from vegetable oils
- **Renewable Diesel:** hydrotreated vegetable oil

### Gaseous Fuels

Cleaner gaseous fuels.

- **CNG:** compressed natural gas
- **Hydrogen Engine:** zero carbon combustion
- **Ammonia Fuel:** carbon-free hydrogen carrier

### Hybrid Powertrains

Engines with electric motors.

- **Parallel Hybrid:** engine and motor both drive
- **Engine Load Shifting:** operates engine efficiently
- **Atkinson Cycle Engine:** efficiency-focused hybrid engine

### Engine Calibration

Tuning for performance and emissions.

- **ECU Calibration:** maps for fuel and timing
- **Model-based Calibration:** using models to reduce testing
- **OBD:** on-board diagnostics
