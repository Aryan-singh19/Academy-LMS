## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Engineering Geology: geology applied to civil engineering
- **Q1(b):** Physical Weathering: mechanical breakdown by temperature and frost | Chemical Weathering: decomposition by chemical reactions | Erosion: removal of material by water, wind or ice
- **Q2(a):** Feldspar: most abundant mineral group
- **Q2(b):** Granite: coarse-grained intrusive igneous rock | Basalt: fine-grained extrusive igneous rock | Shale: fine sedimentary rock from clay
- **Q3(a):** Fault Zone: crushed rock along fault
- **Q3(b):** Joint: fracture without displacement | Unconformity: gap in geological record | Engineering Significance: joints reduce rock strength and allow seepage
- **Q4(a):** Landslide: downslope movement of soil and rock
- **Q4(b):** Site Investigation: surface and subsurface exploration | Core Drilling: obtains rock cores | RQD: rock quality designation from core recovery
- **Q5(a):** Electromagnetic Spectrum: range of wavelengths used
- **Q5(b):** Passive Sensor: records reflected sunlight | Active Sensor: sends its own energy, e.g. radar | IRS Satellites: Indian remote sensing series
- **Q6(a):** Richter Scale: logarithmic measure of magnitude
- **Q6(b):** Engineering Geology: geology applied to civil engineering | Earth Structure: crust, mantle and core | Weathering: breakdown of rocks at surface
- **Q7(a):** Dip: angle of inclination of bed from horizontal
- **Q7(b):** Anticline: upward arching fold | Syncline: downward trough fold | Fold Axis: line about which beds are folded
- **Q8(a):** Active Sensor: sends its own energy, e.g. radar
- **Q8(b):** Spatial Resolution: smallest detectable object size | False Colour Composite: infrared shown as red | Elements of Interpretation: tone, texture, shape, pattern
