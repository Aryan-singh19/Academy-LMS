  **Ans.** measures dip and strike
- **Q.** Define *Anticline*.

- **Q.** Define *Syncline*.

- **Q.** Define *Normal Fault*.  
  **Ans.** hanging wall moves down
- **Q.** Define *Reverse Fault*.

  **Ans.** crushed rock along fault
- **Q.** Define *Joint*.  
  **Ans.** fracture without displacement
- **Q.** Define *Unconformity*.

- **Q.** Define *Engineering Significance*.  
  **Ans.** joints reduce rock strength and allow seepage

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Dip & Strike* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Bedding attitude is measured in the field. Dip — angle of inclination of bed from horizontal; Strike — direction of horizontal line on bed; Clinometer Compass — measures dip and strike.
- **Q.** Explain *Folds* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Rocks bend under compression. Anticline — upward arching fold; Syncline — downward trough fold; Fold Axis — line about which beds are folded.
- **Q.** Explain *Faults* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Rocks break and move along faults. Normal Fault — hanging wall moves down; Reverse Fault — hanging wall moves up; Fault Zone — crushed rock along fault.
- **Q.** Explain *Joints & Unconformities* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Fractures and gaps in rock record. Joint — fracture without displacement; Unconformity — gap in geological record; Engineering Significance — joints reduce rock strength and allow seepage.
