# CE-405 Engineering Geology & Remote Sensing — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Earth processes, minerals, rocks, structural geology, geology for engineering projects and remote sensing applications.

## Unit 1: General Geology

### Scope of Engineering Geology

Geology informs safe construction.

- **Engineering Geology:** geology applied to civil engineering
- **Earth Structure:** crust, mantle and core
- **Weathering:** breakdown of rocks at surface

### Weathering & Erosion

Surface processes shape landforms.

- **Physical Weathering:** mechanical breakdown by temperature and frost
- **Chemical Weathering:** decomposition by chemical reactions
- **Erosion:** removal of material by water, wind or ice

### Geological Agents

Rivers, wind and glaciers modify landscape.

- **River Erosion:** forms valleys and meanders
- **Aeolian Landforms:** wind-formed features like dunes
- **Glacial Landforms:** U-shaped valleys and moraines

### Earthquakes

Earthquakes result from fault movement.

- **Focus:** point of origin inside earth
- **Epicentre:** point on surface above focus
- **Richter Scale:** logarithmic measure of magnitude

## Unit 2: Mineralogy & Petrology

### Minerals

Minerals are naturally occurring crystalline solids.

- **Mineral:** inorganic solid with definite composition
- **Mohs Hardness Scale:** talc 1 to diamond 10
- **Physical Properties:** colour, lustre, streak, cleavage

### Rock-forming Minerals

Common minerals form rocks.

- **Quartz:** SiO2, very hard and resistant
- **Feldspar:** most abundant mineral group
- **Mica:** sheet silicate with perfect cleavage

### Igneous & Sedimentary Rocks

Rocks form by different processes.

- **Granite:** coarse-grained intrusive igneous rock
- **Basalt:** fine-grained extrusive igneous rock
- **Shale:** fine sedimentary rock from clay

### Metamorphic Rocks

Heat and pressure transform rocks.

- **Marble:** metamorphosed limestone
- **Slate:** metamorphosed shale with cleavage
- **Gneiss:** banded high-grade metamorphic rock

## Unit 3: Structural Geology

### Dip & Strike

Bedding attitude is measured in the field.

- **Dip:** angle of inclination of bed from horizontal
- **Strike:** direction of horizontal line on bed
- **Clinometer Compass:** measures dip and strike

### Folds

Rocks bend under compression.

- **Anticline:** upward arching fold
- **Syncline:** downward trough fold
- **Fold Axis:** line about which beds are folded

### Faults

Rocks break and move along faults.

- **Normal Fault:** hanging wall moves down
- **Reverse Fault:** hanging wall moves up
- **Fault Zone:** crushed rock along fault

### Joints & Unconformities

Fractures and gaps in rock record.

- **Joint:** fracture without displacement
- **Unconformity:** gap in geological record
- **Engineering Significance:** joints reduce rock strength and allow seepage

## Unit 4: Geology for Civil Works

### Geological Investigations

Site investigations precede construction.

- **Site Investigation:** surface and subsurface exploration
- **Core Drilling:** obtains rock cores
- **RQD:** rock quality designation from core recovery

### Dams & Reservoirs

Dam sites need sound geology.

- **Dam Site Selection:** strong impervious foundation rock
- **Reservoir Leakage:** through porous or faulted rock
- **Grouting:** sealing rock fractures

### Tunnels

Tunnel stability depends on rock.

- **Tunnel in Folded Strata:** alignment along axis preferred
- **Overbreak:** excess excavation beyond profile
- **Rock Bolting:** reinforcing tunnel rock

### Landslides & Groundwater

Slopes and water affect safety.

- **Landslide:** downslope movement of soil and rock
- **Aquifer:** water-bearing rock layer
- **Slope Stabilisation:** drainage, retaining walls and anchors

## Unit 5: Remote Sensing

### Principles of Remote Sensing

Sensors record electromagnetic energy.

- **Remote Sensing:** acquiring information without contact
- **Electromagnetic Spectrum:** range of wavelengths used
- **Spectral Signature:** unique reflectance of a feature

### Sensors & Platforms

Satellites carry sensors.

- **Passive Sensor:** records reflected sunlight
- **Active Sensor:** sends its own energy, e.g. radar
- **IRS Satellites:** Indian remote sensing series

### Image Interpretation

Images are interpreted visually or digitally.

- **Spatial Resolution:** smallest detectable object size
- **False Colour Composite:** infrared shown as red
- **Elements of Interpretation:** tone, texture, shape, pattern

### Applications in Civil Engineering

Remote sensing supports planning.

- **Land Use Mapping:** classification of land cover
- **Watershed Management:** planning water resources
- **Disaster Monitoring:** floods and landslides mapping
