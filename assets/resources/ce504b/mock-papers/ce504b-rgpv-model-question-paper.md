## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** EMR: energy propagating as waves
- **Q1(b):** Reflectance: ratio of reflected to incident energy | Spectral Signature: reflectance curve of material | Vegetation Red Edge: sharp rise in reflectance near 700 nm
- **Q2(a):** Backscatter: energy returned to radar
- **Q2(b):** Cartosat: high-resolution mapping satellite | Resourcesat: resource monitoring satellite | RISAT: radar imaging satellite
- **Q3(a):** Maximum Likelihood Classifier: statistical classifier
- **Q3(b):** Error Matrix: compares classified and reference data | Overall Accuracy: correct pixels / total | Kappa Coefficient: agreement beyond chance
- **Q4(a):** Digitisation: converting maps to vector
- **Q4(b):** GIS: system to capture, store and analyse spatial data | Spatial Data: data with location | Attribute Data: non-spatial characteristics
- **Q5(a):** Overlay Analysis: combining layers
- **Q5(b):** DEM: digital elevation model | Slope and Aspect: derived terrain parameters | Watershed Delineation: drainage basin extraction
- **Q6(a):** Swath: width of ground imaged
- **Q6(b):** EMR: energy propagating as waves | Atmospheric Window: wavelengths passing through atmosphere | Scattering: redirection of radiation by particles
- **Q7(a):** Radiometric Correction: removes sensor and atmospheric errors
- **Q7(b):** Contrast Stretching: expands range of values | NDVI: (NIR − Red)/(NIR + Red) vegetation index | Filtering: smooths or sharpens image
- **Q8(a):** Slope and Aspect: derived terrain parameters
- **Q8(b):** Site Suitability Analysis: multi-criteria site selection | Route Planning: optimal alignment of roads | Urban Planning: land use and growth mapping
