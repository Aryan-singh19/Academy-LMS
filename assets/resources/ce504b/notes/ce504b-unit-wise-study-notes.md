# CE-504(B) Remote Sensing & GIS — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Remote sensing physics, sensors, image processing, GIS data models, spatial analysis and civil engineering applications.

## Unit 1: Remote Sensing Physics

### Electromagnetic Radiation

Energy interacts with atmosphere and surface.

- **EMR:** energy propagating as waves
- **Atmospheric Window:** wavelengths passing through atmosphere
- **Scattering:** redirection of radiation by particles

### Energy Interaction with Surface

Features reflect energy differently.

- **Reflectance:** ratio of reflected to incident energy
- **Spectral Signature:** reflectance curve of material
- **Vegetation Red Edge:** sharp rise in reflectance near 700 nm

### Resolutions

Sensors are characterised by resolution.

- **Spatial Resolution:** ground pixel size
- **Spectral Resolution:** number and width of bands
- **Temporal Resolution:** revisit frequency

### Orbits & Platforms

Satellites carry sensors in orbits.

- **Sun-synchronous Orbit:** same local time crossing
- **Geostationary Orbit:** fixed over equator
- **Swath:** width of ground imaged

## Unit 2: Sensors & Data Products

### Optical Sensors

Optical sensors record reflected light.

- **Multispectral Sensor:** several broad bands
- **Hyperspectral Sensor:** hundreds of narrow bands
- **Panchromatic:** single broad band, high resolution

### Microwave Sensors

Radar sensors work day and night.

- **SAR:** synthetic aperture radar
- **Backscatter:** energy returned to radar
- **InSAR:** measures ground deformation

### Indian Satellites

India has strong EO programme.

- **Cartosat:** high-resolution mapping satellite
- **Resourcesat:** resource monitoring satellite
- **RISAT:** radar imaging satellite

### Thermal Remote Sensing

Thermal sensors record emitted energy.

- **Thermal Infrared:** 8-14 µm band
- **Emissivity:** emitting efficiency of surface
- **Urban Heat Island:** warmer urban areas

## Unit 3: Digital Image Processing

### Preprocessing

Corrections remove distortions.

- **Radiometric Correction:** removes sensor and atmospheric errors
- **Geometric Correction:** aligns image to map
- **Georeferencing:** assigning coordinates to image

### Image Enhancement

Enhancement improves visual interpretation.

- **Contrast Stretching:** expands range of values
- **NDVI:** (NIR − Red)/(NIR + Red) vegetation index
- **Filtering:** smooths or sharpens image

### Image Classification

Pixels are grouped into classes.

- **Supervised Classification:** uses training samples
- **Unsupervised Classification:** clustering without training
- **Maximum Likelihood Classifier:** statistical classifier

### Accuracy Assessment

Maps are checked against ground truth.

- **Error Matrix:** compares classified and reference data
- **Overall Accuracy:** correct pixels / total
- **Kappa Coefficient:** agreement beyond chance

## Unit 4: Geographic Information System

### GIS Components

GIS integrates hardware, software, data and people.

- **GIS:** system to capture, store and analyse spatial data
- **Spatial Data:** data with location
- **Attribute Data:** non-spatial characteristics

### Data Models

Vector and raster represent geography.

- **Vector Model:** points, lines and polygons
- **Raster Model:** grid cells
- **Topology:** spatial relationships among features

### Map Projections & Coordinates

Earth is projected onto a plane.

- **Map Projection:** transforms globe to plane
- **UTM:** universal transverse Mercator projection
- **Datum:** reference surface, e.g. WGS84

### Data Input & Management

Data enters GIS from many sources.

- **Digitisation:** converting maps to vector
- **Geodatabase:** spatial database
- **Metadata:** data about data

## Unit 5: Spatial Analysis & Applications

### Spatial Analysis

GIS answers spatial questions.

- **Buffer Analysis:** zone around features
- **Overlay Analysis:** combining layers
- **Network Analysis:** shortest routes on networks

### Terrain Analysis

Elevation models support design.

- **DEM:** digital elevation model
- **Slope and Aspect:** derived terrain parameters
- **Watershed Delineation:** drainage basin extraction

### Applications in Infrastructure

GIS supports planning.

- **Site Suitability Analysis:** multi-criteria site selection
- **Route Planning:** optimal alignment of roads
- **Urban Planning:** land use and growth mapping

### Applications in Water & Disaster

GIS manages hazards.

- **Flood Mapping:** flood extent from imagery
- **Groundwater Prospect Mapping:** combining geology and terrain
- **Disaster Management:** risk zoning and response
