## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Mechatronics: synergistic integration of mechanical, electrical and computer engineering
- **Q1(b):** Sensor: element producing a signal related to the measured quantity | Transducer: converts one energy form into another | Signal Processor: conditions signals for display or control
- **Q2(a):** Hall Effect Sensor: senses magnetic field
- **Q2(b):** Piezoelectric Sensor: charge produced by force | Strain Gauge Load Cell: measures force | Thermocouple: temperature sensor based on Seebeck effect
- **Q3(a):** DAC: converts digital value into analog signal
- **Q3(b):** DAQ: hardware and software acquiring signals | Sampling Rate: samples per second | Multiplexer: selects one of several channels
- **Q4(a):** Solenoid: electromagnet producing linear motion
- **Q4(b):** DC Motor: speed varies with armature voltage | Stepper Motor: moves in discrete steps | Servo Motor: closed-loop motor for precise positioning
- **Q5(a):** PWM: pulse-width modulation for motor speed
- **Q5(b):** PLC: rugged computer for industrial control | Scan Cycle: read inputs, execute program, update outputs | I/O Module: interfaces field devices to PLC
- **Q6(a):** Modelling and Simulation: predicting behaviour before building
- **Q6(b):** Mechatronics: synergistic integration of mechanical, electrical and computer engineering | Mechatronic System: sensors, actuators, controller and mechanical structure | Examples: washing machine, ABS brakes, robots
- **Q7(a):** Inverting Amplifier: gain −Rf/Rin
- **Q7(b):** Low-pass Filter: removes high-frequency noise | Optical Isolation: optocoupler separates circuits | Wheatstone Bridge: converts resistance change to voltage
- **Q8(a):** Scan Cycle: read inputs, execute program, update outputs
- **Q8(b):** Ladder Diagram: graphical PLC programming language | Timer Instruction: delays an output | Latching: output held on after input removed
