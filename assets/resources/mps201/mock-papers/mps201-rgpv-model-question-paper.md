- **Q1(a):** Frequency Deviation: due to generation-load mismatch
- **Q1(b):** LFC Block Diagram: governor, turbine and power system | Steady-state Error: −ΔPL/(β) with droop | Integral Control: eliminates steady-state error
- **Q2(a):** SVC: static VAR compensator
- **Q2(b):** Primary Voltage Control: AVR action | Secondary Voltage Control: regional coordination | Tertiary Voltage Control: system-wide optimisation
- **Q3(a):** ML Forecasting: neural networks and gradient boosting
- **Q3(b):** WAMS: wide area measurement system | PMU: synchronised phasor measurements | Oscillation Monitoring: detects poorly damped modes
- **Q4(a):** Ancillary Services: reserves, frequency and voltage support
- **Q4(b):** Deregulation: unbundling of generation, transmission, distribution | Electricity Act 2003: Indian power sector reform law | Open Access: non-discriminatory transmission use
- **Q5(a):** Ramp Rate: rate of change of generation
- **Q5(b):** Flexible Generation: fast-ramping units | Battery Storage: fast frequency response | Demand Response: shifting consumer load
- **Q6(a):** Renewable Impact: reduced inertia challenges
- **Q6(b):** Frequency Deviation: due to generation-load mismatch | Primary Control: governor droop response | Secondary Control: restores nominal frequency
- **Q7(a):** Load Despatch Centre: operates grid, e.g. NLDC, RLDC, SLDC
- **Q7(b):** State Estimation: system state from measurements | Contingency Analysis: security assessment | Optimal Power Flow: economic and secure dispatch
- **Q8(a):** Battery Storage: fast frequency response
- **Q8(b):** System Inertia: stored kinetic energy | RoCoF: rate of change of frequency | Synthetic Inertia: converter-based inertial response
