## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Power Diode: uncontrolled two-layer switch
- **Q1(b):** Forward Blocking Mode: off state with forward voltage | Latching Current: minimum current to latch on | Gate Triggering: turning on by gate pulse
- **Q2(a):** Semi Converter: mix of SCRs and diodes
- **Q2(b):** Three-phase Full Converter: Vdc = (3√3 Vm/π) cos α | Six-pulse Output: lower ripple | Overlap Angle: due to source inductance
- **Q3(a):** Regenerative Braking: power returned to source
- **Q3(b):** Buck Converter: step-down | Boost Converter: Vo = Vs/(1 − D) | Buck-Boost Converter: inverted output of variable magnitude
- **Q4(a):** CSI: current source inverter
- **Q4(b):** Half-bridge Inverter: output ± Vs/2 | Full-bridge Inverter: output ± Vs | Square Wave Output: contains harmonics
- **Q5(a):** Integral Cycle Control: switching whole cycles
- **Q5(b):** Fan Regulator: TRIAC-based speed control | Light Dimmer: phase-controlled lamp brightness | Soft Starter: reduces motor starting current
- **Q6(a):** GTO: gate turn-off thyristor
- **Q6(b):** Power Diode: uncontrolled two-layer switch | SCR: four-layer PNPN device turned on by gate | Holding Current: minimum current to stay on
- **Q7(a):** Chopper: DC-DC switching converter
- **Q7(b):** Constant Frequency: PWM control | Variable Frequency: frequency modulation | Current Limit Control: switch on current limits
- **Q8(a):** Light Dimmer: phase-controlled lamp brightness
- **Q8(b):** Cycloconverter: converts frequency without DC link | Step-down Cycloconverter: output frequency lower than input | Applications: large low-speed drives
