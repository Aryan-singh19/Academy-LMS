## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Soil Exploration: investigation of subsoil
- **Q1(b):** Disturbed Sample: structure altered | Undisturbed Sample: retains structure, e.g. Shelby tube | Area Ratio: indicates sampler disturbance
- **Q2(a):** Bearing Capacity Factors: depend on φ
- **Q2(b):** Shape Factors: for square and circular footings | Depth Factors: for embedment | Inclination Factors: for inclined loads
- **Q3(a):** Basement: provides compensation
- **Q3(b):** Black Cotton Soil: expansive clay | Under-reamed Pile: for expansive soils | CNS Layer: cohesive non-swelling soil cushion
- **Q4(a):** Laterally Loaded Pile: analysed by Broms' method
- **Q4(b):** End Bearing Pile: load to hard stratum | Friction Pile: load by skin friction | Bored Cast-in-situ Pile: drilled and concreted on site
- **Q5(a):** Well Sinking: excavation inside well
- **Q5(b):** Pneumatic Caisson: compressed air chamber | Cofferdam: temporary enclosure to exclude water | Sheet Pile: interlocking piles for cofferdams
- **Q6(a):** Borehole Log: record of soil layers
- **Q6(b):** Soil Exploration: investigation of subsoil | Trial Pit: open excavation for inspection | Boring: drilling holes to sample soil
- **Q7(a):** Isolated Footing: under single column
- **Q7(b):** Immediate Settlement: elastic settlement | Consolidation Settlement: time-dependent in clays | Differential Settlement: unequal settlement causing distress
- **Q8(a):** Cofferdam: temporary enclosure to exclude water
- **Q8(b):** Preloading: surcharge to consolidate soil | Vertical Drains: speed consolidation | Stone Columns: reinforce soft soils
