### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Bearing Capacity Concepts* in detail with a neat diagram.

- **Q.** Explain *Terzaghi's Theory* in detail with a neat diagram.

- **Q.** Explain *Effect of Water Table* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Water reduces bearing capacity. Water Table Correction — reduces unit weight terms; Local Shear Failure — loose soils; Punching Shear Failure — very loose or soft soils.

## Unit 4: Pile Foundations

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *End Bearing Pile*.  
  **Ans.** load to hard stratum
- **Q.** Define *Friction Pile*.  
  **Ans.** load by skin friction
- **Q.** Define *Bored Cast-in-situ Pile*.  
  **Ans.** drilled and concreted on site
- **Q.** Define *Static Formula*.  
  **Ans.** Qu = Qb + Qs
- **Q.** Define *Dynamic Formula*.  
  **Ans.** Hiley's or Engineering News formula
- **Q.** Define *Pile Load Test*.  
  **Ans.** direct measurement of capacity
- **Q.** Define *Group Efficiency*.  
  **Ans.** group capacity / sum of individual capacities
- **Q.** Define *Block Failure*.  
  **Ans.** group fails as a block
- **Q.** Define *Negative Skin Friction*.  
  **Ans.** downdrag in consolidating soil
- **Q.** Define *Laterally Loaded Pile*.  
  **Ans.** analysed by Broms' method
- **Q.** Define *Pile Cap*.  
  **Ans.** connects pile heads
- **Q.** Define *Batter Pile*.  
  **Ans.** inclined pile resisting lateral load

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Types of Piles* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Piles transfer load to deep strata. End Bearing Pile — load to hard stratum; Friction Pile — load by skin friction; Bored Cast-in-situ Pile — drilled and concreted on site.
- **Q.** Explain *Load Capacity of Piles* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Capacity is estimated statically or dynamically. Static Formula — Qu = Qb + Qs; Dynamic Formula — Hiley's or Engineering News formula; Pile Load Test — direct measurement of capacity.
- **Q.** Explain *Pile Groups* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Piles act together in groups. Group Efficiency — group capacity / sum of individual capacities; Block Failure — group fails as a block; Negative Skin Friction — downdrag in consolidating soil.
- **Q.** Explain *Lateral Load & Pile Caps* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Piles also resist lateral loads. Laterally Loaded Pile — analysed by Broms' method; Pile Cap — connects pile heads; Batter Pile — inclined pile resisting lateral load.
