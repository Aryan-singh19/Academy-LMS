# CE-802(B) Foundation Engineering — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Soil exploration, bearing capacity, shallow and pile foundations, well foundations and ground improvement.

## Unit 1: Soil Exploration

### Site Investigation

Exploration determines subsurface conditions.

- **Soil Exploration:** investigation of subsoil
- **Trial Pit:** open excavation for inspection
- **Boring:** drilling holes to sample soil

### Sampling

Samples are disturbed or undisturbed.

- **Disturbed Sample:** structure altered
- **Undisturbed Sample:** retains structure, e.g. Shelby tube
- **Area Ratio:** indicates sampler disturbance

### In-situ Tests

Field tests measure properties.

- **SPT:** standard penetration test N value
- **Cone Penetration Test:** measures tip and sleeve resistance
- **Plate Load Test:** bearing capacity and settlement

### Geophysical Methods

Indirect methods explore large areas.

- **Seismic Refraction:** velocity of waves in strata
- **Electrical Resistivity:** resistivity of strata
- **Borehole Log:** record of soil layers

## Unit 2: Bearing Capacity

### Bearing Capacity Concepts

Foundations must not shear or settle excessively.

- **Ultimate Bearing Capacity:** pressure causing shear failure
- **Safe Bearing Capacity:** ultimate divided by factor of safety
- **Allowable Bearing Pressure:** based on shear and settlement

### Terzaghi's Theory

Classical bearing capacity equation.

- **Terzaghi's Equation:** qu = cNc + qNq + 0.5γBNγ
- **Bearing Capacity Factors:** depend on φ
- **General Shear Failure:** dense soils with clear failure surface

### IS Code Method

Factors adjust for shape and depth.

- **Shape Factors:** for square and circular footings
- **Depth Factors:** for embedment
- **Inclination Factors:** for inclined loads

### Effect of Water Table

Water reduces bearing capacity.

- **Water Table Correction:** reduces unit weight terms
- **Local Shear Failure:** loose soils
- **Punching Shear Failure:** very loose or soft soils

## Unit 3: Shallow Foundations

### Types of Shallow Foundations

Footings and rafts spread loads.

- **Isolated Footing:** under single column
- **Combined Footing:** for two columns
- **Raft Foundation:** for poor soils or heavy loads

### Settlement Analysis

Settlement limits govern design.

- **Immediate Settlement:** elastic settlement
- **Consolidation Settlement:** time-dependent in clays
- **Differential Settlement:** unequal settlement causing distress

### Floating Foundation

Excavation offsets building load.

- **Floating Foundation:** weight of excavated soil balances load
- **Compensated Raft:** partially floating raft
- **Basement:** provides compensation

### Foundations on Expansive Soils

Swelling soils need special treatment.

- **Black Cotton Soil:** expansive clay
- **Under-reamed Pile:** for expansive soils
- **CNS Layer:** cohesive non-swelling soil cushion

## Unit 4: Pile Foundations

### Types of Piles

Piles transfer load to deep strata.

- **End Bearing Pile:** load to hard stratum
- **Friction Pile:** load by skin friction
- **Bored Cast-in-situ Pile:** drilled and concreted on site

### Load Capacity of Piles

Capacity is estimated statically or dynamically.

- **Static Formula:** Qu = Qb + Qs
- **Dynamic Formula:** Hiley's or Engineering News formula
- **Pile Load Test:** direct measurement of capacity

### Pile Groups

Piles act together in groups.

- **Group Efficiency:** group capacity / sum of individual capacities
- **Block Failure:** group fails as a block
- **Negative Skin Friction:** downdrag in consolidating soil

### Lateral Load & Pile Caps

Piles also resist lateral loads.

- **Laterally Loaded Pile:** analysed by Broms' method
- **Pile Cap:** connects pile heads
- **Batter Pile:** inclined pile resisting lateral load

## Unit 5: Well Foundations & Ground Improvement

### Well Foundations

Wells support bridge piers.

- **Well Foundation:** caisson sunk into river bed
- **Well Sinking:** excavation inside well
- **Tilt and Shift:** problems during sinking

### Caissons & Cofferdams

Structures for underwater construction.

- **Pneumatic Caisson:** compressed air chamber
- **Cofferdam:** temporary enclosure to exclude water
- **Sheet Pile:** interlocking piles for cofferdams

### Ground Improvement

Weak soils are improved.

- **Preloading:** surcharge to consolidate soil
- **Vertical Drains:** speed consolidation
- **Stone Columns:** reinforce soft soils

### Soil Stabilisation

Stabilisation strengthens soil.

- **Lime Stabilisation:** for clayey soils
- **Cement Stabilisation:** for granular soils
- **Geosynthetic Reinforcement:** improves bearing capacity
