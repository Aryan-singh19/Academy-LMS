## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Renewable Energy: source replenished naturally on a human timescale, e.g. solar, wind, hydro
- **Q1(b):** Photovoltaic Cell: semiconductor p-n junction that converts sunlight directly into DC electricity | Flat Plate Collector: absorber plate under glazing that heats water or air for low-temperature use | Solar Constant: about 1367 W/m² of radiation received outside Earth's atmosphere
- **Q2(a):** Food Web: interlocking network of many food chains
- **Q2(b):** Pyramid of Energy: always upright because energy is lost at every level | Ecological Succession: orderly replacement of communities until a climax community forms | Pioneer Species: first organisms to colonise bare or disturbed land
- **Q3(a):** Man-Wildlife Conflict: clashes when animals and people compete for space and resources
- **Q3(b):** In-situ Conservation: protection in natural habitat, e.g. national parks and biosphere reserves | Ex-situ Conservation: conservation outside habitat, e.g. zoos, seed banks, botanical gardens | Biosphere Reserve: protected area with core, buffer and transition zones
- **Q4(a):** Sanitary Landfill: engineered disposal site with liner and leachate collection
- **Q4(b):** Particulate Matter: suspended solid or liquid particles such as PM2.5 and PM10 | Electrostatic Precipitator: device that charges particles and collects them on plates | Photochemical Smog: secondary pollution formed when NOx and hydrocarbons react in sunlight
- **Q5(a):** Ozone Depletion: thinning of the stratospheric ozone layer by CFCs
- **Q5(b):** Sustainable Development: growth that balances economy, society and environment | Rainwater Harvesting: collecting and storing rainwater for reuse or recharge | Environmental Impact Assessment: study of a project's likely environmental effects before approval
- **Q6(a):** Fuel Cell: electrochemical device converting hydrogen and oxygen into electricity and water
- **Q6(b):** Renewable Energy: source replenished naturally on a human timescale, e.g. solar, wind, hydro | Non-renewable Energy: finite stock such as coal, oil, natural gas and nuclear fuel | Primary Energy: energy in its natural form before any conversion
- **Q7(a):** Genetic Diversity: variation of genes within a species
- **Q7(b):** Biodiversity Hotspot: region with at least 1500 endemic plants that has lost 70% of habitat | Endemic Species: species found naturally only in one area | Mega-diversity Nation: country holding a large share of world species; India is one of 17
- **Q8(a):** Rainwater Harvesting: collecting and storing rainwater for reuse or recharge
- **Q8(b):** Environment Protection Act 1986: umbrella law empowering the Centre to protect the environment | Water Act 1974: law for prevention and control of water pollution | Air Act 1981: law for prevention and control of air pollution
