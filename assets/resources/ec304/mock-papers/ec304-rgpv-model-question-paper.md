## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Energy Band Gap: energy gap between valence and conduction bands; about 1.1 eV for Si
- **Q1(b):** N-type: doped with pentavalent donors; electrons majority | P-type: doped with trivalent acceptors; holes majority | Mass Action Law: n·p = ni²
- **Q2(a):** Reverse Saturation Current: small current under reverse bias
- **Q2(b):** Transition Capacitance: depletion capacitance under reverse bias | Diffusion Capacitance: dominant capacitance under forward bias | Avalanche Breakdown: carrier multiplication at high reverse voltage
- **Q3(a):** Stability Factor: S = ∂IC/∂ICO
- **Q3(b):** h-parameter Model: hybrid parameters hie, hfe, hre, hoe | re Model: small-signal model with re = 26 mV/IE | Early Effect: base-width modulation with collector voltage
- **Q4(a):** CMOS Inverter: complementary pair with negligible static current
- **Q4(b):** JFET: field effect transistor controlled by gate-junction reverse bias | Pinch-off Voltage: VGS at which the channel closes | Shockley Equation: ID = IDSS(1 − VGS/VP)²
- **Q5(a):** Zener Regulator: keeps load voltage constant
- **Q5(b):** Tunnel Diode: negative resistance region from quantum tunnelling | Varactor Diode: capacitance varies with reverse voltage | Schottky Diode: metal-semiconductor diode with fast switching
- **Q6(a):** Recombination: electron filling a hole, ending both carriers
- **Q6(b):** Energy Band Gap: energy gap between valence and conduction bands; about 1.1 eV for Si | Intrinsic Semiconductor: pure semiconductor with equal electrons and holes | Fermi Level: energy with 50% occupation probability
- **Q7(a):** BJT: three-terminal current-controlled device
- **Q7(b):** Common Emitter: high voltage and current gain; phase inversion | Common Base: low input impedance; current gain near 1 | Common Collector: emitter follower with voltage gain near 1
- **Q8(a):** Varactor Diode: capacitance varies with reverse voltage
- **Q8(b):** LED: emits light when forward biased | Photodiode: generates current proportional to light | Solar Cell: large-area PN junction producing power from light
