# EX-501 Electrical Machine-II — Unit-wise Study Notes

> DC machines, synchronous generators and motors, and single-phase induction motors.

## Unit 1: DC Machines

### DC Generator Construction & EMF

DC generators convert mechanical to DC power.

- **Commutator:** converts AC in armature to DC
- **EMF Equation:** E = PΦZN/(60A)
- **Lap vs Wave Winding:** A = P for lap, A = 2 for wave

### Armature Reaction & Commutation

Armature flux distorts main field.

- **Armature Reaction:** effect of armature flux on main field
- **Interpoles:** improve commutation
- **Compensating Winding:** neutralises armature reaction

### DC Generator Characteristics

Excitation determines characteristics.

- **Shunt Generator:** voltage drops with load
- **Critical Field Resistance:** maximum for self-excitation
- **Compound Generator:** series field compensates drop

### DC Motor Principles

DC motors convert electrical to mechanical power.

- **Back EMF:** Eb = V − IaRa
- **Torque Equation:** T ∝ ΦIa
- **Series Motor:** high starting torque, never start without load

## Unit 2: DC Motor Control & Testing

### Speed Control of DC Motors

Speed depends on flux and voltage.

- **Speed Relation:** N ∝ Eb/Φ
- **Field Control:** above base speed
- **Armature Voltage Control:** below base speed

### Starting of DC Motors

Starters limit current.

- **Three-point Starter:** for shunt motors
- **Four-point Starter:** no-volt coil independent of field
- **Starting Resistance:** limits armature current

### Losses & Efficiency

Losses reduce output.

- **Copper Losses:** I²R in windings
- **Iron Losses:** hysteresis and eddy current
- **Maximum Efficiency:** when variable losses equal constant losses

### Testing of DC Machines

Tests find efficiency.

- **Swinburne's Test:** no-load test for constant losses
- **Hopkinson's Test:** regenerative test of two machines
- **Brake Test:** direct load test for small motors

## Unit 3: Synchronous Generators

### Alternator Construction

Rotating field produces AC.

- **Salient Pole Rotor:** for low-speed hydro alternators
- **Cylindrical Rotor:** for high-speed turbo alternators
- **Synchronous Speed:** Ns = 120f/P

### EMF Equation & Winding Factors

Winding design affects EMF.

- **Alternator EMF:** E = 4.44 f Φ T Kp Kd
- **Pitch Factor:** Kp = cos(α/2)
- **Distribution Factor:** Kd = sin(mβ/2)/(m sin(β/2))

### Voltage Regulation Methods

Regulation is predicted by tests.

- **Synchronous Impedance Method:** EMF method, pessimistic
- **MMF Method:** ampere-turn method, optimistic
- **ZPF Method:** Potier triangle method, accurate

### Two-reaction Theory

Salient pole machines need two axes.

- **Direct Axis Reactance:** Xd
- **Quadrature Axis Reactance:** Xq
- **Slip Test:** determines Xd and Xq

## Unit 4: Synchronous Machine Operation

### Parallel Operation

Alternators are synchronised to grid.

- **Synchronisation Conditions:** equal voltage, frequency and phase sequence
- **Synchroscope:** indicates phase difference
- **Load Sharing:** by governor and excitation control

### Synchronous Motor

Runs at constant speed.

- **Synchronous Motor:** not self-starting
- **Damper Winding:** enables starting and damps hunting
- **V-curves:** armature current versus field current

### Power Factor Control

Excitation controls power factor.

- **Over-excited Motor:** draws leading current
- **Synchronous Condenser:** improves power factor
- **Hunting:** oscillation about synchronous speed

### Power-Angle Characteristics

Power depends on load angle.

- **Power Equation:** P = (EV/Xs) sin δ
- **Steady-state Stability Limit:** δ = 90°
- **Load Angle:** angle between E and V

## Unit 5: Single-phase Induction Motors

### Double Field Revolving Theory

Single-phase field is two rotating fields.

- **Double Revolving Field Theory:** pulsating field as two opposite rotating fields
- **Not Self-starting:** net starting torque is zero
- **Forward and Backward Slip:** s and 2 − s

### Split-phase & Capacitor Motors

Auxiliary winding provides starting torque.

- **Split-phase Motor:** resistance difference creates phase shift
- **Capacitor Start Motor:** high starting torque
- **Permanent Split Capacitor:** capacitor always in circuit

### Shaded Pole & Universal Motors

Small motors for appliances.

- **Shaded Pole Motor:** shading ring creates rotating field
- **Universal Motor:** series motor running on AC and DC
- **Reluctance Motor:** runs at synchronous speed

### Applications

Motors suit different loads.

- **Fans:** PSC motors
- **Mixer:** universal motor
- **Compressor:** capacitor start capacitor run motor
