## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Basic Research: expands fundamental knowledge without immediate application
- **Q1(b):** Hypothesis: testable statement predicting a relationship | Research Design: plan for collecting and analysing data | Research Gap: unanswered question identified from literature
- **Q2(a):** ANOVA: compares means of several groups
- **Q2(b):** Factorial Design: varies several factors simultaneously | Taguchi Method: orthogonal arrays for robust design | Replication: repeating runs to estimate error
- **Q3(a):** Reference Manager: Zotero or Mendeley
- **Q3(b):** Peer Review: evaluation by experts before publication | Impact Factor: average citations per article | Open Access: freely available publication
- **Q4(a):** Reproducibility: others obtain same results with same data
- **Q4(b):** Research Ethics: principles of honesty and integrity | Informed Consent: participants agree knowingly | Conflict of Interest: competing interests affecting judgement
- **Q5(a):** Copyright: protects original works of authorship
- **Q5(b):** Novelty: invention must be new | Inventive Step: not obvious to a skilled person | Industrial Applicability: can be made or used in industry
- **Q6(a):** Feasibility: time, data and resources available
- **Q6(b):** Basic Research: expands fundamental knowledge without immediate application | Applied Research: solves a specific practical problem | Research Problem: clearly stated question the study answers
- **Q7(a):** IMRaD: introduction, methods, results and discussion
- **Q7(b):** Thesis Chapters: introduction, review, methodology, results, conclusion | Figures and Tables: present results clearly | Future Scope: directions for further work
- **Q8(a):** Inventive Step: not obvious to a skilled person
- **Q8(b):** Provisional Specification: early filing to secure priority date | Complete Specification: full description and claims | Patent Claims: define scope of protection
