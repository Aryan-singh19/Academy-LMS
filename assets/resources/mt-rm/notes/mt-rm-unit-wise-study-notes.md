# MT-RM Research Methodology & IPR — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Research design, literature review, data analysis, technical writing, ethics and intellectual property rights for M.Tech students.

## Unit 1: Research Fundamentals

### Meaning & Types of Research

Research is a systematic search for new knowledge.

- **Basic Research:** expands fundamental knowledge without immediate application
- **Applied Research:** solves a specific practical problem
- **Research Problem:** clearly stated question the study answers

### Research Process

Research follows a structured cycle.

- **Hypothesis:** testable statement predicting a relationship
- **Research Design:** plan for collecting and analysing data
- **Research Gap:** unanswered question identified from literature

### Literature Review

Reviews position the work among prior studies.

- **Systematic Review:** structured search with inclusion criteria
- **Citation Database:** Scopus, Web of Science, Google Scholar
- **Annotated Bibliography:** sources with summaries and critique

### Problem Formulation

Good problems are specific and feasible.

- **Objectives:** measurable aims of the research
- **Scope:** boundaries of the study
- **Feasibility:** time, data and resources available

## Unit 2: Data Collection & Analysis

### Data Collection Methods

Data comes from experiments, surveys and simulations.

- **Primary Data:** collected first-hand for the study
- **Secondary Data:** collected earlier by others
- **Sampling:** selecting a representative subset

### Statistical Analysis

Statistics test hypotheses.

- **t-test:** compares means of two groups
- **ANOVA:** compares means of several groups
- **Regression:** models relationship between variables

### Design of Experiments

Experiments are planned to isolate effects.

- **Factorial Design:** varies several factors simultaneously
- **Taguchi Method:** orthogonal arrays for robust design
- **Replication:** repeating runs to estimate error

### Modelling & Simulation

Models test ideas cheaply.

- **Simulation:** imitating system behaviour on computer
- **Validation:** checking model against reality
- **Sensitivity Analysis:** effect of parameter changes

## Unit 3: Technical Writing

### Structure of a Research Paper

Papers follow IMRaD.

- **IMRaD:** introduction, methods, results and discussion
- **Abstract:** concise summary of the whole paper
- **Keywords:** terms used for indexing

### Thesis Writing

A dissertation documents the full work.

- **Thesis Chapters:** introduction, review, methodology, results, conclusion
- **Figures and Tables:** present results clearly
- **Future Scope:** directions for further work

### Referencing Styles

Citations credit prior work.

- **IEEE Style:** numbered citations in brackets
- **APA Style:** author-date citations
- **Reference Manager:** Zotero or Mendeley

### Publishing

Journals and conferences disseminate research.

- **Peer Review:** evaluation by experts before publication
- **Impact Factor:** average citations per article
- **Open Access:** freely available publication

## Unit 4: Research Ethics

### Ethics in Research

Researchers must act honestly.

- **Research Ethics:** principles of honesty and integrity
- **Informed Consent:** participants agree knowingly
- **Conflict of Interest:** competing interests affecting judgement

### Plagiarism

Copying without credit is misconduct.

- **Plagiarism:** using others' work without attribution
- **Similarity Check:** software such as Turnitin
- **Self-plagiarism:** reusing own published text without citation

### Research Misconduct

Fabrication and falsification undermine science.

- **Fabrication:** making up data
- **Falsification:** manipulating data or results
- **Predatory Journal:** publishes without genuine peer review

### Reproducibility

Results must be repeatable.

- **Reproducibility:** others obtain same results with same data
- **Open Data:** sharing datasets publicly
- **Code Availability:** publishing analysis code

## Unit 5: Intellectual Property Rights

### Types of IPR

IPR protects creations of the mind.

- **Patent:** exclusive right to an invention for 20 years
- **Copyright:** protects original works of authorship
- **Trademark:** distinctive sign identifying goods

### Patents

Patents require specific criteria.

- **Novelty:** invention must be new
- **Inventive Step:** not obvious to a skilled person
- **Industrial Applicability:** can be made or used in industry

### Patent Process in India

Applications follow defined stages.

- **Provisional Specification:** early filing to secure priority date
- **Complete Specification:** full description and claims
- **Patent Claims:** define scope of protection

### Other IPR & Technology Transfer

Other protections and commercialisation.

- **Industrial Design:** protects appearance of products
- **Geographical Indication:** product linked to origin
- **Technology Transfer:** licensing research to industry
