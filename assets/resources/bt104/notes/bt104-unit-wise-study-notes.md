# BT-104 Basic Electrical & Electronics Engineering — Unit-wise Study Notes

> DC circuits, AC circuits, magnetic circuits, transformers, machines and basic electronics.

## Unit 1: DC Circuits

### Circuit Elements & Ohm's Law

Basic laws describe electric circuits.

- **Ohm's Law:** V = IR
- **Resistivity:** R = ρL/A
- **Power:** P = VI = I²R

### Kirchhoff's Laws

KCL and KVL analyse networks.

- **KCL:** sum of currents at a node is zero
- **KVL:** sum of voltages around a loop is zero
- **Voltage Divider:** Vout = Vin·R2/(R1 + R2)

### Network Theorems

Theorems simplify circuits.

- **Thevenin's Theorem:** equivalent voltage source with series resistance
- **Norton's Theorem:** equivalent current source with parallel resistance
- **Superposition:** response is sum of individual source responses

### Star-Delta Transformation

Networks are converted for analysis.

- **Delta to Star:** RA = R1R2/(R1 + R2 + R3)
- **Star to Delta:** R12 = R1 + R2 + R1R2/R3
- **Application:** simplifying bridge networks

## Unit 2: AC Circuits

### AC Fundamentals

Alternating quantities vary sinusoidally.

- **RMS Value:** Vm/√2 for a sine wave
- **Average Value:** 2Vm/π for a half cycle
- **Form Factor:** RMS / average = 1.11

### Phasors & Single-phase Circuits

Phasors represent sinusoids.

- **Impedance:** Z = R + jX
- **Power Factor:** cos φ, ratio of real to apparent power
- **Inductive Reactance:** XL = 2πfL

### Resonance

Series RLC resonates when XL = XC.

- **Resonant Frequency:** f0 = 1/(2π√(LC))
- **Quality Factor:** Q = ω0L/R
- **Series Resonance:** impedance minimum, current maximum

### Three-phase Systems

Three-phase supply powers industry.

- **Star Connection:** VL = √3 Vph, IL = Iph
- **Delta Connection:** VL = Vph, IL = √3 Iph
- **Three-phase Power:** P = √3 VL IL cos φ

## Unit 3: Magnetic Circuits & Transformers

### Magnetic Circuits

Magnetic circuits are analogous to electric circuits.

- **MMF:** magnetomotive force NI
- **Reluctance:** S = l/(μA)
- **Flux:** Φ = MMF / reluctance

### Electromagnetic Induction

Changing flux induces emf.

- **Faraday's Law:** e = −N dΦ/dt
- **Lenz's Law:** induced current opposes the change
- **Self Inductance:** L = NΦ/I

### Single-phase Transformer

Transformers change AC voltage levels.

- **EMF Equation:** E = 4.44 f N Φm
- **Turns Ratio:** V1/V2 = N1/N2
- **Ideal Transformer:** no losses

### Transformer Losses & Efficiency

Losses reduce efficiency.

- **Core Loss:** hysteresis and eddy current loss
- **Copper Loss:** I²R loss in windings
- **Maximum Efficiency:** when copper loss equals core loss

## Unit 4: Electrical Machines

### DC Machines

DC machines convert energy with commutators.

- **DC Generator EMF:** E = PΦZN/(60A)
- **Back EMF:** Eb = V − IaRa in a motor
- **DC Series Motor:** high starting torque

### Three-phase Induction Motor

Most common industrial motor.

- **Synchronous Speed:** Ns = 120f/P
- **Slip:** (Ns − N)/Ns
- **Rotating Magnetic Field:** produced by three-phase stator currents

### Single-phase Motors

Small appliances use single-phase motors.

- **Split-phase Motor:** auxiliary winding for starting
- **Capacitor Start Motor:** capacitor gives starting torque
- **Universal Motor:** runs on AC and DC

### Synchronous Machines

Alternators generate power at constant speed.

- **Alternator:** synchronous generator
- **Synchronous Motor:** runs at constant synchronous speed
- **Not Self-starting:** synchronous motors need starting methods

## Unit 5: Basic Electronics

### Semiconductor Diode

Diodes conduct in one direction.

- **PN Junction:** junction of p- and n-type semiconductor
- **Forward Bias:** p connected to positive; diode conducts
- **Zener Diode:** regulates voltage in reverse breakdown

### Rectifiers

Rectifiers convert AC to DC.

- **Half-wave Rectifier:** uses one diode
- **Bridge Rectifier:** four diodes, full-wave output
- **Ripple Factor:** AC content in rectified output

### Transistors

Transistors amplify and switch.

- **BJT:** bipolar junction transistor
- **CE Configuration:** common emitter amplifier
- **Transistor as Switch:** operates in cut-off and saturation

### Digital Electronics Basics

Logic gates process binary signals.

- **Logic Gate:** performs Boolean operation
- **Universal Gate:** NAND or NOR
- **Number Systems:** binary, octal, decimal and hexadecimal
