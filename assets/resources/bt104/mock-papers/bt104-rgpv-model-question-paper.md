## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Ohm's Law: V = IR
- **Q1(b):** KCL: sum of currents at a node is zero | KVL: sum of voltages around a loop is zero | Voltage Divider: Vout = Vin·R2/(R1 + R2)
- **Q2(a):** Power Factor: cos φ, ratio of real to apparent power
- **Q2(b):** Resonant Frequency: f0 = 1/(2π√(LC)) | Quality Factor: Q = ω0L/R | Series Resonance: impedance minimum, current maximum
- **Q3(a):** Ideal Transformer: no losses
- **Q3(b):** Core Loss: hysteresis and eddy current loss | Copper Loss: I²R loss in windings | Maximum Efficiency: when copper loss equals core loss
- **Q4(a):** Alternator: synchronous generator
- **Q4(b):** DC Generator EMF: E = PΦZN/(60A) | Back EMF: Eb = V − IaRa in a motor | DC Series Motor: high starting torque
- **Q5(a):** Forward Bias: p connected to positive; diode conducts
- **Q5(b):** Half-wave Rectifier: uses one diode | Bridge Rectifier: four diodes, full-wave output | Ripple Factor: AC content in rectified output
- **Q6(a):** Application: simplifying bridge networks
- **Q6(b):** Ohm's Law: V = IR | Resistivity: R = ρL/A | Power: P = VI = I²R
- **Q7(a):** MMF: magnetomotive force NI
- **Q7(b):** Faraday's Law: e = −N dΦ/dt | Lenz's Law: induced current opposes the change | Self Inductance: L = NΦ/I
- **Q8(a):** Bridge Rectifier: four diodes, full-wave output
- **Q8(b):** BJT: bipolar junction transistor | CE Configuration: common emitter amplifier | Transistor as Switch: operates in cut-off and saturation
