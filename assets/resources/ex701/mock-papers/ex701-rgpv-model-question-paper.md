## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** Protective Relay: senses fault and trips breaker
- **Q1(b):** Selectivity: isolating only faulty section | Sensitivity: detecting smallest fault | Reliability: operating when required
- **Q2(a):** Time Multiplier Setting: scales operating time
- **Q2(b):** Directional Relay: operates for current in one direction | Differential Relay: operates on difference of currents | Percentage Differential: bias for CT mismatch
- **Q3(a):** Frame Leakage Protection: for metal-clad switchgear
- **Q3(b):** Thermal Overload Relay: models motor heating | Negative Sequence Relay: detects unbalance | Locked Rotor Protection: protects during stall
- **Q4(a):** Breaking Capacity: maximum interruptible current
- **Q4(b):** Pilot Protection: communication between line ends | Carrier Current Protection: PLCC-based scheme | Auto-reclosing: recloses after transient faults
- **Q5(a):** Comparator: compares amplitude or phase
- **Q5(b):** Numerical Relay: digital processing of sampled signals | Fourier Algorithm: extracts phasors | Self-monitoring: continuous internal checks
- **Q6(a):** Peterson Coil: arc suppression coil
- **Q6(b):** Protective Relay: senses fault and trips breaker | Zone of Protection: region a relay protects | Primary and Backup Protection: main and fallback protection
- **Q7(a):** Stator Differential Protection: for internal faults
- **Q7(b):** Buchholz Relay: gas-actuated relay for oil transformers | Harmonic Restraint: prevents tripping on inrush | Restricted Earth Fault: protects winding against earth faults
- **Q8(a):** Fourier Algorithm: extracts phasors
- **Q8(b):** IEC 61850: substation communication standard | GOOSE Message: fast peer-to-peer signalling | IED: intelligent electronic device
