## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** IGBT: high-voltage high-current switch
- **Q1(b):** SiC MOSFET: high voltage, high temperature | GaN HEMT: very fast switching | Switching Loss: energy lost during transitions
- **Q2(a):** Forward Converter: transfers energy directly
- **Q2(b):** ZVS: zero voltage switching | ZCS: zero current switching | LLC Converter: resonant converter for high efficiency
- **Q3(a):** Flying Capacitor Inverter: capacitor-clamped levels
- **Q3(b):** MMC: submodules in arms | Submodule: half-bridge with capacitor | Capacitor Voltage Balancing: key MMC control
- **Q4(a):** Grid-following Converter: injects current following grid
- **Q4(b):** PLL: phase-locked loop for grid angle | SRF-PLL: synchronous reference frame PLL | Grid Code: connection requirements
- **Q5(a):** Wind Converter: back-to-back converter
- **Q5(b):** Vector Control: field-oriented control | DTC: direct torque control | Regenerative Drive: returns energy to grid
- **Q6(a):** Heat Sink Design: based on losses
- **Q6(b):** IGBT: high-voltage high-current switch | Power MOSFET: fast switching at low voltage | Safe Operating Area: voltage-current limits
- **Q7(a):** Three-phase VSI: six-switch bridge
- **Q7(b):** Space Vector: represents switching states | SVPWM: 15% better DC bus utilisation | Sector Identification: locates reference vector
- **Q8(a):** DTC: direct torque control
- **Q8(b):** On-board Charger: AC to DC in vehicle | DC Fast Charger: high-power off-board converter | Wireless Charging: inductive power transfer
