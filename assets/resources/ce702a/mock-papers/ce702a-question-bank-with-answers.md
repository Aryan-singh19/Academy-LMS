### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Pre-tensioning*.

- **Q.** Define *Magnel-Blaton System*.

## Unit 3: Losses of Prestress

### Short answer (2-3 marks)

- **Q.** Define *Elastic Shortening*.  
  **Ans.** shortening of concrete at transfer
- **Q.** Define *Anchorage Slip*.  
  **Ans.** loss due to wedge seating
- **Q.** Define *Friction Loss*.  
  **Ans.** P = P0 e^(−(μα + kx)) in post-tensioning
- **Q.** Define *Shrinkage Loss*.  
  **Ans.** due to concrete shrinkage
- **Q.** Define *Creep Loss*.  
  **Ans.** due to sustained compressive stress
- **Q.** Define *Relaxation Loss*.  
  **Ans.** due to steel relaxation
- **Q.** Define *Total Loss*.  
  **Ans.** about 15-25% of initial prestress
- **Q.** Define *Effective Prestress*.  
  **Ans.** initial prestress minus losses
- **Q.** Define *Loss Ratio*.  
  **Ans.** effective / initial prestress
- **Q.** Define *Overstressing*.  
  **Ans.** initial stress above design to offset losses
- **Q.** Define *Restressing*.  
  **Ans.** re-tensioning tendons
- **Q.** Define *Low Relaxation Strand*.  
  **Ans.** reduces relaxation loss

### Long answer (7 marks)

- **Q.** Explain *Immediate Losses* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Losses occur during transfer. Elastic Shortening — shortening of concrete at transfer; Anchorage Slip — loss due to wedge seating; Friction Loss — P = P0 e^(−(μα + kx)) in post-tensioning.
- **Q.** Explain *Time-dependent Losses* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Losses grow with time. Shrinkage Loss — due to concrete shrinkage; Creep Loss — due to sustained compressive stress; Relaxation Loss — due to steel relaxation.
- **Q.** Explain *Total Loss Estimation* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Typical losses are budgeted. Total Loss — about 15-25% of initial prestress; Effective Prestress — initial prestress minus losses; Loss Ratio — effective / initial prestress.
- **Q.** Explain *Control of Losses* in detail with a neat diagram.  
  **Key points:** Techniques reduce losses. Overstressing — initial stress above design to offset losses; Restressing — re-tensioning tendons; Low Relaxation Strand — reduces relaxation loss.
