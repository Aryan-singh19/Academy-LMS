## Answer Hints (Set A)

- **Q1(a):** BOD: oxygen demand of microbes over 5 days at 20 °C
- **Q1(b):** Separate System: sewage and storm water carried separately | Combined System: single sewer for both | Partially Separate System: part of storm water in sewers
- **Q2(a):** Surface Overflow Rate: flow / surface area
- **Q2(b):** Equalisation Tank: dampens flow variations | Neutralisation: adjusting pH | Industrial Wastewater: may need pretreatment
- **Q3(a):** Rotating Biological Contactor: rotating discs with biofilm
- **Q3(b):** Oxidation Pond: algae-bacteria symbiosis | UASB Reactor: upflow anaerobic sludge blanket | Septic Tank: anaerobic on-site treatment
- **Q4(a):** Greywater Reuse: reuse of non-toilet wastewater
- **Q4(b):** Sludge Thickening: increases solids content | Anaerobic Digestion: stabilises sludge, produces biogas | Sludge Drying Bed: dewatering sludge
- **Q5(a):** Secondary Pollutant: formed in atmosphere, e.g. ozone
- **Q5(b):** Cyclone Separator: removes coarse particles | Electrostatic Precipitator: removes fine particulates | Wet Scrubber: removes gases and particles
- **Q6(a):** Inverted Siphon: carries sewage under obstacles
- **Q6(b):** BOD: oxygen demand of microbes over 5 days at 20 °C | COD: oxygen equivalent of oxidisable matter | TSS: total suspended solids
- **Q7(a):** Aerobic Treatment: microbes use oxygen
- **Q7(b):** Activated Sludge Process: aeration with recycled sludge | SVI: sludge volume index indicating settleability | MLSS: mixed liquor suspended solids
- **Q8(a):** Electrostatic Precipitator: removes fine particulates
- **Q8(b):** Municipal Solid Waste: urban domestic and commercial waste | SWM Rules 2016: Indian rules on waste management | Segregation at Source: separating wet and dry waste
