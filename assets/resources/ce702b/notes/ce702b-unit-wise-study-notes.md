# CE-702(B) Environmental Engineering-II — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Wastewater characteristics, sewer design, primary, secondary and tertiary treatment, sludge, air and solid waste management.

## Unit 1: Wastewater Characteristics & Collection

### Sewage Characteristics

Wastewater quality guides treatment.

- **BOD:** oxygen demand of microbes over 5 days at 20 °C
- **COD:** oxygen equivalent of oxidisable matter
- **TSS:** total suspended solids

### Sewerage Systems

Sewers collect wastewater.

- **Separate System:** sewage and storm water carried separately
- **Combined System:** single sewer for both
- **Partially Separate System:** part of storm water in sewers

### Sewer Design

Sewers flow by gravity.

- **Manning's Formula:** V = (1/n) R^(2/3) S^(1/2)
- **Self-cleansing Velocity:** prevents deposition, about 0.6-0.9 m/s
- **Peak Factor:** maximum to average flow

### Sewer Appurtenances

Structures maintain sewers.

- **Manhole:** access for inspection
- **Drop Manhole:** handles large level difference
- **Inverted Siphon:** carries sewage under obstacles

## Unit 2: Primary Treatment

### Preliminary Treatment

Large solids and grit are removed.

- **Screens:** remove floating matter
- **Grit Chamber:** removes sand and grit
- **Skimming Tank:** removes oil and grease

### Primary Sedimentation

Settleable solids are removed.

- **Primary Clarifier:** removes about 60% suspended solids
- **Surface Overflow Rate:** flow / surface area
- **Detention Time:** typically 2 hours

### Equalisation & Neutralisation

Flow and pH are stabilised.

- **Equalisation Tank:** dampens flow variations
- **Neutralisation:** adjusting pH
- **Industrial Wastewater:** may need pretreatment

### Chemical Treatment

Chemicals aid removal.

- **Chemical Precipitation:** removes phosphorus
- **Coagulation in Wastewater:** improves settling
- **Oil Separator:** removes floating oils

## Unit 3: Secondary Treatment

### Biological Treatment Principles

Microbes oxidise organic matter.

- **Aerobic Treatment:** microbes use oxygen
- **F/M Ratio:** food to microorganism ratio
- **Biomass:** microbial population

### Activated Sludge Process

Suspended growth aerobic process.

- **Activated Sludge Process:** aeration with recycled sludge
- **SVI:** sludge volume index indicating settleability
- **MLSS:** mixed liquor suspended solids

### Trickling Filters

Attached growth process.

- **Trickling Filter:** biofilm on media
- **Recirculation:** improves efficiency
- **Rotating Biological Contactor:** rotating discs with biofilm

### Stabilisation Ponds & Anaerobic Treatment

Low-cost and anaerobic options.

- **Oxidation Pond:** algae-bacteria symbiosis
- **UASB Reactor:** upflow anaerobic sludge blanket
- **Septic Tank:** anaerobic on-site treatment

## Unit 4: Sludge & Tertiary Treatment

### Sludge Treatment

Sludge is thickened and stabilised.

- **Sludge Thickening:** increases solids content
- **Anaerobic Digestion:** stabilises sludge, produces biogas
- **Sludge Drying Bed:** dewatering sludge

### Tertiary Treatment

Advanced treatment polishes effluent.

- **Nutrient Removal:** nitrogen and phosphorus removal
- **Membrane Bioreactor:** biological treatment with membranes
- **Disinfection of Effluent:** chlorination or UV

### Disposal of Effluent

Effluent is discharged or reused.

- **Dilution:** disposal into water bodies
- **Streeter-Phelps Equation:** oxygen sag curve
- **Land Disposal:** irrigation with effluent

### Wastewater Reuse

Treated water is recycled.

- **Greywater Reuse:** reuse of non-toilet wastewater
- **Zero Liquid Discharge:** no effluent discharge
- **Water Recycling:** treated water for non-potable use

## Unit 5: Air & Solid Waste Management

### Air Pollution Sources & Effects

Emissions affect health.

- **Primary Pollutant:** emitted directly, e.g. SO2
- **Secondary Pollutant:** formed in atmosphere, e.g. ozone
- **AQI:** air quality index

### Air Pollution Control

Devices reduce emissions.

- **Cyclone Separator:** removes coarse particles
- **Electrostatic Precipitator:** removes fine particulates
- **Wet Scrubber:** removes gases and particles

### Solid Waste Management

Waste is collected and processed.

- **Municipal Solid Waste:** urban domestic and commercial waste
- **SWM Rules 2016:** Indian rules on waste management
- **Segregation at Source:** separating wet and dry waste

### Waste Processing & Disposal

Waste is treated and disposed.

- **Composting:** aerobic decomposition
- **Incineration:** combustion of waste
- **Sanitary Landfill:** engineered disposal site
