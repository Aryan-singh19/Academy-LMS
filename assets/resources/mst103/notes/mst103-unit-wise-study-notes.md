# MST-103 Advanced Concrete Technology — Unit-wise Study Notes

> Cement chemistry, admixtures, mix design, special concretes, durability, testing and sustainable concrete.

## Unit 1: Cement Chemistry & Hydration

### Cement Composition

Clinker compounds govern behaviour.

- **C3S:** alite, early strength
- **C2S:** belite, later strength
- **C3A:** aluminate, rapid hydration and sulphate vulnerability

### Hydration Process

Cement reacts with water.

- **C-S-H Gel:** main strength-giving product
- **Calcium Hydroxide:** by-product of hydration
- **Heat of Hydration:** exothermic release

### Supplementary Cementitious Materials

SCMs improve performance.

- **Fly Ash:** pozzolan from coal combustion
- **GGBS:** ground granulated blast furnace slag
- **Silica Fume:** very fine pozzolan improving strength

### Microstructure

Pores and transition zone.

- **Capillary Pores:** control permeability
- **Interfacial Transition Zone:** weak zone around aggregates
- **Gel Pores:** very fine pores within C-S-H

## Unit 2: Admixtures & Aggregates

### Chemical Admixtures

Admixtures modify properties.

- **Superplasticiser:** high-range water reducer, e.g. PCE
- **Retarder:** delays setting
- **Air-entraining Agent:** improves freeze-thaw resistance

### Mineral Admixtures

Pozzolanic reaction benefits.

- **Pozzolanic Reaction:** CH + silica forms C-S-H
- **Metakaolin:** calcined clay pozzolan
- **Rice Husk Ash:** agricultural pozzolan

### Aggregate Properties

Aggregates affect strength and durability.

- **Alkali-Aggregate Reaction:** expansive reaction with reactive silica
- **Aggregate Grading:** particle size distribution
- **Recycled Aggregate:** from demolition waste

### Rheology of Fresh Concrete

Flow behaviour.

- **Bingham Model:** yield stress and plastic viscosity
- **Slump Flow:** test for self-compacting concrete
- **Thixotropy:** viscosity reduces with shearing

## Unit 3: Mix Design

### IS Method of Mix Design

IS 10262 procedure.

- **Target Mean Strength:** fck + 1.65s
- **Water-Cement Ratio Selection:** from strength and durability
- **Water Content:** from workability and aggregate size

### ACI Method

American mix design.

- **ACI 211:** absolute volume method
- **Bulk Volume of Coarse Aggregate:** from fineness modulus
- **Trial Mix:** laboratory verification

### High-strength Concrete Design

Strength above 60 MPa.

- **Low w/b Ratio:** about 0.25-0.35
- **Silica Fume Use:** densifies matrix
- **Packing Density:** optimised particle packing

### Statistical Quality Control

Variability in concrete.

- **Standard Deviation:** spread of strength results
- **Acceptance Criteria:** IS 456 compliance rules
- **Characteristic Strength:** 5% fractile

## Unit 4: Special Concretes

### Self-compacting Concrete

Flows without vibration.

- **SCC:** fills formwork under own weight
- **L-box Test:** passing ability
- **V-funnel Test:** viscosity measure

### Fibre Reinforced Concrete

Fibres improve toughness.

- **Steel Fibre:** improves flexural toughness
- **Polypropylene Fibre:** controls plastic shrinkage cracks
- **Toughness Index:** energy absorption measure

### Ultra-high Performance Concrete

Exceptional strength and durability.

- **UHPC:** compressive strength above 150 MPa
- **Reactive Powder Concrete:** fine powders, no coarse aggregate
- **Heat Curing:** accelerates strength

### Other Special Concretes

Concretes for special needs.

- **Lightweight Concrete:** density below 1800 kg/m³
- **Geopolymer Concrete:** alkali-activated binder
- **Pervious Concrete:** allows water to pass

## Unit 5: Durability & Testing

### Durability Mechanisms

Concrete deterioration processes.

- **Carbonation:** CO2 lowers pH causing corrosion
- **Chloride Attack:** depassivates reinforcement
- **Sulphate Attack:** expansive products

### Durability Design

Designing for service life.

- **Exposure Class:** environmental severity
- **Cover Depth:** protects reinforcement
- **Service Life Modelling:** Fick's law for chloride ingress

### Non-destructive Testing

Testing in-situ concrete.

- **Rebound Hammer:** surface hardness
- **UPV:** ultrasonic pulse velocity
- **Half-cell Potential:** corrosion probability

### Sustainable Concrete

Reducing carbon footprint.

- **Embodied Carbon:** CO2 from materials production
- **LC3:** limestone calcined clay cement
- **Carbon Capture in Concrete:** CO2 curing and mineralisation
