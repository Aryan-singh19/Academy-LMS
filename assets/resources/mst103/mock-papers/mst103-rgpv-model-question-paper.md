- **Q4(a):** Lightweight Concrete: density below 1800 kg/m³

- **Q6(a):** Gel Pores: very fine pores within C-S-H
- **Q6(b):** C3S: alite, early strength | C2S: belite, later strength | C3A: aluminate, rapid hydration and sulphate vulnerability
- **Q7(a):** Target Mean Strength: fck + 1.65s

- **Q8(a):** Cover Depth: protects reinforcement
